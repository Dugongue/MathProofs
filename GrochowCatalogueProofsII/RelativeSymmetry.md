# Small symmetric IPS certificates under large group actions

## GP-0728 · Compress an orbit average inside its linear span

Let a finite group $G$ act linearly over $\mathbb Q$ on the $X$ and placeholder $Y$ variables, preserving the two IPS substitutions. Equivalently, evaluation at $Y=0$ and evaluation at $Y=F(X)$ are equivariant for the specified action on $X$. Let $C$ be a size-$s$ circuit certificate, and let $W=\langle gC:g\in G\rangle_{\mathbb Q}$ have dimension $r$. Then there is a $G$-invariant rational IPS certificate of arithmetic size $O(r(s+n^2))$, where $n$ is the number of variables. The bound holds even when $G$ or the orbit of $C$ is exponentially large.

Every translate remains a certificate, because the two evaluation values are $0$ and $1$ and are fixed by the action. The Reynolds average lies in $W$. Take a basis from orbit elements and express the average in that basis. Each basis polynomial is computed by one copy of $C$ fed linear forms for the action, at cost $O(s+n^2)$. The weighted sum is the desired invariant certificate. The statement concerns arithmetic circuit size and the action on the polynomial computed by the circuit. If an orbit basis, the coordinate vector of $C$, and the action matrices of a generating list are supplied, the coefficients can also be recovered without enumerating $G$. The span $U$ of the images of $g_i-I$ over the generators is the space of all coinvariant relations. Indeed the dual annihilating space consists exactly of functionals fixed by all generators, hence by $G$. The Reynolds map gives $W=W^G\oplus U$. Solve for the unique vector in $W^G$ congruent to $C$ modulo $U$. This linear algebra is deterministic polynomial bit time in the supplied rational arrays.

The argument also works for a rational representation of a reductive algebraic group in characteristic zero, using its Reynolds projection and the linear span of the orbit. For the finite-group bit algorithm, the orbit matrices are part of the sufficient condition. [Symmetric IPS and full orbit averaging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16820)

## GP-0740 · Relative averaging preserves degree in modular characteristic

Let $G$ permute the variables and the indexed axioms of a system over a field $K$. Suppose a division-free degree-$d$ linear IPS certificate has a size-$s$ circuit $C$ whose input symmetry lifts for every element of a subgroup $H\le G$. If the index $q=[G:H]$ is invertible in $K$, there is a syntactically $G$-symmetric circuit certificate of degree at most $d$ and size $O(q(s+n))$, where $n$ is the number of variables and placeholders. This gives polynomial overhead for polynomial index even when the characteristic divides both group orders.

Use a separate copy of $C$ for each left coset $gH$, with input labels translated by its chosen representative $g$. Sum the outputs with one unbounded fan-in addition gate and multiply by $q^{-1}$. Each copy is a certificate and evaluation after averaging yields $0$ and $1$. Translates remain linear in $Y$ and preserve the degree bound. For any $a\in G$ and a representative $g$, write $ag=g'h$ with $g'$ the representative of $agH$ and $h\in H$. An $H$-lift of $h$ inside $C$ identifies the translated copy with the $g'$-copy after relabeling inputs by $a$. This extends to a circuit automorphism using the permutation of the cosets and fixing the root gates. Coherence of the lifts as a chosen group action is unnecessary for the usual definition, which requires each variable permutation to extend. If the $H$-lifts are supplied as an action, the coset recipe also gives an action.

For example, a certificate with all but one index treated symmetrically for the full symmetric group on $n$ indices has a syntactically symmetric degree-preserving certificate at cost $O(n)$ copies whenever the characteristic does not divide $n$. This still applies when $n!$ is zero in the base field. The antecedent differs from averaging every group element, and provides a size bound in addition to the catalogue's degree bound.
