# Even conjugators over the ternary field

For the square-zero spaces constructed from a scalar-centroid symmetric bimap with a split nonsingular slice, the ambient sign is the product of the two grade determinants, with the endpoint frame correction for a raw matrix presentation. The following sufficient conditions each give a deterministic polynomial many-one reduction to one ordinary TI instance over the same field. The scalar-centroid and split-slice hypotheses alone still leave an open case. No compiler here assumes a reduction from coTI.

| Sufficient condition | Construction |
| --- | --- |
| In a computed intrinsic input flag, the quadratic relation grades or their complementary quotients have logarithmic dimension and the explicit parity equations cancel the large-grade determinants | Keep the compatible small exterior volumes in one tensor diagram. The number of grades and the total relation dimension may grow. [Filtered relations and raw matrix recovery](FilteredRelations.md) |
| The computed intrinsic input flag has bounded successive dimensions, or suitable probes have a joint commutant with the stated bounded simple factors and polynomially many probe images | Construct a coset in a matrix group, solve subspace transport, and compute both attainable signs. [Flag and probe algorithms](ProbeGroups.md) |
| The intrinsic output subspaces leave \(d=O(\log L)\) dimensions of linear freedom in the output map | Enumerate at most \(3^d\) maps and compute the exact input-sign coset by simultaneous congruence. Every rank-one output line is recoverable even when the determinant polynomial has irreducible higher-degree factors. [Output recovery and fixed-action sign test](Frobenius.md) |

A concrete executable covers \(R=\operatorname{Sym}^2B\) with odd-dimensional relation support \(B\). The function accepts two dense based quotient tables. Each row corresponds to one output coordinate. Columns are the monomials \(x_i x_j\), \(i\le j\), in lexicographic order. It returns two \(1\times1\times1\) tensors. The split scalar-centroid example with input dimension 6 and output dimension 15 gives both affirmative self-pairs and ordinarily isomorphic negative pairs. [Python compiler and basis-change tests](compiler.py)

The reductions have written proofs. Lean verifies the finite-product identity used to assemble graded determinant characters. The independent kernel audit reports only the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound for that identity. The executable checks cover 161 sign computations and 24 additional independent basis-change comparisons, including 12 affirmative and 12 negative pairs. [Lean identity](GradeCharacter.lean) · [Finite checks](controls.py) · [Verification scope](Verification.json)

The remaining task on this route is to enforce the product sign with one polynomial-size ordinary TI instance on the residual inputs, including where these computations leave a large family of output maps and a large unresolved determinant on a relation grade. The calculations above give no bound forcing every promised pair into one of the sufficient classes. [Original special-linear question](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5)
