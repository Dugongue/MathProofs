# Dirichlet GRH, tensor isomorphism and short algebraic certificates

The strongest candidate for the Grochow briefing is the exact dimension at which joint prime counts cease to determine a rational geometrically diagonal tensor with abelian splitting field. Below side 30, two-prime counts always suffice. At side 30 a pair agrees at every pair of good primes, and the three primes 7, 11 and 13 distinguish it. This extends entry 27 of the November 21 briefing to a hierarchy with a sharp threshold at every order. Entry numbers refer to the project's ranking. Priority and independent human review are outstanding. [Proof and example](JointPrimeHierarchy.md)

The strongest companion theorem gives a polynomial-size Hilbert-like Ideal Proof System refutation for every infeasible determinant-constrained isomorphism of explicit quiver representations over every finite field, using the coordinate field equations. A quiver here is a directed diagram of vector spaces and linear maps. For ordinary nonisomorphism the argument works over every field using only the intertwiner and inverse equations. These circuit-size bounds hold independently of GRH. The arithmetic application above supplies a small prime at which such a certificate exists when the rational obstruction is a power obstruction and the stated promises hold. This combination merits a second nomination for the briefing. The proof gives a size bound over all finite fields. Its uniform polynomial-time certificate construction has the narrower scope stated in the rational chapter.
