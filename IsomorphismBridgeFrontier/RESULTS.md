# Permutation groups, graph homomorphisms, and quasigroup algorithms

Four informal proof documents.

| Proof | Main result and relation to published questions |
| --- | --- |
| [Permutation roots and homomorphism extension](PermutationRoots.md) | Proves NP-completeness even for source $C_4$ and prescribed subgroup $C_2$, answering Kantor–Luks §13 Problem 2 and Wuu Open Problem 1.6. Answers Wuu Open Problem 1.8 negatively unless $\mathsf P=\mathsf{NP}$. Gives the exact orbit-size threshold for every fixed root exponent. |
| [Planar and bounded-degree graph homomorphisms](OrthogonalGraphHomomorphisms.md) | Proves coRE-completeness for planar bipartite subcubic probes of any fixed girth even on nonsingular simple targets, and an exact exponential distinguishing-probe bound for positive rational weighted targets. The orthogonal characterization extends Maehara’s unweighted subcubic result. Cai–Young’s general unweighted polynomial-time question remains open. |
| [Exact counts for central quasigroup isomorphism](CentralQuasigroupCounting.md) | Counts isomorphisms in deterministic logarithmic workspace from raw tables when one input is central. Extends the earlier MathProofs decision answer to Johnson–Levet–Vojtěchovský–Widholm Question 7.5. |
| [Joint and constrained quasigroup decomposition](QuasigroupDecomposition.md) | Gives a second proof of the published MathProofs answer to Question 7.4, and covers joint decomposition of multiple Latin operations, constants and unary permutations, as well as specified coordinate equalities for a two-factor split. |

All conclusions are in the input models stated in their proof documents. The degree-three group-isomorphism circuit question remains open. Priority for the new results is unestablished.
