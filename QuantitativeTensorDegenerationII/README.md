# Quantitative tensor degeneration II

Four written proofs quantify diagonal-source degeneration, tensor powers, and arithmetic lifting. Each theorem keeps the displayed coefficient field and Laurent parameter fixed.

| Proof | Result |
| --- | --- |
| [Split conductors](SplitConductor.md) | Minimum diagonal-source order equals the minimum conductor exponent of a split smoothing lattice. Exact values follow for \(k[x]/(x^n)\) over every field and for local algebras with complete-intersection tangent cones over algebraically closed fields. |
| [Filtered debordering](FilteredDebordering.md) | Multiplicative filtrations give simultaneous approximation-order and error-degree bounds. An explicit cyclic self-adjoint family has exact order \(2\), error degree at most \(2\), and tensor rank at most \(2n+h\) for \(h\) quadratic forms on the first radical quotient. |
| [Amplification and cancellation](AmplificationAndCancellation.md) | Powers amplify the sparse pole lower bound linearly in the tensor-power exponent. The full feasible pole region survives finite-dimensional semisimple catalysts and, by [the extension](CentralRadicalCatalysts.md), every catalyst whose radical is generated by central nilpotents. |
| [Contact lattices](ContactLattices.md) | Sparse tensor pairs realize arbitrary integer lattices of marked contacts. Bounded contact is NP-complete on pairs promised to degenerate. A terminally capped family has a degree-one geometric orbit arc and requires exponential image degree for Laurent lifts over the original field. |

The [sparse seed proof](SparseSeed.md) supplies the telescope and associated-graded encoding for the power and contact arguments. The diagonal-source theorems use the rank-\(n\) diagonal tensor. The exponential bounds and catalyst comparisons use their specified sparse sources. [Bläser–Lysikov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.04253) · [Ganesh–Koiran–Oliveira](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10179)
