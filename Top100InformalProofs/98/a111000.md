# OEIS A111000 proof note

Informal argument accompanying the submitted formal work. [Sequence statement](https://oeis.org/A111000).

Proof: E(m) = zeta(m+2) - 1 satisfies 1 <= 2^(m+2) E(m) <= 1 + 4 S (2/3)^m (S = sum 1/(k+3)^2), so 2^n (zeta(n) - 1) -> 1; by riemannZeta_residue_one, (zeta(n) - 1) * zeta(zeta(n)) -> 1; the ratio then factors into three convergent pieces with limit 1 * 1 / 2.
