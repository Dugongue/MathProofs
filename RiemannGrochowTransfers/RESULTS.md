# Riemann hypotheses and tensor isomorphism

These four proof chapters connect the [literature catalog of 120 Riemann consequences](../RiemannHypothesisConsequences/README.md) to ordinary and special-linear tensor isomorphism. Classical RH supplies the squarefree counting and variance estimates. Dirichlet GRH supplies estimates for primes with prescribed character values. Each statement below gives its own dependence.

| Result | Exact scope | Hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Extension degrees and squarefree variance](SquarefreeExtensionSpectra.md) | Any fixed Boolean combination of ordinary or special-linear tensor-isomorphism tests over finite fields has an explicit squarefree extension-degree density. Its counting error is $O(X^{11/35+\varepsilon})$ for fixed data, and the variance in windows of length $H$ is $O(H^{1/2+\eta})$ uniformly for $H\le X^{1-\varepsilon}$. The full-range variance is equivalent to RH for one fixed $3\times3\times3$ pair whose ordinary and special-linear orbits disagree exactly in degrees prime to $3$. | The density is unconditional. The improved remainder and full-range variance use classical RH, through A12 and A14 of the catalog. |
| [Joint prime counts and rational isomorphism](JointPrimeCounts.md) | For products of abelian fields of generator rank at most $r$, joint split counts of order $r$ at primes up to $C\log^2(2D)$ recover the rational multiplication-tensor orbit. There are explicit collisions for every smaller order. An adaptive polynomial-time test uses only integer counts, including when $r$ varies. | Dirichlet GRH gives the prime cutoff. The reconstruction, query bound and sharpness construction are unconditional. |
| [Boolean prime spectra and monomial certificates](PrimeSpecialization.md) | Integral tensor pairs can encode every 3-CNF in their good-prime reductions. Their exact prime density recovers the number of satisfying assignments, so exact density is #P-hard. A separate theorem gives finite modular certificates for linked monomial tensor systems with 2-power Smith factors, including the dyadic condition. | The exact density and #P-hardness are unconditional. Dirichlet GRH gives the quantitative prime windows and monomial cutoff. |
| [Prescribed determinants of étale isotopes](EtaleOrientation.md) | Over arbitrary finite fields, deterministic polynomial-time recognition and decision with three prescribed mode determinants use no finite-field polynomial factorization. Over $\mathbb Q$, products of odd-degree cyclic fields with at least one rational factor admit a polynomial-time modular determinant test using separate small inert primes. A dimension 13 example shows why separate records matter. | The finite-field algorithm is unconditional. Dirichlet GRH gives the rational prime cutoff. |

The two analytic applications use the character-sum machinery behind the catalog’s D01–D05, with smoothing and conductor budgets proved or stated in the chapters. The fixed-pair equivalence in the first chapter concerns variance over extension degrees. The algorithms concern the stated multiplication-isotope families.

**Verification.** The proofs are written mathematical arguments. The companion Lean file checks the two Moore determinant identities used in the finite-field decision, namely covariance under a change of basis and invariance under Frobenius in odd degree. The finite controls in the chapters check the stated identities and examples. Publication priority for the new combinations has not been established.
