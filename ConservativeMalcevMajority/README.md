# Conservative Mal’cev and majority imply weighted balance

Over every finite domain, a common conservative Mal’cev and majority pair forces each nonzero support block of every positively unary-weighted pp-definable marginal to have rank one. The [proof](Proof.md) gives a factorization as a polynomial identity and constructs monotone read-once formulas with at most $|D|n$ leaves for the solution polynomials of the pp-definable relations.

The proof is an AI-assisted written proof. Its structural input is Bulatov and Marx, [The Complexity of Global Cardinality Constraints](https://doi.org/10.2168/LMCS-6(4:4)2010). The supplied finite example can be replayed with `python Verify.py`. The exact replay agrees with [the archived results](results.json) for 27 coordinate assignments and 12 symbolic minors.
