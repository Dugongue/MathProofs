# Four consequences for tensor-isomorphism proof complexity

**Main comparison.** For the four graph-to-tensor constructions below, over every field of characteristic different from two, a degree-$d$ ordinary Polynomial Calculus refutation for any one tensor pair gives a degree-$O(d+1)$ refutation for each of the other three. The constant is independent of the input graphs. This includes $\mathbb F_3$. All four use ordinary three-tensor equations with two-sided inverse witnesses. No field equations for the witness entries are imposed. In characteristic two the same statement holds after replacing the semisimple route's $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ by $\mathfrak{sl}_3$. [Original question, Galesi–Grochow–Pitassi–She §7.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S7.SS5)

Use simple graphs of common order and edge count, without isolates. Equal numbers of isolates permit common deletion: an isolate cannot match a nonisolate since multiplying the latter's neighbor sum by a proposed match kills every term by mismatched adjacency. A fixed bijection on the deleted vertices gives the converse substitution. With arbitrary graphs, the four algorithms can share a preliminary comparison of order, edge count and isolate count, returning the same fixed negative pair in mismatched cases. The theorem on unchanged outputs concerns the isolate-free inputs. It makes no assertion about every alternative construction named in the question, such as the Agrawal–Saxena algebra or the unpadded He–Qiao graph map over small fields.

The proof composes substitutions through the *same* graph equations. The new semisimple and alternating decoders recover those equations from ordinary TI. The forward graph maps and the earlier two decoders supply the remaining directions. Each substituted input axiom has a bounded-degree polynomial-size derivation. Replacing a multiplication by a variable with multiplication by its bounded-degree polynomial image transfers the degree claim. The new field range extends the earlier characteristic-zero four-route comparison. ## Four proofs

1. **Simultaneous congruence survives the FGS compiler in every characteristic.** For any injectively parametrized space of square matrices, the FGS solid-link tensor recovers simultaneous congruence and an arbitrary parameter-basis change in constant PC degree. An untouched singleton ensures the projected forward and backward maps are already inverses at the first stage, despite three potentially indistinguishable marker families. This result also applies to nonsymmetric pencils. [Proof](Alternating.md)
2. **Grochow’s semisimple representation detects uniform hyperedges.** For each fixed arity $k\ge2$, a product of $k$ local adjoint actions detects membership of a $k$-set in the hypergraph. Conjugating the vertex projectors in the adjoint commutant recovers Boolean vertex variables. This is the original graph representation for $k=2$ away from characteristic two. The characteristic-two replacement is certified by a 64-dimensional matrix-span calculation over the prime field. [Proof](Semisimple.md) · [Certificate](adjoint_certificate.py)
3. **One tensor family can have any prescribed finite set of easy prime characteristics.** Given any nonempty finite set $S$ of primes, there are integral zero-one multiplication tensors of side $N$ with $O(N)$ nonzeros and radical square zero. The two tensors in each pair are nonisomorphic after base change to every field. Their least ordinary PC refutation degree is $O_S(1)$ in the characteristics in $S$, and $\Theta_S(N)$ in every other characteristic, including zero. The proof combines prime-specific Tseitin lower bounds on expanders with an exact minimum-degree rule for independent systems and a bounded-degree removal of graph colors. [Proof](Hardness.md) · [Sparse example generator](spectrum_example.py) · [Buss–Grigoriev–Impagliazzo–Pitassi](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~sbuss/ResearchWeb/lineargaps/journalfinal.pdf)
4. **A Grochow–Qiao reduction preserves every witness and its multiplicity.** For $m$ matrix slices the parametrized conjugacy transporter is the tensor transporter times affine $m$-space, as schemes. Every tensor witness over $\mathbb F_q$ therefore has exactly $q^m$ lifts. The projection and zero section are linear, so above a universal degree threshold the two PC minima are identical in the displayed equation systems. The note also extends the one-link FGS conjugacy theorem to redundant and zero parametrizations. [Proof](Matrix.md) · [Grochow–Qiao, Corollary 8.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1907.00309#S8)

## Verification and provenance

These are written mathematical proofs. One auxiliary identity for conjugating an arbitrary ordered product passed an independent Lean kernel and axiom audit. The included background contains the earlier modular and compiler lemmas. The main PC simulations, the spectrum theorem, and the transporter-scheme theorem have not been formalized end to end. Run the two new executable checks with Python 3.9 or later. The first computes the full adjoint envelope in characteristic two. That finite example checks the construction; the asymptotic lower bound uses the expander family in the proof. [Adjoint check](adjoint_certificate.py) · [Tensor check](spectrum_example.py)
