# Four extensions of the pure-cube orbit-closure criterion

| Proposed placement | Result |
|---|---|
| 2–6 | [Local equations and conductor](Conductor.md). A surviving pure-cube direction gives an explicit finite birational chart with conductor $(I^2+J)^m$. Its adjacent polarization-rank boundary is nonnormal after padding. |
| 3–7 | [Ordinary tensor degenerations](Tensor.md). A quadratic-side construction preserves and reflects cubic degeneration under the full product of three general linear groups. The nonzero radical branch in characteristic three admits linear side lengths in the polarization rank. |
| 4–8 | [Grassmannian scheme fibers and normalization](Incidence.md). Determine the full Frobenius-thickened fibers, transfer normalization branch counts, and exhibit nonzero higher direct image on a smooth incidence resolution. |
| 5–9 | [Cubic systems and output flags](Systems.md). Classify degeneration for ordered and variable-output cubic systems, allowing simultaneous rank drops. The scheme fibers can have components of different multiplicities. |

The placements are editorial estimates. The four proofs are written mathematical arguments with finite controls. Lean independently verified the auxiliary [cubic expansion](CubeExpansion.lean). Publication priority remains open.
