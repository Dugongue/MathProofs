# Cross-world atlas for strict special-linear tensor isomorphism

Let \(H\) be the deterministic polynomial-time many-one reduction \(\mathrm{TI}_{\mathrm{SL}}(\mathbb F_3)\le_m^P\mathrm{TI}(\mathbb F_3)\) on dense framed three-tensors. The reverse reduction is available by unused-line padding. Write \(H_R\) for the one-output bounded-error randomized version, and \(H_{\mathrm{adv}}\) for the deterministic polynomial-size circuit version. An *incompatibility* below is a proved implication to \(H\), or a proved contradiction of stated joint assumptions in an explicitly named model. An unconditional theorem on a restricted carrier remains valid when one reasons under \(\neg H\). An oracle construction concerns that oracle model. [The 234-entry atlas](prior/earlier/baseline/ATLAS.md) · [Original open question](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5)

## New additions and proof chapters

The four chapters add 67 technical entries to the previous 234-entry atlas. The tables below compare 29 worlds and joint assumptions.

- [Coefficient rings](CoefficientRings.md) gives an unconditional ordinary compiler for principal-unit determinants. It proves a fixed local ring and residue field have equivalent exact determinant-one collapse assertions, extends to products and bounded-depth uniform families, and separates the full determinant from its underlying norm and its principal component.
- [Algebra and group structures](AlgebraWorlds.md) intersects several previously separate hard regimes. The same carrier can have scalar centroid, commuting ratios to every nonsingular slice, and a split nonsingular form. Each one-mode constraint collapses there unconditionally, while joint orientation removal is equivalent to \(H\). The chapter gives exact symmetric and alternating comparisons and a specified-slice version.
- [Proof systems, randomness, and oracle worlds](ComplexityWorlds.md) gives a bounded-degree exact signed witness encoding and a transfer of Polynomial Calculus lower bounds. An explicit small-bias compiler makes polynomial spectral norm of a randomized reduction's seed acceptance predicate sufficient for \(H\). It also constructs exact relative worlds for the one-way-function comparisons.
- [Integral and descent worlds](DescentWorlds.md) gives a reduction from ternary signed TI to ordinary free integral TI and studies integral local-global and finite-field extension spectra. The integral reduction uses a mod-three graph lattice and auxiliary congruence restrictions to force integral determinant \(+1\).

## Worlds ruled out by the strict ternary branch

| Proposed world | Exact relation to \(\neg H\) |
| --- | --- |
| Same-field full determinant collapse over any fixed finite extension of \(\mathbb F_3\) | **Incompatible.** Base-field reflection gives \(H\). |
| Full determinant-one equality over any fixed nonzero finite commutative characteristic-three ring, on free coefficient modules | **Incompatible.** The local comparisons have the exact collapse degree of their residue fields. Combine and reflect on each fixed component. This includes all fixed truncated polynomial rings over \(\mathbb F_3\), even the dual numbers. |
| Uniform full determinant removal over bounded-depth explicitly presented local \(\mathbb F_3\)-algebras with scalar residue | **Equivalent to \(H\).** Principal-unit constraints compile directly, and the residual signs require the ternary reduction. |
| Uniform determinant-norm removal on all explicitly presented finite characteristic-three algebras | **Equivalent to \(H\).** Here “norm” means determinant on the underlying \(\mathbb F_3\) spaces. Restrict scalars with a ring-action mark and use the actual induced maps. The prime field is included as an input. |
| Fixed local norm-only collapse of odd residue degree and odd residue length | **Incompatible.** The underlying determinant reduces to the norm of the residue determinant, with its exponent odd. The odd field degree reflects the ternary sign. |
| Polynomial-time reduction of ordinary integral TI to ordinary ternary TI | **Incompatible.** Compose the new integral bridge with the proposed reverse reduction. It is enough to reduce the explicit integral carrier family, or to decide ordinary integral TI in polynomial time. |
| Projective collapse in the specified even-format PSL model | **Incompatible.** The exact scalar-twist transfer in the descent chapter preserves and reflects the original signs. This uses its framed projective convention. |
| Signed ternary TI reduces to graph isomorphism | **Incompatible.** Ordinary graph isomorphism reduces to ternary TI. A collapse of ordinary ternary TI to graph isomorphism alone only relates the two ordinary targets. [Graph-to-tensor reduction](https://arxiv.org/html/1907.00309) |
| Remove the joint signs in the unmarked scalar-centroid commuting-ratio square-zero carrier | **Equivalent to \(H\).** The claim still has the same degree if a nonsingular scalar form correspondence is prescribed. |
| Remove even isomorphism for compact special exponent-three groups with the new scalar-centroid commuting-ratio restrictions | **Equivalent to \(H\).** The alternating carrier preserves the same determinant product; its pointwise isometries of the distinguished form have determinant one. |
| A randomized one-output signed-to-ordinary map whose seed acceptance functions have a common polynomial Fourier \(\ell_1\) bound | **Incompatible.** An explicit polynomial small-bias seed list followed by ordinary majority makes a deterministic one-output map. In particular this excludes randomized maps depending, on each input, on only logarithmically many seed coordinates, even when those coordinates are unknown. |
| Two existing premises together: complement TI reduces to TI, and signed TI has a deterministic logarithmic-query adaptive ordinary decision reduction | **Incompatible as a conjunction.** Enumerate possible transcripts, replace negative literals, and combine the balanced positive DNF. |
| Ordinary witness search has worst-case verifiable randomized success, and \(\mathrm P=\mathrm{RP}\) | **Incompatible as a conjunction.** Uniform blinding from arbitrary ordinary witnesses yields signed decision in RP, hence P. |
| \(\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\), or \(\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\cap\mathrm{PZK}\cap\mathrm{coHVPZK}\cap\mathrm C_{=}\mathrm P\cap\mathrm{coC}_{=}\mathrm P\) in the atlas conventions | **Incompatible.** Signed TI is in that intersection. |
| \(\mathrm{VP}=\mathrm{VNP}\) over a fixed finite field **and** failure of even polynomial-size advice reductions for the ternary signed problem | **Incompatible as a conjunction.** Arithmetic witness counting after randomized isolation puts NP in P/poly under that algebraic collapse. This conclusion uses the stronger nonuniform separation. |

## Contrasts that hold in the indicated models

| World or proposed shortcut | What the proofs establish |
| --- | --- |
| Principal-unit-one comparisons over fixed equicharacteristic local rings | **Unconditional ordinary compilation.** The nilpotent part of the determinant is separated from its residue by a marked polynomial of bounded degree. |
| Norm-only equality over \(\mathbb F_3[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)\) | **Unconditional.** All underlying ternary determinants are positive. The full determinant-one comparison on the same ring is still equivalent to \(H\). Concrete scalar pairs distinguish the two tests. |
| Signed equals ordinary over characteristic-two finite rings with scalar residue in each factor | **Unconditional.** The principal-unit compiler handles the nilpotent part and the residue determinant group is trivial. There is no cross-characteristic implication in the construction. |
| Every individual determinant constraint collapses on the same unmarked ternary bimap class | **Unconditional on the new carrier.** An actual automorphism has sign \((-1,-1)\) and fixes the total sign. Under \(\neg H\), the joint comparison is still strict on that class. |
| An even automorphism acts transitively on the nonsingular scalar slices | **Unconditional on the hard carrier.** Prescribing exactly which nonsingular form is carried to which form preserves both answers, and the remaining output orientation still has the full degree. |
| Polynomial Calculus requires linear degree on the signed encoding | **Unconditional for the stated encoding and hard family.** The determinant-one presentation is not merely a large determinant polynomial. The degree lower bound does not imply a Karp separation, and cannot be contradicted just by a decision compiler. |
| All place-by-place checks on the integral carrier | **An actual failure of the proposed local-global criterion.** The mod-three tensor reflection example has local congruence solutions and no integral solution. This is independent of the complexity answer over \(\mathbb F_3\). |
| A finite-field stabilizer has nilpotent geometric component group | **Unconditional descent theorem.** The successful degrees form one divisibility progression. This excludes exact field-natural compilation of known nonprincipal extension spectra into such targets, regardless of target size. |
| One-way functions with the relative tensor collapse | **Both relative cases occur.** There are explicit oracle models with the collapse and one-way functions, and models with the collapse and no relative one-way functions. |
| Strictness with a fair random oracle supplying one-way functions | If \(H_R\) fails, almost every independent fair oracle preserves the fixed-language strictness and supplies the stated relative function. If \(H_R\) holds, almost every such oracle erases the deterministic many-one gap. This comparison is conditional on the unrelativized randomized branch, and does not assert that either branch occurs. |
| Polynomial-size AC0 seed acceptance functions for a randomized one-output reducer | The explicit derandomization gives a deterministic **quasipolynomial** one-output map by bounded-independent sampling and ordinary majority. A polynomial-time map is a separate requirement. |
| Passing every prime-power comparison in the completed local setting | Compatible finite lifts exist by compactness in the stated complete ring model. The integral counterexample still fails over the original global ring. |
| Quasipolynomial graph isomorphism or improved worst-case upper bounds for TI | These statements alone do not give an ordinary signed reduction. Bounds on two running times and an ordering of their many-one degrees are distinct assertions. |
| Bare uniform ternary strictness plus a nonuniform arithmetic collapse | The finite-field Boolean simulation yields a contradiction only with nonuniform strictness. The gap between uniform and advice reductions is part of the hypothesis, not eliminated by that simulation. |

The new constructions give explicit implications, equivalences and relative examples. The unrestricted ternary equality is still open. The research arguments are written proofs, with Lean verification limited to the two supporting coefficient identities. [Verification](Verification.json)
