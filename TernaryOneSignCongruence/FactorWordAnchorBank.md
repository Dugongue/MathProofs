# Factor marks for the alternating-space determinant character

Work over $F=\mathbb F_3$. The input is a pair of explicitly based alternating matrix spaces $D_X$, $X\in\{A,B\}$, on equally based spaces of dimension $2s$. Write $M_X:U_X\to D_X$ for the displayed basis identification and $P_X(x)=\operatorname{Pf}(M_X(x))$ for the formal Pfaffian. A congruence is a pair of invertible linear maps $f:U_A\to U_B$, $Y:V_A\to V_B$ with
$$
Y^{\mathsf T}M_B(fx)Y=M_A(x).
$$
Its tested character is $\det_F(Y)\det_F(f)$. All determinants use the input bases. Mismatching dimensions can be answered immediately.

Suppose $P_A$ is formally nonzero and every absolute irreducible factor has degree at most a fixed $d$. Factor multiplicities, number of factors and the degrees of their individual fields of definition are unrestricted. On this promise, the two criteria below give deterministic polynomial-time reductions of the prescribed character question to a single ordinary 3-TI instance. The target inherits the source promise whenever an ordinary congruence exists. The first criterion supplies compatible anchor branches and an independent determinant calculation on label spaces with large simultaneous commutants. The second criterion directly recovers all possible label maps. These arguments are mathematical proofs in prose.

## 1. Raw factor marks and normalized words

Use the deterministic bounded-degree factor recovery for a nonzero homogeneous polynomial accessible by evaluation. It applies here because alternating elimination evaluates $P_X$ over arbitrary represented extensions. The algorithm reconstructs distinct absolute factors in separate small fields, with multiplicities. Its running time is polynomial for fixed $d$, without requiring a common splitting field. A failed factor recovery can be rejected, since an ordinary congruence transports the Pfaffian up to a nonzero scalar and a change of variables. This uses the earlier workspace proof *An orientation test with factors in separate finite fields*, Section 2. Section 5 below records the algorithm and its polynomial bounds.

Keep the rational quadratic factor lines and squares of rational linear factor lines as symmetric matrices $Q_i$ on each label space, with one representative per line and associated factor type. There are at most $s$ factors. Choose an ordered source tuple of at most a fixed $k$ such matrices and a tuple of coefficients $c_i\in F$ for which
$$
B_A=\sum_i c_iQ_{A,i}
$$
is nonsingular. The algorithm may search all of these source choices. For every source tuple, enumerate target tuples of the corresponding types and nonzero multipliers $\lambda_i\in F^\times$. In the branch require the actual label map to satisfy
$$
f^{\mathsf T}\widehat Q_{B,i}f=Q_{A,i},\qquad
\widehat Q_{B,i}=\lambda_i^{-1}Q_{B,i}.
$$
A genuine congruence induces one such assignment, by Pfaffian covariance and unique factorization. This remains true for squares of linear factors, for which the square of the factor multiplier is used. Extra enumerated assignments are harmless because the branch checks its form equations and includes the original congruence equation.

Set $B_B=\sum_i c_i\widehat Q_{B,i}$ and discard a singular $B_B$. Define normalized generators
$$
S_{A,i}=B_A^{-1}Q_{A,i},\qquad S_{B,i}=B_B^{-1}\widehat Q_{B,i}.
$$
Each permitted $f$ intertwines the two generator lists, and $f^{\mathsf T}B_Bf=B_A$. These identities follow directly by substitution. Compute a basis of the source algebra generated by the $S_{A,i}$ by growing an independent list of words, starting with the identity and multiplying each new word on the right by the generators. At most $\dim(U_A)^2$ matrices enter the basis, and each word recorded has length at most that bound. Evaluate the same recorded words on the target. An actual $f$ intertwines each such pair of words, with no comparison of arbitrary bases of the two generated algebras.

For every recorded word $W_A$, take its image and its kernel. Optionally also take the kernels of $W_A-\eta I$ for $\eta\in F$. These are subspaces of $U_A$, and they are carried to the subspaces defined by the corresponding word and scalar on the target. Scan each of positive dimension at most $c\log_3 L$, where $L\ge2$ denotes dense input bit length and $c$ is a fixed constant. Enumerate its vectors and keep those $a$ with $P_A(a)\ne0$. In each target branch enumerate the analogous subspace's vectors $a'$ and keep those with $P_B(a')\ne0$, discarding dimension mismatches. The paired mark is $f(a)=a'$. All of these lists are polynomial. Every required source-to-target map sends a retained source vector to one of the retained target vectors in its correct factor branch. In particular this gives a polynomial-size list of paired nonsingular anchors under a raw, verifiable condition and does not require enumerating label maps.

The same paired anchor branches can be passed to the [commutative Casimir centralizer construction](CyclicCasimir.md). Retain the same factor and point marks. This composition succeeds when a retained anchor meets the Casimir hypotheses, independently of the label radical criterion in the next section.

The construction can use other polynomial lists of matrix polynomials in the normalized generators provided the same expressions are evaluated on both sides. For example a rational linear factor $\ell$ in the tuple contributes the rank-one normalized operator $B^{-1}\ell^{\mathsf T}\ell$. Its image is the line $F B^{-1}\ell^{\mathsf T}$, and its nonzero vectors can meet the nonsingular locus. A criterion taking the radical of that factor itself would select vectors where the Pfaffian vanishes.

## 2. Read the label determinant through alternating quotients

Use the conventional adjoint relative to the selected symmetric form: $W^*=B^{-1}W^{\mathsf T}B$. For every recorded word take the alternating matrix
$$
\Omega_W=B(W-W^*).
$$
Indeed $\Omega_W=BW-W^{\mathsf T}B$. All actual branch maps $f$ preserve the corresponding alternating forms, since they intertwine the words and preserve the $B$-forms. On each side, start with the whole label space and restrict successively to the radical of the current alternating form on the previous subspace. A branch may require matching dimensions at each step. The final residual is denoted $R_X$. The ordering is given by the source word list and its paired evaluations, so this rule commutes with every eligible $f$.

On each successive quotient the restricted alternating form is nondegenerate, hence the induced map has determinant one in symplectic bases. Put these bases together with a computed basis of $R_X$ in an adapted label frame $\beta_X$, expressed in the corresponding raw coordinates. We obtain the exact formula
$$
\det_F f=\frac{\det\beta_B}{\det\beta_A}\det_F(f|_{R_A}:R_A\to R_B)
$$
in these selected bases. No assumption on a simultaneous commutant dimension is needed.

For a retained nonsingular anchor pair with $f(a)=a'$, Pfaffian covariance fixes
$$
\det_F Y=\frac{P_A(a)}{P_B(a')}.
$$
Thus if $\dim_F R_A\le c\log_3 L$, the branch imposes its prescribed determinant character by attaching the exterior algebra of the actual residual map and pinning the appropriate scalar on its top line. The size of this algebra is $2^{\dim R_A}$, which is polynomial under the threshold. Use the actual inclusions of the residuals into the label modes, so no separate label witness can provide the scalar. A zero residual contributes one. Calculate the required line multiplier from the displayed frame and Pfaffian ratios.

Together with the original alternating evaluation tensor, each branch uses typed linear maps, pairings, vector marks and bilinear multiplications. The basis-identifications on the two occurrences of the ambient congruence map are linked, so the branch cannot replace the single $Y$ by two independent maps. Every branch isomorphism then implies one original congruence with the required character. Conversely any original congruence with the desired character extends in the branch with its actual factor assignments and anchor image, provided the source has one tuple meeting both width conditions. The alternating radical dimension is automatically equal for a genuine branch, and the desired scalar constraint necessarily holds.

Polynomially many such branches reduce to one ordinary TI instance. One can pack all marked tensors into a constant component system by tagging each sort and occurrence with a distinct idempotent of a coordinate algebra, distinguishing idempotents by distinct copy multiplicities, then using inclusion and identification maps to tie every occurrence to its original action. The resulting fixed-size typed system reduces by [the tensor-system compiler](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S4) to one ordinary TI pair per branch. For the OR, use the crossed products of connected triangular multiplication algebras in the workspace's query packing. A binary AND encodes an ordered pair via two tagged summands with linear side lengths by [Grochow–Qiao IV, Theorem 5.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.16317). Applying the crossed-product OR gate along a balanced tree gives polynomial side lengths for a polynomial number of branches.

## 3. An unbounded example with a large commutant

Fix $r\ge2$, let $U=F a\oplus (F^2\otimes F^r)$, and put $m=\dim U=1+2r$. Write $x=t a+w$, and set $\ell(x)=t$. On $U$ take the three symmetric matrices
$$
Q_1=1\oplus I_{2r},\qquad
Q_2=1\oplus(S\otimes I_r),\qquad
Q_3=1\oplus(T\otimes I_r),
\qquad
S=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\quad
T=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The matrix $[S,T]$ is alternating and invertible. Each $Q_i(x)$ below means $x^{\mathsf T}Q_ix$; these are distinct absolutely irreducible quadratics because their ranks are at least three. Construct for each $i$ the linear matrix pencil
$$
L_i(x)=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-x^{\mathsf T}Q_i\\
x&\ell(x)I_m
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Its determinant is $\ell(x)^{m-1}Q_i(x)$, by eliminating the invertible block on the open set $\ell(x)\ne0$, then comparing polynomials. Take $i$ copies of $L_i$ and one extra $1\times1$ block $\ell(x)$ in a block diagonal matrix $L(x)$. Define $M(x)=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&L(x)\\-L(x)^{\mathsf T}&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. The argument $x$ is displayed in a block column so the slice map is injective. Its Pfaffian is a nonzero constant times
$$
\ell(x)^{6(m-1)+1}Q_1(x)Q_2(x)^2Q_3(x)^3.
$$
The basis vector $a$ gives a nonsingular slice, the normalizer $B^{-1}\ell^{\mathsf T}\ell$ for $B=Q_1$ has image $Fa$, and the normalized commutator of $Q_2,Q_3$ gives the alternating form $0\oplus [S,T]\otimes I_r$ up to a nonzero scalar. To obtain this form from a word basis, note that the anti-adjoint parts of the word basis span the anti-adjoint parts of every algebra element. Each of the individual words is block diagonal with scalar on $Fa$ and a $2\times2$ factor tensored with $I_r$ on the complement. Every resulting alternating form is therefore a scalar multiple of $0\oplus [S,T]\otimes I_r$, with at least one nonzero, regardless of the chosen word basis. The final radical is $Fa$. Any simultaneous commutant of these label forms contains $0\oplus(I_2\otimes\operatorname{Mat}_r(F))$, of dimension $r^2$, so the logarithmic commutant hypothesis is unavailable.

For a self-congruence of $M$, the distinct multiplicities force all four factor lines to be preserved individually. Normalizing by $Q_1$ shows the rank-one image $F B^{-1}\ell^{\mathsf T}=Fa$ is preserved, even before choosing multipliers. Since $f(a)=\epsilon a$ with $\epsilon=\pm1$, evaluating each quadratic on $a$ forces its multiplier to be one. In particular $f$ preserves the commutator form and $\det f=\epsilon$ in the associated one-dimensional-radical filtration. The Pfaffian degree $6(m+1)+1$ is odd. Thus $\det Y=\epsilon$ by evaluating the covariance at $a$. Every self-congruence has character $+1$. Twist the ambient basis of the target by one fixed negative determinant map, with corresponding ordered label basis. This produces an ordinary-congruent pair with exclusively negative character. The family therefore gives a genuinely signed raw test, including arbitrary changes of all displayed bases, with unbounded dimensions and unbounded commutants.

## 4. Recover the entire label map from two geometric factor marks

A separate sufficient criterion applies when the Frobenius orbit of one absolute linear factor contains exactly $m=\dim U_A$ mutually independent conjugate lines, and a second rational linear factor has every coordinate nonzero in this geometric basis. Other factors may occur, and their total number can grow. This covers configurations with extra Pfaffian factors, so the linear factor list need not be an independent frame. The condition is verifiable by linear algebra in the field of the selected orbit. A constant number of selected orbits whose arguments together form an independent spanning basis also suffices when the supports of a constant number of additional marked lines connect the basis indices, meaning the equivalence relation generated by shared supports has one class.

Pick one such source marking. Enumerate images of its selected factors among the target factors recovered in Section 1. For each candidate solve the linear equations over $F$ saying the target marked linear forms composed with $f$ belong to the specified source lines. Write equations of line membership over the explicit composite fields for these constant-size tuples. Their degrees are polynomial because the least common multiple of a fixed number of degrees at most $s$ is polynomial in $s$. Field coordinates convert them to ordinary $F$-linear equations; there is no enumeration of field elements in these large fields. An $F$-linear map carrying one specified orbit line to another carries all their matched Frobenius conjugates automatically. Compute the subspace of candidate $f$'s.

If an invertible $f$ satisfies the marked constraints, composition with its inverse identifies the solution space with the source endomorphisms preserving each of its marked lines. Such an endomorphism is diagonal on the marked basis of the dual. The full-support line forces the diagonal entries to coincide. In the multi-line version a shared support likewise ties its scalars, so connectedness forces the same conclusion. Rationality makes the scalar an element of $F$. Thus if a branch has an invertible map, the solution space has dimension exactly one. Discard all other branches and enumerate the at most two invertible maps of each retained line. No search-to-decision is used. This argument applies to geometric lines in nonsplit fields, including when the whole Pfaffian does not split in an extension of polynomial degree.

For each retained $f$, compute the Pfaffian ratio at a source vector $a$ over an explicit extension of polynomial cardinality where $P_A(a)\ne0$. With only absolute linear factors, the ordinary moment curve $u\mapsto (1,u,\ldots,u^{m-1})$ suffices. More generally, when the degrees of all absolute factors are bounded by $d$, the bounded-degree evaluation curve from Section 5 suffices, since it makes each factor a nonzero univariate polynomial. Evaluate $P_B(fa)$ over the same field. Reject zero denominator or ratio outside $F^\times$, and keep the branch precisely when this ratio times $\det f$ equals the desired character. Any actual ambient congruence on this branch has determinant equal to that ratio by Pfaffian covariance after scalar extension. Thus it is enough to keep the original tensor equations and the prescribed label map as typed marks, then apply ordinary TI. Every original transporter gives a retained label map, and every marked TI witness in a retained branch satisfies the exact sign. This construction needs no nonsingular anchor over the base field and no residual width bound.

## 5. Deterministic bounded-degree factor recovery used here

We outline the existing recovery algorithm to make the preprocessing scope explicit. For a constant degree bound $D$ in $h$ variables, take a monomial substitution $x_i=u^{w_i}$ with $w_i=\sum_{j=0}^D i^j B^j$, for integer $B>D h^D$. Distinct monomials of total degree at most $D$ have distinct weights: their base-$B$ digits record the sums of the $j$-th powers of the index multisets without carries, and the first $D$ sums together with the multiset size determine the multiset over the rationals. The weights and the degrees of the resulting univariates are polynomial for fixed $D$. A product of polynomially many nonzero bounded-degree polynomials thus has a nonzero point along the curve over an explicit extension of sufficiently large polynomial cardinality, even when the factors' coefficients are in larger fields.

Apply this to all leading coefficients, individual discriminants and pairwise resultants of the reduced factors on an affine line $v+tu$, with $D$ fixed suitably in terms of $d$. Each quantity is a nonzero polynomial in the two direction vectors because the factors are absolutely irreducible, distinct, and separable along some direction over the algebraic closure. A full-degree line restriction with maximal squarefree radical degree is therefore a good restriction, found by interpolation and deterministic univariate factoring. Complete its direction vector to a basis with transverse coordinates $z$. Normalize the full polynomial in $t$ to be monic. For each collection of at most $d$ irreducible residue factors, work in a common splitting field of that collection, whose extension degree over the line field is at most $s^d$. For each root of multiplicity $e=3^a e'$, with $3\nmid e'$, lift its primary cluster along $z=t'\beta$ by coprime monic Hensel lifting through precision $d3^a+1$. Its degree-$(e-3^a)$ coefficient is $-e'$ times the simple lifted root raised to the $3^a$-th power. Inverse Frobenius recovers its simple lift through degree $d$. Multiply subsets of at most $d$ roots and interpolate the homogeneous coefficients in the transverse coordinates on the bounded-degree curve. A true absolute factor is recovered from exactly its subset of good-line roots. Eliminate spurious subsets by testing divisibility on a second bounded-degree curve of lines and keeping inclusion-minimal passing subsets. A coprime candidate fails on a line where its resultants with the true factors are nonzero. A passing candidate shares a true factor, whose root subset is contained in its subset, and a candidate on the same minimal root subset has the same monic degree in $t$ and equals that factor. Good-line root multiplicities give the factor multiplicities.

This procedure has polynomial work at fixed $d$, including outside the promise if every search and lifting precision uses its stated bounds and failure is allowed. Finite extensions of logarithmic degree over $F$ can be made by enumerating irreducible candidates over this fixed field. For the larger local fields use [Shoup's deterministic irreducible-polynomial algorithm in fixed characteristic](https://www.shoup.net/papers/detirred.pdf); univariate factoring over those fields uses the fixed space of prime-field Frobenius in the squarefree quotient, splitting by its values in $F$, with derivative checks and inverse Frobenius for powers. No computation requires a field simultaneously splitting all factors.

## 6. Fixed corank with zero common radical

The anchor and alternating-word argument also applies to an everywhere-singular class with no assumed common radical. Fix a positive even integer $c_0$. Suppose the generic rank of $M_A(x)$ is $2r=\dim V_A-c_0$, with $r>0$. Assume every nonzero $F$-linear combination of the principal $2r$-Pfaffian minors has absolute factors of degree at most the fixed constant $d$. Take
$$
G_A(x)=\gcd \{\,\operatorname{Pf}(M_A(x)_I): |I|=2r,\ \operatorname{Pf}(M_A(x)_I)\not\equiv0\,\},
$$
defined up to a nonzero constant. Run the factor-mark construction with $G_A,G_B$ in place of the full Pfaffians. A source anchor is acceptable when its slice has rank $2r$, and the width requirement on the word radical $R_A$ is unchanged. Under these conditions there is again a deterministic polynomial-time reduction to one ordinary TI instance for the final character.

The vector of $2r$-Pfaffian minors is the coordinate expression of the divided exterior power of the alternating form. Its congruence covariance is the ordinary exterior matrix on $\bigwedge^{2r}V^*$. Over characteristic zero the identity follows by expanding $\omega^{\wedge r}/r!$. It is an identity with integer coefficients in the entries of the form and of $Y$, so it holds also in characteristic three. In particular the whole minor vector changes by an invertible constant matrix, besides substitution by the label map. Its gcd therefore changes by a nonzero constant and that label substitution. Its $F$-linear span, and thus the bounded-factor-degree assumption on that span, transfer to every ordinarily congruent target.

There are $\binom{\dim V}{c_0}$ minors to examine, polynomial when $c_0$ is fixed. Verify nonzero evaluations of the largest relevant minor size by the bounded-degree curve. In an instance with generic rank $2r$, larger minors vanish identically. At rank $2r$ every nonzero minor is hit, and their lists of recovered absolute factors give the gcd with minimum multiplicities. If the rank is unspecified up to the fixed corank bound $c_0$, scan the even sizes from $\dim V$ down to $\dim V-c_0$, applying the bounded algorithm at each size. The true largest nonzero size is reached on the promise. Alternatively the fixed value $c_0$ can specify the promised input class. All raw preprocessing runs at bounded polynomial work even on a noncongruent target that violates a promise. The assembled branch retains the full input congruence equation.

For the determinant, use the two positive-rank matched anchors $a,a'$ and set $K_X=\ker M_X(a_X)$. A genuine branch map carries $K_A$ to $K_B$. Alternating forms induce nonsingular forms $\overline M_A,\overline M_B$ on $V_A/K_A,V_B/K_B$. Choose adapted ambient frames $\eta_X$ formed from a basis of $K_X$ followed by lifts of a quotient basis. Exact-sequence determinants and Pfaffian covariance give
$$
\det_F Y=
\frac{\det\eta_B}{\det\eta_A}
\det_F(Y|_{K_A}:K_A\to K_B)
\frac{\operatorname{Pf}(\overline M_A)}{\operatorname{Pf}(\overline M_B)}.
$$
Attach the actual action on $K_X$ via its inclusion and use its top exterior line, dimension cost $2^{c_0}$. Multiply its multiplier with that of the $R_X$ line from Section 2, and use the displayed and previously computed calibrations to require exactly the prescribed product sign. Each construction remains polynomial and each true witness on the source family has an appropriate factor and anchor branch.

For a nonempty example, extend the regular alternating pencil of Section 3 by a direct sum of two $3\times3$ alternating pencils of rank two with varying kernel. Each added pencil can be the matrix of the cross-product form with three independent linear coordinates in $U$, chosen so their first coordinate is $\ell$. At $a$ both added blocks have rank two. The common radical of each added block is zero because its three coordinate slices span all alternating forms on its three-dimensional space. The overall generic corank is two. Every maximal nonzero principal minor includes all regular indices and two indices of each added block. Thus its Pfaffian is the regular Pfaffian times a product of one linear entry of each added block. The two entry sets each have three independent members, so their product family has gcd one. The gcd $G_A$ recovers the original factors, and every nonzero $F$-linear combination of the maximal minors is the regular Pfaffian times a homogeneous polynomial of degree two. This family satisfies the conditions under arbitrary raw basis changes.

The unrestricted alternating-space predicate remains open. These constructions cover the stated promises with explicit compatible branches. All proofs in this note are in prose.
