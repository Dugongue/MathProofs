# Classification of tensor orbit closure intersection for one general linear group

Informal proof. November 14, 2026.

Fix a tuple signature with slots V^⊗a_i ⊗ (V*)^⊗b_i, acted on by one GL(V). The signature is fixed and dimensions vary. Orbit closures are over C, with rational input coordinates for bit complexity. Put w_i=a_i−b_i. Call a slot active when some nonnegative integer vector c has c_i>0 and sum_j c_j w_j=0. Discard inactive slots and set A=max_i a_i and B=max_i b_i, using zero for empty maxima. Then:

- If A,B≥2 and some active slot has total order at least three, the problem is TOCI-complete. The hardness reduction is affine over Q and works on all complex points.
- Otherwise, if B≤1 and some active slot has a_i≥2,b_i=1, or if the dual condition holds, the problem is polynomial-time many-one equivalent to EqSLP, the zero test for division-free integer straight-line programs. In particular it belongs to coRP.
- All remaining signature cases are in deterministic polynomial time.

The signature cases are disjoint and the result asserts no separations among the complexity classes. Deterministic polynomial time for the EqSLP case is equivalent to deterministic polynomial time for EqSLP itself. This classifies the one-group setting of [Lysikov–Walter, Question I.12](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04639v2#S1.SS4). The published question also permits products of general linear groups.

## Active slots and diagrams

The first fundamental theorem gives spanning invariants by full contractions with slot occurrences of arbitrary multiplicities. Since primal and dual counts in any contraction agree, no inactive slot contributes to any spanning invariant. Equality of invariants characterizes closure intersection. Thus deletion of inactive slots is an exact reduction, with zero insertion giving a reduction back. Sum the activity witnesses to obtain fixed balanced multiplicities positive on every active slot. Direct each contraction edge from primal to dual, so outdegree and indegree are a_i,b_i. Opposite ports of one vertex may connect. Each disconnected contraction is the product of its connected components. Compare scalar slots directly. [Lysikov–Walter, Theorems II.7 and IV.23](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04639v2).

## Hardness for TOCI

Use the [companion pure-power proof](../PureTensorOCIDichotomy/Proof.md) for the closed marker principle and for a complete orthogonal cubic source with zero contraction in positions 1 and 3. That proof derives the latter from Lysikov–Walter's Theorem V.21 by rational affine maps. It also settles pure opposite powers of orders at least two when one is at least three.

If every active slot of order at least three is pure, select such a slot and an opposite pure slot of order at least two. The latter exists because every mixed slot of order at most two has at most one port of either sign. The pure-power construction applies with zeros in unused slots. Now let t be a mixed slot of order at least three. Take the trace-free cubic f on an orthogonal space U and set W=U⊕L with line basis l and dual basis epsilon annihilating U. Put the pure-line tag (coefficient 1, all factors l or epsilon) in every active slot. Pick a slot p with two primal ports, and add the inverse standard metric in two such ports with line factors elsewhere. Pick a slot q with two dual ports and add the standard metric in two such ports with line factors elsewhere. These may be the same slot. Place f on three selected ports in t including both signs, using the standard metric on U to identify any required dual factor and line factors elsewhere. Choose positions 1 and 3 of f to have opposite signs. If t is also p or q, ensure its metric tag of the respective sign includes the chosen contraction port of that sign.

### Line recovery

Use a balanced multiset containing at least two copies of each active slot. Internally contract the selected opposite-sign positions in every t copy. The trace-free condition kills f. Each metric term in t has its U-factor at exactly one contracted position, and therefore dies against the line factor at the other. Only the pure-line term survives in these copies. At least one leg remains in each copy. Choose the same remaining leg in two different t copies. Both are forced line factors of the same sign. Reserve the first as an output. Reserve an opposite-sign leg as the other output, which exists by balance after internal contractions. If that output can carry U in a metric term of its copy, contract the other same-sign U-leg of that metric with the second forced line leg. This kills the only possible metric term giving U at the reserved output. If the output is already line-only, skip that contraction. Complete all remaining pairings arbitrarily. The resulting equivariant polynomial contraction is C P_L, where P_L=l⊗epsilon and C>0 depends only on the format. Indeed all surviving terms have nonnegative integer coordinates and the all-line term contributes 1.

The projection P_L has closed conjugacy orbit cut out by P²=P and trace(P)=1. The orbit of C P_L is also closed. The closed marker principle reduces comparison to the fiber with stabilizer K=GL(U)×GL(L).

### Orthogonal action in the fiber

Project equivariantly in the fiber to the pure-line coordinates x_i and the metric blocks B_+,B_-. The orbit of their standard values under (h,lambda)∈K is given by

```
x_i=lambda^(w_i)
B_+=lambda^(w_p−2) h h^T
B_-=lambda^(w_q+2) h^(−T) h^(−1).
```

This is closed. For the pure-line part, impose product_i x_i^(c_i)=1 using the strictly positive balanced witness c. Then every x_i is nonzero. Impose product_i x_i^(u_i)=1 for a lattice basis of integer relations dot(u,w)=0, clearing denominators. These equations describe exactly the pure-line orbit: the character on Z^s supplied by the x_i is trivial on the kernel of the weight map to Z, so it factors through the subgroup generated by the weights. A character of that subgroup extends to Z by taking a root in C. For all weights zero, the equations already give x_i=1. Append symmetry of both metric blocks and B_+B_-=x_p x_q I_U. Since B_+ is invertible, after choosing lambda congruence of nonsingular symmetric forms provides h with h h^T=lambda^(2−w_p)B_+. The product equation gives the prescribed B_-. This proves the orbit description.

The stabilizer has lambda^(w_i)=1 for each active slot and h=z o with o∈O(U) and z²=lambda². On the cubic with c primal and d dual ports the scalar factor is lambda^(w_t−(c−d)) z^(c−d)=(z/lambda)^(c−d). It is a sign and is absorbed by −I_U acting on the cubic. Every source orthogonal transformation also occurs with lambda=z=1. Applying the closed marker principle again and using the closed affine data fiber proves correctness on all complex points. The representation factors have fixed orders and dimension grows by one in this last step, so the rational affine encoding is efficient. Membership is by the definition of TOCI.

## Separating trees and cycles when one indegree is at most one

It suffices to consider B≤1, since dualizing interchanges the signs. Every nonscalar connected diagram is either a rooted directed tree or a directed cycle with rooted trees attached. Indeed its edge count is either one less than its vertex count, giving exactly one indegree-zero root, or equals it, in which case every vertex has indegree one. Following incoming edges identifies a unique directed cycle in the second case. Loops and two-vertex cycles are included.

A slot with b_i=1 induces a multilinear operation mu_i:(V*)^a_i→V*, by contracting its outgoing ports, leaving its incoming leg free. A branch is a rooted ordered tree of these operations. Leaves use the slots (0,1). Evaluate each branch on two inputs in parallel, as a pair of covectors in V*⊕V*. Their span is computed by repeatedly applying each paired operation to tuples of existing basis vectors. Multilinearity proves that the computed space is exactly the span. Bases can be chosen from evaluations of actual trees. The span stabilizes within H=2n+1 height levels, where n=dim V and leaves have height zero, since its dimension is at most 2n and a nonincrease means stabilization.

A root slot with b_i=0 yields a scalar when fed its branches. A cycle vertex has b_i=1 and one designated outgoing cycle port. Fixing all its other branches leaves an endomorphism of V*. A cycle evaluates to the trace of the corresponding product. Testing height at most H for each branch suffices by multilinearity. Testing products of length at most 2n² then suffices for the paired matrix letters: their word span stabilizes in End(V*)⊕End(V*) of dimension 2n², starting with the identity pair. Thus these bounded tests separate the orbit closures. The size of an individual expanded tree can still be exponential.

## A single EqSLP instance for the bounded tests

For inputs X,Y, compute a rational arithmetic circuit for the sum of squared differences over that separating family. For each choice of pair (X,Y), (X,X), or (Y,Y), aggregate the tensor products of the two evaluations of all branches of height at most h into a vector G(h) in V*⊗V*. Initialize G(0) as the sum of paired leaf values. Obtain G(h+1) from the same leaf sum plus the sum of paired transitions mu_i^X⊗mu_i^Y on G(h)^⊗a_i for non-leaf slots with b_i=1, interchanging tensor factors as required. This counts each ordered branch once. The argument is the sum-product construction for weighted tree automata. [Seidl, Deciding Equivalence of Finite Tree Automata](https://doi.org/10.1137/0219027) and [Kiefer, Marušić, and Worrell, Minimisation of Multiplicity Tree Automata, Section 2.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.5352).

For root trees, apply each paired root operation to the appropriate power of G(H) and sum. For cycles, for each eligible slot and each outgoing cycle port, put G(H) on each of its other outgoing ports in the paired transition. This gives an operator on V*⊗V*. Let M be the sum of these paired operators over slots and designated ports. Then trace(M^k) sums the products of the two evaluations over all ordered cyclic words of length k with bounded branches. This uses trace(P⊗Q)=trace(P)trace(Q). Multiple starting points only repeat terms with positive multiplicities. Add these traces for 1≤k≤2n² to the root tree scalar, giving s_XY. The arrays, powers, and number of operations all have polynomial size because arities are fixed.

The circuit for s_XX+s_YY−2s_XY is the sum of squared differences over the same collection in all three aggregates. Add squared differences of scalar slots. Over rational inputs it vanishes exactly when the differences all vanish, since every square is nonnegative. Replace each wire by a numerator and denominator circuit, cross-multiplying at addition, and test the final numerator for zero. This turns the circuit into a division-free integer straight-line program in polynomial time. It gives a deterministic many-one reduction to EqSLP and a coRP algorithm using the standard modular EqSLP algorithm.

## EqSLP hardness with an active mixed branch slot

Assume B≤1 and a mixed active slot has (a,1) with a≥2. Its weight is positive and the only possible negative weight in this setting is (0,1), so a leaf covector slot must be active. Keep these two slots and fill all others with zero.

Given an integer straight-line program, layer its binary gates so each input comes from the preceding level, adding copy gates to fill gaps. Level zero contains constants. Include a distinguished coordinate of value 1 in each layer. There are polynomially many states. Make the seed covector carry the level-zero constants and the first 1, and use one a-linear transition mu. Each usual rule for a gate gives a next-level output only from children in its preceding level. A multiplication reads the two gate input coordinates in children 1 and 2. Addition and subtraction use a sum or difference of products with the 1 coordinate in child 2. A copy multiplies by that 1. The next 1 coordinate multiplies the two preceding 1 coordinates. Every extra child supplies the preceding-layer 1 coordinate. These rules specify a tensor of fixed order with polynomially many entries. For a branch evaluated from the seed, induction shows it vanishes unless it is the complete a-ary tree at some available level. That tree yields exactly the layer's gate coordinates and its 1. Unequal child depths cannot match the same previous layer.

Add a state z absent from the seed. Both inputs use the same seed and gate rules. The first also has one rule: child 1 supplies z, child 2 supplies the final-gate coordinate, and all extra children supply the final-layer 1 coordinate. The output is z times this product of coordinates. The second input omits this rule. Branch evaluations never generate z, so they coincide in both inputs. A one-vertex operator in a cycle preserves the direct sum of the gate-state subspace and z. On gate states it strictly raises the level. On z the operator is zero unless child 1 is the open port and all side branches have the final depth, in which case it has eigenvalue equal to the final gate value in the first input and zero in the second. Thus if that value is zero every cycle invariant agrees. If it is nonzero a self-loop at child 1 with final-depth side trees distinguishes the inputs. Only cycle components can occur in this selected signature, as both slots have indegree one. This proves the EqSLP lower bound under deterministic polynomial-time many-one reductions. Swapping primal and dual gives the other sign case.

## Deterministic polynomial cases

If all active slots have total order at most two, every connected nonscalar diagram is an undirected path or cycle. Use the trace-word algorithm on block matrices acting on V⊕V*⊕C from the companion pure-power proof. In addition to its pure slots, each mixed quadratic adds the block V→V and its transpose V*→V*. The proof is unchanged because each such letter matches its two opposite-sign ports. The word lengths needed for the paired matrix span are polynomial, so exact rational linear algebra has polynomial bit cost.

If B≤1 and there is no active mixed branch slot, branch transitions have arity zero or one. Their paired bases therefore come from linear paths of length at most H and have polynomial bit length. Each root uses a constant number of such branches. Cycle operators are unary transitions and the paired word bound gives polynomial bit length there as well. The paired span comparisons use polynomial bit time. The dual case is identical after exchanging the signs. These cases exhaust the remaining signatures.
