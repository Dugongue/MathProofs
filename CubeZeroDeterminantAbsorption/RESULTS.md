# Absorbing odd automorphisms into one-sign tensor isomorphism

Work over F₃ with explicit raw 3-tensors. Write A for one-sign TI, requiring determinant +1 on one designated mode, and F for the test whether a tensor has a stabilizer with determinant −1 on its designated mode. The absorption problem asks for one deterministic polynomial-time many-one map from the independent conjunction A∧F to A. These are informal proofs.

**Unrestricted comparison.** We prove A∧F≤C, where C is the asymmetric correction comparison. The reduction constructs one raw tensor pair with two independent sign coordinates and a fixed third sign. The unrestricted reduction A∧F≤A remains open. [Proof](AsymmetricCorrection.md)

**One-sign absorption from raw multiplication tensors.** Either conjunct may be unrestricted if its partner belongs to one of the families below. The constructions check eligibility and use the actual tested physical mode. Write J for the radical, V=J/J² and W=J².

1. **Small square.** For local scalar-residue multiplication with J³=0, both tests are in deterministic P when dim W=O(√log L), with L the dense input bit length. The proof finds the exact determinant image for each output map by solving simultaneous congruence with an involutive adjoint algebra. [Proof](SmallSquare.md)
2. **Finite residue extension.** For a local algebra with finite residue field K of degree e and f=dim_K W on the left, the same exact algorithm takes time e·3^(ef²) times a polynomial in L. Splitting by Frobenius type can lower the exponent. If f=1 and the active left-input twist has odd order, the runtime is polynomial with no bound on e. This includes central residue fields of arbitrary degree. [Proof](FiniteResidue.md)
3. **Large quadratic image.** In scalar residue, with even dim V, select the symmetric or alternating multiplication map. The determinant reduces to those on its kernel and the quotient of W by its image. Both must have logarithmic dimension, while the image itself may be quadratic in dim V. This gives one ordinary TI instance. [Proof](LargeQuadraticImage.md)
4. **Small commutator corank.** In scalar residue, specialize the alternating commutator over a finite extension of degree e. If the smallest corank c satisfies e·dim W+c=O(log L), a single ordinary TI instance encodes the top exterior lines of the minimizing kernels. This also covers families in which every specialization is singular. [Proof](PfaffianKernels.md)

The fixed-mode construction uses the earlier [square-zero test and the cube-zero encoding](OddAutomorphisms.md) and the [determinant-neutral conjunction](NeutralConjunction.md). The unrestricted odd-automorphism predicate itself already reduces to scalar-residue cube-zero multiplication. That prior map can produce large J² and supplies no general logarithmic or quadratic-image bound. The four-worker audit here checked the algebraic proofs and the reductions against their stated hypotheses. The small congruence test script checks the reference-solution identity on finite examples. The proof status is informal.
