# Four research routes with new theorem proofs

These manuscripts contain complete informal proofs. The ranking reflects the breadth of the result and the audience for its statement. Priority for the new statements remains provisional.

1. [Undecidable extension between fixed three-manifolds](TopologyFixedThreeManifolds.md). A single winding number on a fixed two-circle marking encodes the halting problem for maps from one closed hyperbolic three-manifold into one closed Nil three-manifold. Every extension is surjective on fundamental groups. The equivalent boundary version uses a fixed two-cusped hyperbolic manifold. All finite target quotients admit compatible extensions for every input. There is also a natural all-ring affine scheme retraction.
2. [Exact determinant border partition rank](DeterminantBorderRank.md) and [rigidity of optimal even formulas](DeterminantPfaffianRigidity.md). A specialization theorem carries Chow obstructions through degenerations, giving exact border rank over every field. Every shortest even formula comes from a single symplectic form, and its coefficient scheme is computed over the integers. An [odd-size construction](DeterminantOddNonLaplace.md) answers the uniqueness question of Chen and Zhao negatively for every prime at least five. The ordinary rank formula and the forward Pfaffian construction appeared earlier in [Krishnan and Moshkovitz](https://github.com/sigact-stoc/stoc2027/releases/download/papers-1/631.pdf).
3. [Exact global-field hypersurface smoothing](ArithmeticGlobalFieldSmoothing.md). One algorithm uses one extra affine coordinate to replace a system by a smooth geometrically integral hypersurface, preserving its entire set of rational solutions by coordinate projection. It works over all global fields, including characteristic two. A [projective companion](ArithmeticProjectiveHypersurfaceFamily.md) preserves rational-point decision for canonically polarized divisors in a product. Under the Bhargava–Burungale input, it yields a single smooth projective family whose complex fibers are diffeomorphic and whose rational fibers all pass the étale–Brauer–Manin test, with halting-complete rational-point existence on integer fibers.
4. [The three-function Rademacher Hanner inequality](AnalysisRademacherThreeFunctions.md). Both missing exponent ranges hold for three functions, with equality for real, complex and Hilbert-valued inputs. Common dominance quotas or at most three magnitude profiles give cases for arbitrarily many functions. The same proof gives sharp finite-length moment bounds at a fixed coefficient \(\ell_p\) norm for independent, identically distributed symmetric real summands. The full arbitrary-function inequality remains open in these ranges.
