# Simultaneous computability and Grochow's isomorphism questions

The [foundation manuscript](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/5e2ab397cd7bcaaec1a54642f5e62c0558a8e77d/SimultaneousComputability/README.md) studies one input tested over many fields or rings, with one reduction required to preserve the whole pattern. The [arithmetic frontier](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/5e2ab397cd7bcaaec1a54642f5e62c0558a8e77d/SimultaneousComputabilityFrontier/README.md) develops that distinction for integral points and local obstructions. Here the probes are applied to tensor isomorphism, arithmetic witnesses, and proof complexity.

## Results to show Grochow first

1. **Sharp sparse Polynomial Calculus bounds for finite-field congruence.** In the exact matrix-congruence encoding of Galesi–Grochow–Pitassi–She, augmented with the usual field equations, the worst-case degree over each fixed odd finite field is $\Theta(n)$ and the worst-case sparse proof size is $n^{\Theta(n)}$. The lower bounds already hold for one pair of nonsingular symmetric forms that become congruent over the algebraic closure. Their determinant square class is the only decision difference over the base field. The prior portfolio supplied the linear all-matrix upper bound and an exponential sparse lower bound. The new sparse lower bound adds the factor $\log n$ to the exponent. The same chapter identifies the exact dyadic extension thresholds for systems of bilinear forms and gives hard examples at each nontrivial threshold. [Proof](PC.md) · [Published Question 7.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S7.SS3) · [Prior finite-field bounds](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/5e2ab397cd7bcaaec1a54642f5e62c0558a8e77d/MatrixPCAdvances/finite-field-congruence.md).
2. **A complete scalar-extension profile for ordinary tensors over finite fields.** Take the geometric connected components of the invertible transporter and compute the orbit lengths of Frobenius on them. The pair becomes isomorphic in an extension of degree $e$ exactly when one of those lengths divides $e$. Every finite union of such divisibility conditions occurs. An arbitrary extension reflects ordinary TI on every input exactly when it adds no finite constants. For one modulus, the minimum side among finite étale multiplication tensor pairs is an explicit two-way partition of the maximal prime powers dividing that modulus. [Proof](FiniteFieldProfiles.md) · [Grochow–Qiao Question 7.6](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10494304).
3. **Sharp joint descent of ordinary and determinant-one tensor isomorphisms.** For central simple multiplication tensors with a fixed Galois center and fixed algebra degree, an exact prime-hitting criterion says which lists of extension degrees force an isomorphism to descend. Every counterexample degree list can be witnessed inside one cyclic extension disjoint from the center. The determinant-one version holds at the same tensor side and uses the subgroup of center maps of the allowed sign. The construction also recovers a base witness in polynomial expected field operations when supplied witnesses on a degree-gcd-one subfamily induce one center map. [Proof](Descent.md).
4. **Prescribed field profiles and affine torsors in ordinary three-tensor isomorphism.** Finite étale multiplication tensors realize every subgroup downset of a fixed finite Galois group. With a degree cutoff they realize any finite union of field-embedding tests and can accept at every completion. The minimum side for an everywhere-local failure in this finite étale class is six. The sharp side-six result was already proved for geometrically diagonal tensors in the portfolio. The new results concern programmable profiles and arbitrary affine torsors. Over a field with at least eight elements every affine group-scheme torsor's field-point profile is an ordinary TI profile. Thus reflection of all TI decisions is equivalent to injectivity on the first flat cohomology of every affine finite-type group scheme. [Proof](ArithmeticTI.md) · [Prior side-six tensor classification](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/5e2ab397cd7bcaaec1a54642f5e62c0558a8e77d/DickeAndIsomorphismMethods/GEOMETRY.md) · [Lin–Shinder–Zimmermann, Corollary 2.6 and Example 2.7](https://content.algebraicgeometry.nl/2023-6/2023-6-024.pdf) · [Milne](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.05708).
5. **Side-two integral tensors with any exact entry degree.** The ideal class of a single fractional ideal controls a $2\times2\times2$ tensor-isomorphism problem, idempotent conjugacy, unimodular symmetric congruence, and integral rank-two decomposition simultaneously. All completion tests are positive. The first accepting extension of integer rings has degree exactly the ideal-class order. A general split-diagonal classification gives the entire profile by ideal classes and one common column permutation. This extends the small-tensor arithmetic in Grochow–Qiao–Stange, Part V, to exact base-change profiles in the split genus. [Proof and explicit cubic example](IntegralCubes.md) · [Part V, Section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00735#S4).

## Two joint consequences for degrees of descent fields

**Prescribed prime-divisibility formulas.** Fix a finite set of input primes and a monotone conjunctive normal form with nonempty clauses in the tests $\ell\mid d$. There are two ordinary central simple multiplication tensors and a common number-field center $L/K$ such that the formula holds at $d$ exactly when some degree-$d$ field disjoint from $L$ admits a witness. On the finite cube of probe composita in [the descent theorem](Descent.md), the same formula holds with each bit indicating whether its prime-degree probe occurs in the compositum. The center may be taken abelian with minimum generator number at most one less than the maximum clause width.

For a clause with $k\ge2$ literals, take a private elementary abelian factor of rank $k-1$, with a distinct prime for each clause. Impose the $k$ constraints $x_1=0,\ldots,x_{k-1}=0,\sum x_j=1$, one for each literal. They are jointly inconsistent and every proper subfamily has a solution. Use the product of all constraints assigned to a literal as its center-map coset, with unconstrained coordinates on the other factors. A width-one clause uses an empty center-map constraint for its literal. Realize the desired product group over $K$ by disjoint fresh prime-conductor cyclotomic factors as in the descent chapter. Its exact realization theorem deletes precisely the constraints whose prime divides $d$. Thus the remaining constraints are consistent exactly when the original formula holds. The rank assertion follows from distinct Sylow primes for the separate clauses. In particular a cyclic center can realize every vertex-cover condition of a finite graph on the prime divisors of $d$.

**Eventual periodicity for arbitrary central simple pairs.** For fixed equal-degree central simple algebras with common Galois center $L/K$, the positive degrees $d$ for which some degree-$d$ extension disjoint from $L$ gives an ordinary tensor isomorphism form an eventually periodic set. In fact it is a finite union of intersections of additive submonoids of $\mathbb N$.

For a fixed center map $g$, let $\delta$ be the difference of the two corresponding Brauer classes. Consider only the finitely many $K$-places under a nonzero local invariant of $\delta$. At each such $v$, take the degrees of those finite separable local fields $F/K_v$ for which, for every $w\mid v$, every field factor of $L_w\otimes_{K_v}F$ has degree over $L_w$ divisible by the order of the local invariant at $w$. Finite sums of these degrees form the local additive submonoid. Any extension splitting $\delta$ contributes such a sum at every $v$, by restriction of local invariants.

Conversely, given $d$ in all the local submonoids, choose the indicated local étale algebras of rank $d$. Realize each by a separable monic polynomial over $K_v$ of degree $d$, choosing distinct translated primitive polynomials for repeated factors. Local polynomial approximation preserves the isomorphism types, by Krasner's lemma and stability of the number of real roots at archimedean places. Simultaneously require the global polynomial to be Eisenstein at an auxiliary finite prime of $K$ splitting completely in $L$. Weak approximation on coefficients meets every condition. Eisenstein over $L$ gives degree $d$ and disjointness from $L$, and the local invariant criterion plus Albert–Brauer–Hasse–Noether gives the witness for $g$. Taking the union over $g$ proves the assertion, since an additive submonoid of the nonnegative integers is eventually all multiples of its gcd if it has a positive member. The oriented version restricts the same union to the allowed signs. [Local Brauer restriction and descent](Descent.md).

The notes give written proofs and provisional priority. The companion [Lean theorem](SplitCubeDeterminant.lean) verifies the exact slice-pencil determinant of the explicit integral cube. It verifies the example's coefficient identity. The broader proof-complexity and descent arguments are in the manuscripts. No result here assumes Dirichlet GRH.
