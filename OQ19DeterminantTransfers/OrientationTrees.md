# A single ordinary tensor comparison for determinant constraints on rooted algebra actions

Let \(F=\mathbb F_3\), fix a constant \(C\), and let \(L\) be the dense input length. Regard a raw three-tensor as a bilinear map \(U\times Y\to Z\) by dualizing its first two roles. The inverse determinant of either dualized map equals its original determinant over \(F\).

**Theorem.** Consider raw tensors equivalent to the action \(A\times M\to M\) of a finite-dimensional unital associative \(F\)-algebra \(A\) on a faithful module \(M\). Let \(J=\operatorname{rad} A\). Suppose the quotient is split commutative, \(E=A/J=F^{\mathcal V}\), with primitive idempotents \(e_v\). The directed graph with an arrow \(u\to v\) when \(e_u(J/J^2)e_v\ne0\) is an outward rooted tree with at most two children per vertex. In every radical grade the spaces
\[
 (J^i/J^{i+1})e_v\quad\text{and}\quad e_v(J^iM/J^{i+1}M)
\]
have dimension at most \(C\log_2(L+2)\), including the grades at \(i=0\). Then the class is recognizable from the raw entries in deterministic polynomial time, and a prescribed determinant triple for the original three maps is testable by one ordinary three-tensor isomorphism instance over \(F\). There is no additional restriction on Loewy length, tree depth or the number of identically typed primitive factors.

## 1. Recognizing the action and the split radical

Check that \(\dim Y=\dim Z>0\) and \(U\to\operatorname{Hom}(Y,Z)\) is injective. Let \(\mathcal S\) be its image. Compute the left idealizer \(A=\{a\in\operatorname{End}(Z):a\mathcal S\subseteq\mathcal S\}\) by linear equations. If \(\mathcal S=A s_0\) for an invertible \(s_0:Y\to Z\), the changes \(u\mapsto T(u,-)s_0^{-1}\), \(y\mapsto s_0 y\), \(z\mapsto z\) put the tensor into the faithful action form on \(M=Z\). Conversely any tensor equivalent to a faithful unital action has this property. For a represented unital algebra its left idealizer equals itself by evaluating at the identity. Independent raw rebasing conjugates the left idealizer in the output role and replaces \(s_0\) by an invertible map between the new input and output coordinates. [Prior action recognition and transporter formula](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/84b007e1048cdf24df3ff66050cd5cc33bf979c7/DeterminantTensorRoutes/GeometryRoute.md)

In an algebra basis let \(B\) be the span of \(a^3-a\) on vectors supported on at most three coordinates. This equals its span on all \(a\). Every monomial of the vector-valued polynomial \(a^3-a\) uses at most three coordinates. For any fixed vector, group its monomials by their exact supports. The contribution of any such support is an inclusion–exclusion combination of evaluations on restrictions of the vector to subsets of that support. Computing \(B\) thus takes polynomial time over the fixed field. If the quotient by \(J\) is split, then \(B\subseteq J\). Conversely, for \(j\in J\) use the identity
\[
 j=-\sum_{h=0}^{s-1}\big((j^{3^h})^3-j^{3^h}\big), \qquad j^{3^s}=0 .
\]
It shows \(J\subseteq B\). To check the promise, test that \(B\) is a two-sided nilpotent ideal and that \(A/B\) is commutative with all basis elements fixed by cubing. Cubing in this quotient is linear over \(F\), so every element is fixed. The quotient is reduced and its finite field factors must all equal \(F\). These tests imply \(B=J\). Compute ideal powers by basis multiplication, stopping when they vanish or stabilize. Split the quotient into primitive factors by simultaneous eigenspace splitting for multiplication by its basis elements, whose minimal polynomials divide \(x^3-x\). Projection of the identity to those one-dimensional spaces gives the idempotents.

The slice module \(\mathcal S\) has a cyclic generator exactly when each \(e_v\)-part of \(\mathcal S/J\mathcal S\) has dimension at most one. Under this condition choose one nonzero vector in each nonzero part and lift their sum to \(s_0\). Then \(\mathcal S/A s_0=J(\mathcal S/A s_0)\), so nilpotence implies equality \(\mathcal S=A s_0\). Test invertibility of \(s_0\). Any other cyclic generator \(s_1\) equals \(a s_0\) for some \(a\in A\), so an invertible one can exist only if \(s_0\) itself is invertible. This also recovers the normalizing coordinates and their determinants. Compute all the corner and module dimensions in the theorem by the actual graded actions. Arbitrary lifts of the \(e_v\) to \(A\) give these same actions, since changes in \(J\) multiply a layer into the next one. Check the graph and rank conditions directly.

## 2. One vertex permutation on all three modes

On normalized tensors write the three transporter maps \(g,f,h\) in the label, input and output roles. The tensor equation is \(g(a)f=ha\). Evaluating at \(1\) gives \(u=g(1)=hf^{-1}\in(A')^\times\), with invertibility in the algebra following from Cayley–Hamilton on the faithful module. We have
\[
 \phi(a)=f a f^{-1},\qquad g=L_u\phi,\qquad h=uf .
\]
Here \(\phi:A\to A'\) is an algebra isomorphism, since \(g\) is invertible and \(L_u\) is multiplication by a unit. The identities also prove the converse. In particular the recognition conditions are invariant under ordinary tensor transport.

The quotient \(p:E\to E'\) of \(\phi\) permutes the primitive factors and preserves the rooted graph. The label map \(g\) preserves \(J^i\) and is \(p\)-semilinear on each layer for the **right** \(E\)-action, since \(L_u\) commutes with right multiplication. Both \(f\) and \(h\) preserve the module flag \(J^i M\) and are \(p\)-semilinear on each layer for the **left** \(E\)-action. This is immediate for \(f\). For \(h\) note that \(u\) acts on the module layers by its quotient residue in the commutative \(E'\).

Keep the normalized tensor in the system. Include a separate algebra role \(A^{\rm alg}\) with multiplication and action on the input module role. Faithfulness and intertwining force its map to be \(\phi\) induced by the actual \(f\). Include its quotient map to \(E\), the multiplication of \(E\), and the linear parent map \(e_v\mapsto e_{\operatorname{parent}(v)}\) with zero at the root. These records keep \(p\) linked to the actual tensor maps while allowing every genuine tree permutation.

## 3. The determinant line on a permutable rooted tree

Suppose a space \(X=\bigoplus_v X_v\) is linked to a rooted binary tree by the projection action of its vertex coordinate algebra. Choose a basis in each \(X_v\) and an order on the children of every node, separately on both inputs. The frame of a subtree starts with the local basis and continues with its child subtree frames in their chosen order. The orders enter only the coordinates of the records.

Include the componentwise exterior algebra \(\mathcal H=\bigoplus_v\bigwedge X_v\), its multiplication, the projection action of the vertex algebra and the degree-one inclusion \(X\to\mathcal H\). Matching forces the exterior action on every fiber since multiplication and the unit determine it from the degree-one vectors. Link the module \(P=\bigoplus_v P_v\) of top lines \(P_v=\bigwedge^{\dim X_v}X_v\) by inclusion into \(\mathcal H\) and its projection action. The unit supplies the top line when \(X_v=0\).

Use two more vertex-indexed line modules, \(L_*=\bigoplus_v L_v\) and \(Q_*=\bigoplus_v Q_v\), for the subtree and child-subtree determinants, with these four records in their displayed bases.

1. A linear map from the vertex algebra to \(Q_*\) sends the leaf idempotents to the chosen bases of their \(Q_v\) and is zero elsewhere.
2. A linear map from \(L_*\) to \(Q_*\) sends the unique child's basis to its parent's \(Q_v\) when there is just one child and is zero elsewhere.
3. A bilinear map \(L_*\times L_*\to Q_*\) sends the bases of two children \(x,y\), in their chosen order, to the basis of their parent's \(Q_v\). The reversed order has coefficient \((-1)^{b_xb_y}\), where \(b_x,b_y\) are their subtree dimensions. Other pairs map to zero.
4. A bilinear map \(P\times Q_*\to L_*\) multiplies line coordinates at each matching vertex and vanishes across distinct vertices.

For a given map on \(X\) and a tree permutation \(\pi\), these records force the new line scalars by induction up the tree. At a leaf the factor of the absent children is one. At a node with two children the product of their determinants is multiplied by \((-1)^{b_xb_y}\) exactly when the chosen child order is reversed. This is the determinant sign for interchanging two blocks of those dimensions. The equation on the reversed input pair is then consistent because this sign squares to one and the two dimensions are carried to their counterparts. The local top line supplies the remaining determinant factor. Conversely the actual subtree determinants give invertible maps on all these roles satisfying every record. The root line, linked by inclusion into \(L_*\), therefore carries the determinant in the tree frames. The total auxiliary dimension is \(O(|\mathcal V|+\sum_v2^{\dim X_v})\), in addition to \(X\), and the number of records is constant. The construction uses the usual determinant-line sign for direct sums and specializes earlier intrinsic graph orientation to a direct rooted recursion. [Knudsen–Mumford, determinant lines](https://www.dam.brown.edu/people/mumford/alg_geom/papers/1976b--DetDiv%2BKnudsenSequel-GT.pdf) · [Prior intrinsic graph determinant tests](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/84b007e1048cdf24df3ff66050cd5cc33bf979c7/RawResidueDeterminantTests/IntrinsicGraphDeterminantTests.md)

## 4. Arbitrarily many grades in a fixed tensor signature

Take \(t\) to be the nilpotence index of \(J\), with \(t=1\) for \(J=0\). Distinct indices on the two inputs rule out transport. On any of the three roles write \(X^0=X\supseteq X^1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq X^t=0\) for its flag. Use a grade algebra \(G=F^t\) with primitive idempotents \(a_0,\dots,a_{t-1}\), its multiplication and the predecessor map \(a_i\mapsto a_{i-1}\) with zero at \(i=0\). Its maps fix every grade label. Put \(\mathcal F_X=\bigoplus_{i=0}^{t-1} X^i\) with the \(G\)-projection action. Use the map \(X\hookrightarrow\mathcal F_X\) onto the zeroth copy and the endomorphism \(S_X\) of \(\mathcal F_X\) given by each inclusion \(X^i\hookrightarrow X^{i-1}\) and zero on the zeroth copy. An intertwiner of these records acts on each copy by the restriction of the original map, by injectivity of each inclusion. The quotient map to \(\mathcal F_X/\operatorname{im} S_X\) therefore forces the actual map on \(\operatorname{gr}X\).

Use one copy of the original binary tree for every grade and attach them along a new rooted spine \(s_0,\ldots,s_{t-1}\). Each \(s_i\) has the root of grade copy \(i\) as a child and has \(s_{i+1}\) as its other child when \(i+1<t\). Give the spine nodes zero blocks. In the coordinate algebra of this enlarged tree, include its parent map and the two linear maps \(a_i\mapsto e_{s_i}\) from \(G\) and \(e_v\mapsto\sum_i e_{(i,v)}\) from \(E\). The first fixes the spine, so it fixes the grade copies. The second forces their permutations to agree with the same actual map \(p\) on \(E\). Include the projection action on \(\operatorname{gr}X\), using the actual \(E\)-summands in its respective grade. Every genuine original transporter extends to these records by Section 2.

Apply Section 3 with the grade child ordered first at each spine node. The root line then reads the product of the actual grade determinants. In the adapted basis obtained by lifting the concatenated grade frames, the original map is block triangular with those same grade maps on its diagonal. Its determinant in the prescribed coordinates is thus the root scalar times the ratio of target to source adapted frame determinants, including the raw normalization. Pin the three calibrated signs by vector marks on the three respective terminal lines. This uses the same raw transporter throughout. A genuine transporter with the prescribed signs extends to all the records, and any matching of the records restricts to such a transporter.

The number of tree nodes and total dimension of the flag copies are polynomial in \(L\). Every local exterior algebra has dimension at most \(2^{C\log_2(L+2)}\), and the number of roles and records is constant. All records, including the vector marks, have tensor order at most three. Thus the fixed-signature tensor-system compiler gives one ordinary three-tensor comparison over \(F\), of polynomial dense size. Failed recognition outside the class, mismatched raw formats, or unequal dimensions of corresponding constructed roles can be sent to a fixed negative instance. [Chen–Grochow–Qiao–Tang–Zhang, Theorem 4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S4)

## 5. Unbounded examples and scope

Take the path algebra \(A\) of the complete outward binary tree of height \(H\), with one arrow per edge. Take \(M=A^*\), the dual of the right regular module, with left action \((a\lambda)(b)=\lambda(ba)\). This is faithful by testing \(b=1\) against every \(\lambda\). The grade \(J^i/J^{i+1}\) is based by paths of length \(i\), with at most one such path ending at a given vertex. Its right \(e_v\)-parts have dimensions at most one. Acting on a dual path by a path deletes a matching terminal segment, or gives zero. It follows that \(J^iM\) is based by the duals of the paths ending at vertices of depth at most \(H-i\), since these and only these paths can be extended by \(i\) more arrows. Thus the grade at vertex \(v\) is zero unless \(\operatorname{depth}(v)=H-i\), when its dimension is \(\operatorname{depth}(v)+1\). This is logarithmic in the vertex count. All three mode dimensions and the Loewy length grow.

The sibling pairs of leaves can be interchanged independently. Each swap extends by permuting path coordinates in \(A\) and by inverse transpose on \(M\). These give at least \(2^{2^{H-1}}\) distinct label permutations, and every base for that action contains a leaf from each sibling pair. For even \(H\ge2\), one bottom sibling swap interchanges \(H+1\) pairs of paths, and has sign \(-1\) in all three roles. This exhibits the permutation signs the tree records track without fixing the identities of the leaves.

The proof gives a polynomial many-one reduction for this raw recognizable action class. It combines elementary split-radical recognition, a shared intrinsic tree and Loewy filtration, and linkage of all three determinant lines in one fixed tensor signature. The general one-sign alternating-space problem and the unrestricted reduction in Open Question 1.9 remain open. The argument here is written mathematics. [Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5)
