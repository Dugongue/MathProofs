# Four informal results on group, tensor, and matrix isomorphism

1. **Succinct class-two group isomorphism and free tensor isomorphism over prime-power rings.** Over a fixed or specified prime, isomorphism of class-at-most-two p-groups in centered power-commutator coordinates and free three-tensor isomorphism over variable prime-power modulus are polynomial-time many-one equivalent. In the forward direction the divided-square construction retains the entire central power information at p = 2. [Proof](GroupTensorEquivalence.md)
2. **Least extension degree for rational tensor isomorphism.** Finding the least extension degree is NP-hard even for pairs that become isomorphic over a real number field. A polynomial-time recognizable family has NP-complete threshold testing and admits polynomial-time feasibility tests at specified binary degrees. On this family even a multiplicative factor exponential in a small power of the input length is NP-hard to approximate. The proof works over any fixed number field. [Proof](RationalExtensionDegree.md)
3. **Matrix-tuple isometry in characteristic two with unitary or exchanged residue factors.** An algorithm handles arbitrarily long radicals, with the precise hypotheses on the fixed residue factors and the base field stated in the proof. It uses invariant flags to pass from one affine orbit condition to the next and produces a transporter and exact count. [Proof](UnitaryResidueIsometry.md)
4. **A polynomial kernel for ordinary tensor isomorphism.** Remove repeated Kronecker slice modules and retain only their multiplicities in a linked partition. A single smaller ordinary tensor comparison preserves every feasible label change. Its side is linear in the trimmed dimensions and the label dimension. Witness recovery is polynomial, and exact count and uniform sampling transfer with computable fiber sizes. [Proof](SliceModuleKernel.md)

These are informal proofs. The reductions specify their input models and running times. Targeted comparisons with primary sources and previous MathProofs constructions are included in the proof documents. Publication priority and independent human review remain pending.
