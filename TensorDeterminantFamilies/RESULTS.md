# Determinant restrictions in three-tensor isomorphism over the field of three elements

These informal proofs give deterministic single-instance polynomial reductions on the families specified below. The four unrestricted many-one degrees remain in the same known order. No claim of strictness, unrestricted collapse or publication priority is made. All tensor transporters have three independent mode maps, including in the symmetric examples.

* [Cyclic tensors](CyclicTensors.md). On tensors presented in common cyclic coordinates, each of the three signed restrictions has exactly the degree of unrestricted one-sign TI. First-mode and first-two-mode decision even coincide on the same pair. The reverse reduction packs three distinguished rotations of a local algebra action tensor.
* [Removing matrix multiplication factors](MatrixFactors.md). The three gcds of fixed-label slice multiplicities give a classification by parity. One even factor leaves at most the one-sign degree. Two even factors give ordinary TI degree. The stratum with all gcds equal to one retains the unrestricted degree at every tier. The reduction computes the exact determinant correction for every feasible label map before composition.
* [Symmetric and alternating matrix pencils](Reflection.md). For each family the four restricted degrees, in order, are ordinary TI, one-sign TI, one-sign TI and two-sign TI. The lower bound uses quiver reflection and the prior informal sign-projection construction. The label changes and the two matrix maps are independent.
* [Tensor systems equivalent to their transpose](Transpose.md). Transposing a transporter with fixed-label adapters removes one of the simultaneous determinant constraints. This covers symmetric and alternating matrix spaces, including identically singular ones, under independent left and right changes of basis. The [prior sign change of coordinates](StructuralComparison.md) is a supporting dependency.
* [Centroids that are chain rings](ChainCentroid.md). If every mode module has only even cyclic summand lengths, all four restricted problems have unrestricted ordinary TI degree. A single norm on the centroid tangent line carries all three physical signs. Taking a square root on that line implements the required predicate on the same transporter.
* [Filtered local Frobenius multiplication tensors](Frobenius.md). All three signs can be tested by one ordinary TI instance when the skew radicals of the individual diagonal pairing spaces have logarithmic dimension. The algebra may be noncommutative and ungraded. The number of those spaces and the other filtration dimensions can grow.

The [affine-profile check](check_cyclic.py) exhausts the rotation-invariant affine sign profiles. The [small tensor and marker controls](check_profiles.py) check complete sign profiles in two formats and the units and automorphisms of the degree-four marker. These finite calculations supplement the mathematical proofs. [Chen, Grochow, Qiao, Tang and Zhang, open questions](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5)
