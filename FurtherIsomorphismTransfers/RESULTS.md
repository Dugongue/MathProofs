# Four further isomorphism transfers

The four routes start from quadratic dynamics, determinantal presentations, quantum channels, and multiparameter persistence. The ranking considers scope, computational consequence, and which proof would be most worth showing to Joshua Grochow. Each proof is informal. Publication priority remains unestablished.

| Rank | Result | Main consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Tensor isomorphism in bounded-degree symplectic dynamics](DIFFERENTIAL.md) | Over every field, arbitrary ordered 3TI is equivalent to polynomial conjugacy of explicit quadratic symplectic automorphisms. Arbitrarily nonlinear conjugators cannot identify extra instances. All iterates have degree at most two. In characteristic two the constructed instances can all have order eight. |
| 2 | [Polynomial-bit-time rational TI for integral ternary determinants](DETERMINANTS.md) | Both GL-TI and SL-TI are in deterministic polynomial time for rational square three-slice pencils of size at least three with geometrically irreducible determinant. This includes singular curves and the full infinite-projective-stabilizer branch. |
| 3 | [Graph isomorphism with both channel spectra fixed](QUANTUM.md) | GI-complete entanglement-breaking quantum channels have fixed Choi and superoperator spectra per input order, both maximally mixed marginals, and exponentially small diamond distance from depolarizing. The raw rational Choi orbit saturation is recognizable in polynomial time. |
| 4 | [Persistence isomorphism with exponential holonomy](DIAGRAMS.md) | Relabelable finite-poset local systems admit exact GI reductions under checkable bounds on shortest transport states and generator count, even when the full holonomy group is exponentially large. The determinant-one result uses GI queries and gives actual gauges. |

## What changed in each proof

**Dynamics.** The cotangent lift makes the triangular map exactly symplectic. Three distinct Jordan lengths attach the three tensor modes to intrinsic subquotients of the linear part. For an arbitrary conjugator, translating its image of the origin leaves the derivative unchanged, because any new bottom-to-top derivative coupling would enlarge its rank after subtracting the identity. On the fixed subspace the quadratic conjugator terms cancel modulo the image of the linear nilpotent. Polarizing what remains recovers the tensor transporter. This explains why the permission to use arbitrary polynomial conjugators does not enlarge the orbit relation. The main proof also supplies the Hamiltonian flow and an exact finite-field control. [Control program](check_dynamics.py)

**Rational pencils.** The adjugate identifies the cokernel as a rank-one torsion-free module over the integral determinant cone. This reduces strict transport at each proposed projectivity to a linear system of dimension at most one. In the infinite-stabilizer ternary case, the Lie equation recovers three rational eigenlines and yields a binomial determinant. The whole remaining projective transporter is a finite union of rational one-parameter families. A polynomial generator of each everywhere-present strict transporter has monomial determinants, so Smith normal form and exact rational-power tests decide the full SL conditions.

**Quantum channels.** In each pair of graph vertices a rational orthogonal change can switch a local rank-one encoding of an edge to a nonedge and leaves the channel's global linear spectrum unchanged. The same switch preserves entry multisets needed for the Choi spectrum. A spectrally separated subspace forces any local-unitary transporter to be monomial on both sides with a common permutation. The rest of the channel recovers the graph and leaves explicitly described phase freedoms.

**Persistence.** Retain only distinct shortest-path matrices and their transition relations. One bounded generator tuple and its word-basis coordinates certify the other required loop comparisons, without listing the group generated by the loops. Root conjugacy then lifts to every vertex. The determinant constraint reduces to the common root determinant and its centralizer character. A separate branch uses full characteristic polynomials for small semisimple holonomy.

## Cross-composition with the arithmetic results

The dynamics construction works over every field and preserves the answer under extension. Therefore the earlier Frobenius realizations also prescribe where two explicit quadratic symplectic automorphisms are polynomially conjugate modulo good primes, including all finite residue extensions. Choose rational bases for the concise modes and omit finitely many primes where their defining ranks drop or coefficients have denominators. The specialized dynamics construction then still uses the correct concise tensors.

Here is the needed arithmetic input. Let a finite Galois group $\Gamma$ over $\mathbb Q$ and a conjugacy-invariant power-closed subset $S$ containing the identity be given. For each conjugacy class of cyclic subgroups $H$ whose generators are outside $S$, take the virtual $\Gamma$-set $\sum_{D\le H}\mu([H:D])\,|D|[\Gamma/D]$. Its mark at a subgroup $C$ is $|N_\Gamma(H)|$ if $C$ is conjugate to $H$, and zero otherwise. This follows by counting the cosets fixed by $C$ and applying Möbius inversion on the subgroups of $H$. Sum these virtual sets and separate the positive and negative terms into actual sets $X,Y$ of equal size, since the trivial-subgroup mark is zero. If the virtual sum is empty, take the same one-point set on both sides. The restrictions of $X,Y$ to $\langle g\rangle$ are isomorphic exactly when $g\in S$. Indeed the fixed point counts of all powers determine the cycles of $g$. Finite étale algebras translate this condition over the good residue extension of degree $j$ to $\mathrm{Frob}_p^j\in S$. Their multiplication tensors are isomorphic exactly when the algebras are isomorphic, by normalizing an isotopy at the unit. The dynamics reduction now gives the prescribed polynomial-conjugacy profile. [Finite étale Galois sets](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03QR)

The earlier everywhere-local construction gives $2^r$ mutually nonconjugate rational quadratic Hamiltonian time-one maps on a phase space of dimension $O(r)$, with every pair conjugate by linear symplectic maps over every completion of $\mathbb Q$. To see the linear size, start from the marked bilinear algebras for the two norm parameters $1,3$ in the explicit biquadratic Hasse example. Take $r$ copies with one common left identity. Use distinct right-multiplication eigenvalue tags on all the other marked summands and set cross-copy products to zero. The common left identity and the eigenspaces are intrinsic, so the algebra transporter splits into the independently prescribed norm equations in the $r$ copies. Each contributes two locally equivalent and globally distinct choices, with constant dimension per copy. Apply the prior linear-side tensor transporter construction, then the linear-dimension dynamics construction. This gives explicit pairwise nonconjugate representatives and does not classify all polynomial forms.
