# Two determinant certificates for signed tensor isomorphism

Both results concern explicit three-tensor pairs over F₃.

- [Koszul determinant certificate](Koszul.md) gives one ordinary TI query for a one-sign problem in specified formats when a square Koszul flattening has logarithmic corank. The sign is read by two calibrated determinant lines. An explicit family attains corank zero with all modes unbounded.
- [Field-norm certificate](FieldNorm.md) eliminates one designated determinant when a covariant extension-field action makes that mode logarithmic in rank over the extension. A two-label square-class mark enforces the Frobenius contribution in even extension degree and odd module rank. The other designated determinants can remain on the same transporter.

These are informal proofs of sufficient conditions. Universal coverage and publication priority remain open. The motivating containment is [Chen, Grochow, Qiao, Tang and Zhang, Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5).
