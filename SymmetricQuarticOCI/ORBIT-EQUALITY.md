# ORDINARY ORBIT EQUALITY COROLLARY (same balanced source action)

For the affine map in PROOF.md, exact GL(V) orbit equality of the source pair of tensors is also equivalent to exact GL(W) orbit equality of their encoded single symmetric tensors. Forward direction is equivariance. If g T(x_1,x_2)=T(y_1,y_2), each Q_ba is a conjugation covariant and both endpoint values are E_ba. Thus g centralizes all E_ba. Commuting with E_aa makes g block diagonal; commuting with every transfer E_ba forces its four blocks to agree. Hence g=beta(h) for h in GL(V). Equivariance and injectivity of the data embedding imply (y_1,y_2)=h(x_1,x_2). This corollary concerns only the stated balanced representations. It does not claim unrestricted TI/SL containment or use exact orbit equality to infer the independently proved closure reflection.
