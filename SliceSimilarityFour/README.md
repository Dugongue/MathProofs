# Four extensions of identical slice similarity

The starting construction encodes ordinary tensor isomorphism in algebras whose matched slices have the same similarity class. It also gives a single conjugator on each marked low-dimensional subpencil. These four results transfer those properties to matrix substitutions, subgroup representations, projective varieties and exact threshold computations. [Starting proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/HessianFour/Tensor.md)

| Result | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Identical rational canonical forms for small matrix substitutions](Tensor.md) | For substitution size $q\le h$, arbitrary coefficient matrices give equal rational canonical forms over fields and equal polynomial Smith forms over finite chain rings in the final-digit version. A pair separates at $h+1$. The partially symmetric version also gives metric congruences on the marked subpencils. |
| [Labeled conjugacy on every small subgroup](Representations.md) | Uniformly over prime-power rings, restrictions to every subgroup generated by at most $h$ elements are conjugate by a single determinant-one matrix. Full image conjugacy is TI-complete. The odd prime-field representations have constant Jordan type. |
| [Smooth projective varieties with identical small restrictions](Geometry.md) | Over any field, abstract isomorphism of the constructed varieties is equivalent to input TI. They have one Hilbert polynomial and identical projective-bundle restrictions over every base linear subspace of dimension at most $h-1$. |
| [Exact threshold for marked subpencil distinction](Algorithms.md) | In the original replicated construction the first distinguishing direction dimension is the replication factor times the minimum rank in the symmetric difference of the two input matrix spaces. The proof gives explicit tests and families with many pairwise inequivalent outputs sharing all small tests. |

The four results have written proofs. The finite scripts check supporting instances, and [QuadraticUnitProduct.lean](QuadraticUnitProduct.lean) independently checks the expansion used in the group construction. The notes credit the closest primary results. Publication priority for the exact extensions remains open. Prepared for MathProofs with AI assistance.
