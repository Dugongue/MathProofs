# Four further transfers from Alpöge's Riemann release to tensor isomorphism

The four proofs below extend work on the first four transfers. The arithmetic proof retains Dirichlet GRH and the additional Dedekind RH assumptions stated for each result. The other three do not need a hypothesis on zeros of an $L$-function. All four are written proofs with independent mathematical cross-review. The verification record states the scope of the Lean check.

1. **Nonabelian Frobenius certificates.** Signed tensor-square amplification turns differences of permutation characters into bounded prime certificates. Normal field factors admit an exact comparison through $C\log^2(2B_0)$. For general factors in a supplied Galois model, joint fixed-point counts of aligned residue Frobenii recover the Galois set and construct and count rational algebra isomorphisms. A concrete nonabelian family uses only the Dirichlet endpoint, because the square of the character difference factors through the abelianization. [Proof](NonabelianFrobeniusTensors.md)
2. **Full quadratic norm-one descent.** The released quadratic Hasse norm theorem reduces arbitrary Smith incidence in linked cubic tensor systems to power equations in a norm-one torus. Each exact local-to-global obstruction is an explicit bit. The proof characterizes surviving bits by local Galois images and constructs a nonsplit quadratic family with $2^m$ globally distinct tensor orbits that are locally identical. [Proof](QuadraticNormOneDescent.md)
3. **Canonical transport in all positive-depth residue classes.** The theta-lattice row criterion applied to group stabilizer layers gives polynomial-time canonization, witness construction and exact counting in a supplied residue class over an explicitly presented finite local ring. It handles mixed variance, prescribed determinants, all Smith profiles and dyadic depth one. [Proof](CanonicalCongruenceTransport.md)
4. **Special-linear transport on square-zero multiplication isotopes.** The trace Gram criterion recovers the radical whenever its regular multiplicity weights are nonzero in the field. Together with Frobenius recovery in finite characteristic, the result computes the separate left and right norm subgroups and the entire determinant transporter coset. Over a fixed finite field it yields a single ordinary tensor-isomorphism query on the recognized raw locus. [Proof](SquareZeroIsotopes.md)

The proof scopes include explicit arithmetic or algebraic hypotheses and do not settle unrestricted special-linear tensor isomorphism. [Verification record](Verification.json) · [Alpöge's release](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf)
