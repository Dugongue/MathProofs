# Affine schemes, spherical Hanner moments, and étale tensor proof degree

These written crossover arguments build on the MathProofs portfolio and the cited literature. Priority remains provisional.

- [Affine schemes as relative mapping classes](UniversalRelativeMappingSchemes.md). Every finitely presented affine scheme over the integers is realized on a simply connected four-dimensional pair with one fixed two-sphere marking. The construction retains the coordinate ring and realizes ring-valued points naturally.
- [The closed-manifold realization](UniversalSixManifoldMappingSchemes.md). The source can be a closed simply connected stably parallelizable smooth six-manifold when the marking varies. The integral target can also be a fixed closed smooth manifold.
- [Stable scheme universality of a fixed bilinear map](BilinearSchemeUniversality.md). The residue fields of its scalar ring give the exact criterion for realizing all affine schemes up to smooth factors over a perfect field.
- [Exact complexity by bilinear value image](BilinearComplexityTrichotomy.md). Raw coefficient-only systems are trivial for zero image, NP-complete for finite nonzero image in the fixed-table presentation, and computably enumerable complete for infinite image.
- [Sharp spherical Hanner curvature](SphericalHannerRiesz.md). A single Hessian identity treats positive, logarithmic, and negative moments. It gives the exact Riesz threshold and an explicit comparison for the Jensen deficit.
- [Étale tensor proof degree](EtaleMatchingDegree.md). For finite-field multiplication tensors the minimum degree of the standard cubic proof system is compared in both directions to rectangular permutation systems, with an explicit Nullstellensatz lower-bound family.
