# From early phase invariants to exact isomorphism reductions

Two informal proofs developed from the earlier Rune, Ley, Sigil, Ward, and Trellis experiments.

- [Graph isomorphism on a fixed cubic](FixedCubicCollinearity.md). Collinearity isomorphism of rational point sets on one fixed smooth cubic is GI-complete. There are also explicit constructions on the cusp with polynomially bounded integer coordinates, and versions over finite fields of polynomial cardinality.
- [Cyclic phase equivalence with binary moduli](CyclicPhaseEquivalence.md). Additive row and column shifts, permutations, and one common cyclotomic automorphism reduce to a single graph-isomorphism instance without factoring the phase modulus or expanding it into that many vertices. The method extends to sparse gain graphs of bounded induced cycle length and to full-support tensors under independent monomial transformations in every mode.

The cubic proof uses the entire collinearity relation. The phase proof uses indexed pair labels and generalized Chinese remaindering to recover one global unit. Both statements and their group actions are specified in the proofs. Publication priority has not been established.

The residue test matched exhaustive unit orbits and reconstructed witnesses for all 44,099 ordered triples across moduli 2 through 20. An exact determinant check recovered the whole intended relation for all 1,100 labelled graphs through order five. An independent anchored comparison also rejected the published $\mathrm{BH}(12,4)$ example after considering every anchor, transpose, and conjugation choice, and accepted a planted equivalence. These are computational controls for the proofs. [Butson switching, Example 3.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.18296v1#S3)
