# Lean components for ordinary even-order tensor isomorphism

This packet supports the [almost-all ordinary even-order theorem](../OrdinaryFourTIFusions/EvenOrder.md). The theorem concerns dense balanced tensors over a fixed finite field, with variable even order, a random first input, and arbitrary second input.

- [EvenPhysicalAction.lean](EvenPhysicalAction.lean) formalizes the variable-order ordinary tensor action. For a nonzero source whose stabilizers have scalar mode maps, one transporter gives a bijection between all filtered transporters and the product-one scalar tuples that pass the filters. The determinant of a scalar-adjusted mode map is computed explicitly.
- [EvenFlattening.lean](EvenFlattening.lean) checks covariance of the square-cut flattening, its side dimension, and regrouping around any retained physical mode.
- [EvenReciprocalShear.lean](EvenReciprocalShear.lean) proves that the rectangular crossed contraction is affine under the square-zero shear and that the update preserves the inverse-transpose row pairing.
- [EvenReciprocalLaw.lean](EvenReciprocalLaw.lean) turns the exact shear into a bijection of primal and dual pairs and proves the uniform-average and good-baseline transfer identities.
- [EvenReciprocalRank.lean](EvenReciprocalRank.lean) gives exact image dimensions for one-sided matrix multiplication and bounds the rank lost by composing a square linear map with rectangular multiplications.
- [EvenSingularProjection.lean](EvenSingularProjection.lean) proves surjectivity and covariance of the kernel-form projection. The image of any subspace on which the kernel form vanishes is trace zero, with codimension at most that of the source subspace.
- [EvenSingularColumns.lean](EvenSingularColumns.lean) gives the finite-field dependent-column probability bound used for conditioning the singular projection.
- [EvenSingularPrefix.lean](EvenSingularPrefix.lean) gives equal completion counts for every independent list of fixed length and its exact event law inside a uniform ordered basis.
- [EvenSingularConditioning.lean](EvenSingularConditioning.lean) bounds the change in any event probability by the discarded fraction when conditioning a finite uniform law.
- [EvenCells.lean](EvenCells.lean) proves the exact field-cell ratio test and dimension identity for a supplied cell decomposition, and the reduction of witness recovery to a bounded-torsion correction.
- [EvenCellsSplit.lean](EvenCellsSplit.lean) proves that a tensor product of finite-field factors admits a field decomposition and that tensor products of finite products of finite fields are reduced. This is an algebraic existence result.
- [EvenCellsBlocks.lean](EvenCellsBlocks.lean) identifies the unique active primary coordinate for each cell incidence and verifies that variables inherited from the same source block remain shared across cells.
- [EvenCellsPower.lean](EvenCellsPower.lean) proves that a coordinate power factors through the image of the cell map exactly when it vanishes on its kernel.
- [EvenCellsPrefix.lean](EvenCellsPrefix.lean) replaces each arbitrary-length cell equation by a linear-length chain of ternary rows and proves uniqueness of the auxiliary prefix values.
- [EvenProductCount.lean](EvenProductCount.lean) checks the product-state count over a finite abelian group and the size bound on each table entry.
- [EvenScalarCertification.lean](EvenScalarCertification.lean) proves the scalar stabilizer test once the self-comparison branches are known to cover the stabilizer and their homogeneous subgroups are supplied by generators.
- [EvenFilteredTransport.lean](EvenFilteredTransport.lean) combines those checks to give exact counting after a complete transporter search and a scalar stabilizer certificate. On a yes-instance of order d the unrestricted count is (q − 1)^(d − 1).

The sources were checked using Lean 4.33.0 and Mathlib revision db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d. The audited theorem dependencies use only standard Lean axioms. The coverage theorem is recorded below. End-to-end recognition and binary runtime remain to be formalized. The scalar transporter equivalence assumes a certified scalar stabilizer and a verified transporter.

The mathematical dependencies are the [four-tensor coverage proof](../FourTIAlmostAll/FullCoverage.md), its [transporter construction](../FourTIAlmostAll/SearchAndLifting.md), and the [even-order extension](../OrdinaryFourTIFusions/EvenOrder.md). The Lean files use the relevant Mathlib finite product, matrix, and tensor-product APIs.

## Dense execution and coverage components

- [EvenRootSpec.lean](EvenRootSpec.lean) states the deterministic binary-machine theorem with fixed-field digits and arbitrary balanced even tensor order. [EvenDenseDecode.lean](EvenDenseDecode.lean) verifies the word decoder on the exact source and comparison encodings.
- [EvenWordDriver.lean](EvenWordDriver.lean) scans prepared trials, emits the first valid transporter and the exact unfiltered count, and handles independent word-level filters. Its completeness proof uses the enumerator and trial-correctness premises stated in the file. [EvenSolverAssembly.lean](EvenSolverAssembly.lean) assembles the target contract from those premises, the coverage theorem, and binary compiler proofs.
- [EvenBranchBound.lean](EvenBranchBound.lean) and [EvenBinaryBounds.lean](EvenBinaryBounds.lean) bound the number of kernel-form and repeated-primary trials by the square of the dense input size and charge every visited trial. They verify the witness output size in words.
- [EvenLiveRowLayout.lean](EvenLiveRowLayout.lean) and [EvenLiveRowAddress.lean](EvenLiveRowAddress.lean) build signed ternary rows with repeated-variable endpoint pins and verify the source bank at the address 3r+e. [EvenLiveRowKernel.lean](EvenLiveRowKernel.lean) proves that the prefix extension also preserves the exponent test for recovery.
- [EvenLiveSingularEstimate.lean](EvenLiveSingularEstimate.lean) bounds the probability error of every event after a surjective crop of the exact two-kernel fiber. The proof uses the checked basis extension, kernel-space equivalence, equal completion counts, and finite uniform conditioning bound.
- [EvenPrimaryMoment.lean](EvenPrimaryMoment.lean) proves a field-dependent upper bound on the expected primary budget for uniform matrices and uniform prescribed-trace fibers, including characteristic two. [EvenPrimaryGate.lean](EvenPrimaryGate.lean) verifies the logarithmic rejection bound for correlated mode budgets. [EvenPrimaryMarginal.lean](EvenPrimaryMarginal.lean) verifies the finite marginal-error transfer used before the gate. [EvenPrimaryCoverage.lean](EvenPrimaryCoverage.lean) combines these bounds under the stated pointwise budget cap. [EvenPrimaryFourier.lean](EvenPrimaryFourier.lean) verifies the conversion from the squared Fourier variation bound to real L1 error.
- [EvenRecognizerScan.lean](EvenRecognizerScan.lean) checks a finite scalar stabilizer test on a complete list of self-comparison branches. [EvenStabilizerRank.lean](EvenStabilizerRank.lean) gives the rank lower bound for an action with a nonscalar physical mode. [EvenStabilizerCount.lean](EvenStabilizerCount.lean) converts it to the fixed-tensor count. [EvenStabilizerUnion.lean](EvenStabilizerUnion.lean) gives the aggregate nonscalar exception bound by summing over the possible basis changes.

The driver still requires executable branch production, the physical Smith and witness procedures, the complete source coverage theorem, and binary-time compilation. No end-to-end solver theorem is asserted. The reused binary encoding is from [Problem 1.8](../Problem18/README.md). The primary-budget proof reuses the order-four primary tail proofs and their supporting Problem 1.8 lemmas. Reused source modules are included under their existing import names.

## Dense kernel trials, anchor estimates, and Smith packing

- [EvenNextBankPairs.lean](EvenNextBankPairs.lean) verifies enumeration of the two-sided kernel forms directly from certified row-major basis words. The list contains at most n² trials. [EvenNextTargetPairs.lean](EvenNextTargetPairs.lean) proves that an isomorphism transports every source pair into the target list, including preservation of every retained-mode rank test. [EvenNextSourceChoice.lean](EvenNextSourceChoice.lean) verifies that the source list contains a successful pair invariantly whenever the additional shape test is covariant.
- [EvenNextAnchorSpecialized.lean](EvenNextAnchorSpecialized.lean) bounds the event-probability error for the actual dense singular crop conditioned on the two exact kernels. For crop width b and quotient dimension s, the bound is (q^b − 1)/(q^s(q − 1)), with b ≤ n². [EvenNextAnchorFullRank.lean](EvenNextAnchorFullRank.lean) derives trace zero and a codimension bound from the full-rank test on a selected retained kernel form.
- [EvenNextRecipHighOrder.lean](EvenNextRecipHighOrder.lean) derives a polar rank of at least 4n² for the specified higher-order shear and n ≥ 8, assuming surjectivity of the selected reciprocal row map. In characteristic two this yields an explicit squared total variation bound for the anchor difference on the trace-zero space. [EvenNextRecipOddAnchor.lean](EvenNextRecipOddAnchor.lean) applies the rank estimate to every nonzero trace label and derives the corresponding squared total variation bound on the full matrix space in odd characteristic.
- [EvenNextSmithPhysical.lean](EvenNextSmithPhysical.lean) checks the actual Smith matrix-packing command on installed prefix banks. The proof gives counted execution, exact written coefficients, and preservation of the other state. [EvenNextSmithReference.lean](EvenNextSmithReference.lean) proves equivalence of the reference verdict with the original arbitrary-arity equations.
- [EvenNextScalarDensity.lean](EvenNextScalarDensity.lean) bounds the fraction with a nonscalar stabilizer by q^(−n²) for n ≥ 5 and all orders at least four. This bounds that failure event alone.

These modules reuse Problem 1.7's field-cell packing, trace pairing, and polar rank lemmas, Problem 1.8's machine definitions and Fourier bounds, and Mathlib's finite algebra and matrix results. The audited declarations depend only on standard Lean axioms. The coverage estimates are stated below. Full raw trial and witness execution and the end-to-end binary time theorem remain in progress.

## Reciprocal coverage at even order at least six

- [EvenP1LawSelectedPhysical.lean](EvenP1LawSelectedPhysical.lean) identifies the exact joint law of the selected entries of a uniform reciprocal basis and proves that surjectivity of the selected rectangle supplies surjectivity for the full shear.
- [EvenP1RectTail.lean](EvenP1RectTail.lean) gives a quantitative finite-field cutoff bound for the actual quotient rank of every nonscalar label. [EvenP1ProbLabel.lean](EvenP1ProbLabel.lean) relates this rank to the annihilator constraints of a nonzero functional on the selected output.
- [EvenP1Condition.lean](EvenP1Condition.lean) proves that conditioning each stage on independent rows costs at most two. [EvenP1Dense.lean](EvenP1Dense.lean) identifies the conditioned dense cut with the exact reciprocal basis pair. [EvenP1DenseMarginal.lean](EvenP1DenseMarginal.lean) transfers the conditional Fourier estimate to the raw dense anchor in odd characteristic and characteristic two.

This pass reuses the previous exact shear and polar-rank proof, the Problem 1.8 finite Fourier identities, and the singular crop development's dependent-column counting lemmas. The modules audited for this packet depend only on the standard Lean axioms.

[EvenP1Coverage.lean](EvenP1Coverage.lean) proves the assembled marginal bound. In every finite field of size q, for n ≥ 200 and balanced even order at least six, the actual reciprocal anchor has L1 error at most 9q^(−n²) on the dense source conditioned on an invertible cut. The statement covers each retained physical mode in either cut half. The reference law is uniform on the full matrix space in odd characteristic and on the prescribed trace slice in characteristic two. [EvenP1ProbSurjectivity.lean](EvenP1ProbSurjectivity.lean) gives the q^(−n²) failure fraction before conditioning. [EvenP1BadMass.lean](EvenP1BadMass.lean) transfers that bound to the actual reciprocal basis law with a factor of at most four.

This closes the higher-order reciprocal coverage component. The singular and order-four coverage results are given below.

## Singular coverage at even order at least six

- [EvenP2FormsCoverage.lean](EvenP2FormsCoverage.lean) bounds the fraction with a nonzero kernel form that fails any retained-mode rank test by q^(−n) on each side. [EvenP2FormsFiber.lean](EvenP2FormsFiber.lean) identifies these events on exact kernel fibers and supplies one suitable form pair for each nonexceptional singular fiber.
- [EvenP2Corank.lean](EvenP2Corank.lean) proves the high-corank exception bound 2/n by counting vectors killed by a uniform square cut.
- [EvenP2CropAnchors.lean](EvenP2CropAnchors.lean) identifies the decoded crop anchors with the literal projected squared partial traces. The crop and polar lemmas each charge event error at most q^(−n²) under the small-corank gate and n ≥ 64.
- [EvenP2SpectralFiber.lean](EvenP2SpectralFiber.lean) transfers the checked uniform primary means to the actual dense fiber. The budget of that fixed pair sums the anchors in both halves, with each physical mode used once.

The proof reuses the order-four trace and polar methods, the finite Fourier and dependent-column estimates, and the checked primary expectation. It sums the mode expectations before applying Markov's inequality, so the failure estimate does not grow with tensor order.

[EvenP2Coverage.lean](EvenP2Coverage.lean) combines the exact-fiber results on the raw dense tensor. For every finite field of size q, balanced even order at least six, and n ≥ 64, the fraction of sources whose cut is singular and whose shape test rejects is at most 2/n + 2q^(−n) + C_q/log n, where C_q is the checked field-dependent primary-budget constant plus the transfer allowance, multiplied by log q. The proof uses only standard Lean axioms.

## Order-four coverage

- [EvenP3SelectedForms.lean](EvenP3SelectedForms.lean) and [EvenP3DefectTail.lean](EvenP3DefectTail.lean) bound the second moment of the radical dimension of a nonzero form chosen from an exact cut kernel. The mean is at most four over the raw uniform source, with missing choices charged zero.
- [EvenP3SingularCropLaw.lean](EvenP3SingularCropLaw.lean) and [EvenP3SingularLiveCrop.lean](EvenP3SingularLiveCrop.lean) give the factor-four conditioning bound for any event after the live crop of a uniform exact two-kernel fiber. [EvenP3SingularFiberMean.lean](EvenP3SingularFiberMean.lean) then bounds each live repeated-primary expectation for unequal widths under the numerical hypotheses in the statement.
- [EvenP3SingularEnlarged.lean](EvenP3SingularEnlarged.lean) verifies the characteristic polynomial of a squared partial trace on the supported enlarged blocks. [EvenP3Padding.lean](EvenP3Padding.lean) bounds the additional primary budget in terms of the corresponding squared form defect.
- [EvenP3SingularPack.lean](EvenP3SingularPack.lean) connects those estimates to the two actual enlarged characteristic polynomials computed from arbitrary selected forms of sufficient live width on the raw exact fiber. For a form of radical dimension k the padded mean is at most 8(C + 1) + 2 + 8k², with C the field-dependent primary-budget constant.
- [EvenP3RecipLowSparse.lean](EvenP3RecipLowSparse.lean) and [EvenP3RecipLowMass.lean](EvenP3RecipLowMass.lean) verify the low-label rank bound from selected quotient rows and the conditional law of those rows. [EvenP3RecipHighPhysical.lean](EvenP3RecipHighPhysical.lean), [EvenP3RecipHighBatch.lean](EvenP3RecipHighBatch.lean) and [EvenP3RecipHighRepeat.lean](EvenP3RecipHighRepeat.lean) verify the cross-grid rank bridge, the bound for a single batch and the independent-batch estimate.

[EvenP3SingularCoverage.lean](EvenP3SingularCoverage.lean) verifies the assembled raw order-four singular coverage estimate. For n ≥ 1024 and any finite field of size q, the fraction of all dense source tensors with a singular cut and no eligible enlarged trial passing the exact gate q^D ≤ n is at most 2/n + 8/⌊n/2⌋² + 2(8(C + 1) + 34) log(q)/log(n), where D is the sum of the two enlarged repeated-primary budgets.

[EvenP3RecipCoverage.lean](EvenP3RecipCoverage.lean) verifies the reciprocal anchor marginals on the actual dense source conditioned on an invertible cut, for n ≥ 10¹⁹. It covers both orientations and each retained physical mode, with the uniform comparison law on all matrices in odd characteristic and on the prescribed trace slice in characteristic two. [EvenP3RecipLowCommon.lean](EvenP3RecipLowCommon.lean) and [EvenP3RecipHighClose.lean](EvenP3RecipHighClose.lean) prove that the simultaneous exceptional probabilities are at most 8/qⁿ and 16/q^⌊n/16000000⌋. The Fourier estimate sums by rank on the low-label region before adding the high-label contributions.

[EvenP3RecipPrimary.lean](EvenP3RecipPrimary.lean) verifies the original comparison gate q^D ≤ n, where D is the sum of the four reciprocal primary budgets. [EvenP3Coverage.lean](EvenP3Coverage.lean) combines the singular and invertible estimates on the same uniformly sampled raw source. The explicit bound holds for n ≥ 10¹⁹ and is O(1/log n) for each fixed field.

The proof reuses the checked exact-fiber and reciprocal-basis laws, Mathlib linear algebra, and the trace and Fourier machinery from Problems 1.7 and 1.8. Audited declarations use only the standard Lean axioms. Witness recovery and the final binary runtime theorem remain in progress.

- [EvenP4HigherRecip.lean](EvenP4HigherRecip.lean) proves the full higher-order reciprocal spectral gate on the actual anchors. The estimate holds conditional on an invertible cut and transfers to the raw source. The joint gate uses the same threshold q^D ≤ n^d as the higher-order singular branch.
- [EvenP4OrderFourLimit.lean](EvenP4OrderFourLimit.lean) verifies that the complete order-four rejection bound tends to zero, including the factor n² from the primary-budget transfer.

- [EvenP4Coverage.lean](EvenP4Coverage.lean) gives one side-length cutoff for each positive error tolerance, for every balanced even order at least four. These components have matching Lean source receipts and their audited theorems use only the standard Lean axioms. The standalone packet contains the portable field-cell and reference modules.
