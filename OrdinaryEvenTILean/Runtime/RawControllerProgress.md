# Raw controller progress for ordinary even-order tensor isomorphism

These checked modules extend the fixed-finite-field solver and reuse the existing MathProofs implementations of Problems 1.7 and 1.8, prior even-order TI proofs, and their original attributions.

- A checked command inverts an installed nonsingular cut, constructs its field units and writes the exact complete reciprocal trial.
- A selected raw input tensor is loaded into the canonical balanced-cut bank in its exact coordinate order.
- The nonsingular trial is produced directly from the original dense input words, including initialization, cut loading, inversion and serialization.
- The reciprocal execution clock is bounded by a polynomial in the original input bits.
- A total binary program runs in polynomial time and emits the exact reciprocal trial on every raw input with a nonsingular selected cut.
- The inverse and raw-controller address bounds hold within polynomial-length words.
- The physical stencil builder and residue comparison visit the real installed chart and coordinate banks.
- The checked CRT probe installs its recovered coordinates, builds the chart stencil and consumes the actual residue words.
- An exact binary clock implements the retained-form enumeration directly on the original raw pair for one selected cut.
- The selected raw cut’s basis construction is connected to the ranked candidate scan.
- The ranked scan output equals the reference list in coefficient-code order, including the empty over-cap case.
- The physical scan emits the dense field-word encoding of precisely the retained full-rank forms on an initialized cut.
- The live anchor matrix and primary basis feed a paid matrix-vector product and a checked coordinate solve for one restricted-anchor column.
- The physical factor archive cursor feeds the powered-annihilator kernel and publishes the corresponding primary-space basis.
- A physical rank scan emits only the complete native form blocks meeting the retained-rank condition.
- The checked singular filter reconstructs and ranks a candidate while protecting the live form and required banks.

The fixed-field theorem is still conditional on a complete raw trial-record producer covering both branches and on the full scalar and recovery controller executions. Statements on prepared or live internal banks state those assumptions explicitly.
