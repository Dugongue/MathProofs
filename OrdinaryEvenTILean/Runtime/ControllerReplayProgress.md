# Controller replay progress for ordinary even-order tensor isomorphism

These checked modules extend the fixed-finite-field solver and reuse the existing MathProofs implementations of Problems 1.7 and 1.8, prior even-order TI proofs, and their original attributions.

- The paid rank-filtered raw producer writes its exact retained forms into an isolated host capture while preserving the input.
- Both selected ranked cuts are captured in order from one original pair.
- The charged two-cut capture and selected-pair replay have a polynomial bound in the original pair bits.
- The two-cut capture writes the boundary between the two actual form lists to the caller register.
- The program computes both word offsets from the live pair indices, installs the two exact form vectors and preserves the other field slots.
- The native form projection call computes its exact coefficient bank with framed preservation.
- Two checked rectangular products and a shuffle produce the exact higher-order contraction in physical slots.
- The checked sweep order and word format agree with the reference higher-order singular record.
- The higher-order trial clock is bounded by a polynomial in the original pair bits.
- The complete higher-order singular trial record is produced in its reference mode order under the installed-form and cut contract.
- The existing all-factor sweep produces the chart rows, then real endpoint reads and label conversion install its logical prefix into a fixed-capacity bank. Native CRT-site correspondence is a separate open integration step.
- A paid CRT probe reads and consumes the live prefixes of its fixed-capacity chart and site banks.
- The paid scalar scan works on a live logical prefix in a preallocated bank.
- The live restriction-column computation preserves its caller inputs outside the specified scratch region.
- Archived primary extraction preserves the original anchor and the initializer reads the actual kernel rank to set the chart width.
- The actual live kernel rank and basis feed the complete restriction chart program.
- The checked column loop populates the whole restriction chart for one live primary basis.

The fixed-field theorem is still conditional on a complete raw trial-record producer covering both branches and on the full scalar and recovery controller executions. Statements on prepared or live internal banks state those assumptions explicitly.
