# Live controller proofs for ordinary even-order tensor isomorphism

These checked modules extend the fixed-finite-field solver and reuse the existing MathProofs implementations of Problems 1.7 and 1.8, prior even-order TI proofs, and their original attributions.

- The reusable binary clock turns a bounded IMP execution on its invocation domain into a total polynomial-time word callback, for arbitrary word-list outputs.
- The guard composition runs a word callback on accepted inputs and returns the literal failure word on rejected inputs.
- The exact solver and both counting phases assemble from a shared ordered trial producer and two promised-domain controller certificates. The recovery may return any sound transporter while retaining the reference successes on accepted sources.
- The final interface wires the checked side-frame serializer and the concrete CRT recovery semantics into the fixed-field solver assembly.
- The nested Cartesian serializer is compiled on its prepared pair-and-payload-list frame. It emits the exact raw and trial words and charges the full stream.
- From a raw order-four pair and a selected cut index, the paid procedure emits the literal finite-field form coefficients.
- The raw request parser retains the input pair and both trial windows, then reads the order, width, and trial tags from the original words.
- The paid inverse call initializes its layout from three caller registers and preserves the installed input matrix.
- One diagonal-block trace entry is computed directly from the live product bank.
- The checked live-bank inverse uses the finite general linear group cardinal and the existing uniform binary matrix-power machine.
- The scalarity scan evaluates each literal matrix and aggregates the result across all modes.
- The exact scalar branch verdict can use the comparison CRT witness and homogeneous kernel probes.
- One paid CRT probe call returns the exact activity bit and kernel-vector bank for an installed homogeneous constraint system.
- The homogeneous generator theorem uses at most the number of coordinates times the modulus many CRT recovery calls.
- The replacement CRT solver preserves successful pair attempts on accepted sources and returns a verified original transporter.
- The paid residual recovery reads prepared incidence indices and field labels, installs the corresponding logarithmic residue system, and runs the checked CRT coordinate recovery.

The full fixed-field theorem still requires the raw trial producer and execution of the complete scalar and comparison controllers. Statements on prepared or live internal banks state those assumptions explicitly.
