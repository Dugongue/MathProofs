# Four transfers from Alpöge's Riemann release to tensor isomorphism

These four written proofs use different parts of the published Lean development. The release declarations are identified in [Verification](Verification.json) by their archive member names and hashes. The analytic consequence retains Dirichlet GRH as a hypothesis. The other three proofs use algebraic or local arguments.

1. **Dimension-free prime signatures for abelian étale multiplication tensors.** Under Dirichlet GRH, primes up to $C\log^2(2D)$ suffice to compare arbitrary products of abelian field factors, where $D$ is the product of order discriminants. A mean-square argument budgets primitive conductors without an extra factor in the tensor dimension. Retaining the same rational component labels across the primes makes the test exact. The proof also gives short shared panels, directed field-inclusion probes, and ordered-element tests. It uses the Dirichlet endpoint **LFunction_eq_zero_re_of_isPrimitive**. [Proof](AbelianPrimeSignatures.md)
2. **Unitary tensor isomorphism over finite rings.** For an explicit quadratic étale extension $S/R$ of a finite local principal ideal ring, unitary isomorphism on free $S$-modules reduces to ordinary free tensor isomorphism over $R$. Hyperbolic doubling gives ordinary free TI over $S$ into the unitary problem. Both constructions extend to the five natural actions studied by Chen–Grochow–Qiao–Tang–Zhang. The descent handles nonfree supports and links direct and dual occurrences to one transporter. The upper reduction uses the release's **traceGram** construction and its perfect trace pairing. [Proof](TraceFormTransfer.md)
3. **A Hasse principle and matching algorithm for unitary tensor systems.** Coupled trace tensor records over quadratic étale algebras reduce to a perfect matching of rational field factors. Exact compatibility on each edge is tested using multiplicative row relations and quadratic local norms. An intrinsic generation test makes the Hasse principle valid for the unmarked system. The argument invokes **GlobalTheory.QNorm.hasseNormSt** for the quadratic norm equations. [Proof](HasseUnitaryTensors.md)
4. **Exact transport at a Smith-controlled congruence depth.** Over $\mathbb Z/p^a\mathbb Z$, a supplied residue class of mode maps admits deterministic transporter decision, construction, and exact counting, including optional prescribed determinants, under an explicit bound on the visible Smith valuations. The method works beyond the square-zero range and includes $p=2$. It transforms the nonlinear transport problem into a row-lattice test followed by exact $p$-adic reconstruction. It uses the released **PositivityTheory.Sph.below_iff_rows** and the congruence stratification in **PositivityTheory.Sph.latSt**. [Proof](SpectralTransfer.md)

The four written arguments passed independent mathematical review. The Lean companion checks the multiplicative norm gluing lemma for a three-mode record.
