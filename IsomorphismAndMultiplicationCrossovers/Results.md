# Four consequences for tensor and group complexity

These are informal mathematical proofs. Publication priority and independent verification remain open.

1. [Tensor isomorphism inside the flip representation class](Braids.md). Local equivalence of involutive algebraic Yang–Baxter operators is TI-complete over each fixed finite field even on a family where all strand representations are equivalent to ordinary permutations.
2. [Product subspaces and equations](Equations.md). Exact outer-product packing over every field yields full complex Kronecker multiplicities in a range of partition lengths and larger determinants from orbit equations surviving on small supports.
3. [Catalysts in matrix multiplication restrictions](Catalysts.md). Unital algebra-action catalysts cancel for a single input-saturated conversion between products with equal outer dimensions. With one simple module type they also cancel for batches with matched input sizes and a common target aspect ratio.
4. [Sampling mixed generating witnesses](Groups.md). In a solvable extension of a direct product of nonabelian simple groups, a feasible mixed generation problem with a free slot has witness density at least the reciprocal of the group order. This gives exact uniform sampling on positive instances and a relative approximation scheme for the witness count.
