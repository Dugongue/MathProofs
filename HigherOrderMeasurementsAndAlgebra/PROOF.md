# Four published questions in measurement theory and algebra

This collection gives four informal answers in their stated scopes. The quantum measurement proof gives two separate answers to questions raised in the same paper. Numerical or finite controls are used only as checks on examples.

1. **Algebraic versus spectral unbiasedness.** Designolle and Farkas left the relation between their two definitions of a \(k\)-fold unbiased measurement open beyond their established cases. They coincide for every \(k,n\ge2\) and every finite outcome rank. The proof recovers the operator identities from averaged even moments and shows that spectral unbiasedness is hereditary. [Original question, Section VI](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20728v1#S6) · [Proof, Sections 1–3](HigherOrderUnbiasedMeasurements.md)
2. **The maximal-eigenspace parent.** The same paper asks when its equation (17) holds for spectral \(k\)-fold unbiased measurements. It holds for every such tuple. This determines the exact generalized, uniform-noise and depolarizing visibilities and gives an explicit joint measurement achieving them. The universal optimization over arbitrary tuples in Conjecture 10 is a separate problem. [Original question, Section IV.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20728v1#S4.SS2) · [Proof, Sections 4–5](HigherOrderUnbiasedMeasurements.md)
3. **Multilinear sparsity lifting.** Hu and van Melkebeek asked for a more efficient sparsity-to-ROABP-width gadget using only multilinear codeword monomials. A deterministic intersecting-code construction gives \(O(n)\) output variables and degree blowup \(O(n)\), independent of the input degree. The exact width after substitution equals the original sparsity in every order over every field. [Original question, Section 5](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/125/download/) · [Proof](MultilinearSparsityLifting.md)
4. **Counting perfect evolution algebras.** Kaygorodov, Martín González and Viruel conjectured that, for each dimension, the count of perfect evolution algebras over a finite field is polynomial on residue classes. The proof treats all dimensions and all prime powers, including small characteristic, by reducing the twisted monomial fixed-point count to ordinary similarity and centralizer sizes. [Original Conjecture 8](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22248v1#S2.SS3) · [Proof](PerfectEvolutionAlgebrasPORC.md)

The [idempotent monodromy note](EvolutionAlgebraIdempotentMonodromy.md) treats the same evolution-algebra survey's Conjecture 2. It credits the generic characteristic-zero assertion as a consequence of Esterov's theorem and gives a direct proof over every odd characteristic. The proposed limiting distribution holds over odd prime powers. In characteristic two the exact distribution instead comes from fixed vectors of \(\mathrm{GL}_n(2)\), so for \(n\ge3\) the printed unrestricted finite-field consequence is false.

The proofs are informal. The targeted literature checks located and credited the antecedents used in each argument. Publication priority is unverified.
