# Special-linear tensor isomorphism on pencils, three-slice tensors and nilpotent centroids

**Every finite-field matrix pencil is covered.** For every explicitly represented finite field, special-linear equivalence of arbitrary $2\times m\times n$ tensors is decidable deterministically in time polynomial in $m,n,\log q$. The algorithm includes singular and rectangular pencils and fields where the regular part has no rational eigenvalues. It uses no finite-field factorization or discrete logarithms. The proof combines strict Kronecker equivalence with determinant corrections for the projective slice transformations. [Pencil theorem and literature comparison](Pencils.md)

The general reverse reduction still lacks a proof. The forward reduction pads each leg with one unused coordinate to absorb its determinant. Going back requires a polynomial-size ordinary TI pair with the same special-linear answer over the original field. [Chen, Grochow, Qiao, Tang and Zhang, Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135#S1.SS5)

| Result | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Generic three-slice orbit splitting](Groups.md) | A polynomial-time scalar separates special-linear orbits within each ordinary orbit on an explicit nonempty Zariski-open set of $3\times n\times n$ tensors over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, for every $n\ge2$. When $3\mid n$, the construction combines invariants of weights three and four. The note also proves a finite-field version with an arithmetic condition. |
| [Quadratic relations in the ternary square-zero construction](QuadraticRelations.md) | On the project's full square-zero comparison over $\mathbb F_3$, the sign moves to the space of quadratic relations. The variable change is unrestricted. The resulting reduction to ordinary TI is polynomial when the relation dimension is logarithmic in input size. |
| [Orientation from centroid layers](Centroid.md) | For a tensor with centroid $\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^h)$, only the multiplicities of chains of lengths indivisible by $q-1$ enter the exponential cost. This permits all three tensor modes to grow. |
| [Exact profiles for matrix-slice algebras](Tensor.md) | A determinant-coset computation gives a direct polynomial-time test for invertible translates of split products of truncated polynomial algebras, in faithful representations, over fixed finite fields and over the rationals. |

The reduction theorems have written mathematical proofs. An independent Lean kernel and axiom audit verified the product of determinants on the layers of a given chain length over a commutative ring, using the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. The scripts check selected arithmetic claims on finite examples. A complete software implementation of the pencil decision algorithm and publication priority remain unverified.
