# Four research programs from MathProofs

| Program | Object | First theorem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bounded-degree arithmetic traces](ArithmeticTraces.md) | The point functor restricted to extensions of degree at most a fixed bound | Every affine system over a number field has a smooth, geometrically integral hypersurface replacement preserving all of those point sets naturally, with local points everywhere. The integral version preserves bounded-degree integral points and yields a computably enumerable complete decision problem under everywhere local solubility. |
| [Infinitesimal tensor stabilizers](InfinitesimalTransport.md) | Frobenius height, first distinguishing jet, and least splitting degree of tensor transporters | Rank-one networks have sharp character-torsion and splitting bounds $2^r$ for $r$ independent character relations. Linear-rank ordinary tensors attain exponential splitting degree and an exponential delay in distinguishing pointed stabilizer functors. A thin-algebra realization recovers integral homology torsion. |
| [Curvature of phase averages](PhaseAverages.md) | Signed Hessians and Jensen deficits of randomized norm energies | For $1<p<\infty$, $p\ne2$, phase and Gaussian Hessians are comparable by constants independent of the number of summands. The circle upper constant must grow on the order of $1/(p-1)$ as $p\downarrow1$. Spherical averages in every ambient dimension at least three have uniform comparisons down to the endpoint. An intrinsic overlap graph classifies equality in complex $L^p$-embeddable spaces. |
| [Degree-constrained sum-of-squares tensor ranks](DegreeConstrainedSOS.md) | The closure of sums of squares with separate degree and product-rank limits | On homogeneous quadratics, for $r=2,3$ the border-rank-$r$ cone with approximation degree at most $2d$ is exactly the positive semidefinite factor-width-$rd$ cone. A general filtration estimate bounds the gap in factor width by at most $r-1$ at rank $r\ge2$. |

The proofs are written. Lean 4.33.0 independently checked the [diagonal-stable subspace lemma](DiagonalStable.lean) used in the fourth program's general support bound. The audit used only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The manuscripts give antecedents and precise questions for each program. Publication priority is provisional.
