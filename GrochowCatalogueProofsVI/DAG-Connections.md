# Proof dependencies and verification

[Finite-ring reduction](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/a32630798eebd129ef621986ca4e0b8ce5edfcf3/GrochowCatalogueProofsV/PresentedModuleTensorReduction.md) |

[Lysikov–Walter, Section V](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04639v2#S5) · [Mixed quartic reduction](PureMixedQuarticTOCI.md) · [Symmetric reduction](SymmetricOrthogonalTOCI.md) |
