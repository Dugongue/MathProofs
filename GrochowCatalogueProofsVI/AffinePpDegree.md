# Additive proof degree under affine primitive-positive interpretations

## GP-0718

Consider a fixed primitive-positive interpretation of one finite rational domain $E$ in another finite rational domain $D$, of fixed dimension and fixed gadget arities. Suppose its decoding map on valid tuples is the restriction of one affine linear form in the tuple coordinates. Every degree-$\delta$ rational Polynomial Calculus derivation for the original instance transfers to a derivation of the pulled-back target in the interpreted instance of degree at most $\max(\delta,C)$, where $C$ depends only on the interpretation and the fixed constraint templates. All local certificates can be prepared once. The transfer is polynomial in the size of the derivation for fixed $\delta$, and it preserves polynomial bit complexity. This covers same-domain pp definitions and includes noninjective affine decoding maps.

### Local axiom certificates

Write the fixed interpretation as a set $F\subseteq D^\ell$ and a map $\pi:F\to E$ with $\pi(y)=c_0+\sum_{j=1}^{\ell}c_j y_j$ on $F$. Replace every original variable by a disjoint $\ell$-tuple. Enforce membership in $F$ on each tuple, and enforce the preimage of every original constraint by its fixed pp gadget, with fresh existential variables. Projection through $\pi$ maps exactly onto the original solutions. The pulled-back target vanishes on the interpreted solutions if and only if the original target vanishes on the original solutions.

For any original polynomial axiom, its affine pullback vanishes on the finite grid of satisfying assignments of the corresponding local gadget with its domain tests. A local certificate can be constructed explicitly on a grid $D^k$ as follows. Reduce modulo the $k$ domain polynomials $\prod_{a\in D}(z_i-a)$, using a separate ordinary univariate division in each variable. The quotient ring is the ring of rational functions on the finite set $D^k$ under pointwise operations. Its indicator basis consists of products of univariate Lagrange polynomials. For every grid point where the pulled-back axiom is nonzero, select a violated gadget polynomial and multiply it by that point indicator times the ratio of the two values there. Summing these products yields the pulled-back axiom modulo the domain polynomials. The same reduction gives the residual domain multipliers. The gadget arity, grid, and degrees are fixed, so these multipliers and a corresponding PC proof have fixed degree and bit size. The same argument applies to original domain axioms using just the membership gadget. Repeated coordinates can be identified after computing the local certificate.

### Degree through the global derivation

Transport the original derivation line by line by the affine substitution $x_i\mapsto\pi(y_i)$. An original linear-combination rule remains the same linear combination. A multiplication by $x_i$ is performed by computing the constant multiple of the previous line and its $\ell$ multiples by the tuple coordinates and adding them with coefficients $c_j$. In any nonzero multiplication step of the original derivation the previous line has degree at most $\delta-1$, since multiplication by a variable raises degree by one in the polynomial ring. Affine substitution cannot increase degree. Thus each intermediate term in that simulation has degree at most $\delta$, and only the fixed local axiom proofs may have degree as high as $C$. Pulling back a polynomial line of fixed degree introduces polynomially many terms, and rational substitution with fixed coefficients adds polynomially many bits to the original coefficient bounds. The complete simulation uses only the interpreted constraints and their domain polynomials.

This strengthens the multiplicative substitution-degree bound for interpretations with affine decoding. For a decoder of interpolation degree greater than one, the same construction yields the usual multiplicative degree bound. [Primitive-positive transfers](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28243#S3)
