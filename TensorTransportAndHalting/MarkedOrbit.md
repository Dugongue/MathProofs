# Halting in one partially observed image under matrix multiplication symmetries

**Unconditional integer theorem.** Fix a sufficiently large integer N, the multiplication tensor of the full matrix ring M_N over the integers, and one rank-three square-zero integer matrix J consisting of three Jordan blocks of size two and zero blocks. There is a fixed pattern of prescribed entries in the image of J, with fixed integer values except for one input n, such that determining whether any integral algebra automorphism realizes those observations is computably many-one equivalent to halting. Every positive instance has an automorphism given by conjugation by a unimodular integer matrix. The same reduction works when all integral tensor autotopisms are allowed and the observations concern only their output map on J.

**Rational version and a tractable case.** Assuming an existential Diophantine definition of the integers in the rationals, rank two suffices over the rationals, with one varying prescribed entry in fixed format. At rank one, for the same off-diagonal rectangle observation model used below, existence of a conjugate with the prescribed entries is decidable in polynomial bit time over the rationals. The objects to compare are fixed throughout each family. The input specifies entries the image of a distinguished element must attain. The results are informal.

### A division-free arithmetic compiler at rank three

Use an identity anchor I₃ in a partial integer matrix. The decision asks for an integer completion of rank at most three, with rank computed over the rationals. A completed matrix with that anchor has the form

    [ I   V ]
    [ U  UV ]

by the Schur complement. Thus every row of U and every column of V is an integral triple, their coordinates can be prescribed individually against the anchor, and one prescribed interaction is their dot product. Conversely every consistent assignment to the triples completes all the other entries. The same matrix identity works over any field. Everything below can also be interpreted over a commutative ring by explicitly requiring this anchored factorization.

Write the triple coordinates in order (d,a,b), with e_j their standard vectors. For each arithmetic variable x introduce a master row U_x=(x,1,x). Pin its middle coordinate and require zero pairing with a fixed column e_d−e_b. For each j in {d,b} introduce a column C(x,j) that has value 1 in position j, zero off positions j,a, and unknown value at a. Require zero pairing with U_x. It follows that C(x,j)=e_j−x e_a. Thus a fresh row with coordinate a equal to one can be forced to take value x at coordinate j by a single zero pairing.

Introduce the row T_x=(0,1,x) using C(x,b). Introduce a master column with last entry 1, whose first two entries are tied by a zero pairing with the fixed row e_d−e_a. Require T_x to pair to zero with this column. The resulting column V_x equals (−x,−x,1). For each j in {d,a} introduce a row R(x,j) with entry 1 in position j, zero off positions j,b, and unknown entry at b. Its zero pairing with V_x forces R(x,j)=e_j+x e_b. Now a fresh column with coordinate b equal to one can be forced to take value −x at coordinate j by a single zero pairing.

Addition x+y=z requires zero pairing of a fresh row (x,1,y) and a fresh column (1,−z,1). Multiplication xy=z requires zero pairing of (x,1,z) and (−y,0,1). The transfer pairs just constructed enforce all variable coordinates. Pin constants in the variable's master row. Every arithmetic gate is realized with fresh interaction rows and columns, so constraints impose no unrequested dot products. Unspecified entries remain independent blanks. Only prescribed constants 0, 1 and −1 are needed apart from the separate input pin n. Given a solution, every added value and all matrix entries are determined by addition, negation and multiplication. Conversely the anchor and pairings extract an actual solution, without divisions or assumptions that nonzero scalars are units.

Take a fixed arithmetic circuit presenting the halting set by the Davis–Putnam–Robinson–Matiyasevich theorem over the integers, using sums of four squares for nonnegative existential variables when necessary. Treat its input as a variable until the end, then pin just its master row's d-coordinate to n. Dimensions and the observation mask stay fixed. The compiler proves that the partial rectangular matrix X(n) has an integral rank-three completion if and only if n is a halting input. Given a finite list of polynomial equations instead, use a circuit for the equations and build the same anchored table in polynomial size. [Jones, Universal Diophantine Equation](https://doi.org/10.2307/2273588)

### Turn the completion into a marked conjugacy question

Let X have dimensions u by v, N=u+v, and use the ordered decomposition of the free module into sizes u and v. Impose that the image of J is zero outside the upper off-diagonal u by v rectangle, and within that rectangle prescribe exactly the entries specified in X(n). Each candidate image Y is square-zero. Over a field, any two square-zero matrices with the same rank are conjugate, by choosing a basis of the image, a preimage for each basis vector, and a complement in the kernel.

The integral detail matters. For an accepting completion write X=[I;U][I,V]. The image of X is the direct summand generated by the columns of [I;U]. The first three input vectors in the v-block are preimages of these columns and split off a complementary free summand to the kernel. More explicitly, integral basis changes on the u-block and v-block separately remove U and V and put X in the form with an identity corner and all other entries zero. They combine into one block-diagonal conjugation of Y. Hence every accepting Y is conjugate over the integers to the same fixed J, which may itself be chosen as that canonical block matrix. The reverse implication follows from invariance of rank and the forced anchor. Rational basis changes or integral conjugators can be enumerated and tested, proving computable enumerability in the respective cases.

An integral algebra automorphism can realize every such conjugation. Conversely every algebra automorphism preserves matrix rank, even without invoking a classification of automorphisms. This holds also for arbitrary tensor autotopisms. Indeed such an isotopy consists of three invertible linear maps F,G,H with F(X)G(Y)=H(XY). Extend scalars to the rationals in the integral case. Writing L_X for left multiplication gives L_{F(X)} G=H L_X, and rank L_X=N rank X. Also H(X)=F(X)G(I), and G(I) is invertible because right multiplication by it is H composed with the inverse of F. Thus H preserves rank. Conjugation gives F=G=H and is a legitimate autotopism. One partially observed vector H(J) therefore suffices in either variant. One can choose a fixed source basis containing J since J is primitive, so the observations concern one column of H in fixed input and output bases.

### The rational rank-two threshold

Over the rationals the rank-two completion compiler cited below realizes arithmetic using two coordinates and inverses of arbitrary nonzero scalars. To spell out the single moving pin, under the rational Diophantine premise take a fixed rational equation F(t,w)=0 representing halting. Multiply it by (z²−13)(z²−17)(z²−221) for a fresh variable z. None of the extra factors has a rational root. At every real or p-adic completion one factor has a root. For odd primes outside 13 and 17, at least one of the three unit residues 13,17,221 is a square and lifts by Hensel. At 13 use 17 congruent to 4, at 17 use 13 congruent to 8², and at 2 use 17 congruent to 1 modulo 8. Apply the compiler once to this fixed polynomial equation with t as an independent variable. Its scalar representation is t=t⁺−t⁻ with two nonzero holder rows (t⁺,1),(t⁻,1) against an identity anchor I₂. Set their first coordinates to n+1 and 1, respectively. Only one prescribed entry varies. A rank-two completion now represents halting over the rationals, with local completions on every input. The rectangle construction and conjugacy argument transfer both statements to the marked image over the respective fields. [Rank-two compiler, Sections 1–2](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/RankTwoAndRingUniversality/RankTwoCompletion.md) · [Arithmetic premise and status](https://github.com/elliotglazer/diophantine-z-q)

At rank one with arbitrary rational observations in that rectangle, a completion has entries u_i v_j. Every prescribed nonzero value forces its row and column factors nonzero. Build the bipartite graph of these edges. In each connected component set one factor to one, propagate along edges by rational division and check consistency around cycles. Reject if a prescribed zero has both ends forced nonzero. If at least one nonzero entry is present and all checks pass, set all unforced factors to zero. If no nonzero entry is prescribed, a nonzero rank-one completion exists precisely when a cell is available to hold one nonzero entry. Products and ratios along simple paths have polynomial bit length. The field conjugacy argument now decides the marked rank-one case. This low-rank algorithm concerns the rectangular observation pattern, without additional linear combinations of coordinates.

### Connection to the perfect pairing

There is also a two-mode presentation over the rationals. The isometries of a perfect bilinear pairing, with the two factors distinguished, are pairs (S,S⁻ᵗ). With independent coordinate pins in both transporters, prescribe

    S = [ I  U  0 ]
        [ 0  I  V ]
        [ 0  0  I ].

The upper-right rectangle of its inverse is UV. Choosing the middle identity of size two and prescribing entries of this product realizes rank-two partial matrix completion. Every S produced has determinant one and (S−I)³=0. The one-sided version, with independent prescribed coordinates only in S, has a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm. For each column with zero-filled base c and free row positions J, list c and c+e_j for j in J. These attainable vectors span all attainable completions of that column. By determinant multilinearity a nonsingular completion exists exactly when the column lists have a linearly independent transversal. Linear and partition matroid intersection supplies one in polynomial time. [Edmonds, Matroid intersection](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-5060(08)70817-3)

These statements apply to partial observations of actual transporters. With the observations removed, the identity already answers every instance in these families. Publication priority for the precise observation and rank restrictions has not been established.
