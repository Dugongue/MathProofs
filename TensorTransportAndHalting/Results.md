# Four halting problems for tensor transport and algebra symmetries

All four integral theorems here are unconditional informal deductions. A set is halting-complete when it and the halting set reduce computably to one another. Each construction fixes the tensor or algebra providing the operations. What varies is a target table, a matrix, a partial observation of one marked image, or the index of a fiber.

| Fixed structure | Input question | What is already supplied or decidable |
|---|---|---|
| The multiplication table of the 2 by 2 integer matrices and a fixed finite set of determinant-one changes of its four-dimensional basis | Can a word in those changes reach the given table? | An explicit unrestricted integral isomorphism is supplied. Its image modulo every modulus belongs to the allowed group image. [Proof](Profinite.md) |
| One finite-rank commutative algebra A over the integers | Can the given 2 by 2 determinant-one matrix be triangularized by an A-basis change? | All underlying integer matrices are conjugate to the same unipotent matrix. An A-basis exists over every p-adic completion. A cube-zero augmentation ideal makes triangularization exactly a square-image test. [Proof](Roots.md) |
| The multiplication tensor of a full matrix ring and one rank-three square-zero matrix J | Can an algebra automorphism realize the partially specified image of J? | The observation mask is fixed and one observed integer changes. For a fully specified matrix the orbit of J admits an elementary Smith-form test. [Proof](MarkedOrbit.md) |
| One torsion-free class-two nilpotent group, its commutator tensor and a split quotient onto ℤ² | Does the fiber over (1,n) contain an element with infinite cyclic commutator image? | Membership in the conjugacy class of any given element is decidable. Each fiber has an explicit element with commutator image of rank two. [Proof](Conjugacy.md) |

For the specified square-zero matrix J in the third theorem, a fully specified integral Y is conjugate to J precisely when Y²=0, its rank is three and its three nonzero Smith invariants equal one. Indeed its image is then primitive in the ambient lattice and lies in the kernel. Split the kernel over the image, and choose preimages lifting a basis of the image to split off the quotient by the kernel. In this integral basis Y is J. This explains exactly what data one loses by observing only some entries of the image.

The companion-matrix and marked-image proofs also have rational versions conditional on an existential Diophantine definition of the integers over the rationals. The latter uses rank two over the rationals. The unrestricted finite-field TI and SL-TI decision problems remain decidable by enumeration. None of the reductions in this packet turns an arbitrary halting question into an unrestricted pair of finite-field TI instances.
