# Exact counting of epimorphic lifts through nonsemisimple abelian kernels

Let \(G,H\) be finite groups given by their Cayley tables, \(A\triangleleft H\) a specified abelian subgroup, and \(\psi:G\twoheadrightarrow P=H/A\) a specified quotient map. Prescribe arbitrarily many point values for a prospective lift. For each nonzero primary component \(A_p\) of exponent \(p^a\), write \(R_p=(\mathbb Z/p^a\mathbb Z)[P]\) and \(U_p=A_p/J(R_p)A_p\). Conjugation makes \(A_p\) a module for \(R_p\). Assume
\[
\operatorname{End}_{R_p}(A_p)\longrightarrow\operatorname{End}_{R_p}(U_p)
\]
is surjective for each \(p\). One can recognize this condition in polynomial Cayley-table time and, under it, count all epimorphic lifts with the prescribed values and produce one when the count is positive. This combines the earlier affine lift equations and independent-row count for semisimple kernels with passage to the semisimple head.

Choose a section of \(H\to P\). The lifts are an affine system of additive congruences over \(A\), whose equations record section multiplication and the action of \(P\). Add the prescribed point values to this system. If consistent its solutions have the form \(a^0+W\), where \(W\) is the group of crossed homomorphisms vanishing at the prescribed points. Put \(K=\ker\psi\). The restriction of any lift to \(K\) is a \(P\)-equivariant homomorphism from \(K_{\mathrm{ab}}\) to \(A\). The action on \(K_{\mathrm{ab}}\) is independent of the choice of preimage of an element of \(P\). The image of the lift is all of \(H\) exactly when its restriction covers \(A\), since it already covers \(P\).

Project each component of the restriction to \(U_p\). This gives a fixed collection of maps plus the image of the linear restriction map \(\rho\) on \(W\), whose \(p\)-component has source \(K_{\mathrm{ab}}/pK_{\mathrm{ab}}\). An image submodule of \(A_p\) covers \(A_p\) if and only if it covers \(U_p\), by Nakayama's lemma. Primary components of any subgroup of \(A\) split, so these tests suffice simultaneously.

The homogeneous restriction image is stable under the independent action of each \(\operatorname{End}_{R_p}(U_p)\). Indeed all the homogeneous equations hold after applying an endomorphism of \(A_p\), including the required zero point values, and every head endomorphism lifts by hypothesis. Primary projections also preserve the equations. Write \(U_p=\bigoplus_i T_i^{b_i}\) with nonisomorphic simples \(T_i\), and \(D_i=\operatorname{End}_{R_p}(T_i)\). Each \(D_i\) is a finite field. Projecting by the individual coordinate idempotents splits the allowed row variations independently. Matrix units make the allowed row subspace \(V_i\) the same in every copy of a given simple, and the scalar endomorphisms make it \(D_i\)-linear. The admissible rows on that block therefore vary independently in cosets of \(V_i\).

Surjectivity on the block is equivalent to independence of its \(b_i\) rows as maps to \(T_i\) over \(D_i\). This follows since any proper submodule in the semisimple target is killed by some nonzero homomorphism to a simple. Let \(s_i\) be the rank of the row offsets modulo \(V_i\). Invertible row operations make the first \(s_i\) rows independent modulo \(V_i\) and the rest zero modulo \(V_i\). The first rows are then arbitrary in their cosets. The remaining rows must be independent in \(V_i\), yielding
\[
|V_i|^{s_i}\prod_{j=0}^{b_i-s_i-1} (|V_i|-|D_i|^j).
\]
Use zero when the number of required rows exceeds the dimension. Multiply this formula over blocks and primes and by \(|\ker\rho|\). Independent rows also give a witness by choosing their preimage in the affine lift system.

For effectiveness, obtain composition factors of \(A_p\) by finding a smallest nonzero cyclic submodule in each quotient, scanning all elements. A smallest such module is simple. Each simple is killed by \(p\). The intersection of the kernels of bases of the equivariant maps from \(A_p\) to the resulting simple representatives equals \(J(R_p)A_p\). The reason is that every simple quotient occurs among the composition factors, and the head is semisimple and separated by its maps to simples. Smith form over the abelian group coordinates supplies the affine system, its fiber counts and restrictions, and the induced map on endomorphism groups. Its image cardinality can be compared to the cardinality of the head endomorphism group without enumerating all endomorphisms. Decomposing the head and performing the row calculations require only polynomial bit time, using direct-summand projections found by linear equations. These computations require no table of elements of the group ring.

The hypotheses include finite projective \(R_p\)-modules, since any head endomorphism composed with the quotient projection lifts across that projection by projectivity. They also permit nonsplit group extensions. A direct application to group epimorphism algorithms is that a final central or abelian lifting step with this property needs anchors only for generators of the quotient, while the head rank test takes care of surjectivity on the kernel. This proof is informal.
