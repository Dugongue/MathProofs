# Eleven results on symmetric circuits, sketching, and finite group actions

1. **Symmetric permanent completeness fails in positive characteristic.** For every field of characteristic \(p>0\) and \(n>p\), \(1+\prod_{i,j}x_{ij}\) has a depth-two matrix-symmetric circuit and no symmetric affine projection to a permanent of any dimension. [Proof](complexity.md#1-the-permanent-loses-symmetric-projection-completeness-in-every-positive-characteristic)
2. **Symmetric Boolean-cube sums miss matrix invariants.** In every positive characteristic there are matrix-invariant families of degree at most \(n^2\) that require a witness gate orbit of size \(n^{\Omega(\log n)}\) for infinitely many \(n\), even with unrestricted numbers and permutation actions of auxiliary bits. This separates the finite-characteristic version of \(\mathsf{symVNP}\) from all matrix invariants. [Proof](complexity.md#2-symmetric-boolean-cube-sums-omit-matrix-invariants-in-every-positive-characteristic)
3. **Randomized SVD with small injection failure.** An oblivious subspace injection at the target rank with injectivity \(1-\varepsilon\) and failure \(\delta=O(\varepsilon)\) gives \(1+O(\varepsilon)\) relative Frobenius accuracy at every fixed confidence, including for the best rank-constrained fit in the sampled range. This answers the small-failure follow-up to Simons Workshop Problem 5.2. [Proof](subspace-injection.md#application-to-randomized-low-rank-approximation)
4. **Multiple-response least squares with small injection failure.** The same guarantee holds for arbitrarily many responses, with constants independent of their number. The order \(\delta=O(\varepsilon)\) is necessary in the unrestricted worst case. This gives a sharp failure scale for the follow-up to Workshop Problem 5.1. [Upper bound](subspace-injection.md#application-to-multiple-response-least-squares) · [Matching scale](subspace-injection.md#the-least-squares-failure-scale-is-optimal)
5. **Sharp deficit for one extra dimension.** The worst-case expected drop below \(1-\varepsilon\) in the least eigenvalue of the compression to an \((r+1)\)-space is \(\Theta(\delta+(\delta^2\varepsilon)^{1/3})\) over \(r\) and ambient dimensions. A rank-one mixture in the plane attains the scale. [Proof](subspace-injection.md#expected-deficit-after-adjoining-one-direction)
6. **Dihedral group-sum tensors in characteristic two.** Order three leaves positive-dimensional fibers for every natural dihedral action. The exact generic cutoff is four for odd \(n\ge5\), and five for \(n=3\). These are the unnormalized group-sum tensors. [Proof](invariants.md#sharp-dihedral-moment-cutoffs-in-characteristic-two)
7. **One real orbit can fail to determine the concrete action.** Left and right quaternion actions give identical full-span observations and different permutation subgroups. The obstruction survives arbitrary per-group dense open choices of seeds. Two generic orbits are sharp in the worst case for concrete and permutation-action recovery. [Proof](invariants.md#sharp-worst-case-obstruction-to-one-orbit-real-group-and-action-recovery)
8. **One Gram graph recovers involution-generated groups.** Single-neighbor colors reconstruct the regular action of every finite involution-generated group from a generic real orbit, even against arbitrary free competing orbits. [Proof](invariants.md#one-real-gram-graph-recovers-involution-generated-groups)
9. **One Gram graph recovers actions with small irreducibles.** Covariance eigenspaces reconstruct the group and action on the orbit span when every real irreducible constituent has dimension at most two, including every finite abelian group. The answer allows competing generic representations of arbitrary dimension. [Proof](invariants.md#one-orbit-recovery-for-actions-with-irreducible-constituents-of-dimension-at-most-two)
10. **Counting epimorphisms to abelian Hall extensions.** For an arbitrary Cayley-table source and an abelian normal Hall kernel in the target, deterministic polynomial time gives the exact count and a witness if the quotient has bounded order or is nilpotent of fixed class and fixed generator rank. The allowed kernel exponents are unrestricted. [Proof](groups.md#1-counting-epimorphisms-onto-a-group-with-an-abelian-normal-hall-subgroup)
11. **Parallel coset intersection.** Deterministic logspace-uniform NC produces a representative and generators for the full intersection when one permutation group has bounded point-orbit size, even when the intersection has exponential index. [Proof](groups.md#2-uniform-nc-coset-intersection-with-a-bounded-orbit-permutation-group)
