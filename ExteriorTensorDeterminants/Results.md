# Determinant tests for raw exterior multiplication and large intrinsic modules

We study the determinant constraints in Open Question 1.9 over fixed finite fields. By a raw exterior multiplication tensor we mean three independently rebased copies of the complementary exterior spaces, with coefficients of the trilinear form (x,y,z) ↦ vol(x∧y∧z) and no exterior frames supplied. The exterior format is specified. An ordinary TI query to the explicit standard tensor can verify membership in the promised class. The algorithms compute the exact set of simultaneous transporter determinant tuples in the raw coordinate frames. [Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1)

| Input class | Result |
|---|---|
| Balanced exterior multiplication over the field of three elements, including the earlier projective-orbit counterexample | A deterministic polynomial-time direct algorithm that also recognizes the standard exterior orbit from raw inputs of the indicated format. Compute a Cartan subalgebra from the raw derivation equations, reconstruct the exterior supports, and use finite-field descent and integer Smith form for the frame scalars. A separate elementary randomized version is included. [Proof](DirectSolver.md) |
| Arbitrary positive complementary exterior degrees over a fixed odd field | A Las Vegas polynomial-time direct algorithm. A safe field supplies cyclic Cartans and a determinant-one simultaneous change of frame aligns them. The root stars leave at most twice the natural dimension many Frobenius-compatible matchings, each checked by scalar equations. [Chart construction](ComplementaryDegrees.md) · [Direct matching](MarkedFrames.md) |
| Large intrinsic modules with a computable operator algebra | A deterministic polynomial-time Turing reduction to ordinary TI when the determinant-relevant natural and multiplicity dimensions are logarithmic. A natural determinant can drop out because its powers cancel across layers. Large even-dimensional module pieces can require no determinant computation whatsoever. This includes an unbounded example with scalar pair adjoints and no small spanning point lists. [Proof](LargeModules.md) |
| Large blocks with movable frames | An invariant orbit of marks suffices when it is transitive under determinant-one stabilizers and one mark admits a polynomial-size projective list or a suitable subspace list. The lists only have to be invariant after fixing the mark. [Proof](MarkedFrames.md) |

An exterior frame is allowed to move under a transporter. It need not occur in any invariant list of polynomial length. The signed comparison survives the frame choice because both ends can be aligned using an actual stabilizer whose three determinants equal one. The root method then replaces testing arbitrary permutations of the exterior basis by at most 2m matchings, where m is the natural dimension. The tests also enforce Frobenius descent, so even when coordinates are computed in a splitting field of even degree their signs are read over the intended field.

In the operator-algebra route, a single map on a natural matrix-algebra module appears across all modes and radical layers. Combining its powers before allocating a determinant computation can remove entire high-dimensional factors from the problem. The method solves only the remaining active small determinants and retains the actual transporter equations throughout.

The algorithms are informal deductions using the earlier mixed-tensor packing, determinant-line construction, extension transfer, and projective-list analysis. They settle the promised families above. Unrestricted TI versus SL-TI still calls for a method handling large active factors and tensors for which no suitable determinant-neutral frame action is available. Publication priority for the specific tensor algorithms is unverified.
