# Simultaneous lifting through a nonabelian semisimple kernel

Let $E$ be a Cayley-table group and let its normal subgroup $A$ be an internal direct product of nonabelian minimal normal subgroups $A_i$ of $E$. Suppose $q=(q_1,\ldots,q_d)$ generates $E/A$, with $d\ge d(E)$ and $d=O(\log N)$ where $N\ge |E|$. Access the tuple by coordinate. The algorithm below computes a generating lift of the same length. It uses $O(\log N)$ workspace between tuple queries and suspends only $O(\log(|A|+1)+(\log\log(N+4))^C)$ bits at each tuple query for a fixed constant $C$. The proof is informal.

Find the minimal normal factors of $A$ through the inclusion-minimal nontrivial principal normal closures in $E$ that lie in $A$. Every normal subgroup of a product of nonabelian simple groups is a product of simple factors, so these minimal normals are exactly the factors $A_i$. Their own simple components are likewise obtained as minimal normal closures within each factor. Use component ordinals and local element ranks as compact labels. Choose a chief series through $A$ by the ordinary normal-closure scan below $A$ and the same scan in $E/A$ above $A$. These determinations use fixed-depth table scans and undirected reachability and require no access to $q$.

## Crown coordinates

For each nonabelian chief factor $B$, write $L_B$ for the image of conjugation by $E$ in $\operatorname{Aut}(B)$. The group $L_B$ contains all inner automorphisms, and their group is identified with the centerless $B$. This is its unique minimal normal subgroup, since a distinct minimal normal would centralize $\operatorname{Inn}(B)$, whose centralizer in $\operatorname{Aut}(B)$ is trivial. Access the table of $L_B$ through scans of $E$, testing equality of factor actions. Test equality modulo inner by an additional scan of $B$.

Two nonabelian chief factors $B,B'$ are $E$-equivalent exactly when there is an isomorphism $f:B\to B'$ intertwining the actions modulo inner automorphism. One direction restricts the isomorphism of the semidirect products over $E$ to their kernels. Conversely the action defects are inner conjugations by unique elements of $B'$ since $B'$ is centerless. Multiplying defects gives their cocycle identity, and the isomorphism and the defects define the required extension isomorphism. Such an $f$ identifies $L_B,L_{B'}$ and their outer action maps, because both images contain every inner automorphism.

These equivalences and the associated maps are accessible in logspace. The actions on simple components of $B=S^k$ and $B'=T^k$ are transitive. Fix one component $S_0$. Guess its image $T_j$ and the images in $T_j$ of a generating pair of $S_0$, and check that this gives an isomorphism. For every component $S_v$ choose the least $g\in E$ taking $S_0$ to $S_v$. Use conjugation by $g$ on both factors to propagate $f$ on $S_v$ and identify its target component. These assignments are forced up to componentwise inner automorphism for any valid $f$. Test that the component assignments are bijective and that the propagated map intertwines the actions of every element of $E$ modulo inner automorphisms. Each test compares two component actions up to an inner adjustment found by scanning the simple target component. The independence of inner adjustments on distinct components shows why the propagated map passes whenever a valid map with the same initial data exists. Isomorphism checks and evaluations on a simple group use two generator images and Cayley-graph word search. [Aschbacher–Guralnick, two-generation](https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-8693(84)90183-2) · [Reingold](https://omereingold.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/sl.pdf)

The automorphisms of $L_B$ inducing the identity on $L_B/B$ are exactly the conjugations by $f\in\operatorname{Aut}(B)$ passing that same test for $B=B'$. Restriction to $B$ determines the automorphism of $L_B$ because the centralizer is trivial. Every such $f$ is generated by the described transport data plus arbitrary componentwise inner adjustments at every component except the first, already fixed by its generator images. There are at most $|B|$ joint adjustment codes. Compact ranks in the simple factors and chief-factor ordinals suffice to store the whole code.

Fix an equivalence class of $\delta$ nonabelian chief factors and identify their conjugation images with a common $L$. Their maps $\alpha_B:E\to L$ identify exactly the $\delta$ crown coordinates. The maps have distinct kernels because an earlier factor's conjugation map is nontrivial on its upper chief term and all later maps are trivial there. Each kernel contains the crown kernel by definition. An onto map from the crown quotient $L_\delta$ to $L$ must map at least one socle factor nontrivially, since the quotient by the socle has order $|L/B|<|L|$. Each nontrivial image of a socle factor must be the full monolith, by minimality, its order, and its trivial center. At most one can map nontrivially, since distinct factors commute. The map's kernel thus contains the product of the remaining factors and equals it by the order of the target. Hence the $\delta$ distinct kernels exhaust the coordinate kernels. Equivalently, a direct proof of exhaustion before taking the crown quotient is to apply the first nontrivial chief image argument to *any* eligible onto map from $E$: its first nontrivial chief image is the target monolith, and the map is exactly the transported conjugation action on that factor, by faithfulness of the target action on its monolith. This proves that its kernel is one of the enumerated kernels. Then the map into all $\delta$ copies of $L$ realizes the crown as the fiber product over $L/B$, since their outer actions agree, the joint kernel is the crown kernel, and the order is $|L_\delta|$. [Lucchini–Thakkar, §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.07633)

## Choosing and assembling the lifts

Let $m>0$ of this class's factors lie in $A$. The remaining $\delta-m$ maps factor through $E/A$. The corresponding tuples induced by $q$ generate $L$ and belong to distinct orbits under the automorphisms of $L$ acting trivially on $L/B$: those maps surject jointly onto the corresponding projection of the crown, itself a quotient of $E/A$. These are the forbidden orbits. The outer tuple $q$ induces in $L/B$ is common to all factors in this class because the action of $A$ on every factor in its product is inner.

For each slot take the least lift in $L$ of its outer entry. Set $b=|B|$, $T=\lceil8/5+\log_b\delta\rceil$, and $t=\min(d,T)$. Scan candidates altering that common baseline in its first $t$ slots by elements of $B$. Compute $T$ as the first integer $k\ge2$ with $b^{5k-8}\ge\delta^5$. Candidate descriptions have $O(\log b+\log\delta)$ bits. When $d\ge T$, the calculation in the proof of Lucchini–Thakkar Proposition 6 gives at least $b^{T-8/5}\ge\delta$ distinct orbits of generating tuples with these first-slot changes: Lemma 5 lower-bounds the number of generating candidates, while the size of the orbit group is at most $b\log_2 b$. The size calculation applies to this arbitrary fixed suffix and outer tuple, independently of the forbidden orbits. When $d<T$, the scan considers all lifts of the outer tuple, and a generating lift of $q$ in $E$ exists by Gaschütz's lemma. Its crown projections show that at least $m$ distinct allowed orbits occur in the scan. Use the first $m$ candidates that generate, avoid the forbidden orbits, and are first among candidates in their own orbit. All comparisons enumerate the compact automorphism codes and compare entries one slot at a time. Assign the chosen candidates to the $m$ factors of $A$ in order. [Lucchini–Thakkar, Lemma 5 and proof of Proposition 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.07633#page=5)

For each output slot $j$ the assignments impose independent corrections on the factors $A_i$. Set the baseline rule to be the least lift into $E$ of $q_j$. To compute the contribution of $A_i$, first locate that factor's assigned candidate and retain only its compact code of up to $t$ corrections. Query $q_j$ once. From the returned label compute both the baseline lift in $E$ and the selected candidate's $j$th action in $L$, using the same quotient label. On $A_i$ the required right correction is the unique element whose inner action changes the baseline action to the assigned action. Find it by a scan and keep only its compact rank or its product into a compact $A$ accumulator. The correction exists since the two actions have the same outer class, and other factors of $A$ act trivially there. Discard the ambient representatives and action data before the next factor's search or tuple query. After forming the product of the corrections in compact labels, query $q_j$ again, construct its least lift, apply the accumulated correction and return the result. In particular neither a full ambient group label nor a full possibly high-degree permutation in $\operatorname{Aut}(A_i)$ is retained across a query. The resulting subgroup $H$ surjects onto $E/A$ and all crowns for classes represented in $A$. If $H$ were proper, take a maximal subgroup $M$ containing it. Since $MA=E$, some $A_i$ is outside $M$ and $MA_i=E$. The image of $H$ in that factor's crown is full. Hence $M R_E(A_i)=E$ as well, and the crown lemma forces $M=E$. This proves generation. [Lucchini–Thakkar, Lemma 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.07633#page=7)

## Generation of a candidate with small suspended workspace

It remains to test whether a candidate $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_d)$ generates the monolithic $L$ while keeping only compact state between queries to $q$. Let $B=S^k$ be the monolith, $F=L/B$, $H=\langle x\rangle$, and $K=H\cap B$. The tuple already projects to a generating tuple of $F$. For a component or an ordered pair of distinct components, make the orbit graph for the component action of the candidate and its inverses. It has at most $k^2$ vertices. Deterministic undirected search supplies a path word $P_v$ from a fixed root to each vertex $v$, with the empty word at the root. The Schreier loops $P_w^{-1}x_j^{\pm1}P_v$ for edges $v\to w$ generate the stabilizer in $H$. The edge loop expressions telescope for any stabilizing word. Evaluate the action of a loop on one or two simple elements by following their current component ordinals and their local ranks. For each signed letter, get the required $q_j$ before constructing any ambient representative, then compute and apply the candidate action or its inverse. [Reingold](https://omereingold.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/sl.pdf)

Fix a component $S_u$. Let $F_u$ stabilize $u$ and let $T_u$ be the preimage in $\operatorname{Aut}(S_u)$ of the prescribed image of $F_u$ in $\operatorname{Out}(S_u)$. The Schreier loops give the action image $I$ of $H_u$ in $T_u$. Test whether $I$ contains every inner automorphism, using Cayley reachability with compact automorphism labels given by two generator images of $S_u$. Each edge calls a Schreier loop action. A failure means the projection $K_u$ of $K$ into $S_u$ is proper. If $I$ contains every inner automorphism then $I=T_u$, because $H_u$ maps onto $F_u$. Now $K_u$ is normal in $S_u$ and is either trivial or full. It is trivial exactly when the action of $H_u$ on $S_u$ factors through $F_u$.

To test this factorization, enumerate all epimorphisms $\theta:F_u\to T_u$ agreeing with the prescribed outer action. In a fixed $F_u$ chief series, the first term with nontrivial image maps onto the monolith of $T_u$, namely $S_u$ identified with its inner group. Its chief factor maps isomorphically to $S_u$. The isomorphism is determined by two generator images, so enumerate the chief factor and this isomorphism and transport the conjugation action of $F_u$ along it. The action of $T_u$ on its monolith is faithful, and therefore every epimorphism sought occurs. Check the map's target membership, surjectivity and outer action by table scans independent of $q$. In particular this enumeration allows the answer that no such epimorphism exists.

For each candidate $\theta$, compare its action on the quotient of each Schreier loop to the actual action of that loop. Fix a tuple-independent transversal $s_v\in F$ taking $u$ to $v$ with $s_u=1$. For an edge $v\to w$ labelled by $\bar x_j^{\pm1}$, compute the compact automorphism $\theta(s_w^{-1}\bar x_j^{\pm1}s_v)$. Query $q_j$ before looking up the ambient representatives of $s_v,s_w$ or the quotient letter. Along a loop these edge factors telescope, and their images can be composed in a compact simple-automorphism register. A candidate agrees on every loop precisely when the desired action factorization holds, since these loops generate the stabilizer. Conversely if $K_u$ is trivial the factorization is an epimorphism to $T_u$ and occurs in the scan. Thus $K_u=S_u$ exactly when the inner test succeeds and every such $\theta$ fails a loop comparison.

After every component projection is full, $K$ is a subdirect product of nonabelian simple factors. It equals $B$ exactly when no pair projection is the graph of an isomorphism: the ordinary subdirect-product argument follows by induction, projecting to the first $k-1$ factors and using their product expression when the kernel at the last factor is trivial. For a pair $(u,v)$ test every isomorphism $f:S_u\to S_v$ obtained by two generator images. The pair stabilizer $H_{u,v}$ normalizes its graph if and only if the two component actions commute with $f$, tested on the pair-orbit Schreier generators. If they normalize the graph, then $K\le H_{u,v}$ and its two component actions are inner. Inner actions in the normalizer have conjugators related by $f$, by the centerlessness of the simple factors. Thus the pair projection lies in the graph and equals it since the projections are full. Conversely a graph pair projection of the normal subgroup $K\lhd H$ is normalized by the pair stabilizer. This detects every diagonal obstruction. Passing the component and pair tests means $K=B$, hence $H=L$.

## Workspace accounting

Keep the current candidate codes and comparison automorphisms in compact labels of $O(\log |B|+\log\log N)$ bits. Chief positions and component identifiers are ordinals, and no original table labels are retained for them during a tuple call. To compare candidate or forbidden tuples on a slot, query that slot once and construct both ambient representatives afterward. The generation test's automorphism reachability graph has at most $|S|^2$ vertices. Its adjacency routine calls the Schreier loop procedure, whose path routines use graphs on at most $k^2$ vertices. This is fixed-depth composition, and all graph and path indices need only $O(\log |S|+\log(kd+1))$ bits. Loop evaluation keeps only a fixed number of component ordinals and local element ranks or pair-image automorphism codes. In the epimorphism check, the selected chief term is stored by ordinal and the candidate map by two simple ranks. Quotient table access, transversal lookup, verification of a candidate isomorphism, multiplication and ambient action evaluations use at most $O(\log N)$ space *between* tuple calls. The candidate and ancillary scans retain no such table scratch across the calls. The factorwise product of the corrections itself retains only a compact $A$ element. The per-call suspended bound follows, as does polynomial work between recursive calls. All algorithms here use deterministic scans and reachability in fixed-depth composition.
