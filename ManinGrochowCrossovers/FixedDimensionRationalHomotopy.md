# Rational homotopy equivalence in fixed dimension

**Theorem.** Fix a dimension bound $d\ge 6$. Rational homotopy equivalence of finite simply connected simplicial complexes of dimension at most $d$ is polynomial-time many-one equivalent to rational three-tensor isomorphism. The upper reduction holds for every fixed $d$. The lower reduction uses triangulated closed simply connected stably parallelizable smooth six-manifolds with torsion-free homology.

Šimková established decidability of rational homotopy equivalence of finite simply connected simplicial sets. The additional result here is a polynomial reduction to one ordinary tensor-isomorphism instance in each fixed dimension, with matching hardness for $d\ge6$. [Šimková, Theorems C and D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21182)

## Computing a finite model

Let $M=(\Lambda V,\mathrm d)$ be the rational minimal Sullivan model of the input complex $X$. We construct it through generator degree $d$ using rational piecewise polynomial forms. Let $I$ be the integration map to simplicial cochains, $E$ the Whitney map, and $H$ the Dupont homotopy with $\mathrm dH+H\mathrm d=1-EI$, using the appropriate sign convention. A cohomology class represented by a simplicial cocycle $a$ lifts to $Ea$. A closed polynomial form $\omega$ with zero cohomology class has primitive $H\omega+Eb$, where $\mathrm db=I\omega$ is solved by rational linear algebra. [Šimková, Theorems 6.13 and C](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21182)

In each degree $j\ge2$, begin with the partial model on generators of smaller degrees. Add degree-$j$ closed generators for the cokernel on $H^j$ of its map to the forms on $X$. For a basis of the kernel on $H^{j+1}$, add one degree-$j$ generator with differential a cycle representing each basis element. Its image in forms is the computed primitive of the image of that cycle. There are no degree-one generators, and $2j>j+1$. Hence no product involving a new generator has degree $j+1$, and the nonclosed generators kill the specified kernel without changing $H^j$. This is the ascending-degree minimal model construction.

Through degree $d+1$, every monomial has length at most $\lfloor(d+1)/2\rfloor$. In each degree step the number of monomials, new generators, and differential coefficients is therefore polynomial in the previous numbers and the input size. The images in forms are stored simplex by simplex in at most $d+1$ barycentric variables. Whitney forms have bounded polynomial degree. A multiplication of at most $\lfloor(d+1)/2\rfloor$ previously computed forms increases degree by at most this constant times their maximum polynomial degree. In fixed dimension the Dupont formula uses a bounded number of affine substitutions and integrations on standard simplices or intervals per simplex. On forms of bounded polynomial degree these operations again give bounded polynomial degree and use rational linear operators of bounded bit size. Induction for the fixed $d$ steps bounds the polynomial degree of all stored forms in terms of $d$ alone. Each form therefore uses $O_d(\#\text{simplices})$ coefficient slots.

Each step uses rational linear algebra on polynomial-size matrices and sums and bounded-length products of rational coefficients. Batch the cohomology kernel calculations and primitive solves by using multiple right-hand sides. Fraction-free elimination bounds all resulting bit lengths polynomially in the matrix dimensions and entry sizes. The number of degree steps is fixed, so the resulting finite graded algebra
$$
C_X=(\Lambda V^{\le d})/(\Lambda V^{\le d})^{>d+1}
$$
and its differential and multiplication tables can be computed in polynomial bit time.

## One tensor test

A rational homotopy equivalence gives isomorphic full minimal models, hence isomorphic $C_X$ and $C_Y$. Indeed an isomorphism preserves the subalgebras generated in degrees at most $d$ because any element in one of those degrees belongs to that subalgebra. Conversely, a graded differential-algebra isomorphism $C_X\cong C_Y$ specifies images of the generators through degree $d$ in the actual partial model. The free graded-commutative algebra property extends the assignment multiplicatively. Preservation of each generator's differential is checked through degree $d+1$, which the finite model retains. This map induces a cohomology isomorphism through degree $d$. Extend it to each higher generator of degree $j>d$ by taking a primitive of the image of its differential. That image is closed and the full target model has $H^{j+1}=0$. The resulting map of full minimal models is a quasi-isomorphism, since all cohomology above $d$ vanishes in both. Thus $C_X\cong C_Y$ is equivalent to rational homotopy equivalence.

Compare dimensions in each degree. With agreeing dimensions, encode the products $C_X^i\otimes C_X^j\to C_X^{i+j}$ and differentials $C_X^i\to C_X^{i+1}$ as one tensor system of orders three and two. Change basis once per graded piece and use the dual transformation on every dual occurrence. This simultaneous orbit relation is exactly graded differential-algebra isomorphism. Records of order two use the order-padding construction of Lemma 5.1. The tensor-system reduction of Futorny–Grochow–Sergeichuk sends all the records to one ordinary three-tensor in polynomial time. Its linked blocks impose equal transformations for repeated occurrences and inverse-transposes for dual occurrences, including two occurrences of the same degree in a product. [Futorny–Grochow–Sergeichuk, Theorems 1.1 and 4.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.09219)

## Hardness in dimension six

The padded symmetric-cubic construction takes rational TI to similitude of the cup cubics of two simply connected six-manifolds with equal Betti numbers. The binary framed surgery construction supplies triangulations in polynomial time. Miller's formality theorem makes rational homotopy equivalence of those manifolds equivalent to graded rational cohomology isomorphism. By Poincaré duality this is precisely cubic similitude, with the multiplier giving the scalar on top cohomology. This supplies hardness in dimension six and hence for every fixed bound $d\ge6$. [Rational Tensor Isomorphism and six-manifold maps](RationalTensorMaps.md) · [Miller](https://mathscinet.ams.org/mathscinet-getitem?mr=528561)
