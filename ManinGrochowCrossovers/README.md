# Twenty MathProof results for Joshua Grochow

The crossover adds five entries, led by a characterization of fixed-dimensional rational homotopy equivalence as a Tensor Isomorphism problem. The matrix problems in Question 7.3 and the Rank Principle separation remain the strongest opening for Grochow's proof-complexity work. [Question 7.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S7.SS3) · [Part V](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00735)

The placements weigh mathematical scope, relevance to Grochow's published program, and evidence for priority. The new main theorems have written proofs with provisional priority. Lean independently verifies one supporting algebraic identity. Sarkisjan's earlier orbit algorithm answers the rational TI decidability question in Grochow–Qiao's Remark 3.4. The crossover establishes polynomial equivalences for rational homotopy type and for actual nonzero-degree maps. [Priority and proof](RationalTensorMaps.md#decidability-and-priority)

## Ranking

1. **Exact proof degree for matrix conjugacy and congruence.** The two formulas give the minimum ordinary PC degree for each geometrically negative input over every field on the literal systems of Question 7.3. For diagonal projections of ranks $r$ and $r+1$ in side $n=2r+1$, conjugacy has sparse size $\Theta(n^2\log n)$ and congruence has size between $\Omega(n^4)$ and $O(n^4\log n)$, with unrestricted-degree lower bounds. The degree part has a proposed full answer. The unrestricted size part remains open, including the exponential-to-factorial gap for proportional prime-power gaps. Lean checks supporting lemmas. [Conjugacy degree](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/GeneralCongruenceSparseConjugacy/ConjugacyDegree.md) · [General congruence degree](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/GeneralCongruenceSparseConjugacy/RESULTS.md) · [Sparse bounds](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/SparseMatrixProofBounds/RESULTS.md)

2. **Rank Principle separates sparse PC from circuit IPS.** Over every field, $PQ=I_s$ with inner dimension $1\le r<s$ has exact PC degree $r+2$. At $s=r+1$, sparse PC size is $r^{\Theta(r)}$ while a division-free determinant gives polynomial-size Hilbert-like IPS. This sharpens the lower bound in Galesi–Grochow–Pitassi–She's Theorem 1.5 and should be compared with earlier exponential PCR lower bounds for weak-rank encodings over $\mathbb F_2$. The encoding is one-sided and uses no coordinate field equations. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/ExactMatrixProofDegrees/RankPrinciple.md) · [Weak Rank Principle precedent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08760)

3. **Fixed-dimensional rational homotopy equivalence is rational Tensor Isomorphism complete.** The upper reduction applies to simply connected finite simplicial complexes in every fixed dimension. Hardness starts by dimension six, on actual triangulated manifolds. On that six-manifold family the same test decides whether a nonzero-degree map exists. [Fixed-dimensional proof](FixedDimensionRationalHomotopy.md) · [Mapping proof](RationalTensorMaps.md)

4. **Integral Tensor Isomorphism is equivalent to oriented diffeomorphism of triangulated six-manifolds.** The equivalence also holds for oriented homotopy equivalence and degree-one domination at equal second Betti number. The construction takes binary coefficients and outputs polynomial-size triangulations of simply connected, stably parallelizable examples. Part V relates integral tensors to Wall invariants. The new reduction realizes those invariants at polynomial cost. [Proof](IntegralTensorManifolds.md)

5. **Same-prime-support equivalence for fixed finite rings.** Ordinary TI over fixed nonzero finite commutative rings with the same characteristic prime divisors has the same deterministic polynomial many-one degree, including free and presented mode modules. The clean example is $\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z$ versus $\mathbb F_2$. Follow-ups put TI completeness inside one final-digit congruence class and recover prescribed-residue transporters over explicit finite local rings. The comparison holds with fixed coefficient rings. Part V supplies related reductions with the ring held fixed. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/PrimePowerTensorIsomorphism/PROOF.md) · [Written audit](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/PrimePowerTensorIsomorphism/AUDIT.md) · [Priority and follow-ups](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/FixedRingFour/README.md)

6. **Sharp sparse PC versus circuit IPS on manifold cup-product equations.** For a fixed target, the literal degree-one equations have sharp linear PC degree and exponential sparse PC size in the second Betti number, with polynomial-size Hilbert-like IPS and linear certificate degree in characteristic different from two. Real Sum-of-Squares degree is also linear. In characteristic two, geometric infeasibility of any such cup system has degree at most six and polynomial sparse size. [Proof](ManifoldProofComplexity.md) · [Lean lemma](CubicIdempotentCorrection.lean)

7. **Exponential sparse PC lower bounds for ordinary TI.** Binary-coefficient tensors of side $n$ require $2^{\Omega(n)}$ monomial occurrences and degree $\Omega(n)$ for the original cubic action and inverse equations, for each fixed characteristic. The positive-characteristic argument uses Riis's matching designs and gives explicit families. The characteristic-zero argument uses hard parity instances. This addresses the lower-bound direction of Question 7.2. [Positive-characteristic proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/TensorSparsePCBounds/Proof.md) · [Characteristic-zero proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/TensorSparsePCBounds/CharacteristicZero.md) · [Riis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07280)

8. **Balanced reduction from higher-order TI to order three.** Lean checks the orbit equivalence for ordinary tensors over every field and the side bound $O(n^{\lceil d/3\rceil})$ for order $d$ and input sides at most $n$. A written mixed-signature result preserves degenerations and reduced orbit-closure germs up to smooth equivalence. The matching side exponent lower bound is for a specified polynomial equivariant model. [Lean theorem](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/BalancedOrdinaryReduction.lean) · [Degeneration proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/TensorDegenerationFallout/LINEAR-WIDTH.md) · [Lower-bound model](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/GrochowCatalogueProofsIV/TensorOrderReductionBounds.md)

9. **Exact smallest finite quotients separating class-two loops.** The general formula minimizes a cyclic character's modulus times its alternating matrix image size and classifies all minimum-index kernels. From a compact bracket tensor the decision problem is NP-complete. A pencil family gives exact counting hardness and bounded-length loops that are conjugate over the reals. [General theorem](GeneralFiniteSeparator.md) · [Hardness and counting](MinimalFiniteLoopQuotients.md)

10. **Polynomial-size class reduction in the Lazard range.** For Cayley-table $p$-groups of class less than $p$ and arbitrary exponent, isomorphism reduces to class-two $p$-group isomorphism with query order $N^{O(1)}$ and absolute constants. The decision oracle also yields search, exact counting and automorphism generators. This extends Grochow–Qiao's class-reduction program beyond the exponent-$p$ case. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/GrochowResearchSynthesis/LAZARD-CLASS-REDUCTION.md) · [Prior linear-length reductions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16317)

11. **Special-linear equivalence of finite-field matrix pencils.** A deterministic polynomial bit-time algorithm covers the whole $2\times m\times n$ format, including rectangular and singular pencils, without finite-field factorization or discrete logarithms. A separate result gives deterministic polynomial-time special-linear equivalence when one slice space has maximum rank two, allowing unbounded slice-space dimension. Both give sufficient cases of the reverse TI reduction. [Pencils](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/TISLPencilsAndOrbits/Pencils.md) · [Upper rank two](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/GrochowCatalogueProofsX/UpperRankTwoSpecialLinear.md) · [Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5)

12. **Exact joint-prime tests and sharp tensor genus growth.** Two good-prime counts determine abelian rational forms of the diagonal tensor below side 30, and side 30 is attained. The wider hierarchy gives sharp first collision dimensions, including common-degree products of arbitrary Galois fields. The maximum all-place genus has logarithm $(n/4)\log n+O(n)$ in the stated abelian class and $(n/16)\log n+O(n)$ when every factor is nonabelian Galois. The abelian shortest-test algorithm uses Dirichlet GRH for its prime search bound. Lean verifies the binary rank-three threshold. [Hierarchy](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/DirichletGrochowPrimeTests/JointPrimeHierarchy.md) · [Galois extension](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/ExactMatrixProofDegrees/JointGalois.md) · [Abelian genera](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/MatrixCongruenceAndPrimeGenera/GENUS.md) · [Nonabelian genera](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/ExactMatrixProofDegrees/NonabelianGenus.md) · [Lean check](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/DirichletGrochowPrimeTests/RankThreeReviewed.lean)

13. **An unmarked local-algebra construction for form transport.** For fixed degree at least three over any field, the relation $z^d=f$ encodes a formal form into a commutative unital algebra. The construction recovers the extra line intrinsically and retains actual form transporters. The new coefficient-field tag yields prime-field multiplication tensors with exact transporter fibers. Earlier tensor-system results already imply the broad all-fields complexity classification. [Construction](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/GrochowFarDAGII/Tensor.md) · [Prime-field tag](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/CubicAnchorFour/Algorithms.md) · [Priority audit](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/CubicAnchorFour/Priority.md)

14. **Exact partition rank of the determinant.** With one variable group per column, the answer is $n-1$ at even $n\ge4$ and $n$ at odd $n$ and $n=2$, over every field. A Pfaffian gives the even upper bound. Ordered-line Chern classes and a roots-of-unity argument give the lower bound. The nearest precedents give the four-column answer and a largest-prime lower bound. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/GrochowOpenProblemSolutions2/DETERMINANT-PARTITION-RANK.md) · [Partition-rank precedent](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2026.90) · [Chern-class precedent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21015)

15. **Unbounded integral cubic genera in second Betti number three.** Stably parallelizable simply connected six-manifolds can have arbitrarily many distinct integral homotopy types with pairwise isomorphic cohomology rings at every prime and a common rational homotopy type. This is a constant-rank local-global failure for integral cup-tensor isometry. [Proof](IntegralCubicGenera.md)

16. **Projective schemes at the first distinguishing matrix substitution.** Two pencils can agree on the rational canonical forms of every matched substitution through size $h$, with a first marked rank-drop scheme at $h+1$ realizing an arbitrary projective scheme times a split orthogonal Grassmannian. The follow-ups recover the scheme unmarked, including nilpotents, below the fiber dimension and give parsimonious $\#\mathrm P$-completeness for point counting over a fixed finite field. [Substitution threshold](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/SliceSimilarityFour/Tensor.md) · [Universality](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/SharpSubstitutionFour/Geometry.md) · [Unmarked recovery and counting](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/FirstRankDropFour/README.md) · [Compression](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/RankCondenserFour/README.md)

17. **Short circuit IPS for bounded-mode TI and marked quivers.** For one fixed tensor mode, every finite-field negative instance has a polynomial-size Hilbert-like IPS certificate with coordinate field equations only on the forward entries of that mode. The geometric bounded-mode theorem uses just the original tensor encoding. For determinant-constrained quivers, polynomial-size finite-field certificates use all coordinate field equations. The latter includes named-subspace module isomorphism. [Bounded-mode geometric proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/ExactMatrixProofDegrees/BoundedModeIPS.md) · [Finite-field proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/ExactMatrixProofDegrees/FiniteModeIPS.md) · [Marked quiver proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/DirichletGrochowPrimeTests/FiniteFieldMarkedIPS.md)

18. **The full tensor asymptotic spectrum descends by Galois quotient.** For a normal algebraic extension $E/F$, restriction realizes the spectrum over $F$ as the topological quotient of the spectrum over $E$ by the Galois action. In finite Galois degree $d$, transfers of the first $d$ powers of a separating tensor already distinguish distinct spectral orbits. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/TensorSpectraDeterminantsGroupLifts/GaloisSpectrumDescent.md)

19. **Every finite group as an integral tensor stabilizer scheme.** From a Cayley table, the construction gives a concise integral three-tensor with coefficients in $\{0,1,-1\}$ whose special-linear stabilizer is exactly the associated constant group scheme, including at primes dividing the group order. This asserts scheme-level equality and controls the graph transporter as well. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/ExactTensorTransporters/Integral.md)

20. **Characteristic-three cubic orbit closures and their boundary.** The nonzero restriction on the polarization radical yields an affine-space product over the polarization orbit closure. The zero-restriction locus has a Grassmannian incidence description. The companion computes Frobenius-thickened fibers and an explicit boundary conductor. ## Previously in the Top 20

- **One mixed quartic suffices for orbit-closure intersection completeness.** A linear-side affine map packs two mixed quartics into one tensor in $W^{\otimes2}\otimes(W^*)^{\otimes2}$ with diagonal general-linear action over $\mathbb C$. A partial-trace anchor recovers the subgroup used in the reduction. This addresses the single-record order-four case of Lysikov–Walter. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/09884ecd143d669cf5a5b9f1734264d8240c4e10/GrochowCatalogueProofsVI/PureMixedQuarticTOCI.md)

- **Algorithms for almost all ordinary even-order tensors.** Over a fixed finite field the written theorem gives an invariant recognizable first-input class of density $1-o(1)$ for balanced even order $d\ge4$. Decision, witness and exact counting against arbitrary targets use polynomial bit time with an exponent independent of $d$. The latest Lean work verifies native scalar probes and other components. Full controller execution remains an assumption of the end-to-end formal results.
