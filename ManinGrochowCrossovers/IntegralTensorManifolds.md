# Integral tensor isomorphism on triangulated six-manifolds

**Theorem.** Free three-tensor isomorphism over binary-encoded integers is polynomial-time many-one equivalent to oriented diffeomorphism of closed simply connected stably parallelizable smooth six-manifolds with torsion-free integral homology and $H^3=0$, given by triangulations. The equivalence also holds for oriented homotopy equivalence and for existence of a degree-one map when the second Betti numbers agree. The reduction constructs triangulations in polynomial time.

Grochow–Qiao–Stange–Sun reduce integral TI to symmetric trilinear form equivalence under integral basis change. Clear any denominators by a common scaling for the pair. The geometric construction uses the framed surgery realization from the Manin mapping-degree results. The added ingredient is polynomial-time construction on binary-encoded cup cubics. [Grochow–Qiao–Stange–Sun, Corollary 1.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00735v1#S1.SS3) · [Framed realization of cubics](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/b373edcc6eb25fa29980380503da5096ffe84588/ManinTop20/ManinPersistenceAndDegrees.md#framed-realization-of-cubics)

### Binary coefficients from primitive sparse relations

For a symmetric integral trilinear form $\mu$ on $\mathbb Z^n$, express $P(x)=6\mu(x,x,x)$ as a sum of monomials $6m x_i x_j x_k$, allowing repeated indices. A copy of $(S^2)^3$ with its own degree-two coordinates $a,b,c$ has diagonal cup cubic $6abc$. Use one such summand per monomial. Copies of $S^2\times S^4$ supply the original coordinates and all additional scalar coordinates, with zero cubic.

Impose $b=x_j$, $c=x_k$, and $a=mx_i$. For the last equation, read $|m|$ in binary. Start at $t_0=x_i$ for the leading nonzero bit. For each following bit $\epsilon_\ell$ introduce a fresh coordinate and impose $t_\ell=2t_{\ell-1}+\epsilon_\ell x_i$. Finally impose $a=\operatorname{sgn}(m)t_{\rm last}$. Omit zero monomials. Every relation involves at most four signed coordinate cycles counted with multiplicity. Ordering the auxiliary coordinates by definition makes their relation matrix unit triangular, so the dual attaching cycles are primitive and independent with free quotient of rank $n$. Their number and total support are polynomial in the bit length.

Take the oriented connected sum of the summands, using $S^6$ if the list is empty, and perform framed surgery on the dual $2$-sphere of each relation. The disjoint embedded representatives and framing are constructed below. The initial sum is stably parallelizable, and the oriented rank-four normal bundles of the spheres are trivial because their stabilized bundles are trivial. The ambient stable framing extends over the attaching handles since the discrepancy lies in $\pi_2(SO)=0$. Removal of the spheres in codimension four preserves simple connectivity, as does gluing the pieces $D^3\times S^3$. The trace uses index-three handles from the initial side and index-four handles from the final side.

Degree-two cohomology from the trace restricts to the initial side as exactly the kernel of evaluation on the attaching cycles, by relative handle cohomology. Its restriction to the final side is an isomorphism. Triple evaluations agree on the two oriented boundary classes of the trace. Thus the new diagonal cubic is $P$, and polarization gives cup tensor $6\mu$. The second homology is free by primitivity and the reverse handle index. Each surgery decreases both the Euler characteristic and $2b_2$ by two, so the third Betti number remains zero. Integral duality and the universal coefficient theorem give torsion-free homology.

### Polynomial triangulation of the surgery construction

Factor spheres give the individual coordinate cycles. Choose disjoint parallel copies before taking connected sums. In a product of three spheres, copies of axis $i$ use fixed points on the other factors. In each factor choose disjoint lists of fixed points used by different axes. Two different axes then have different points on their common fixed factor. In $S^2\times S^4$ use $S^2$ times distinct points. In each factor use small rational polyhedral disks in a fixed affine chart around the marked points. Their products give explicit product normal charts. Form the connected sum using one central $S^6$ with one separate neck per summand. All connected-sum balls can be placed away from the sphere copies and their product charts. The initial triangulation is polynomial: subdivide the fixed charts at the disk and ball boundaries and use triangulated products. Only a polynomial number of rational coordinate boxes, each of fixed dimension, is used.

For a relation use distinct parallel copies with the specified signs, then connect-sum them along a chain of at most three tubes. Choose distinct terminal points in small flat disks on the spheres. Tube cores are disjoint rational polygonal arcs with polynomially many pieces. Route a core along a simple path in the dual graph of the ambient top simplices, starting and ending in chosen incident top simplices. Cross each facet through a distinct rational portal in its interior. In a top simplex join two portals, or a portal and a terminal, by two segments through an interior waypoint. For each waypoint avoid the line of its two prescribed endpoints and the affine spans of either endpoint with each earlier segment in that simplex. This ensures disjointness from all earlier arc pieces. Avoidance is effective with polynomial bit lengths: test polynomially many points from an affine image of a rational moment curve in the simplex interior, since each proper affine hyperplane excludes at most six of these points. Arc interiors avoid the marked spheres because they stay inside the top simplices and facet interiors. The number of pieces is at most a constant times the number of top simplices per arc.

Use thin rational polyhedral strips and collars to thicken the cores. At an arc corner inside an affine chart choose a rational linear functional increasing in both oriented directions. The corner is then a piecewise linear graph over an interval, so subtraction of the graph in transverse coordinates straightens it. At a facet crossing first unfold the two incident simplices into one rational affine chart. This gives explicit interval charts with five transverse coordinates. Choose transverse frames at the ends of successive charts, using the product frames at the terminal disks. They can be connected with a bounded number of elementary shears, positive scalings and signed coordinate interchanges per chart in the fixed transverse dimension. Each elementary change has an explicit piecewise-linear prism interpolation. For a shear $x_i\mapsto x_i+\alpha x_j$ with $i\ne j$, at time $t\in[0,1]$ add $\alpha\min(wt,\max(-wt,x_j))$ to $x_i$ on $|x_j|\le w$. The other coordinates are fixed, so subtraction gives the inverse there. For positive scaling of one coordinate, triangulate the rectangle in its coordinate and time and send those triangles affinely to the two triangles of the trapezoid of the chosen bottom and top widths. Extend by the identity on the other transverse coordinates. Signed interchanges preserving orientation are products of a bounded number of shears and positive scalings. Concatenate the elementary changes in separate time intervals. These interpolations use a bounded number of rational affine pieces in fixed dimension. The tube itself is $S^1\times I$ in two transverse coordinates, with its radial coordinate and the other three transverse coordinates giving four normal coordinates. The elementary connected-sum chart at a terminal glues this annulus to the punctured $2$-disk with its product framing. Signs of the local identifications are chosen to orient the connected sum. This realizes each dual cycle by a single framed $S^2$.

All chart widths may be taken rational of polynomial bit length. Choose separate small corner and terminal charts, then shrink the interval collars in their complements. For finitely many disjoint rational polytopes in a fixed chart, a positive lower bound for their separation in sup norm is obtained by solving rational linear programs. This bounds the sizes needed to keep all other collars disjoint and inside their charts. All linear systems on chart dimensions are of fixed size. The resulting $S^2\times D^4$ neighborhoods and their product coordinates are given by polynomially many rational affine pieces: use the punctured product sphere charts and the interval neck charts just described.

Triangulate the ambient pieces compatibly with these neighborhoods and product coordinates. In each original simplex take an arrangement of the face hyperplanes of the affine pieces. Refine shared faces by the subdivisions induced from all incident charts. Extend face refinements across convex cells by coning in increasing dimension. This gives consistent refinements without propagating each cut through unrelated simplices. An arrangement of $h$ hyperplanes in fixed dimension has polynomially many faces and vertices, and the vertices are computed by fixed-size rational linear systems. The same procedure gives compatible subdivisions across affine coordinate changes of the product charts. Thus the triangulated neighborhood boundary and its parametrization by $S^2\times S^3$ have polynomial size. Remove the neighborhoods and glue $D^3\times S^3$ using these product parametrizations. Refine the standard product triangulation at the prescribed boundary and cone the face refinements into the product cells. This adds only polynomially many simplices. The local connected-sum and product neck charts can be rounded in the smooth charts of the initial sum, so this PL surgery gives the triangulated model of the smooth surgery above. No number of geometric copies depends on a coefficient's magnitude beyond its binary addition chain.

### Complexity in both directions

Wall's classification determines the oriented diffeomorphism type of a simply connected spin closed smooth six-manifold with torsion-free integral homology by the third Betti number, integral cubic and first Pontryagin class. For the promise the third Betti number and Pontryagin class are zero. Thus oriented equivalence of the cup tensors is equivalent to oriented diffeomorphism. Every oriented homotopy equivalence preserves the cup cubic, so the same condition characterizes oriented homotopy equivalence. [Wall, Classification problems in differential topology V](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01389738)

A degree-one map induces a split injection on integral cohomology by Poincaré duality and the transfer. Equal second Betti numbers and zero third cohomology make this an isomorphism in every degree. It follows that the map induces oriented cup-tensor equivalence. Conversely the diffeomorphism supplies a degree-one map. The Part V symmetrization has a common output dimension for each input format, so equal second Betti numbers are harmless for hardness. Scaling a pair of integral cubics by six preserves and reflects integral equivalence.

For the reverse reductions compute an integral degree-two cohomology basis and its cubic from the triangulations by polynomial-bit-time integer normal forms and simplicial cup products. The orientation gives the top evaluation. The promised characteristic data then reduce the three problems to symmetric integral trilinear form equivalence and hence to free integral three-tensor isomorphism. The output triangulations are PL models of the displayed smooth manifolds. In dimension six such a PL model determines the smoothing up to diffeomorphism. The proof does not assume that an arbitrary geometric Calabi–Yau family realizes the input invariant tables. [Grochow–Qiao–Stange–Sun, Corollary 1.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00735v1#S1.SS3)
