# Eight results on tensor approximation, group actions, and algebraic complexity

1. **Strictly better tree tensor approximation.** For any tree with $m\ge3$ nodes and any positive edge rank caps, a polynomial-time algorithm achieves a squared Frobenius approximation factor $m-1-1/(96r)$ with exact singular-vector projections. Rational input admits a deterministic polynomial-bit-time algorithm with factor $m-1-1/(192r)$. Here $r$ is the clamped first leaf rank cap. This answers the strict-improvement question in Workshop Problem 6.1 for every nondegenerate tree and recovers exactly representable inputs. [Proof](tree-tensor.md)
2. **Yu's Conjecture A6.2 is false.** For every $n\ge4$, real full-spark $n\times\lceil 3n/2\rceil$ matrices can have every maximal permanent zero. The counterexamples extend to width $2n-2$, sharp against the cited characteristic-zero guarantee, and to algebraically closed fields of characteristic different from two. [Proof](complexity.md#permanental-rank)
3. **Factor closure of VNP over finite-imperfection-degree fields.** This includes the explicit $\mathbb F_p(t)$ example in Bhargav–Dwivedi–Saxena's open problem. Over any infinite field of characteristic $p$, the proof recovers the power of each irreducible factor corresponding to the $p$-part of its multiplicity. [Proof](complexity.md#vnp-factor-closure)
4. **Conjugator search reduces to ordinary decision for a class of affine permutation groups.** Each point-orbit constituent can have its own regular abelian normal Sylow subgroup and any abelian local complement action. The reduction allows nonscalar actions and nonsplit global extensions. [Proof](groups.md#1-conjugator-search-with-abelian-local-complements)
5. **Minimum faithful actions in polynomial time for abelian-by-abelian Hall extensions.** The input is a Cayley table, and the construction needs no Hall subgroup supplied. Kernel and quotient ranks and exponents are unrestricted. [Proof](groups.md#2-minimum-faithful-actions-for-abelian-by-abelian-hall-extensions)
6. **One generic Gram graph recovers a class of even-order groups.** The quotient by $G'Z(G)^2\langle g:g^2=1\rangle$ must be odd and the faithful real orthogonal representation must lack four-dimensional quaternionic constituents. The graph determines the group law and regular action against arbitrary competing generic faithful modules. [Proof](invariants.md#one-orbit-recovery-for-even-groups-with-an-odd-residual-quotient)
7. **The ordinary dihedral invariant cutoff is cubic for even polygons in characteristic two.** Combining this new case with prior cases gives the exact cutoff for every $n\ge3$ over any infinite field. The sole exception is $n=5$ outside characteristic five, where degree four is required. This holds for both generic geometric orbit separation and invariant-field generation. [Proof](invariants.md#exact-ordinary-dihedral-invariant-degree-over-every-infinite-field)
8. **Quadratic Newton convergence with linear site cost for sparse product Hamiltonians.** At nondegenerate local minima with a supplied bounded-width elimination order and bounded interaction terms per edge, each Newton step costs $O(d)$ for fixed site dimension and width. Gap dominance also gives a unique global product minimum and a contractive initializer. This is a scoped answer to Workshop Problem 6.2. [Proof](product-state-newton.md)
