# Single equations in free class-two nilpotent groups

The [written proof](Proof.tex) gives a decision procedure for solvability of a single equation with constants in the free nilpotent group of class two and rank $r$, uniformly in $r$. The reduction uses the universal exterior-square commutator map. Symplectic normal forms decompose its rational fibres into finitely many orbits, and strong approximation on the acting group reduces each congruence-constrained orbit to finitely many decidable $p$-adic tests.

This is an AI-assisted proof. The decision procedure uses the standard effective theorem for $p$-adic fields and makes no efficiency claim. The LaTeX source is self-contained.
