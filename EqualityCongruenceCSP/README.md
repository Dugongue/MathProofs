# Equality–congruence CSP classification and witnesses

The [cumulative manuscript](N_Complete.md) gives AI-assisted written proofs for fixed finite-signature multisorted templates whose carriers are copies of the integers and finite sets. It assumes exact addition and a pin for 1 in each integer sort, and fixed Boolean combinations of affine equalities and congruences in the integer fibres. The arithmetic hypotheses and core requirement for the classification are specified in the manuscript. The structural and computational inputs are listed in [the bibliography](REFERENCES.md).

- An effective block-generic criterion decides whether every finite exact modular pp image has a Taylor polymorphism. On this side there is a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm for finding complete witnesses and computing the exact affine and convex hulls of the numerical solution set. It also decides affine equality consequences and certifies when a rational affine objective is unbounded in both directions.
- For a core, failure of the finite-image Taylor condition yields NP-completeness. The [hardness supplement](HARDNESS.md) proves the hardness implication that the archived manuscript takes as an input. The polynomial-bit witness bound in Chapter II gives NP membership.
- Appendix P gives a two-integer-sort example separating the block-generic criterion from its shared-coefficient predecessor.

The [integer avoidance source and fixtures](witness_code/README.md) accompany Chapter II. All 11 fixtures were independently recomputed with exact integer arithmetic. The manuscript preserves references to its original cumulative release, and the prerequisite proof is reproduced inline in Appendix P.
