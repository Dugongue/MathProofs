# Four results on tensor, graph, and algebra isomorphism

These are informal proofs. They build on the earlier rank-one frame, local slice algebra, and Hom-complex constructions.

| Result | Exact statement and scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Minimum extension degree for tensor isomorphism](DescentTransfer.md) | Over every fixed finite field, there is a polynomial-time recognizable family of raw balanced tensors that are geometrically isomorphic but for which deciding whether any extension degree at most a binary bound enables isomorphism is NP-complete. Testing a specified binary degree is polynomial. Counting admissible subfields is #P-complete under polynomial-time Turing reductions. The maximum minimum degree has logarithm asymptotic to $\sqrt{2m\log(2m)}$ at side $m+4$. |
| [TI-completeness at low slice rank and scalar adjoints](HomTransfer.md) | For every fixed positive integer $k$ and finite field, TI is many-one complete on a raw recognizable class with square matrix side $n$, all geometric slice ranks $O(n^{1/k})$, and scalar fixed-label adjoints in all three choices of label mode. An odd-characteristic extension gives TI-completeness in the succinct commutator representation of class-two groups with characteristic maximum-order abelian subgroups of the stated large size. |
| [Canonization from local generators](LocalCanonization.md) | Deterministically canonize and construct transporters for raw local ratio-algebra tensors in $q^{O(d^2+e^2)}\operatorname{poly}(N)$, with $d=\dim J/J^2$ and $e=\dim V/JV$. For free modules and for split local associative or nilpotent Lie algebras the exponent depends only on $d^2$. This handles large radical layers and unbounded Loewy length without a TI oracle. |
| [Graph isomorphism in irreducible determinant forms](GraphTransfer.md) | GI reduces deterministically to exact or projective linear equivalence of a family of absolutely irreducible homogeneous determinant forms over $\mathbb F_3$. Given extension-field evaluations of a promised form in raw coordinates, a Las Vegas algorithm recovers the graph and a normalization. The graph survives discarding the matrix pencil. |

The first theorem separates a degree test from the problem of choosing a sufficiently small degree on the same explicitly recognizable class. Its key step realizes vertex-cover constraints as equality of two powered Frobenius cycle types. The second shows that small geometric slice rank and scalar fixed-label endomorphism algebras can coexist with full TI hardness. These are the two strongest candidates in this packet for discussion with Grochow. The group consequence uses commutator data, whose length can be exponentially smaller than a Cayley table.

The proofs cite the classical finite-field and matrix-group algorithms, the published reductions, and related graph polynomial and cycle-type results. Publication priority for the specific combinations and independent human review are pending. The statements do not claim resolutions of previously named open problems. Exact controls checked 32,768 graph/divisor fixtures and 10 finite-field descent fixtures, 30 adjoint or rank reconstruction fixtures, 108 triangular orbit comparisons against exhaustive enumeration, and the determinant/Hasse identities on 64 small graphs. General correctness rests on the proofs.
