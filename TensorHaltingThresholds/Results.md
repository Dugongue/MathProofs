# Four halting thresholds for tensors

These four informal proofs examine what a tensor can encode when its coefficients, its optimal value, its local behavior, or its finite marginals are already known. Here a test is **halting-complete** if its yes-instances and the halting set reduce computably to one another.

| Tensor input | Question answered | Arithmetic premise |
|---|---|---|
| A rational order-three tensor whose square flattening is an orthogonal projection | Does a rational best rank-one approximation attain the known real optimum? If a rational optimizer is supplied, does a second one exist? Both tests are halting-complete in fixed formats. Both matrix analogues are decidable. | Rational Diophantine definition for the fixed-format halting conclusion. Variable-format equivalence to rational polynomial feasibility is unconditional. [Proof](Approximation.md) |
| A fixed rational matrix-product bulk tensor and a variable boundary | Is an endpoint ever entangled with the rest of a finite chain? This is halting-complete with pairwise commuting bulk matrices, strictly positive states and every proper site marginal maximally mixed. | Unconditional by integer DPRM. The number of sites is unbounded. [Proof](Network.md) |
| An identity-containing triple of square rational slices | Can a k-plane be mapped into a single k-plane by all slices? The expansion excess is promised to be zero or one. Two regular slices have a polynomial-time algorithm. Three slices encode arbitrary rational polynomial feasibility. | Rational Diophantine definition for the fixed-format halting conclusion with local witnesses at every completion. [Proof](Expansion.md) |
| An integral order-three tensor with one varying coordinate | Is its ordinary rational rank r or r+1? All its local ranks equal r. A fixed 2 by 2 by 2 catalyst gives an explicitly certified restriction on every input. Cancelling that catalyst recovers the halting-complete decision. | Rational Diophantine definition for the fixed-format halting conclusion. [Proof](Rank.md) |

The three rational halting classifications assume a fixed existential definition of the integers over the rationals. The cited manuscript claims one, and its repository reports an incomplete Lean formalization. The network proof uses the established representation of halting over nonnegative integers. All four proofs in this packet are informal. Priority for the exact combinations of restrictions is unverified. [Arithmetic source and status](https://github.com/elliotglazer/diophantine-z-q)

**Relevance to tensor isomorphism.** Prescribed-dimension expansion is invariant under ordinary three-mode basis changes. The construction establishes the difficulty of evaluating that invariant. Rational rank-one attainment uses a Euclidean metric. Catalyst cancellation concerns mode-wise linear restrictions, which may be singular. Entanglement across chain lengths quantifies over arbitrarily many sites. A reduction of any of these decision problems to isomorphism of two given tensors would require a further argument. Finite-field tensor isomorphism and its determinant-one variant are decidable by finite search.
