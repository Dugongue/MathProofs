# GP-0091 · Integral tensor cancellation with torsion

**Result.** There is an exact modular characterization of stable integral tensor equivalence. It implies ordinary equivalence whenever the common torsion exponent is 1, 2, 3, 4, or 6. Cancellation fails already with 5-torsion and one new free direction. The proof is written. The finite witness can be checked separately.

Let T and T' belong to tensor products of corresponding finitely generated abelian groups M_i and M'_i of the same isomorphism types. A stabilized factor is M_i ⊕ Z^{r_i}, with tensor image under the natural inclusion. Let D be a common torsion exponent. Put W_i equal to the inverse image in M_i of the saturation of the rational support of T in M_i/torsion. The rational support is the span of all contractions in that factor of the rational tensor. Write M_i = W_i ⊕ C_i, where C_i ≅ Z^{c_i}, and similarly for T'. The decomposition exists because the saturated lattice has a free quotient. This also works when the rational tensor is zero.

Two data determine T: its image T_free in the tensor product of the free quotients, and its image T_D in the tensor product of the M_i/D M_i. Indeed, choose free–torsion splittings. The full tensor product is the sum of its free-only block and the tensor blocks with a torsion factor. The latter are killed by D and inject into its reduction modulo D. Tensor product commutes with this reduction. An isomorphism maps T to T' exactly when it maps both data.

For D > 1 consider the following criterion. Choose isomorphisms f_i: W_i → W'_i inducing a rational support equivalence (the tensor T_free lies in the product of the saturated supports). Also choose isomorphisms h_i: M_i/D M_i → M'_i/D M'_i carrying T_D to T'_D and restricting to f_i modulo D on the W_i summands. On the quotients C_i/D C_i, allow any unit determinant if r_i > 0, and require the determinant to be ±1 modulo D if r_i = 0. For c_i = 0 the determinant is 1. Then an equivalence after adding the prescribed free slots exists if and only if such data exist. Taking all r_i = 0 characterizes ordinary equivalence. When D = 1 the criterion only requires the rational support equivalence induced by the integral maps on W_i.

For sufficiency choose integral lifts of h_i. The block on W_i is f_i. The block from C_i into W'_i has arbitrary lifted entries, including the specified torsion entries. For r_i = 0, an invertible matrix over Z/D of determinant ±1 lifts to a unimodular integer matrix. For r_i > 0, append one diagonal entry equal to the inverse determinant modulo D and identities on the other slots. This enlarged block has determinant 1 modulo D and lifts to a unimodular integer matrix. The elementary lifting fact follows by elimination over each local prime-power component of Z/D, where a unimodular column has a unit entry. Elementary transvections, and pairs of diagonal inverse units written as elementary products, generate the special linear group there. Apply the Chinese remainder theorem to the transvection coefficients and lift to Z. Rank 1 and 0 satisfy the same assertion directly. On the stabilized modules these lifts match both tensor data and hence the integral tensors.

For necessity an integral stabilized transporter g_i acts as some isomorphism f_i on W_i onto W'_i, since rational supports are intrinsic and all torsion is intrinsic. When r_i = 0, use its actual reduction as h_i. For a stabilized factor let x_i and y_i be reduction modulo D of g_i and g_i^{-1}, respectively, restricted to the original summands and projected to the original summands in the target. In an unstabilized factor take the same definitions without any projection. The tuples x_i and y_i carry T_D to T'_D and back because the tensors have zero coordinates in the added summands. Moreover, x_i and y_i restrict to f_i and f_i^{-1} on W_i and W'_i modulo D. Let E_i and E'_i be the eventual images of y_i x_i and x_i y_i on the original reduced factors. These contain the corresponding entire reduced W_i and W'_i. Fitting decomposition over a finite module gives a direct-sum complement K_i and K'_i from the eventual kernels. Intertwining by x_i gives an isomorphism E_i → E'_i, with inverse obtained using the inverse of y_i x_i on E_i. The original reduced modules have the same type, so K_i and K'_i are isomorphic by cancellation for finite abelian groups, with each group homomorphism automatically Z/D-linear. Extend x_i|E_i by any such isomorphism K_i → K'_i. The tensor T_D lies in the image of the tensor product of the E_i: powers of the y_i x_i fix T_D as a tuple, so applying a sufficiently large common power writes it in that image. Therefore the extensions carry T_D to T'_D. When r_i = 0, the projected maps are inverse and this extension is just the actual reduction of g_i. Thus the determinant conditions on those factors hold.

If every unit modulo D is ±1, every stable equivalence satisfies the criterion for no stabilization. The positive integers with this property are precisely 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6, by the formula for Euler's totient and the Chinese remainder theorem.

## Counterexample

Take M_1 = Z e_0 ⊕ Z e_1, M_2 = Z f_0 ⊕ (Z/5) f_1, and M_3 = Z g_0 ⊕ (Z/5) g_1. Write concatenations for tensor products. Put

    T  = e0 f0 g0 +   e1 f1 g0 +   e1 f0 g1 + e0 f1 g1
    T' = e0 f0 g0 + 2 e1 f1 g0 + 2 e1 f0 g1 + e0 f1 g1.

Any ordinary transporter has first factor e_0 ↦ a e_0, e_1 ↦ b e_0 + d e_1 where a,d=±1. The next factors are f_0 ↦ e f_0 + s f_1, f_1 ↦ v f_1 and g_0 ↦ f g_0 + t g_1, g_1 ↦ w g_1, with e,f=±1 and v,w nonzero modulo 5. There are no other forms of the transporter, because the free tensor forces the first rational support line and torsion is intrinsic. The free entry requires a e f = 1. The entries at e_1 f_1 g_0, e_1 f_0 g_1 give d v f = 2 and d e w = 2 in the residue field. The zero entries at e_0 f_1 g_0 and e_0 f_0 g_1 give s = −b v/a and t = −b w/a. The zero at e_1 f_1 g_1 gives d(vt+sw)=0, hence b=0 modulo 5 and s=t=0, since 2 is nonzero. The remaining e_0 f_1 g_1 equation is a v w = 1. Multiplying the two equations with right side 2 now gives 2²=d² e f v w=1, impossible modulo 5.

After adjoining one free direction e_2 in the first mode, fix e_0 and act on (e_1,e_2) by the integer matrix [[2,5],[5,13]], whose determinant is 1 and whose reduction modulo 5 is diag(2,3). The other two mode maps are identities. This is an explicit stabilized transporter.

The rational support argument extends the ordinary free-mode cancellation in the project's functorial tensor reductions. The finite-module image argument uses Fitting decomposition and ordinary cancellation of finite modules.
