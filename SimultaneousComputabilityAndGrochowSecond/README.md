# Simultaneous computability and Grochow's questions, second pass

The simultaneous-computability viewpoint compares several probes of one object and retains actual witnesses when they matter. Applied to isomorphism, it distinguishes scalar-extension decisions, refutations of negative decisions, and reconstruction of a base-field isomorphism. This briefing continues the [first pass](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/990ed5ac8b956f82c10370d60b7bd54aa4ad5568/SimultaneousComputabilityAndGrochow/README.md) and audits its priority claims.

## Results to show Grochow

1. **Exact Polynomial Calculus degree for odd unipotent block length in characteristic two.** Let two nonsingular matrices over $\mathbb F_q$ be congruent over an algebraic closure and noncongruent over $\mathbb F_q$. Suppose their asymmetries are unipotent with one common Jordan block length on both sides. With field equations in the original variables, the refutation degree is exactly $\max(3,q)$ for odd block length and lies between $\max(3,q)$ and $2q$ for even block length. Expanded size is polynomial at fixed $q$. Exactly the even-degree extensions admit congruence. The proof reduces a whole pure Jordan chain to length two or three and converts the surviving Artin–Schreier invariant into a low-degree refutation. This settles the degree in one arithmetic class of Question 7.3. [Proof](PCFallout.md) · [Galesi–Grochow–Pitassi–She, Question 7.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S7.SS3).

2. **Complete classification of joint TI densities at primes.** For a finite list of fixed integral tensor pairs, the distribution of yes/no patterns across primes can be any rational probability distribution whose support has a greatest pattern, and every distribution satisfies that condition. The construction can prescribe supported patterns at any finite list of primes, while making every rational comparison negative. For arbitrary number-field tensors, one finite Galois action on geometric transporter components simultaneously determines the answer at every good prime and every residue-extension degree. [Proof](ModularProfiles.md) · [Grochow–Qiao, journal Question 7.6](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10494304).

3. **Deterministic descent from witnesses that disagree on the center.** For central simple multiplication tensors with an explicitly presented common Galois center, extension witnesses generate cosets of possible center maps prime by prime. A point in the intersection gives a base witness deterministically in polynomially many field operations on the supplied inputs. The criterion holds under the previous sharp degree-list guarantee and applies to more inputs because it uses the witnesses themselves. Balanced tensor products and Euclidean cancellation of semisimple modules let the algorithm avoid constructing probe composita. The manuscript includes the determinant-one version in the stipulated bases. [Proof](WitnessFallout.md) · [Grochow–Qiao, combined preprint, Question 10.6](https://arxiv.org/html/1907.00309#S10.SS2).

4. **TI can change while every scalar-extension rank agrees, already at format $2\times4\times4$.** The explicit pencils over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$ agree at every completion, are nonisomorphic over the base, and have equal tensor rank over every extension. Side four is optimal among simple regular square pencils. Other pencils in the same format realize any prescribed quadratic entry field over any number field while preserving the entire rank profile. In the separate finite étale multiplication class, the full rank profile detects exactly the cyclic Burnside marks. [Proof](ArithmeticFallout.md).

## Priority and verification

The first pass overstated two novelty claims. The earlier portfolio already contains the $n^{\Theta(n)}$ odd-field sparse bound, and it already had the finite-field Lang criterion and the all-spectra construction over $\mathbb F_3$. The first pass supplied an independent hard-family proof, the labelled dyadic extension taxonomy, and the sharp minimum side for one divisibility cone in the finite étale class. The new component theorem gives simultaneous good reduction for arbitrary number-field tensors, and the joint density classification addresses a different realization problem.
