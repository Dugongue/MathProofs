# A single nonabelian chief factor for graph-induced coset intersection

## GP-0494

Coset intersection is graph-isomorphism hard already when the group generated by the two input subgroups is a full symmetric group. In particular it is hard at nonabelian chief multiplicity one. On the explicit class used below, where one subgroup is induced by vertex permutations on pairs and the other is a full partition stabilizer of the pairs, the problem is polynomial-time equivalent to graph isomorphism. Both reductions act on permutation generators and specified cosets.

Given graphs $X,Y$ on $n\ge5$ vertices with equal edge count, work on the $\binom n2$ unordered vertex pairs. Let $H$ be the group induced there by $S_n$. Let $K$ permute the edge set of $X$ arbitrarily and its complement arbitrarily, independently. Choose any permutation $t$ of the pair set mapping edges of $X$ onto edges of $Y$ and nonedges onto nonedges. The left coset $tK$ is exactly the set of permutations of pairs effecting this color change. Thus $H\cap tK$ is nonempty if and only if the two graphs are isomorphic. Construct generators using the usual transpositions in each of the indicated sets and the vertex generators for $S_n$. Unequal edge counts can be rejected directly. The finitely many small orders can be padded by a common number of isolated vertices without changing isomorphism. If a fixed negative instance is needed, use, at order five, two graphs of equal edge count with differing degree multisets.

It remains to verify the generated group, since the parameter is measured there. At least one block of the edge/nonedge partition has size at least two, so $K$ contains a transposition swapping two distinct pairs. The orbit of those two pair-vertices under $H$ gives all edges of one of two ordinary graphs on the pair-vertices, according to whether the two pairs intersect. Both graphs are connected for $n\ge5$. For intersecting pairs adjacency is the usual one-coordinate change, and disjoint pairs can be joined through an intersecting pair. For disjointness adjacency, two pairs that share a vertex can be joined through a pair outside their union, since they use only three vertices. The group generated by $H$ and $K$ contains the transpositions along every edge of this connected graph by conjugation. Edge transpositions on a connected graph generate the full symmetric group. Hence the only nonabelian chief factor of the generated group is its alternating subgroup, of multiplicity one.

For the upper reduction on the stated class, a full partition stabilizer $K$ and coset representative $t$ define two colored complete graphs on the underlying vertex domain. One assigns each pair its original partition class, and the other gives the same colors to the transported partition sets. Intersection with $H$ is precisely color-preserving isomorphism of those graphs. This reduces to ordinary graph isomorphism by first using vertex and edge incidence vertices with the partition labels as colors, then replacing vertex colors by distinct rooted tags of polynomial size. For example attach to every original incidence vertex two private cliques of sizes chosen distinctly by its color and larger than the incidence-graph order. Each clique shares only its root with the incidence graph. Their roots are exactly the vertices lying in two such large maximal cliques, so each original type and incidence edge can be recovered from the resulting uncolored graph. This proves the exact comparison for the displayed class. An algorithm fixed-parameter tractable in chief multiplicity on general inputs would, in particular, give a polynomial-time graph-isomorphism algorithm.
