# The sharp signed local-genus bound for abelian Galois fields

**Theorem.** Fix a number field \(F\) and an integer \(n=\prod_p p^{k_p}\). Among multiplication tensors of abelian Galois fields \(E/F\) of degree \(n\), the maximum size of the signed local genus within the fixed ordinary orbit is
\[
2^{\epsilon(n)}\prod_p p^{k_p(k_p-1)},\qquad
\epsilon(n)=
\begin{cases}
1&4\mid n,\ \text{or }n\text{ is odd and divisible by a prime square},\\
0&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
The maximum is attained even with all decomposition subgroups cyclic. In particular side \(2^d\) supports \(2^{1+d(d-1)}\) signed classes in one such local genus for all \(d\ge2\).

**Proof.** Let \(G=\operatorname{Gal}(E/F)\). Tate's norm theorem identifies the norm quotient \(\mathfrak K(E/F)\) with the kernel of restriction from \(H^3(G,\mathbb Z)\) to the product of \(H^3(D_v,\mathbb Z)\), with \(D_v\) the decomposition groups. For finite abelian \(G\), \(H^3(G,\mathbb Z)\) is dual to \(\bigwedge^2 G\). Thus the order of the norm quotient is bounded by \(|\bigwedge^2G|\), with equality for locally cyclic extensions. [Frei–Loughran–Newton, Theorem 6.1 and Lemma 6.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1508.02518).

The \(p\)-primary cyclic factors of \(G\) have orders \(p^{e_1},\ldots,p^{e_t}\) with \(\sum_i e_i=k_p\). Their exterior-square contribution has logarithm to base \(p\) equal to \(\sum_{i<j}\min(e_i,e_j)\), since the exterior square of a cyclic group vanishes and \(\mathbb Z/p^{e_i}\otimes\mathbb Z/p^{e_j}\cong\mathbb Z/p^{\min(e_i,e_j)}\). This sum is at most \(\binom{k_p}{2}\), by comparing each summand to \(e_i e_j\) and summing. Equality is attained when all \(e_i=1\).

The only possible extra factor in formula (1) of the [determinant calculation](determinants.md) is two, from sign. The automorphisms act as regular translations. The sign of a translation of order \(m\) is \((-1)^{(m-1)n/m}\). If \(v_2(n)=1\), an involution gives a global odd automorphism. If \(n\) is odd and squarefree, \(G\) is cyclic and there is an unramified place with Frobenius generating \(G\) by Chebotarev. There the embedding permutation is an odd-length cycle, whose centralizer is generated by the cycle, so every element has sign \(+1\). Thus the sign factor is trivial in both cases.

For attainment use \(G=\prod_p C_p^{k_p}\) and choose a locally cyclic realization over \(F\). [Frei–Loughran–Newton, Proposition 5.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1508.02518). When \(4\mid n\), every even-order translation in this group has order with 2-adic valuation one, so \(n/m\) is even and every global translation is even. When \(n\) is odd and has a square prime factor, translations are also all even and \(G\) is noncyclic. Locally the embedding action is a union of regular \(D_v\)-sets, and their commuting permutations give the local algebra automorphisms. If \(|D_v|\) is even, rotate one even-length orbit. If \(|D_v|\) is odd and \(D_v\) is proper, exchange two of its orbits pointwise via a \(D_v\)-equivariant matching. The resulting permutations are odd. For the two cases where \(\epsilon(n)=1\), an odd-order \(D_v\) is always proper. This gives the claimed sign factor alongside equality for the exterior square. The earlier biquadratic count of eight is the \(n=4\) case. [Earlier biquadratic example](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/TISLWorlds500/Arithmetic.md).



**Equality criterion.** Equality holds exactly when every Sylow subgroup of \(G\) is elementary abelian and all local decomposition groups are cyclic. Indeed \(\binom{k_p}{2}=\sum_{i<j}e_i e_j+\sum_i\binom{e_i}{2}\), so an \(e_i>1\) forces a strict loss in the norm bound. For elementary abelian Sylow subgroups, each inclusion of the \(p\)-primary part of \(D_v\) into that of \(G\) is inclusion of vector spaces. It induces an injection on exterior squares. If \(D_v\) is noncyclic, one such exterior square is nonzero and the dual restriction on \(H^3\) is nonzero, so the norm quotient is strictly smaller. The previous attainment proof gives both factors of the upper bound whenever the stated conditions hold.
