# Group isomorphism search, counting, and epimorphisms

These are AI-assisted informal proofs in the Cayley-table model.

| Proof | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Search and counting for all finite p-groups](AllPGroupsSearchCounting.md) | Deterministic polynomial-time reductions for every finite $p$-group, with queries staying at the same prime. Includes automorphism generators and applies prime by prime to finite nilpotent groups. |
| [Same-prime marking](SamePrimePGroupMarkers.md) | An unmarked $p$-group of polynomial order encodes prescribed independent covector lines in an arbitrary $p$-group's Frattini quotient. |
| [Frattini-class-two search and counting](FrattiniClassTwoSearchCounting.md) | The query groups retain Frattini class at most two, including at $p=2$. |
| [Epimorphisms onto Fitting-free groups](EpimorphismsToFittingFreeGroups.md) | Open Question 3(1). Arbitrary source and Fitting-free target. Polynomial-time decision, search, and counting. Decision also lies in logspace-uniform $\mathsf{AC}^{3}$. |
| [Epimorphisms from odd generalized Heisenberg quotients](HeisenbergQuotientEpimorphisms.md) | The cited genus-one branch of Open Question 3(3). Polynomial-time decision, search, and counting from raw tables, with arbitrary target. |
| [Epimorphisms onto groups of bounded order](ConstantTargetEpimorphisms.md) | The constant-size clause of Open Question 3(2). Deterministic logspace decision and logspace transducers for search and counting for each fixed target-order bound. |

The results leave the corresponding questions for arbitrary finite groups, bounded-generator targets, and genus-two pairs open.
