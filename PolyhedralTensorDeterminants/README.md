# Geometry and determinant-one tensor isomorphism

Four constructions use triangle ratios, polygon holonomy, polyhedral face incidences, and surface homology to compute actual determinant images of multiplication tensors over finite commutative local rings.

| Result | Main conclusion |
|---|---|
| [A surface Bockstein controls determinants](D.md) | For any regular closed surface map over the dual numbers over $\mathbb F_2$, the ordinary multiplication orbit splits into two determinant-one orbits for an orientable surface and one for a nonorientable surface. Exact formulas also cover the hemi-icosahedron and triangular and hexagonal tori. |
| [Ceva ratios compose tensor-isomorphism tests](A.md) | The three side ratios of a triangle give one supplied-transporter instance that succeeds exactly when at least two of three ordinary TI instances succeed. Graphs of sides extend this to monotone formulas and give exact determinant images and witness extraction. |
| [Hexagon holonomy and square classes](B.md) | Weighted alternating polygons have an exact ordinary and determinant-one classification. The hexagon is the first even polygon in this construction that can split an ordinary orbit. |
| [Dodecahedral diamonds and Platonic counts](C.md) | Commuting diamonds change the exact dodecahedral determinant image from cube powers to square powers times the antipodal sign. The five Platonic incidence algebras have explicit orbit counts. |

The articles give the written proofs. Lean sources check component identities and finite controls check the constructions in explicit cases. [Audit](Audit.json)
