# Relator bounds for mixed-characteristic extension data

## GP-0417

Let $Q$ be a finite group, $P$ a $p$-Sylow subgroup, and $A$ a finite $Q$-module whose additive group is an abelian $p$-group of exponent at most $p^e$ and generator rank $d$. If $P$ has a presentation with $r$ relators, then $H^2(Q,A)$ has at most $rd$ nontrivial invariant factors, each of order at most $p^{\min(e,v_p(|Q|))}$. In particular, the number is at most $d$ for cyclic $P$ and at most $3d$ for dihedral, generalized quaternion and semidihedral $P$. For general $P$, writing $m=\log_p|P|$, one can take $r\le m(m+1)/2$. These are bounds on the finite abelian group of extension classes for an arbitrary prescribed action, and no bound on the total dimension of the full cochain space is required.

### Bound by the number of relators

For $P=\langle s_1,\ldots,s_q\mid w_1,\ldots,w_r\rangle$, consider $A$-by-$P$ extensions inducing the prescribed action. Choose a lift for each $s_i$; then evaluating $w_j$ yields a defect element of $A$. Changing the lifts by elements of $A$ changes the tuple of defects by the image of an additive homomorphism $D:A^q\to A^r$. To see additivity, rewrite each word using the lifts and carry all corrections to the left, conjugating them by the preceding quotient prefixes. Each correction contributes a fixed action matrix applied with sign determined by whether that generator or its inverse appears. Thus the defect tuple modulo $\operatorname{im}D$ is well-defined.

This map $H^2(P,A)\to A^r/\operatorname{im}D$ is additive by the fiber-product definition of Baer sum: evaluate the relators on paired lifts and use addition on the two kernel coordinates. It is injective. If two defect tuples agree modulo $\operatorname{im}D$, first change lifts to make them identical. Now build the common presentation adjoining the generators of $A$, their abelian relations, the $q$ specified actions on these generators, and the $r$ relator equalities to the common defects. The presented subgroup from $A$ is normal, and quotienting by it gives exactly $P$. The presented group has order at most $|A||P|$ and maps onto both given extensions, each of that order. Both maps are isomorphisms respecting the kernel and quotient.

A subgroup of a finite abelian $p$-group with generator rank at most $rd$ has generator rank at most $rd$, for example by comparing order-$p$ subgroups. This proves the rank and exponent bounds $rd$ and $p^e$ for $P$. For $Q$, the standard restriction-corestriction identity is multiplication by $[Q:P]$, a unit on these $p$-primary cohomology groups, so restriction to $P$ is injective. Restriction to the trivial subgroup is zero in degree two, and transfer from it shows that group order annihilates the cohomology. Applying this to $P$ gives the further exponent bound $p^m$.

The named tame Sylow families have presentations with at most three relators. In general a polycyclic sequence along a composition series of $P$ has $m$ factors of order $p$. Use one power relation per generator and one ordered conjugation relation per pair. Collection gives at most $p^m$ words in ordered exponent form, and the presentation maps onto $P$ of that order. This proves the stated general relator count.

This puts a bound $rd\min(e,m)\log_2 p$ on the bits needed to list invariant-factor orders and on one full element in such coordinates. Obtaining those coordinates for a succinct presentation is a separate algorithmic question. The result complements the portfolio's treatment of fixed quotient action and extension-lifting linear relations.
