# TI equals local commutative Gorenstein algebra isomorphism at radical depth four

Date: 2026-09-13. Explicit sufficient family equivalences; no web search or Lean formalization. No claim of novelty or a strict GI/TI separation.

## 1. The all-field theorem

Over EVERY field K, ordinary TI is polynomial-time equivalent to isomorphism of commutative split-local Gorenstein algebras A with

    (rad A)^4=0.

Hardness already holds on STANDARD GRADED algebras with Hilbert function

    (1,D,D,1).

No primitive idempotents, grading, distinguished ideal, symmetrizing functional, or transporter are marked in the output isomorphism problem. The reverse reduction uses the ordinary multiplication tensor: isotopy of unital associative algebras is equivalent to algebra isomorphism.

The forward reduction starts from homogeneous quadratic-space equivalence, which is proved TI-equivalent over every field in the [quadratic-space equivalence proof](QuadraticCarrier.md). The new construction below removes the distinguished half of a symmetric trivial extension by an intrinsic rank cutoff. It is an actual polynomial-size compiler, not an assumed recovery gadget.

For H subset Sym^2(V), with dim V=n and dim H=r, the output has

    N=2n+1,
    w=N(N+1)/2-r,
    D=N+w,
    dim A=2D+2=(N+1)(N+2)-2r.

All structure constants are obtained by linear algebra and multiplication of degree-two monomials. No roots or field extensions are used.

## 2. Padding gives a uniform multiplication-rank gap

Take Z of dimension n+1 and put U=V direct-sum Z, of dimension N. Regard H as a subspace of Sym^2(U), supported in V. Set

    W=Sym^2(U)/H,
    beta(u,v)=class(u v) in W.

For u in U let B_u:U->W be v |-> beta(u,v). For EVERY nonzero u,

    rank B_u >= n+1 > N/2.

If u lies in V, the subspace uZ has dimension n+1 and intersects Sym^2(V), hence H, trivially. If u has a nonzero Z component, B_u is injective. To see the latter, choose a Z basis in which that component is z_1, so u=v+z_1. A product u x contained in Sym^2(V) must have zero z_1^2 and z_1 z_j coefficients, forcing all Z coefficients of x to vanish; its remaining z_1 V term then forces x=0. This is a formal polynomial argument valid also in characteristic two.

In particular beta has zero annihilator on U and its image spans W.

## 3. The standard graded Gorenstein algebra

Define the commutative local algebra

    C=K direct-sum U direct-sum W,

where the only nonzero radical products are u v=beta(u,v). Then rad(C)^3=0 and soc(C)=W. Form its symmetric trivial extension

    A=C direct-sum C*,
    (c,f)(d,g)=(cd,cg+fd).

Because C is commutative, A is commutative. The functional lambda(c,f)=f(1) has symmetric nondegenerate pairing lambda(ab), so A is Frobenius; being split-local and commutative, it is Gorenstein.

Writing Y=W* and identifying the dual pieces by degree, A has the explicit grading

    A_0=K,
    A_1=U direct-sum Y,
    A_2=W direct-sum U*,
    A_3=K*s.

The products, in addition to the unit, are

    u v=beta(u,v),
    u eta = [v |-> eta(beta(u,v))] in U*,
    u phi=phi(u)s,
    eta w=eta(w)s.

All products not forced by these identities and commutativity vanish. Associativity is precisely the identity eta(beta(u,v)) obtained by the two bracketings of u v eta.

Surjectivity and zero annihilator of beta imply that A is generated by A_1. Its radical powers are A_1+A_2+A_3, A_2+A_3, A_3,0; thus the Hilbert function is (1,D,D,1). This proves all output promises explicitly, including the one-dimensional socle.

## 4. Intrinsic recovery of the forgotten half

Let R=rad A. Both L=R/R^2 and B=R^2/R^3 are intrinsic. For ell in L, let

    mu_ell:L->B,    x |-> ell x.

In the constructed coordinates L=U direct-sum Y and B=W direct-sum U*, write ell=(u,eta). Its multiplication matrix is

    [ B_u        0   ]
    [ B_eta    B_u^t ],

where B_eta(v)(x)=eta(beta(v,x)). If u=0, this matrix has rank at most N. If u!=0, its rank is at least 2 rank B_u: the Y inputs supply rank B_u inside U*, while the images of U inputs supply another rank B_u after projection to W. Section 2 therefore gives rank mu_ell>N.

Consequently the subset

    {ell in L : rank mu_ell <= N}

is EXACTLY the linear subspace Y. This rank characterization is invariant under every unmarked algebra isomorphism. The reduction does not solve equations for this locus: it writes the algebra table, and the proved characterization guarantees soundness of every possible output isomorphism.

Multiplication induces the perfect pairing

    L times B -> R^3.

Define Ann(Y) inside B to be the annihilator of Y under this pairing. In the construction it is exactly U*. Thus both quotients

    L/Y=U,    B/Ann(Y)=W

are intrinsic. Since YL is contained in Ann(Y), the multiplication L times L->B induces precisely

    beta:Sym^2(U)->W

on these quotients. Its kernel is the padded original subspace H. We have recovered the original quadratic-space data, not just its dimensions or a necessary invariant.

Therefore A(H) isomorphic to A(H') implies GL equivalence of the padded H,H'. Conversely a GL equivalence of padded spaces induces C(H) isomorphic to C(H') and extends contragrediently to their trivial extensions, giving A(H) isomorphic to A(H'). All maps are over the same field.

## 5. Padding cancels over every field

For arbitrary subspaces E,F of a vector space, one has

    Sym^2(E) intersect Sym^2(F)=Sym^2(E intersect F).

Choose a basis adapted to E intersect F and extend separately to E,F; the degree-two monomial bases prove the identity in every characteristic. Hence every quadratic space H has a unique smallest supporting variable subspace supp(H), obtained as the intersection of all E with H subset Sym^2(E).

This support is intrinsic under linear changes of variables. If an isomorphism U->U' carries the padded H to H', it carries supp(H) to supp(H'). These supports lie in the original V,V'. Because the original formats have dim V=dim V', the restriction between supports extends to a linear isomorphism V->V'. That extension carries H to H'. Conversely any original isomorphism extends by an arbitrary isomorphism of the padding spaces.

No algorithm for finding supp(H) is assumed or required. This is an existence-and-recovery proof for a polynomial-time many-one reduction. Input format discrepancies are rejected before construction, so the common rank threshold N is fixed on each output pair.

Combining Sections 2-5 yields, over every field,

    quadratic-space GL equivalence
      iff A(H) isomorphic to A(H').

Together with the existing quadratic-space TI equivalence and the multiplication-tensor upper bound, this proves the theorem in Section 1 for the ENTIRE stated Gorenstein algebra-isomorphism family.

## 6. A single-cubic consequence, including characteristic three

On L=U direct-sum Y define the homogeneous cubic

    F_H(u,eta)=eta(beta(u,u)).

If char K!=2, its full third polarization is twice the symmetric trilinear multiplication form

    tau((u,eta),(v,theta),(w,kappa))
      =eta(beta(v,w))+theta(beta(u,w))+kappa(beta(u,v)).

This identity is valid in characteristic three as well. Although polarization does not reconstruct an arbitrary cubic in characteristic three, reconstruction is NOT needed for the soundness direction: an equivalence of F_H and F_H' induces equivalence, up to a scalar, of tau and tau', hence an isomorphism of the associated standard graded Gorenstein algebras, and therefore equivalence of H,H'. The converse is direct: a map of U and the induced dual map of W preserve the displayed cubic.

To spell out the trilinear-to-algebra step, for a concise symmetric trilinear form tau on L use K direct-sum L direct-sum L* direct-sum K*s, with x y=tau(x,y,-) and x phi=phi(x)s. A linear equivalence of tau up to a nonzero scalar extends by the corresponding dual and socle scalings to an algebra isomorphism. For the present tau this is exactly A(H).

Overall scalar multiples introduce no new orbits on this family: the explicit change eta |-> c eta sends F_H to c F_H for every c in K*. Hence exact GL cubic equivalence and projective GL cubic equivalence coincide on the whole GL saturation of the constructed cubic family.

Thus, in every characteristic other than two, TI is equivalent to exact equivalence of the displayed SINGLE homogeneous cubic forms; the same holds for projective equivalence. For the reverse reduction on this constructed family, polarization followed by the preceding algebra table and its multiplication tensor suffices, including in characteristic three. The general upper bound for arbitrary cubic forms can instead use the finite symmetric-algebra tensor-system encoding; it must not discard pure cubes through polarization in characteristic three.

The algebra theorem in Section 1 retains characteristics two and three without qualification. No characteristic-two cubic-form conclusion is inferred from this polarization argument.

## 7. Complexity and scope

N is linear in the original variable dimension and w,D are quadratic. Quotient bases, beta, the evaluation pairings, and every multiplication coefficient are computed by polynomial-size exact linear algebra. The output is a single unmarked associative multiplication tensor of polynomial size. Finite-field operations have polynomial dependence on log|K|; rational bit sizes are polynomial; other fields use the exact field-arithmetic model.

The rank cutoff and support cancellation are proved intrinsic properties, not algorithmic oracles. They establish both directions for arbitrary output isomorphisms.
