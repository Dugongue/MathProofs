# The Frobenius candidate absorbs ordinary tensor isomorphism

Write $C_{\mathrm F}$ for the common L5 and L7 degree over $\mathbb F_3$. On the active branch of L5 the problem tests the first determinant on a comparison of raw multiplication tensors of commutative local algebras with scalar residue and one-dimensional socle. Its default branch is ordinary TI, denoted $O$.

**Theorem.** An independent $C_{\mathrm F}$ comparison and an arbitrary ordinary TI comparison reduce to one $C_{\mathrm F}$ comparison in deterministic polynomial time. Thus $C_{\mathrm F}\wedge O\equiv_m^p C_{\mathrm F}$. This also absorbs a polynomial list of ordinary comparisons after ordinary conjunction packing.

## Ordinary TI data in a graded auxiliary algebra

The prior [ordinary Gorenstein encoding](GorensteinCarrier.md) sends ordinary TI to standard graded commutative Gorenstein algebras $E$ of Hilbert function $(1,D,D,1)$, via quadratic spaces and the symmetric trivial extension of a cube-zero algebra. The integer $D$ can be made even. In the construction with padded variable dimension $b$ and relation dimension $h$, one has $D=b+b(b+1)/2-h$. Choose $b$ larger than twice the old variable dimension and in a residue class modulo four making $D$ even. The rank cutoff only needs the padding dimension to exceed half of $b$. Every positive comparison has a graded isomorphism. Its socle scale can be chosen to be either sign by scaling the square-zero dual ideal of the target extension.

Take $P=E\otimes_F F[t]/(t^m)$, with $m>4$ odd, and write $s=m+2$ for its odd socle degree. The tensor product has perfect complementary-grade pairings and is generated in degree one. In its graded tangent the locus $x^4=0$ is exactly $E_1$: projecting away the positive part of $E$ detects any nonzero $t$ coefficient. The graded algebra generated by that intrinsic tangent subspace recovers $E$. Since these algebras are standard graded, every ungraded isomorphism induces a graded one. Therefore $P,P'$ compare ordinarily exactly when the original ordinary pair does, and if so with either socle scale.

Here $\dim P/2=(D+1)m$ is odd. Choose paired grade bases relative to the socle generator at each endpoint. For any isomorphism of socle scale $\lambda$, the two determinants in complementary grades multiply to $\lambda^{\dim P_j}$ on a pair of grades $j,s-j$. Thus the determinant on all positive pretop grades is $\lambda^{\dim P/2-1}=1$ in the chosen bases.

## Combine the signed algebra and the auxiliary comparison

Recover the actual algebras $R,R'$ from an active L5 input by the prior multiplication-tensor recognizer, retaining the raw first-mode calibrations. The admissible values of $\dim R$ and last nonzero radical degree $d$ must match. For the construction suppose $\dim R$ is even and $d\ge2$. Choose $m$ as above with $s>2d+2$. Form $G=R\#P$ by taking the fiber product over the residue and identifying the two chosen socle generators. Cross-products of the radicals vanish. Its socle is one-dimensional: multiplication by each branch radical tests the component in that branch, so an element killing the full radical must lie in the common socle. Its dimension is $\dim R+\dim P-2$, even. See [Ananthnarayan, Avramov and Moore](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.1304).

Put $k=d+1$ and $\ell=s-d$. In $G$, the power $J_G^k$ is wholly in $P$ and its annihilator is $J_R+J_P^\ell$. This follows from perfect pairings of $P$, which give $\operatorname{Ann}_P J_P^k=J_P^{s-k+1}$. This annihilator identifies the old $R$ tangent in $J_G/J_G^2$. Given an isomorphism $\Phi:G\to G'$, project $\Phi|_R$ onto $R'$ assigning the shared socle to $R'$. Its only discarded terms lie in $J_{P'}^\ell$, which squares to zero since $2\ell>s$. Therefore the projection $g:R\to R'$ is multiplicative. It induces an invertible tangent map, hence is an isomorphism by nilpotence and the fact that tangent lifts generate the algebra. Its socle scale is that of $\Phi$.

To recover the other branch, project $\Phi|_P$ onto $P'$ assigning the shared socle to $P'$, and denote the linear map by $h$. For $x\in J_P$ write $\Phi(x)=h(x)+r_x$, with $r_x$ in a complement of the socle in the old radical. Because $xJ_R=0$, products $r_x g(J_R)$ lie in the common socle. Hence $r_x$ annihilates $J_{R'}^2$. It follows that
$$
h(xy)-h(x)h(y)=r_xr_y
$$
is a socle-valued symmetric bilinear defect, zero if either argument is in $J_P^2$. In that case the corresponding old component lies in $J_{R'}^2$ modulo socle by preservation of $J_G^2$. The tangent action of $h$ is invertible since it is the map induced on the quotient by the intrinsic old tangent.

Correct $h$ by a map from the tangent to degree $s-1$ of $P'$. Perfect pairing of degrees one and $s-1$ lets either new cross term equal half the defect. The correction vanishes on $J_P^2$ and its values annihilate $J_{P'}^2$ as well as each other. Thus the adjusted map is a unital isomorphism $\psi:P\to P'$, with the same induced actions on all graded pieces as $h$.

## Preserve the exact determinant

Use the ordered splitting $G=R\oplus P_{\mathrm{pre}}$, where the second summand consists of all positive pretop grades. The upper-left block of $\Phi$ is $g$. The lower-left block has image in pretop grades at least $\ell$ and vanishes on the socle. The upper-right block sends these high grades into the old socle, since it preserves radical powers and $\ell>d$. When eliminating $g$ by the Schur complement, the correction on the pretop block therefore maps into these high grades and vanishes on them. It is strictly filtration-increasing. The pretop block itself is triangular by grade, with the same diagonal actions as $\psi$. Its determinant is one by the paired-frame calculation. Thus $\det\Phi=\det g$ in the chosen frames.

Conversely, whenever the ordinary comparison is positive, choose the auxiliary witness $\psi$ with the same socle scale as any given $g:R\to R'$. The connected sum extends these two maps, with total determinant $\det g$. The full algebra isomorphisms give exactly the old relative determinant when the independent ordinary query succeeds, and none when it fails.

Calibrate $g$ against the raw first-mode frames. In both even-dimensional local algebras all multiplication by units is determinant neutral, by the common scalar residue on radical grades. An isotopy of unital multiplication tensors consists of an algebra isomorphism with left and right unit factors, by evaluating the isotopy at the unit. If the frame calibration calls for negative determinant, reflect one target coordinate in the first physical mode to convert it to the usual positive test. The input family is invariant under that rebase. If the original active dimension is odd, multiplication by the unit $-1$ makes the sign freely adjustable, so use ordinary conjunction. If $d=1$, then $R$ is the algebra of dual numbers, since $J$ equals the one-dimensional socle. Reflecting that radical line corrects the sign, so ordinary conjunction suffices here too. The default branch also uses ordinary conjunction followed by the known reduction from $O$ to $C_{\mathrm F}$. The reverse reduction appends a fixed ordinary yes instance.

All tables and calibration frames are found by polynomial-time linear algebra, with $m$ linear in a bound on $d$. This is an informal proof, and the unrestricted placement of $C_{\mathrm F}$ relative to ordinary and one-sign TI remains open.
