# Four crossovers from topology, character theory, and graph structure

These are informal proofs. The source searches and finite controls support discovery and review. Publication priority remains unestablished.

| Result | Transferred idea and conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| [Rational homotopy equivalence with fixed cup products](Geometry.md) | A Plücker relation places a rational three-tensor in a Bianchi–Massey invariant. The resulting rational homotopy problem on supplied tables is rational TI-complete even for smooth simply connected spin seven-manifolds with one fixed torsion-free integral cohomology ring per format, zero $p_1$, and trivial ordinary rational Massey products. |
| [Exact exponential invariant separation](Spectral.md) | Character balance on a sparse three-partite support turns a Fibonacci recurrence into a sharp separation-degree lower bound for single tripartite tensors. The exponential scale is tight for unitary comparisons with simple Gram spectra and also occurs for real orthogonal comparisons with double Gram eigenvalues. The construction gives the exact first distinction in Haar-scrambled copy moments. Computing or closely approximating least degree is NP-hard on the stated sign pairs. |
| [Orthogonal proof complexity and simple spectra](OrthogonalProofs.md) | A three-edge-colored expander gives spectrally simple real orthogonal nonisomorphism with linear Polynomial Calculus and Sum of Squares degree and polynomial sparse proof size. A sign-parity formula gives the exact separating degree for positive versus signed tensors, including NP-complete least-degree calculation with simple Gram spectra. The general real simple-spectrum class has a quadratic separating-degree upper bound. |
| [Schreier-tree lifting for semigroup minimum generation](Combinatorial.md) | Covering-graph normalization and the support of lifted permutation cycles solve the one-free-element problem at split primitive permutation-wreath chief layers. This extends exact polynomial-time Cayley-table minimum semigroup generation to classes containing unbounded products of simple factors with primitive permutation action. It gives a further restricted answer to [Collins–Grochow–Levet–Weiß Question 8.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.00133#S8). |

The orthogonal results exhibit a change from a quadratic upper bound at simple Gram spectra to exponential examples at multiplicity two. These bounds concern invariant degrees in the tensor coordinates. The Polynomial Calculus and Sum of Squares degrees refer to the ordinary change-of-basis equations.
