# Counting, scalar extension, and constructive tensor isomorphism

Four informal results. TI uses independent basis changes on the three tensor modes. SL-TI requires each determinant to be one. The arguments extend the earlier tensor port compiler, determinant-controlled local algebra, and commutative unit solver. [Previous proof packet](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/TensorIsomorphismComposition/Results.md)

## 1. A single stabilizer order encodes a batch of TI answers

Given polynomially many ordinary TI comparisons over a fixed finite field, one exact ordinary tensor stabilizer-order query recovers every verdict. Over an explicit finite commutative ring R of known cardinality at least two, one query even recovers the number of local factors accepting each comparison, without computing the decomposition of R. Thus for Z/NZ the reduction does not require factoring N. Its time outside the query is polynomial in the input size and log |R|, for ring arithmetic of polynomial bit cost.

Attach a pair (A_i,B_i) to a directed prime-length successor cycle, with A_i at zero and B_i elsewhere. A permissible cycle automorphism is either the identity, or any translation if A_i≅B_i. Give all cycles distinct prime lengths, so their successor components cannot be exchanged. Encode each successor edge by a tuple vertex and two position-colored subdivisions. Compile the decorated graph by the earlier triangular algebra construction. Use seven global colors for the ports, tuple vertices, two subdivision types and three private tensor roles. One rank-tagged arrow from a private source per vertex records its color. If D₀ is the maximum of one and the leaf side lengths, use ranks D₀+1 through D₀+7. The largest corner rank D therefore does not depend on any chosen cycle length. [Tensor port compiler](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/TensorIsomorphismComposition/Results.md) · [Grochow–Qiao, weighted path algebras](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.00309)

Fix a local factor R_h with residue field F_(q_h) and maximal ideal m_h. For its compiled algebra A_h, the off-diagonal ideal J_h is the commutator ideal and is cube-zero. The quotient is a product of copies of R_h, so an automorphism permutes the quotient vertex idempotents. Alignment of the actual idempotents is unique by conjugation in 1+J_h. Existence uses u=Σ_i Φ(e_i)e_(σ(i)), and uniqueness follows since a centralizer of all vertex idempotents is diagonal. For any allowed permutation, the aligned maps consist of arbitrary invertible maps on unused corners and the leaf TI transporters. Each leaf transporter is a torsor under its source stabilizer. Hence the number of automorphisms is the number of allowed cycle shifts times a common factor consisting of |1+J_h|, the free-corner GL orders, and the leaf stabilizer orders.

Use the multiplication tensor of A as the query. A unital associative autotopism has the unique form (L_aφ,R_bφ,L_aR_bφ), with units a,b and an algebra automorphism φ, by evaluating at the unit and dividing. This adds |A_h×|² in the local count. Each prime dividing the common factor, including the unit factors, divides q_h ∏_(d≤D)(q_h^d−1). Indeed units of A_h lift from (R_h)^v modulo J_h, and GL_d(R_h) has order |m_h|^(d²)|GL_d(F_(q_h))|. Leaf stabilizers are subgroups of products of such GL groups. Therefore for a cycle length ℓ outside that bad prime set the valuation of the stabilizer order at ℓ is exactly the verdict bit of its test.

Over a field, choose distinct primes avoiding the bad set, testing by modular powers. Over a finite ring, there are at most B=(1+D(D+1)/2)ceil(log₂|R|) distinct bad primes in the union over all local factors. This bound follows by taking the logarithm of the product of the bad integers and using ∏_h q_h≤|R|. Use 2B+1 globally distinct prime lengths for each test and for an always-yes calibration test. A polynomial number of polynomial-size primes is found by trial division. The full stabilizer is the product of the local stabilizers by the ring's Chinese remainder decomposition. At a good prime the valuation counts exactly the accepting local factors. Majority among the valuations for each test corrects the at most B bad primes. The calibration gives the number of local factors, and the original comparison holds exactly when all accept. Both the query and its order have polynomial bit lengths.

Over a field, a single query for a full stabilizer generating set instead recovers a witness for each positive test. The projection of an autotopism onto φ is a surjective homomorphism by the formula above. The induced translation group of a positive cycle is full, so at least one generator moves zero. Align the idempotents and read the maps on the three leaf corners to obtain the witness. These are reductions *to* stabilizer queries, a different direction from counting-to-decision. [Grochow–Qiao, Part IV, §7.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.16317#S7.SS4)

## 2. Exponential extension degree even with ordinary and two-sign witnesses

There are pairs of tensors over F₃ of side lengths O(k) that are geometrically ordinarily isomorphic and whose first extension with ordinary TI has degree 2^Ω(k). More generally we transform any ordinary TI pair into commutative associative multiplication tensors with supplied ordinary and all three two-mode determinant-one base witnesses, with linear side-length growth. Its exact SL extension spectrum is the original ordinary spectrum.

For the quantitative example, use algebras on e₀,…,e_k whose cross products vanish, with e_i²=e_(i+1) for i<k and e_k²=±e_k at the two endpoints. The iterated square-image spaces are the tail spans, so any isomorphism is triangular. Descending in i, once the later e_j map to t_j e_j, their orthogonality to e_i and their nonzero squares remove each later coefficient from the image of e_i. Hence the map is diagonal, with t_(i+1)=t_i² and t_k=−1. This requires a 2^k-th root of −1. Over F_(3^f) that is equivalent to v₂(3^f−1)≥k+1. The valuation is one for odd f and 2+v₂(f) for even f, by factoring odd powers and repeatedly squaring from 3²−1=8. The successful degrees are the multiples of 2^max(1,k−1) for k≥1. The field-uniform linear-length algebra isomorphism to ordinary TI construction uses fixed identity and Kronecker blocks, so scalar extension preserves this proof. [Grochow–Qiao, Part IV, Corollary 5.4, action 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.16317#S5.SS3)

For the general transformation, convert ordinary TI to symmetric matrix-space isometry and then to symmetric trilinear form equivalence using Grochow–Qiao's linear-length constructions. In their displayed form all monomials use a separate slice or auxiliary coordinate, so every all-three-equal basis coefficient vanishes. In characteristic three, symmetry implies F(v,v,v)=0 for every v since the other diagonal evaluation terms have multiplicity divisible by three. Trim the contraction kernel to obtain concise F, testing equality of the two kernel dimensions first. All rank and gadget operations commute with scalar extension. [Grochow–Qiao, Part IV, Theorem 6.1 and construction (11)](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.16317#S6)

From concise F on V build a radical V⊕V*⊕Ks, with vv'=F(v,v',−), vℓ=ℓ(v)s, and all other products zero. Triple products in V are associative by symmetry, and the other radical triples are zero. Glue its s to the socle coordinate s of the previous 15-dimensional local algebra B₀, with zero cross products and one common scalar unit. Call the result C(F). It is a commutative local algebra of odd dimension 15+2 dim V. Recall that B₀ has main basis 1,x,y,x²,xy,y²,x²y,xy²,s with x³=y³=x²y²=s and other degree-four products zero. Its three sidearm generators z,w₁,w₂ pair by the dot product times s, and have three products xz,yw₁,yw₂ with x,y. All radical triples involving the sidearms vanish. [The 15-dimensional algebra and its multiplication table](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/TensorIsomorphismComposition/Results.md)

Here is the extraction and determinant calculation. Let m be the radical of C(F), S=m⁴, W=m²/m³ and Y the radical of the S-valued product pairing on W. The quotient W/Y is the nondegenerate block x²,xy,y², and Y=Y₀⊕V*, with Y₀ the sidearm products. The image Z of m∩Ann(m²) in m/m² is exactly the sidearm generators. In fact nonzero V components pair nontrivially against V*, and nonzero x,y components multiply xy nontrivially modulo m⁴. In X=(m/m²)/Z the kernel of squaring into W/Y is V. The product X×Z→Y yields Y₀, and its rank-one and rank-two lines in X/V distinguish x and y. Mixed lines have rank three.

An isomorphism C(F)→C(G) thus scales x,y modulo V and Z by a,b, and acts on Z by O. Cubing kills higher corrections, the sidearms and the V term, the latter by F(v,v,v)=0. In x²y² the V part has fourth power zero and the sidearms give no radical triple terms. Thus a³=b³=a²b², so a=b=1 and s is fixed. The Gram relation gives det(O)²=1 and O appears again on Y₀ via multiplication by x,y. Triple products of V lifts recover F. Changes by sidearm lifts give zero and changes by m² annihilate V² modulo m³ by definition of Y. The pair product V×(Y/Y₀)→S similarly gives evaluation. Consequently the maps on V and Y/Y₀ are inverse dual, and all the remaining main graded factors have determinant one. This proves determinant one for every C(F) self-isomorphism over every extension and proves that C(F)≅C(G) exactly when F≅G. Any form isomorphism extends using the inverse dual and the identity on B₀.

Take D=C(F)×C(G). A block-preserving algebra automorphism has determinant one, and a swap exists exactly when the factors are isomorphic, giving determinant −1 by using mutually inverse maps on the odd-dimensional blocks. Reflect the multiplication tensor in its output using the involution S₀ acting as −1 on just the first factor. It multiplies by a central unit, so the reflected product is again a unital associative commutative multiplication, and (I,I,S₀) is an ordinary transporter. Before reflection, the unit formula implies det R=det P det Q/(det φ). Thus all three final determinants can be one exactly when det φ=−1. If that automorphism exists, multiplying on the first and second modes by units that act as −1 on an odd block supplies both corrections. With φ=id the same unit choices give final determinant triples (a,b,−ab), for a,b∈{±1}. Any two entries can be set to one explicitly, over the base field already. If the initial formats or contraction dimensions disagree, take two unequal-dimensional local C algebras, which cannot swap over any extension. This realizes the empty spectrum while retaining all the proper-subset witnesses. The construction leaves unrestricted SL-TI containment open.

## 3. A polynomial-time formed TI algorithm beyond cyclic asymmetry

Fix a finite field F_q. Compare tensors with nondegenerate bilinear forms supplied at both endpoints in each mode, with optional prescribed determinants. Let m_(f,s) be the multiplicity of elementary divisor f^s of a mode asymmetry S=B⁻¹B^T. Put

```text
R = Σ_modes Σ_(f,s with m_(f,s)≥2) deg(f) m_(f,s)^2.
```

Decision and exact transporter counting take deterministic q^R poly(L) time on an input of bit length L. In particular every multiplicity-free list of elementary divisors gives polynomial time, with no bound on the degrees of the irreducibles, the nilpotent layers, or the number of distinct powers of the same irreducible. The same bound gives transporter search with the finite-field extraction oracle in Section 4. This addresses a larger regime of the published question about genuinely asymmetric forms. [Chen–Grochow–Qiao–Tang–Zhang, Open Question 1.11](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2)

First reject unequal asymmetry similarity types, and use rational canonical form to align the two copies of each asymmetry. The residual maps belong to the unit group of its endomorphism algebra E. Keep the tensor and each endpoint form as components of one explicit linear representation W, using the contragredient on the form slots. The requirement is exactly to send the combined source vector to the target. Correct the prescribed determinant values by the known alignment maps.

The radical quotient of E is ∏_(f,s) Mat_(m_(f,s))(F_q[t]/f). Indeed maps between blocks of equal length are read mod f, whereas a map from length s to length t has image divisible by f^max(t−s,0), so a return through unequal lengths contributes zero modulo f. The kernel acts triangularly with zero diagonal blocks when ordered by length on each successive f-power layer. It is nilpotent, and the displayed quotient is semisimple, proving the assertion. Write U=1+rad E, taking the product over modes. The singleton residue factors have a multiplicative complement A consisting of block-diagonal coefficient-field units. In F_q[t]/f^s the coefficient field is the image of a sufficiently high q-power Frobenius, and it maps isomorphically to F_q[t]/f. Enumerate the units of just the repeated-type matrix factors, at most q^R choices, and use their block-diagonal coefficient-field lifts h. Every candidate linear map belongs to some U A h. The group A normalizes U, and the quotient residue determines all physical determinants since elements of U are unipotent.

### A flag adapted to the residue-field characters

Construct the F_q-algebra generated by the action of A on W without enumerating A. First treat each tensor slot's coefficient field as independently acting, even if the same variable occurs more than once, and split the resulting direct sum of tensor products of coefficient algebras into explicit field factors. The arities are at most three. On each factor K_t the actual A acts by a scalar character. Using the finite-field embedding synchronization in the earlier unit solver, its exponent signature has entries e_ti modulo |K_i×|, one for each variable i, obtained by summing signed Frobenius powers from the slots. Here K_i is that variable's native residue field. Negative exponents arise from duals.

The scalar character on K_t generates its unique subfield of degree d_t, where d_t is the least divisor of [K_t:F_q] with (q^d_t−1)e_ti=0 modulo |K_i×| for every i. Two character factors define the same component of F_q[A] precisely when their signatures differ by a common q-Frobenius power. For this, the maps from the group algebra onto their generated fields have maximal kernels, and equal kernels are equivalent to an F_q-isomorphism identifying the character values. Finite-field isomorphisms on the coherent copies are Frobenius powers. The distinct kernels are comaximal, so the image algebra is the product of the distinct subfields, embedded by the computed Frobenius identifications. Divisor lists and Frobenius exponents used here are bounded by explicit field degrees. This gives matrices for the idempotents and for a basis of each field component.

Since U is a p-group in characteristic p, its fixed space on each nonzero quotient of W is nonzero, by counting vector orbits modulo p. Since A normalizes U, that fixed space is invariant under the computed image algebra of A. Find it by linear equations, take a one-dimensional line over one simple coefficient field in it, then repeat on the quotient. Lifting in the corresponding isotypic component gives an adapted flag and scalar coordinates on its simple grades. U acts trivially on each grade, and A acts there by known field scalars.

### Canonicalizing the unipotent action

Process grades from the top down. The subgroup H fixing the already selected coordinates acts on the current coordinate by translations with an additive image. For the current orbit representative this image is computed from the images of generators of H, and the achievable values form an affine F_p-coset C=c+M in the grade field K. We use a deterministic selector equivariant under every scalar of K×. Select zero if present. Otherwise use the subspace polynomial P_M(X)=∏_(u∈M)(X−u) and ν(C)=P_M(c) raised to the inverse of the dim_(F_p) M-th iterate of the p-Frobenius. The sparse polynomial with only Frobenius-power monomials is computed by extending an F_p-basis, using

```text
P_(M+F_p b)(X) = P_M(X)^p − P_M(b)^(p−1) P_M(X).
```

This identity follows by multiplying over the p cosets of M. The value P_M(c) is independent of the representative and nonzero. Scaling C by t scales this value by t^(p^dim M), so ν(tC)=tν(C). Normalize C by ν(C), choose a representative of the normalized *coset* canonically by row reduction, and rescale. The result is equivariant.

Use a product of powers of generators to reach the chosen grade value, then replace H by the kernel of its additive translation map. This takes a sequence of F_p-coordinate kernels of index at most p, using Schreier generators at each stage. Keep lists polynomial by sifting in an explicit central refinement of the radical-power series of 1+rad E, whose factors have order p. More explicitly keep at most one pivot h_i per grade. Strip successive leading coordinates of each generator, inserting a new pivot if needed. Also sift powers h_i^p and commutators of all pivot pairs, restarting these checks whenever a pivot is installed. All inserted pivots belong to the subgroup, and at most dim_(F_p) rad E can be installed. When the checks pass the ordered pivot products form a subgroup: by descending induction h_i normalizes the subgroup generated by deeper pivots, and h_i^p lies in that subgroup. All input generators sift, so the pivot products exhaust the subgroup and give its order as p to the number of pivots. This supplies the polynomial kernel and order calculations. [Related unipotent stabilizer methods, Eick–O'Brien et al., §5.2](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~obrien/research/autpgroup.pdf)

Two U-equivalent vectors with a shared higher prefix have identical feasible cosets at the current grade, since the transporter belongs to the prefix stabilizer. Thus the selected vector C_U(x) depends only on the U-orbit. An equivariantly selected path for x scales to a selected path for ax, using that A normalizes U and the choice at each grade is scalar-equivariant. Therefore C_U(ax)=a C_U(x).

For each lift h, compute canonical representatives of h·source and target. Compare them by one scalar system over the coefficient-field torus A, adding the determinant constraints via norms and known lift determinants. On every occupied grade the quotient of the two coordinates gives the prescribed scalar. Zero support has to match. This is the earlier monomial unit problem and takes polynomial time. For every accepted a there are exactly |Stab_U(h·source)| possible U factors. The pivot computation has already determined that order, and Section 4 counts the acceptable scalar solutions. The disjoint residue choices sum these counts. The corresponding U maps can be constructed, so solving for the scalar gives a transporter as well.

For a noncyclic example, take C to be the direct sum of companion blocks for f^s with 1≤s≤k for a fixed non-self-reciprocal irreducible f, and use B=[[0,I],[C,0]]. Its asymmetry is diag(C⁻¹,C^T), with each f^s and its reciprocal exactly once. The commutant is noncommutative and the residue unit group has exponentially many elements in k, yet R=0.

## 4. Decision and exact counting in polynomial time, with search equivalent to root extraction

Consider explicit fixed-arity tensor systems over a fixed finite field F_q, in which each transporter variable is a unit of an explicitly represented commutative algebra E_i, acting through supplied linear representations. There may be polynomially many variables and components. A component may repeat a variable or carry its dual action. There is a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm deciding whether a transporter exists and counting all transporter tuples. The search problem for these systems is polynomial-time Turing equivalent to extracting an e-th root in an explicit extension of F_q, with e in binary and the input promised to be an e-th power of a nonzero element. The hardness direction uses only field-multiplication actions and tensor components of order at most three.

For each component form the tensor product A of the algebras in its slots, at most a fixed number, acting linearly on the tensor component. A dual action uses the transposed algebra representation and the inverse unit. For source v and target w, compare Av and Aw by linear algebra, and if equal find r with rv=w. The image of r in A/Ann(v) is unique and invertible because one also has v=tw for some t. Thus the constraint is one equation that the product of mapped units, with inverses for dual slots, equal r in that commutative quotient. A zero component row with both endpoints zero can be discarded. These equations together are exactly an equation Φ(x)=b for one homomorphism from the direct product of input unit groups to the direct product of output unit groups. The problem counts variable tuples, even when a supplied representation has a kernel.

### Exact counts and witnesses

For each algebra the high q-Frobenius separates the coefficient fields from the nilpotent radical. The maps respect that separation. For principal units, use the triangular integer presentation from a basis of each F_p-layer of the radical filtration, as in the previous packet. The number of solutions of a consistent equation is |ker Φ|=|domain|/|image|. If the target group is presented as Z^t/L and the image columns are M, the image order is [Z^t:L]/[Z^t:L+M Z^s]. Hermite or Smith normal form computes those indices and also gives a preimage of any reachable target by retaining the basis changes. The coordinate presentations, indices and witnesses have polynomial bit lengths. [Kannan–Bachem integer normal forms](https://doi.org/10.1137/0208040)

For the residue fields use the coherent embeddings and the gcd-based partition of the joint exponent into coprime factors D from the previous solver. Each μ_D is stored in a carrier field whose multiplicative order m is divisible by D. After adding the equations restricting each variable to its native subfield, diagonalize the exponent matrix over the integers on each μ_D. Unimodular row and column operations preserve the solution count. A diagonal entry s yields no solution unless the transformed right-hand side b satisfies b^(D/gcd(D,s))=1, and it yields gcd(D,s) choices otherwise. A free variable gives D choices. Inconsistent zero rows give count zero. Multiplication over rows, coprime factors, and principal-unit parts gives the exact count, without any discrete logarithms.

To find a witness, solve each consistent diagonal equation y^s=b by asking the root oracle in the full carrier field for z^((m/D)s)=b, then put y=z^(m/D). The latter powering is onto μ_D, so this call meets the power promise. Free variables can be one. Inverting the normal form transformations gives each projected variable, and transferring it into its native field intersection and multiplying the CRT pieces reconstructs the variable. This proves the search upper bound. It also supplies unconditional polynomial search if all diagonal gcds d=gcd(D,s) are polynomially bounded numerically. Indeed choose integers a,c with as+cD=d. On μ_D the equation is equivalent to y^d=b^a, using the earlier consistency test. Intersect X^d−b^a with X^D−1 by modular exponentiation, then extract a root from the resulting degree-at-most-d finite field polynomial by splitting its finite commutative algebra over the fixed prime field. In the split residue regime of Section 3 the relevant D divide q−1 and the root steps are constant-size.

### Encoding a binary exponent by tensor equations

For a root instance over K/F_q, use one named vector space K for each variable of a repeated-squaring chain, all with unit maps given by multiplication by a scalar of K×. The action is explicit and can equivalently be specified by marking multiplication by a field generator: its linear centralizer is the regular algebra K. Realize z=xy by the usual K-multiplication map in component slots x,y,z. Its intertwining equation on the same component at both endpoints is exactly z uv=(xu)(yv) for all u,v∈K. Repeating a sort gives squaring, and an identity term can be pinned by a marked vector 1. Produce the binary exponent e using O(log e) such components, then pin the final variable by sending the marked vector 1 to the input b. Valid transporters yield an e-th root by evaluating the first map at one. Conversely every root extends via its powers. All spaces and component arrays have polynomial sizes in the explicit field degree and log e. The equivalence is for a family of marked tensor systems, with no assumption about the complexity of arbitrary tensor search or about the hardness of the root problem.
