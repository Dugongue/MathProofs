# Exact simultaneous conjugator counts with prime regular orbit images

## GP-0487

Let two ordered lists of permutation groups act on equally large labeled sets. Form the group generated by all groups in each list. Suppose every nonsingleton orbit of either generated group has prime size and the generated group's induced action there is cyclic regular. The primes may vary across orbits. Given a bijection of the generated-group orbits, there is a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm counting and finding the permutations respecting it and conjugating every source group to its partner. For a partial bijection with at most $k$ unmatched orbits this gives an exact $k!\operatorname{poly}(L)$-time algorithm, where $L$ is the generator input size. All hypotheses are recognizable from the permutations.

Compute the collective orbits. On each orbit separately, restrict the input generators and check that a nontrivial restriction generates a regular cyclic group of the orbit size and that all the other restrictions lie in it. Use the powers of a chosen generator applied to one point as the coordinates $\mathbb F_p$ for an orbit of size $p$. A transporter must conjugate the collective group and hence its full regular cyclic image on each orbit. Assume the orbit bijection matches sizes, and align the coordinates using the given bijection. Every candidate has the form $x\mapsto a_j x+b_j$ on orbit $j$ of size $p$, with $a_j\in\mathbb F_p^\times$ and $b_j\in\mathbb F_p$, since precisely these permutations normalize the regular translations. The shifts never affect the conjugation.

The $p$-coordinate projection of each input group is the row span over $\mathbb F_p$ of the translation vectors of its generators on orbits of size $p$. The group is the direct product of these projections over its distinct primes, since taking a suitable integer power isolates each prime component. The shared slopes $a_j$ must take each source row space to its paired target row space by right diagonal scaling.

For each such pair choose pivot columns by the leftmost independent prefixes on the source and target. Diagonal scaling preserves dependence among column subsets, so their pivot lists must agree. After checking agreement, use bases with the pivot columns normalized to the identity, giving matrices $A,B$ and pivot indices $P_i$ for their rows $i$. Diagonal scaling of the source followed by pivot normalization must equal the target, by uniqueness of the normalized row-space representation in these pivots. Thus the equations are
$$
 B_{ij}=A_{ij}\,a_j/a_{P_i}.
$$
Check identical zero patterns, and collect each nonzero ratio constraint $a_j/a_{P_i}=B_{ij}/A_{ij}$ in one graph on the orbit indices of size $p$, combining every pair of marked groups. Propagate along a spanning forest and check cycles. In a consistent connected component its root slope is arbitrary nonzero and determines every other slope. Therefore each component contributes $p-1$ choices and each orbit contributes $p$ shifts. Multiply these factors over all primes. A witness uses any consistent root values and arbitrary shifts. Matched singleton orbits contribute no choice.

With partial orbit matches enumerate the bijections of the remaining orbits, preserving sizes, and sum the answers. Distinct alignments produce disjoint sets of permutations. The total count has polynomial bit length, since it is at most the factorial of the degree. This turns the earlier ratio propagation for a single projective transporter into one simultaneous equation system, with an exact count of all its conjugating lifts.
