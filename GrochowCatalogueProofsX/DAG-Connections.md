# Proof dependencies

- GP-0364 uses a presentation to avoid expanding the central kernels in the earlier quotient-lift route. GP-0375 uses integer collected coordinates and Smith form for infinite cyclic derived groups. [Central extension cosets](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/cbce8131e2cb46db9fcc20e31490cb13eb850e95/CentralExtensionCosets/PROOF.md) · [Earlier finite-field Heisenberg epimorphisms](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/cbce8131e2cb46db9fcc20e31490cb13eb850e95/GenusTwoGroupEpimorphism/HEISENBERG.md)
- GP-0407 and GP-0427 use compact class-two Lie coordinates for scalar alternating forms. GP-0491 makes the earlier orbit coordinates intrinsic to the labeled action and outputs a parallel coset transversal. The representative identities have an independent [Lean source](SchreierProjectionCertificates.lean) and [axiom report](Verification.json). [Compact class-two coordinates](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/cbce8131e2cb46db9fcc20e31490cb13eb850e95/GrochowCatalogueProofsIX/ParallelClassTwoIntersection.md)
- GP-0487 uses the projective support-ratio method to count simultaneous conjugators across marked prime orbits. GP-0523 recovers the common translation group directly from all left-translation differences of one Latin operation. [Projective marked span transport](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/cbce8131e2cb46db9fcc20e31490cb13eb850e95/GrochowCatalogueProofsIX/ProjectivePinnedSpanTransport.md)
- GP-0229 uses the pencil machinery in [GP-0234](RankTwoMatrixSpaceEquivalence.md). GP-0269 uses the same rank-two classification and the prior pencil determinant formulas, then handles the wide mixed-compression case by computing its full determinant image. [Upper-rank-two SL-TI](UpperRankTwoSpecialLinear.md) · [Pencil determinant formulas](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/cbce8131e2cb46db9fcc20e31490cb13eb850e95/TISLPencilsAndOrbits/Pencils.md) GP-0275 connects scalar restriction and tensor gadgets to the existing [Functorial Tensor Reductions](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/tree/cbce8131e2cb46db9fcc20e31490cb13eb850e95/FunctorialTensorReductions).

Only the two coset-representative identities are covered by the formal certificate. The other dependencies support written proofs with the scopes stated in the index.
