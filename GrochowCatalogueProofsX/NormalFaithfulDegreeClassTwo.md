# Minimum normal faithful degree from alternating-form ranks

## GP-0407

Let $G\le S_n$ have class at most two and odd prime exponent $p$. Write $Z=Z(G)$ and $D=G'$ in Lie coordinates. For $\lambda\in Z^*\setminus\{0\}$, write $\beta_\lambda$ for the alternating form on $G/Z$ given by $\lambda([x,y])$, and set $w(\lambda)=p^{1+\operatorname{rank}\beta_\lambda}$. The smallest degree of a faithful action with normal point stabilizers is the minimum weight of a basis of $Z^*$. If $p^{\dim D}\le n^c$ for a fixed constant $c$, the degree in binary and an optimal collection of stabilizer generators are computable in deterministic logspace-uniform NC from the input permutation generators. The formula also gives a polynomial-time procedure for Cayley-table inputs in this class.

A normal stabilizer $N$ contributes a regular quotient orbit of degree $[G:N]$. Put $Z_0=N\cap Z$, $j=\dim(Z/Z_0)$, and $a=\dim(G/(N+Z))$ in the Lie model. The index is $p^{a+j}$. When $\lambda$ annihilates $Z_0$, $\beta_\lambda$ has rank at most $a$, since $[N,G]\subseteq N\cap Z$ kills $N+Z$ in both arguments. Conversely for any nonzero $\lambda$ a normal stabilizer $N_\lambda$ has intersection $\ker\lambda$ in $Z$ and index exactly $w(\lambda)$. To construct it, quotient out $\ker\lambda$. The preimage of $\operatorname{rad}\beta_\lambda$ in the quotient is central, since the bracket is now scalar. Choose a linear complement to the line from $Z$ in that preimage. Its inverse image is normal and has the required intersection and index.

Faithfulness of a collection of normal stabilizers is equivalent to trivial common intersection with $Z$. Every nontrivial normal subgroup of a finite $p$-group meets the center by the class equation for conjugation on that subgroup. Replace any orbit with $j>0$ by the $N_\lambda$ from a basis of the annihilator of $Z_0$. Together they retain exactly $Z_0$ in $Z$. Their combined degree is at most $j p^{a+1}\le p^{a+j}$, by $j\le 2^{j-1}\le p^{j-1}$. Orbits with $j=0$ can be discarded. Thus an optimal action can be taken from functionals on $Z$ whose list spans $Z^*$. Deleting redundant functionals leaves a basis of no larger degree, and every basis realizes its weight.

Weights depend only on $\lambda|_D$. The annihilator of $D$ contributes $\dim Z-\dim D$ elements each of cost $p$. A minimum-weight basis in $D^*$, lifted arbitrarily, completes this to an optimal basis of $Z^*$. Indeed any $Z^*$-basis contains a subset whose restrictions are a $D^*$-basis, and every other element costs at least $p$. Enumerate $D^*$ under the stated promise, compute the form rank of each vector, and sort by rank with a fixed tie order. Select an element exactly when it increases the rank of the prefix. This is the weighted basis greedy rule and uses parallel independent prefix rank tests. Here $Z,D$, brackets, kernels and complements are computable by the compact class-two coordinates and uniform NC linear algebra. The powers and sum have polynomial bit length even when the action degree is exponential. The stabilizers are returned as generators in the original ambient action. In the Cayley-table model one can enumerate all of $D^*$ in polynomial table time.
