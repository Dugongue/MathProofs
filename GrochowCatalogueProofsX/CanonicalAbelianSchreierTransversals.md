# Canonical parallel Schreier transversals in elementary abelian actions

## GP-0491

Given permutation generators for an elementary abelian $G\le S_n$ and $H\le G$, with the unary index $[G:H]$, deterministic logspace-uniform NC outputs a complete transversal for $H$ in $G$ as an ordered list of permutations. The order depends on the represented subgroups and point labels and is independent of the generating lists. The exponent may be a supplied varying prime $p\le n$. For a trivial ambient group return the identity.

Compute the $G$-orbits and choose each orbit's least point. An abelian transitive action has regular image, since a stabilizer in an abelian action fixes the entire orbit. Hence each point labels one translation via its effect on the least point. Compute any linear coordinates for the regular image by the parallel orbit-prefix method from GP-0502. In these temporary coordinates list all orbit translations ordered by destination labels, test all prefix ranks, and select the ones that increase the rank. The selected translations are intrinsic to the labeled orbit and its regular image. Change from the temporary coordinates to this ordered basis by linear algebra. Order the orbits by their least points. This embeds $G$ into the product of intrinsically coordinated orbit spaces. To evaluate a translation as a permutation, add its vector to the coordinate of each point in the orbit and invert the point-coordinate table. [Parallel elementary abelian action coordinates](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/cbce8131e2cb46db9fcc20e31490cb13eb850e95/GrochowCatalogueProofsIX/ParallelClassTwoIntersection.md)

Take the reduced row echelon basis for each of the embedded subspaces $G,H$. It is computable by parallel prefix rank tests on columns, after first selecting independent rows, and then by inversion on a full minor to normalize the pivot columns to the identity. Uniqueness of reduced row echelon form ensures independence from the chosen generators. Extend the basis of $H$ to one of $G$ by using the ordered echelon rows of $G$. Select a row exactly when the span of $H$ and that row and its predecessors grows. The rank tests can run in parallel. The span $U$ of the selected extra rows is a complement to $H$ in $G$.

The vectors in $U$ give distinct representatives of all cosets. Output them in order of their base-$p$ coefficient tuples in the selected ordered basis, and convert each to a permutation via the orbit tables. The unary index makes the explicit output polynomial in input size, with one parallel conversion per coefficient tuple. Orbit operations, inverses on full minors, arithmetic and rank computations are logspace-uniform NC. No special ordering of the points within an orbit is required. [Parallel matrix rank](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02579205)
