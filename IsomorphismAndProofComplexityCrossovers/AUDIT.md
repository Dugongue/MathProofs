# Four crossovers in isomorphism and algebraic proofs

These are AI-assisted informal proofs, with the exact statement and verification scope in each manuscript.

| Result | Connection and outcome |
| --- | --- |
| [Algebraic-group torsors as ordinary three-tensor transporters](torsors.md) | The Peirce encoding for associative algebras, the linear-algebraic group realization theorem and Galois descent combine to preserve the entire isomorphism variety up to split rational factors. The construction gives a pair over $\mathbb Q$ of shape $328\times328\times328$ with connected stabilizers, isomorphic over every completion and nonisomorphic over $\mathbb Q$. |
| [Exact Geometric IPS degree and Frobenius elimination](geometry.md) | Artin–Schreier translations determine the full ideal of relations among the axioms of an explicit Boolean polynomial system. On the fixed field $\mathbb F_2$, the minimum geometric degree is $3\cdot2^{n-1}$ despite a linear-size ordinary formula proof. A polynomial-size geometric circuit attains this degree. The same argument gives sufficient conditions for polynomial-size simulation based on the support of the original variables. The note also gives a flat quasi-finite counterexample to the generalization proposed in footnote 7 of the arXiv version. |
| [Degree-free sparse simulation of low-rank inversion](proof-complexity.md) | Truncated adjugates are computed in the finite function algebra, then the proof is projected onto occupied residue classes of a free module. This yields exponential sparse-size lower bounds at constant pointwise defect rank, despite arbitrarily high annotation degrees or binary-encoded pure-power pullbacks. |
| [Quantitative sublogarithmic group membership depth](groups.md) | Restriction of scalars supplies low-degree equations for unitary groups. The revised algebraic-group growth argument applies to the needed classical simple factors, and the bounded-register construction turns it into logspace-uniform, polynomial-size Cayley-table circuits with positive-input witness extraction and explicit depth $O(\log N\,(\log\log\log N)^{5/2}/\sqrt{\log\log N})$. |

The torsor proof verifies the actual transporters, including the conjugacy of the vertex idempotents, the split unit factors and all field extensions. An independent worker checked the dimension, group projection and descent. The explicit norm construction was also tested in five finite fields, checking every basis product for a constructed norm witness. Replay it with python check_torsors.py. The local and global rational conclusions use the classical Hasse norm counterexample cited in the proof.

The Geometric IPS result is an exact lower bound for degree, and for formulas and affine-labelled branching programs via their degree bounds. The unrestricted arithmetic circuit certificate is explicitly small. The finite-field examples include one cubic axiom in addition to the Boolean equations. The proof-size simulations and the rank bound are for the stated formal equations and sparse representation. The Cayley-table circuit bound strengthens the earlier sublogarithmic result with no explicit closed-form rate, while unrestricted $O(\log\log N)$ depth in the cited question remains open.

Federated searches across the registered notes and Lean source catalogs were used for discovery. Imported results are attributed by the primary sources in the manuscripts. The proofs are informal and independent human validation and publication priority remain open.
