# A sign correction controlled by ordinary tensor isomorphism

Let $O(X,Y)$ denote ordinary tensor isomorphism over $\mathbb F_3$. Let $P(X,Y)$ denote the existence of a transporter of total determinant $+1$. Here the total determinant is the product of the three mode determinants. This is the one-sign tier, by the castling equivalence from the earlier work.

There is a deterministic polynomial-time construction producing **one** $P$-instance with decision
$$
O(X,Y)\ \land\ O(B_1,B_2)\ \land\
     \big(P(X,Y)\ \lor\ O(C_1,C_2)\big).
\tag{1}
$$
The conjunct $O(B_1,B_2)$ is optional. In particular, two-to-one conjunction holds when one of the two conditions admits an ordinary-TI many-one reduction. The additional result in (1) is a conditional disjunction: the ordinary $C$-test can correct the sign of an existing $X,Y$ transporter. This is also exact for arbitrary positive Boolean formulas of polynomial size and logarithmic depth in place of either ordinary test, using the earlier ordinary packing theorem. No sign oracle is used in this construction.

## 1. A total-sign-neutral, odd-dimensional ordinary data block

Write $\chi$ for the total determinant sign. We need an encoding $H$ of a pair $C_1,C_2$ into equal-format concise, absolutely indecomposable tensors of odd dimensions, such that every stabilizer of each tensor has $\chi=+1$, and
$$
O(H(C_1),H(C_2))\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad O(C_1,C_2).
\tag{2}
$$
For unequal source formats we can directly set the ordinary comparison to false and omit this helper. Below sizes in a matched source pair are chosen jointly, so every step needing a common upper bound uses the same bound for both members.

First encode ordinary TI by an alternating matrix-space isometry instance. Apply the usual suspension: augment the alternating data space on $B$ by a space $A$ of larger dimension, adjoining all cross forms $A\wedge B$. Take the $A\times A$ block zero. With $a=\dim A>b=\dim B\ge3$ and nonzero original data space, the resulting tensor $T_C$ has scalar pair adjoint in each of the three fixed-label views. For the two matrix slots every elementary cross form has image equal to a coordinate plane. Preservation of these planes makes the two adjoint operators diagonal, and the cross equations identify a common scalar on each bipartite part. The nonzero bottom space makes the scalars equal. When the fixed label is one of the other slots, an endomorphism of the slice space satisfies $f(M)=hM$ for each alternating form $M$. Since $hM$ is alternating, $hM=Mh^{\mathsf T}$, and the same scalar conclusion applies. The suspension reflects ordinary TI because a left-right equivalence of alternating spaces with scalar pair adjoints is a congruence up to scalar. The unique largest common totally isotropic subspace is $A$, and the forms annihilating it recover the original data. These are the existing alternating suspension and scalar-adjoint facts. Trivial constant-sized and zero-space cases may be decided directly. [Grochow and Qiao, Part IV, Theorem 2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.16317v2#S2)

Tensor $T_C$ with three edge pairings of dimension two, one for each unordered pair of the three modes. At a physical mode the two incident edge spaces then contribute a factor of four to its dimension. This is the determinant-neutral matrix-multiplication amplification from the earlier norm-tier proof. For clarity, each amplified pair-adjoint algebra is precisely the full endomorphism algebra on the corresponding shared edge, acting as the identity on the other factors. Indeed the images of the two separate mode actions on the amplified tensor are tensor products of the images of the actions on its original tensor and on its edge-pairing tensor. Their intersection is the tensor product of the intersections, by decomposing into complementary vector subspaces. The original intersection is scalar. In the edge tensor, acting separately on two physical modes can transfer an operator only along their shared pairing. Pair-adjoint algebras are therefore intrinsic marks. On each common endomorphism algebra a transporter acts by an inner automorphism, which one can remove by an actual paired edge action, acting by an arbitrary matrix at one endpoint and by its inverse dual at the other endpoint. After the three corrections the remaining maps centralize the two incident matrix algebras in every mode, and so are the original three maps tensored with identity. Thus the amplified tensor reflects ordinary TI and has scalar centroid, the intersection of two distinct incident edge algebras at each vertex. In particular it is geometrically indecomposable. Every transporter of the amplified pair has mode determinants $+1$: the original maps occur with multiplicity four, and each auxiliary edge map occurs with multiplicity divisible by two at both endpoints.

Now adjoin one fixed $1\times1\times1$ nonzero block by direct sum. All three dimensions become odd. Its ordinary automorphisms multiply the three factors by scalars of product one and therefore have total sign $+1$. The new block cannot exchange with the larger amplified block, as their centroid primitive formats differ. Thus this step adds no total sign and reflects ordinary TI. This intermediate tensor may be decomposable.

Finally apply the determinant-preserving scalar-centroid glue from the earlier comparison. One may use the following explicit glue. In matrix-pencil notation for a concise tensor $M:W\to\operatorname{Hom}(V,U)$, adjoin two labels $x,y$, a twofold evaluation copy $Q=U\otimes (\mathbb F_3^2)^*$, and a fixed square Jordan pencil $G(x,y):R\to L$:
$$
\widehat M(w,c)=\begin{pmatrix}M(w)&\operatorname{ev}_{c}&0\\0&0&G(c)\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The three blocks of $G$ use the scalar forms $x,y,x+y$ with distinct even lengths larger than $\dim U$; give each a single nilpotent Jordan term $uJ$ where $u\ne0$ vanishes at $(1,1)$. By the determinant factor multiplicities the induced $c$-action is scalar. At the invertible anchor the fixed-label equivalences commute with the distinct joint Jordan types and hence lie in the Jordan centralizer of each block. Its unit determinants are even powers and thus one. The odd or even sign in the $c$-action is trivial since $c$ has dimension two. In the glued tensor, the old labels are intrinsically those whose rank is at most $\dim U$, as every nonzero marker label has larger rank just from $G$. They identify $U$ by their image span and $Q\oplus R$ by their common kernel. Inside that kernel, $Q$ is identified by the common kernel after projection to the added output $L$. Thus every old transporter is recovered on subquotients, and every old transporter extends by the tensor product of its $U$-action with identity on the evaluation copy. The evaluation copy's action has determinant one on every lift, since the marker action has determinant one and the copy occurs with multiplicity two. All other newly added dimensions are even and all new determinant contributions trivial.

The glue is absolutely indecomposable. For a centroid element, invariance of the intrinsic flags holds over the algebraic closure by forming the actual tensor automorphisms which apply $I+\tau c_i$ on mode $i$ and the inverse corresponding mode action on a different mode for generic $\tau$. The evaluation equations force the diagonal centroid actions to be common scalars on $U$, the marker labels, and $Q$. Conciseness of the old and Jordan tensors then forces the same scalar on all remaining diagonal blocks. After subtraction, the label shear has support $V\to U$ upon applying a marker label, whereas the output shear has support $R\to U$. Equality forces both to vanish by conciseness. The remaining input shears vanish by the jointly zero kernels of the evaluation and Jordan pencils. Thus the centroid is scalar over the algebraic closure. The glue adds only even dimensions, so the resulting $H(C)$ has all sides odd and every ordinary stabilizer is total-even. This proves (2).

For the optional ordinary conjunct one can use the same construction without adjoining the $1\times1\times1$ block. It gives absolutely indecomposable **even** ordinary data tensors $J(B_1),J(B_2)$ and is stronger than needed: on any existing transporter the total sign is $+1$. The operator dimensions, anchor lengths and entire dense tensor sizes in these constructions are polynomial. By increasing the even Jordan lengths one may choose the large sides to distinguish the outputs from any specified finite set of prior formats.

## 2. Realize the ordinary condition as optional sign correction

Use the previous castling preparation to make the $X,Y$ signed test equivalent to one on concise, absolutely indecomposable tensors $X',Y'$ with odd sides. Briefly, a support deficiency in a tested mode or mixed dimension parities make the total sign automatically correctable by basic complements or scalar maps. In these cases merely encode the ordinary comparison by the odd neutral helper (2). In the remaining case $X,Y$ are concise, with all sides of the same parity. If these are even, direct-sum a distinct oversized odd evaluation tensor of format $(ab,a,b)$, with $a,b$ odd, to both sides. The evaluation tensor's stabilizer has total sign one, since the two factor determinants appear once in their own slots and to odd powers in the large slot. Its centroid is scalar, and choosing a distinct format makes it an independently matched block. This preserves the total sign and makes the full format odd. For already odd sides this padding can be omitted. Apply the determinant-neutral glue from Section 1. The ordinary and total-sign answers are preserved, and the data tensor now is absolutely indecomposable with odd sides. Zero cases are dealt with by the same ordinary branch.

To the two sides respectively form
$$
X'\oplus H(C_1)\oplus H(C_2) \quad\text{and}\quad
Y'\oplus H(C_1)\oplus H(C_2).
$$
Choose the common helper format distinct from the data format. Since all three blocks are absolutely indecomposable, their centroid idempotents force their matching and permit the two helper blocks to exchange precisely when $O(C_1,C_2)$ holds. If they do not exchange, the helper contribution to the total sign is always $+1$. When they may exchange, choose any isomorphism of the helpers and its inverse for the two local maps. Their determinant products cancel, and in each mode the swap factor is $(-1)^{ab}=-1$, as the two equal blocks have odd dimension in that mode. Over the three modes the swap supplies total sign $-1$. Thus the two helper blocks supply exactly the optional $-1$ correction controlled by $O(C_1,C_2)$, independently of the data transporter.

For the optional conjunct append $J(B_1)$ to the source and $J(B_2)$ to the target, with distinct format from the other blocks. The corresponding comparison enforces precisely $O(B_1,B_2)$ and contributes no total sign. The new tensors have a total-even transporter exactly in the cases of (1). If the output one-sign convention tests a single mode, the previous castling equivalence turns this total-sign instance into that convention.

The result can absorb any ordinary TI comparison in the displayed position, including one obtained by a published or prior determinant test on a justified family. It leaves the unrestricted case of two signed inputs open. In particular, the helper swap in this proof depends on ordinary equivalence of its marked blocks. It does not observe their relative sign.
