# Four routes to determinant-constrained tensor isomorphism

Informal proofs over finite fields. Each route retains the original tensor equation. The full problem is [Open Question 1.9 of Chen–Grochow–Qiao–Tang–Zhang](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5).

| Route | Proved result | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Overlapping flags](FlagRoute.md) | A deterministic one-instance reduction. Compute the common endomorphism algebra of raw contraction flags and compile only the simple factors whose determinant contributions survive modulo a known correction group. | Those relevant matrix factors have logarithmic size. Individual flag layers can be large. |
| [Smallest-mode stabilizer search](GroupRoute.md) | The full determinant coset, with $q^r\operatorname{poly}(r,\log q)$ ordinary TI queries. | Works on arbitrary tensors. Polynomial time for logarithmic smallest concise mode rank $r$ over a fixed field. |
| [Pfaffian volume](VolumeRoute.md) | Compress a determinant pin by evaluation of translated or Frobenius-deflated Pfaffians. There is a deterministic one-query reduction for an explicit raw characteristic-three class with all widths growing. The [divided-power extension](DividedPowerAppendix.md) works through the socle and removes the sparsity, coordinate-degree and exact-power hypotheses in its randomized small-Hasse-span construction. | Sufficient covariant alternating data are supplied or recovered. In the general compression, the relevant derivative span is polynomially bounded. |
| [Associative action tensors](GeometryRoute.md) | Recognize action tensors from raw slices. Over semisimple algebras, determine every prescribed determinant triple in deterministic polynomial time. Over general algebras, two computed correction groups give a one-query criterion that can hold with scalar center and unbounded radical. A domain satisfying the criterion is TI-complete. | The action tensor is recognized, and in the general algebra case the correction groups satisfy the stated conditions. |

The flag route uses Ward and Trellis to keep several intrinsic subspaces tied to one mode map. The volume route applies Ley to covariant alternating data and Rune to discard the differential operators that carry no Pfaffian information. The smallest-mode algorithm likewise keeps the same pinned images throughout a stabilizer chain. The action route extends the exact coupled norm idea from weighted trace tensors to units of associative algebras. These results use existing module algorithms, typed tensor compilers, and classical divided-power algebra. Publication priority for the new combinations remains unverified. The unrestricted containment in Open Question 1.9 remains open here.
