# Label erasure and proof transfer into ordinary tensor isomorphism

An ordinary multiplication tensor can encode arbitrary block partitions in all three modes, with linear overhead in the sum of the input dimensions. The resulting Polynomial Calculus (PC) degree bounds hold uniformly in the number of blocks and in every characteristic. The algebra stores the labels in its multiplication, and a directed path in the quotient of its strict ideal by its square recovers them with constant-degree equations.

| Proof | What transfers |
| --- | --- |
| [Linear block erasure](MarkerProof.md) | Block-preserving bilinear maps become ordinary 3-tensors of side $O(N+1)$. If $N$ is the sum of the three input dimensions and $C$ is an absolute constant, a degree-$D$, size-$S$ ordinary PC refutation gives a labelled refutation of degree $\max(D,C)$ and expanded size $S+(N+1)^{O(1)}$. Labelled refutations transfer to ordinary PC with degree $O(D+1)$ and polynomial expanded size at fixed $D$. Both transfer statements allow coordinate field equations over the same finite field. The semantic construction works over arbitrary commutative rings with free blocks. |
| [Named quiver diagrams](DiagramProof.md) | Arbitrary finite diagrams, including loops and parallel arrows, give ordinary tensors of unital algebras with cube-zero strict ideal. Witness maps compose and the projection has a section over every commutative base change. Tensor rank is within a constant factor of the compiled side. The raw PC degree is preserved to within absolute constant factors. In each positive characteristic there is an explicit family requiring PC degree linear in rank and admitting constant-degree refutations in PC with the matrix inversion rule. |
| [Arithmetic witnesses and forms](ArithmeticProof.md) | For finite free triangular algebras with acyclic strict support, ordinary tensor transporters are the labelled transporters times explicit affine spaces and split tori. This gives exact finite-ring witness counts and an exact Picard-group correction for forms over affine bases. Over a Dedekind domain, every labelled form has an ordinary tensor form on free modules, with the free lifts parametrized by an explicit power of the Picard group. |
| [Coordinate recovery and certificates](LabelProof.md) | Consecutive forbidden edges recover every ordered label in quadratic PC and by polynomial-size constant-depth Hilbert-like IPS formulas. At the bounded-length graded algebra step, coordinate-only substitution preserves arbitrary expanded PC proofs up to additive polynomial preparation. Polynomial-size Hilbert-like IPS circuits transfer through the ordinary tensor layer. |

The ordinary-to-labelled PC lift preserves sparse proofs at every degree. For the reverse ordinary tensor transfer, substitution can turn a single variable into a polynomial with many terms. The expanded bound there is polynomial at fixed degree, whereas the circuit bound is polynomial without a degree cutoff. On an explicit family of ordinary $0,1$ tensors in each fixed positive characteristic, the resulting lower and upper bounds separate expanded PC size $N^{\Theta(\sqrt N)}$ from polynomial Hilbert-like IPS circuit size. Here $N$ is the ordinary tensor side length. [Ordinary tensor family](MarkerProof.md) · [Circuit separation](LabelProof.md)

This gives an alternative all-characteristic compiler for the block-erasure step in the proof-complexity program of Galesi, Grochow, Pitassi and She. The exact rank-weight augmentation of Futorny, Grochow and Sergeichuk is a different compiler, and its positive-characteristic obstruction concerns its own outputs. The new quantitative conclusion is uniform PC degree transfer with arbitrary block counts and linear side overhead. The literature comparison is scoped to the cited constructions.
