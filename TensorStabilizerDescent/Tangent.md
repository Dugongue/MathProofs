# Moving one determinant test onto the kernel and cokernel of an explicit matrix

Here is a field-level step that can be applied to a raw tensor without any graphical hypothesis. Its size bound depends on two measurable dimensions. The determinant-line identity is the standard multiplicativity for exact sequences. The proposed use is to compile the remaining signed test in ordinary TI.

Let T be an ordered tensor of positive format (a,b,c), with ambient space M. Let E be the direct sum of the endomorphism spaces of the three modes, with their displayed matrix-unit bases. Compute the infinitesimal action matrix
$$
 d_T:E\longrightarrow M,\qquad (X,Y,Z)\longmapsto (X\otimes1\otimes1+1\otimes Y\otimes1+1\otimes1\otimes Z)T.
$$
Set K_T=ker(d_T) and N_T=coker(d_T). These spaces can be computed by Gaussian elimination in every characteristic. No smoothness of the stabilizer is assumed. A general-linear transporter g induces ordinary linear maps g_K and g_N on these two spaces, using conjugation on E and the tensor action on M.

**Universal identity.** In chosen ordered bases there are computable nonzero scalars ρ_T such that every witness T→U obeys
$$
 (\det g_1)^{bc}(\det g_2)^{ac}(\det g_3)^{ab}
   =(\rho_U/\rho_T)\det(g_N)/\det(g_K).
$$
To see this, extend a kernel basis to a basis [K,B] of E and let l_T be its determinant in E. Extend d_T(B) by chosen cokernel basis lifts to form a basis of M of determinant m_T. Put ρ_T=m_T/l_T. Conjugation has determinant one on each entire endomorphism space, including when the characteristic divides a mode dimension. If i denotes the determinant on the image in the d_T(B) bases, 1=(l_U/l_T) det(g_K)i. The determinant of the tensor action on M is (m_U/m_T)i det(g_N). Eliminating i proves the identity. It is valid even when K or N is zero. The two-dimensional scalar kernel in E is fixed pointwise on positive-dimensional modes. It may optionally be removed from K by quotienting E by the same fixed coordinate subspace from the outset and using a fixed quotient basis.

**One ordinary instance.** When a,b,c are pairwise coprime, the kernel of the displayed character on determinant triples is exactly the image of scalar changes (uI,wI,(uw)⁻¹I) over every field: the cross product of (a,0,−c) and (0,b,−c) has relatively prime coordinates. The two columns extend to a unimodular basis, so the inversion uses integer monomials, requiring no root choices. Over the field of order three it suffices that at least two of the three mode dimensions are odd: reducing the two columns modulo two gives rank two, with the displayed character as their nonzero orthogonal vector. In either case, existence of an SL transporter is equivalent to existence of a GL transporter satisfying the displayed character test.

Keep the original tensor in a labelled tensor system. Realize each endomorphism space by evaluation, with the action of the original mode forced to be conjugation. The maps into their direct sum, the tensor products giving M, the kernel inclusion, and the projection M→N are all linear or bilinear data and force exactly the induced maps. Include d_T for clarity. Above each of K,N use exterior spaces from degree one to their dimensions, with the usual spanning wedge multiplications, and the unit line in degree zero. Mark the vector of scalar 1/ρ_T in the line det(N) ⊗ det(K)* relative to the displayed bases. By the identity the original witnesses extend exactly when their determinant character is one. The polynomial compiler for labelled, mixed-variance tensor systems produces one ordinary TI comparison. Its uniform form first codes the variable number of labels, as in the [uniform compiler proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/5072239cac626156b25b8e37ab6dff94f907184d/LinearStructureUniversality/PROOF.md). [Futorny, Grochow and Sergeichuk](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.09219)

In particular, if h=max(dim K,dim N) is O(log L) for input length L over a fixed finite field and the stated format condition holds, this is a polynomial many-one reduction of SL-TI to one ordinary TI instance. Its construction for arbitrary h costs poly(L,2^h) using the uniform compiler. Preprocessing checks the dimensions and the width bound on each input; an incompatible profile rejects. No constraints on tensor entries, hidden bases, graph structure or sizes of individual mode frames enter that test. The full character identity holds regardless of width. In balanced n × n × n formats, dim N is at least n³−3n². Thus the criterion demonstrably does not yield a polynomial-size reduction on that unrestricted family. The approach uses the infinitesimal action of the integral proof while avoiding any inference that equality of tangent spaces would decide orbit equivalence.
