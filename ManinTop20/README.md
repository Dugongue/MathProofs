# Twenty MathProof results for Fedor Manin

Conjecture 7.2 remains the lead result for an email about its proof. The nilmanifold answer to Question 1.4 and the fixed six-manifold degree set are the highest-ranked additions. The list ranks results by relevance to Manin’s work and the strength of the conclusion. New topological results have written proofs with provisional priority. Lean verified the stated integer normalization, and the earlier checked bilinear reduction has the arithmetic premise described in #1. [Manin’s papers](https://www.math.utoronto.ca/manin/papers.html) · [New proofs](ManinGeometry.md) · [Earlier proofs](ManinNewProofs.md)

## Ranked proofs

1. **Conjecture 7.2 and the extension dichotomy.** For every fixed nonzero integral bilinear map, solvability in Manin’s coefficient-only language is undecidable. Forced unimodular frames allow the reduction to interpret the adjoint center in that language. Koymans–Pagano supply the arithmetic input, and Manin’s Theorem 8.1 gives the negative side of the extension dichotomy for simply connected finite targets. The written proof is unconditional. The Lean reduction still takes number-ring undecidability as a premise. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/BilinearDiophantineUndecidability/PROOF.md#1-manins-bilinear-conjecture) · [Lean](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/ManinBilinearReduction/ManinSingleFile.lean) · [Manin §§7–8](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.10632#S7)

2. **A nilmanifold answer to Question 1.4.** Based-loop finite bars have uniformly bounded log length on every compact nilmanifold, as do free-loop bars in class two. Free-loop bars have unbounded log length already on a fixed Engel nilmanifold. The cutoff exponents are sharply $4/3$ there and $5/3$ on a fixed seven-dimensional class-three example. [Written proof](ManinGeometry.md#4-based-and-free-persistence-on-nilmanifolds) · [Block–Manin–Weinberger Question 1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.16907v2#S1.SS3)

3. **A halting-complete degree set for two fixed six-manifolds.** The target is $(S^2)^3$ and the source is closed, simply connected, and smooth. The construction realizes exact enumerable divisibility-upward spectra subject to a prime-power coverage condition. The halting example has every residue modulo every modulus and every degree after each prime localization and rationally. [Written proof](ManinGeometry.md#1-a-halting-complete-mapping-degree-set-for-fixed-six-manifolds)

4. **Undecidable gaps in the least positive mapping degree.** For the fixed target $(S^2)^3$, the source and a degree-$K$ map are supplied, the integral homology is fixed, and the least positive degree is 1 or $K$ for any fixed $K\ge2$. A distinct construction for $\mathbb{CP}^4$ supplies localized degree-one witnesses at every prime and arbitrary fixed approximation gaps. [Six-manifold proof](ManinGeometry.md#an-exact-gap-with-fixed-homology-and-a-supplied-map) · [Projective-space proof](ManinGeometry.md#2-an-undecidable-gap-in-the-minimum-positive-degree-to-complex-projective-space)

5. **One boundary sphere at the sharp dimension.** For every even $n$ a fixed $2n$-dimensional pair with one boundary sphere has a halting-complete extension problem to $S^n$ in a single Whitehead-square coordinate. Each instance extends after each prime localization. The projective-space construction gives the exact dimensional threshold for undecidability of extending a complex line in a fixed trivial ambient bundle. [Written proof and checked integer lemma](ManinGeometry.md#3-one-spherical-boundary-at-the-first-unstable-dimension)

6. **Higher and relative persistence for two-stage targets.** The relative mapping space with prescribed boundary restriction has linear cutoff $C(L+1)$ for scalable closed two-stage targets and cutoff $C(L+1)^{4/3}$ for general closed simply connected two-stage targets. For the absolute problem the linear bound holds also on every rationally null component of an arbitrary such target. The general linear cutoff in Question 1.3 remains open here. [New written proof](ManinGeometry.md#5-relative-persistence-with-a-prescribed-boundary) · [Earlier absolute proof](ManinNewProofs.md#5-higher-persistence-for-two-stage-targets)

7. **Exact computability of the Lipschitz extension optimum.** The compactified minimum is uniformly computable for semialgebraic maps to a sphere with chordal metric. On one fixed pair the envelope over the finite minima has exactly the Turing degree of halting. [Written proof](ManinGeometry.md#6-computing-the-true-minimum-lipschitz-extension-value)

8. **The quantitative threshold for computable Lipschitz extension.** Rational H-space type through degree $d$ is exactly the condition for computable Lipschitz extension bounds on every fixed pair of dimension at most $d+1$. At the first failing degree one pair has worst-case extension cost that eventually exceeds every computable function, even allowing the prescribed boundary up to homotopy. [Written proof](ManinNewProofs.md#1-when-lipschitz-extension-bounds-are-computable)

9. **Undecidable compatibility of local extensions.** One fixed pair and one varying boundary sphere admit extensions after inverting 2 and 3, at every prime, and after one suspension. Asking whether some two localized extensions agree relative to the boundary in the common localization is halting-complete. [Written proof](ManinNewProofs.md#2-undecidable-gluing-of-local-extensions)

10. **Undecidability on an entire smooth manifold boundary.** For each target in Manin’s negative class, one fixed simply connected compact smooth manifold with simply connected boundary has a two-parameter family on its entire boundary realizing the universal computably enumerable relation. [Written proof](ManinNewProofs.md#3-undecidability-on-the-whole-boundary-of-a-smooth-manifold)

11. **Nonzero mapping degree at the first nonformal dimension.** At every connectivity $k-1$, existence of a nonzero-degree continuous map between the promised smooth $(4k-1)$-manifolds is tensor-isomorphism complete on invariant-table input. The cup ring is fixed per input format. This extends the seven-manifold construction through Manin’s positive-weights theorem and an exterior-square construction for odd $k$. [Written proof and checked algebraic step](ManinNewProofs.md#4-nonzero-mapping-degree-at-the-first-nonformal-dimension)

12. **Universal boundary extension degrees on a fixed pair.** For each simply connected finite target outside rational H-space type, one fixed source pair and two boundary spheres realize the universal computably enumerable relation through their degree parameters. Fixing the first degree selects a set, and the second degree asks whether it belongs to that set. Equality of two degree sets is $\Pi^0_2$-complete. This is the strongest direct topological companion to #1. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/PortfolioTop30/ManinExtensions.md#2-every-computably-enumerable-set-as-boundary-extension-degrees)

13. **The finite-image dichotomy over finite-type rings.** For any fixed bilinear map between finitely generated modules over a commutative finite-type $\mathbb Z$-algebra, the raw solvability problem is decidable exactly when the values generate a finite module as a set. A prime-support reduction handles torsion, zero divisors, and positive characteristic. This gives the algebraic classification beyond Manin’s integral lattice case. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/PortfolioTop30/ManinExtensions.md#1-the-finite-image-dichotomy)

14. **Two pairing terms suffice on a path.** Halting reduces to systems of every fixed nonzero integral pairing with at most two pairing terms per equation, coefficients $\pm1$, bounded variable occurrence, and bounded primal pathwidth. Two fixed templates read the binary input. The one-term scalar-multiplication case with right sides $0,1$ has a linear-time algorithm. The main theorem is written and its arithmetic gate identity is Lean-checked. [Proof and Lean identity](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/PortfolioTop30/ManinExtensions.md#3-two-pairing-terms-on-a-path-suffice)

15. **Undecidability with two explicit bounded-denominator solutions.** Every instance of a fixed halting reduction for a nonzero integral pairing comes with rational solutions of common denominators dividing 2 and 3. This supplies solutions over every $\mathbb Z_p$ and every $\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z$. The result controls local solvability within Manin’s exact equation language. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/PortfolioTop30/ManinExtensions.md#4-integral-halting-completeness-with-two-supplied-rational-solutions)

16. **Quasi-isometric rigidity for Amann’s nilpotent family.** A uniform cohomology-ring obstruction proves rigidity for the specified family in every parameter $k\ge2$, extending Amann’s cases $k=2,3$. The connection is the use of rational minimal models to distinguish geometric types. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/AmannNilpotentRigidity/Proof.md) · [Amann’s conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.04542)

17. **Exact homological cuts for persistent classes.** Over a fixed finite field, a cochain certificate and incidence tree decomposition give an exact algorithm to find a minimum-cardinality set of simplices to delete that destroys a specified homology class. The result applies to the robustness of a finite-death persistence bar and to any supplied class. It connects to Manin, Block, and Weinberger’s interest in persistence through a discrete algorithmic problem. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/GrochowCatalogueProofsIII/IncidenceHomologicalCuts.md) · [Persistent homology of function spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16907v2)

18. **Arithmetic hardness of unlabelled unit-distance realization.** Injective unit-edge realization in $\mathbb Q^{24}$ is polynomial-time many-one equivalent to Hilbert’s tenth problem over $\mathbb Q$, even on graphs realizable over the reals and every $\mathbb Q_p$. The proof also treats the edge-preserving version that allows collisions of nonadjacent vertices. This is the closest geometric realization analogue in the portfolio to the arithmetic reductions in Manin’s work on algorithmic geometry. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/RationalUnitDistance/PROOF.md) · [Manin and Weinberger on immersibility and embeddability](https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnae170)

19. **Scheme universality for binary rank-two matrix completion.** Binary specified entries realize every affine finite-type $\mathbb Q$-scheme up to an open subset of a product with affine space. The projection is smooth and surjective and has points in each rational fiber over its field of definition. The result preserves nilpotent structure and gives a reusable way to move exact arithmetic into a geometric realization space. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/RankTwoGeometricUniversality/PROOF.md)

20. **An undecidable conjugacy-class question in a fixed nilpotent group.** One torsion-free class-two group has a split quotient to $\mathbb Z^2$ such that deciding whether the fiber over $(1,n)$ contains an element with infinite cyclic commutator image is halting-complete. Its commutator tensor is fixed and the corresponding local witness exists over every $\mathbb Z_p$ and over $\mathbb R$. This is a geometric-group companion to the fixed-target constructions. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/TensorTransportAndHalting/Conjugacy.md)

## Previously ranked

- **A relative homology localization approximation scheme at fixed genus.** A deterministic algorithm constructs a $(1+\varepsilon)$-optimal mod-two representative of a specified relative class on an orientable triangulated surface with binary-encoded rational weights. The running time is $4^g\operatorname{poly}(L,1/\varepsilon)$ and allows arbitrary numbers of boundary components. This is a quantitative algorithm for finding small representatives. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/GrochowCatalogueProofsV/RelativeHomologyFPTAS.md)

- **Exact surface homology localization from translation constraints.** The reduction takes an unweighted finite abelian translation system to a closed orientable simplicial surface of linear size, where minimum cycle support is exactly three times the minimum number of violated constraints. The reverse reduction preserves the optimum. The new content is exact objective preservation, linear size, and arbitrary finite abelian coefficients. The earlier binary approximation hardness has published precedent. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/SurfaceHomologyLocalization/PROOF.md) · [Borradaile–Maxwell–Nayyeri](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.02801)

- **Homology localization with a variable prime at constant width.** Exact weighted localization has an algorithm exponential in incidence width for fixed coefficient field. If the prime is binary-encoded, the threshold problem remains NP-hard under zero-error expected-polynomial-time reductions on closed orientable simplicial surfaces of constant incidence width. This isolates arithmetic as a source of hardness even when the incidence topology is simple. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/GrochowCatalogueProofsVII/HomologyWidthBinaryPrime.md)

- **Multipersistence from priced boundary matroids.** With marked cells and the specified valued basis oracles, one can recover relative filtration grades up to component translations and read persistence ranks for queried comparable grades. This gives an inference result from partial access to the chain data, with a separate reconstruction statement for binary boundaries. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/StrangeConvexFour/Persistence.md) · [Manin, Block, and Weinberger on persistent homology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16907v2)

- **Rational-point decision for smooth affine hypersurfaces.** Over each fixed number field, deciding whether a smooth geometrically integral affine hypersurface has a rational point is computably many-one equivalent to Hilbert’s tenth problem over that field. The construction also supplies a uniform Turing reduction and a degree-eight restriction over $\mathbb Q$. It is an unconditional example of arithmetic decision complexity surviving a strong geometric restriction. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/RationalPointDecision/PROOF.md#1-smooth-affine-hypersurfaces-over-each-fixed-number-field)

- **Few edge lengths in sparse graph drawings.** Every $h$-degenerate graph has a plane drawing with $O_h(n/\sqrt{\log n})$ distinct edge lengths. Bounded treewidth and bounded maximum degree jointly give a uniform bound. This is a quantitative metric-geometry result for discrete configurations. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/SparseGraphDistanceNumber/PROOF.md)

- **One versus infinitely many rational points with a marked point.** Rational polynomial solvability is computably many-one equivalent to the promised problem of distinguishing one rational point from infinitely many on a smooth projective geometrically integral $\mathbb Q$-variety, with a rational point supplied. A fixed-family halting-completeness consequence additionally assumes an existential definition of $\mathbb Z$ in $\mathbb Q$. The distinction between nonemptiness and the geometry of the remaining solution space parallels the difference between existence of extensions and their full set. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/RationalPointDecision/PROOF.md#2-exactly-one-or-infinitely-many-points-on-a-marked-projective-variety)

- **Locally $R$-equivalent points with undecidable global $R$-equivalence.** Assuming an existential definition of $\mathbb Z$ in $\mathbb Q$, there is one smooth projective variety on which deciding $R$-equivalence of two rational points is halting-complete. The supplied points are directly $R$-equivalent over every completion. The associated family keeps a common real diffeomorphism type. The analogy is a fixed-space connectivity problem with an arithmetic obstruction. [Conditional written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/LocalGlobalREquivalence/PROOF.md)

- **Exact rotor-routing descent on ribbon covers.** For a regular ribbon cover with a wired sink fiber, an invariant affine compression of the rotor actions exists exactly when the covering degree is coprime to the base sandpile order. The examples include covers of arbitrarily large ribbon genus over a planar base. This is the closest additional match to Manin’s work on combinatorial topology and cover-dependent geometry. [Written proof and finite checks](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/c5fef3140265c1dff49ad6e18c7f40b71f6a8ff0/GrochowFarDAG/TopologyBridge.md)
