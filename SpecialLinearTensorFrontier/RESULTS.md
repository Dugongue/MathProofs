# Four further approaches to special-linear tensor isomorphism

These are informal proofs toward [Open Question 1.9 of Chen–Grochow–Qiao–Tang–Zhang](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5).

| Route | Exact result | Where it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Rank-drop hyperplanes](DecisionRoute.md) | Recover the full determinant coset with polynomially many ordinary TI decisions when the rational factor normals span one physical mode. Use maximal minors through rank tests. For split square determinants, recover even repeated factors deterministically by univariate probes. One explicit balanced even-dimensional scalar-stabilizer family has a deterministic oracle-free orbit recognizer. | The factor normals span. General maximal-minor extraction is randomized. The explicit growing family meets the raw scalar-stabilizer benchmark. |
| [Coupled four-layer words](LayerRoute.md) | Replace the factor search by a single transporter in a matrix algebra and account for the common intermediate states through the word identity. With appropriate factor widths, one ordinary TI query enforces the exact residual determinants or a prescribed base-field determinant tuple. | The matrix algebra is supplied. Determinant-active semisimple matrix degrees are logarithmic. Radicals and extension-field degrees can be large. |
| [Projective product through point counting](ArithmeticRoute.md) | Evaluate the previous exponential projective product by exact counts on a bounded number of variables over the coefficient field. This gives a deterministic one-instance reduction on the promised nonsingular branches. | The determinant factors have bounded absolute degree and essential width. The raw coefficient image admits bounded-bandwidth additive equations, and the finite field is fixed with the stated coprimality condition. |
| [Frobenius forms on singular pencils](FrobeniusFormRoute.md) | Recover an unbounded-degree formal factor by interpolation and read all three determinants from a linear form and a bilinear form attached to the same transporter. | The high-multiplicity hyperplane, nonsingular alternating part, and stated congruences in the side lengths hold. The explicit growing family has every base-field slice singular. |

The federated search included the original character-assembly, Frobenius, torsion and marking results, alongside linked source catalogs. The prior Moore-determinant circuit does provide a succinct determinant test. Its compilation into an ordinary TI instance remains open, so the proofs here use alternative data on which the required transporter action is explicit. The raw balanced example is a concrete instance where exact pins become cheap because the tensor provides a polynomial list of destinations. The general search still lacks a guarantee that such a list, or one of the other determinant readers, exists for each arbitrary pair. The four-layer proof avoids unknown intermediate states on its supplied-algebra domain, and the unrestricted case continues to include the full matrix algebra with unbounded determinant-active degree.

Exact finite controls checked the balanced shift-family stabilizer at side four, the full four-layer coverage in a dual-number example, and the bordered determinant and covariance formulas under raw rebasings in the singular Frobenius family. The new proofs are informal, and publication priority for their specific combinations is unverified.
