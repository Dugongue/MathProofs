## The strongest consequence of combining the routes

There are pairs of smooth projective rational varieties over the rationals with the **same Tate Chow motive** whose reductions are isomorphic at any prescribed rational density of good primes between zero and one. For positive density the examples can be chosen geometrically isomorphic. One can specify the exact power-closed Frobenius profile for each positive-density example, including all finite extensions of each good residue field. There are also smooth projective rational varieties whose entire everywhere-local genus is any prescribed finite abelian group. Within each such genus, every form is itself rational and has the same Tate motive.

For the first assertion, take the rational tensor pair realizing the profile in the arithmetic proof. Apply the smooth blowup construction with the same padded format. This construction is defined by a fixed configuration of rational base points, linear spaces, and the standard and graph Segres, so it spreads out over the integers after inverting the finitely many denominators in the tensor data. The same seven base points remain distinct in every characteristic. The projective transporter theorem applies over each remaining residue extension. Thus all isomorphism tests, including the distinction between geometric and rational isomorphism, are exactly those of the tensor pair. The motive and extension-point-count conclusions follow from the blowup formula within one format. The density-zero examples can use geometrically inequivalent tensors of the same format.

For the local-genus assertion, use the explicit tensor realizing the prescribed finite abelian group from the arithmetic proof. Choose consecutive padded dimensions for the two input factors. The projective automorphism calculation in the geometric proof splits off precisely the central scalar factor removed by the Segre projectivization. Galois descent therefore identifies **all** rational forms of the blowup with the rational tensor forms, compatibly with every completion. The arithmetic local-genus calculation transfers exactly through this bijection. The result concerns ordinary TI and abstract geometric isomorphism. It gives no runtime bound for finding the geometric isomorphism.

The Frobenius profile also governs isomorphism over unramified local rings at good finite places. To see this, spread out the quotient of the tensor transporter by the connected stabilizer to a finite étale component scheme, as in the arithmetic proof. For the ring of Witt vectors of a finite residue extension, a fraction-field point maps to a component point that extends to the ring by properness of the finite scheme. Hence a fraction-field solution gives a residue component. A residue component yields a residue transporter by Lang's theorem. The transporter is smooth at good places, so this point lifts through all finite Witt truncations and to the complete ring. This relates exact modular profiles to ordinary TI over these unramified rings and fields. ## How the four proofs were assembled

- The geometric proof starts from the prior survey's intrinsic projective-factor recovery. It switches to seven disjoint smooth centers, recognizes their blowdown through the nef cone and canonical divisor, and recovers the actual tensor equation from the graph of its Segre restriction.
- The arithmetic proof combines Burnside marks, Chebotarev and the earlier explicit tensor transporter for algebraic-group torsors. Tate–Shafarevich calculations determine the local genera. No search for modular witnesses is part of the existence proof.
- The wide-block algorithm combines Peirce normalization with short-support linear relations and reduced-norm determinant calculations. The graph stores only the relations sufficient to reconstruct its block maps, even when a block contains exponentially many vectors.
- The rank-one algorithm combines the paired point-configuration view and short relations with the well-established compound determinant test of CP decomposition. The finite-field adjoint recovers rank-one lines from an arbitrary slice basis. [Exact finite-field control](check_rank_one.py)
