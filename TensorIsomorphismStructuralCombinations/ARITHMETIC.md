# Arithmetic profiles of rational tensor isomorphism

## Results and scope

1. Let E/Q be a finite Galois extension with group Γ. Every conjugacy-invariant subset S of Γ that contains the identity and is closed under taking powers is the exact modular isomorphism profile of two rational three-tensors. There is a common finite exceptional set of primes. For every p outside it and every j ≥ 1, their reductions are isomorphic over F_(p^j) exactly when Frob_p^j belongs to S. The tensors can be multiplication tensors of finite étale Q-algebras of dimension at most max(1,|Γ|²). They are geometrically isomorphic.
2. Consequently the possible densities of yes primes for ordinary TI are exactly the rational numbers in [0,1]. Density zero is equivalent to geometric nonisomorphism. Every positive rational density is attainable by geometrically isomorphic pairs, and a density a/b has a family with side O(b⁶). Density one is also attainable by rationally inequivalent pairs.
3. For each r ≥ 1 there are 2^r explicit pairwise inequivalent rational three-tensors of side 56+272r, all mutually isomorphic over every completion of Q. These are exactly the rational orbits in the local genus of the base tensor. Their stabilizers are geometrically connected. The profile in item 1 can be combined with these families, with total side bounded by 56+272r+16|Γ|².
4. Every finite abelian group B occurs as the local genus of an explicitly described rational three-tensor with geometrically connected stabilizer, with the group law furnished by the Tate–Shafarevich group of a torus. If B is a product of cyclic groups of orders m_i ≥ 2, the tensor side is O(1+Σ_i m_i² log m_i). The side bound is in the numeric orders and omits the coefficient height of the chosen splitting fields.

The proofs use the companion [transporter construction](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/IsomorphismAndProofComplexityCrossovers/torsors.md) for the local-genus claims. The modular profile and density proofs below are independent of it.

The construction can also realize the same profiles and local genera for determinant-one tensor isomorphism by adding one unused coordinate in each mode. The proof below uses ordinary three-tensor transporters first.

## Exact prescription of a Frobenius profile

For a finite Γ-set X and a subgroup C let m_C(X)=|X^C|. For a cyclic subgroup H of Γ put

$$
 Z_H=\sum_{D\le H}\mu([H:D])|D|[\Gamma/D],
$$

as a virtual Γ-set. The function μ is the ordinary number-theoretic Möbius function. For any subgroup C, the mark of Γ/D is the number of x in Γ for which x⁻¹Cx ≤ D, divided by |D|. Thus for each x in the formula for m_C(Z_H), its contribution is zero unless x⁻¹Cx≤H and is then the sum of μ([H:D]) over x⁻¹Cx≤D≤H. The latter sum equals one if x⁻¹Cx=H and zero otherwise, by Möbius inversion in the divisor lattice of the cyclic group H. It follows that

$$
 m_C(Z_H)=
 \begin{cases}
 |N_\Gamma(H)|,& C\text{ conjugate to }H,\\
 0,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
$$

This is the cyclic case of the classical Gluck–Yoshida idempotent formula, here with a direct counting proof. [Bouc on the Gluck–Yoshida formula](https://www.lamfa.u-picardie.fr/bouc/units-beamer.pdf)

Choose one H from each conjugacy class of cyclic subgroups with generators outside S. The choice is well-defined because any two generators are powers of each other. Take Z=Σ_H Z_H and split the positive and negative orbit terms as X−Y. Since 1∈S the trivial subgroup is omitted and |X|=|Y|. The total size of the two sets is at most

$$
 |\Gamma|\sum_H 2^{\omega(|H|)}
 \le 2|\Gamma|\sum_H \varphi(|H|)
 \le 2|\Gamma|^2.
$$

For the first expression, multiply |D| in the coefficient by the coset cardinality |Γ|/|D| and count the nonzero values of μ. The first inequality uses 2^ω(n) ≤ 2φ(n), from the prime factors of n. For the last inequality, different cyclic subgroups have disjoint sets of generators. If the sum is empty take X=Y to be a one-point set.

For g∈Γ, the restrictions of X and Y to the cyclic group generated by g are isomorphic exactly when the fixed point counts of all powers of g agree. Indeed the fixed point counts give the cycle counts by divisor Möbius inversion. If g∈S, all powers of g are in S, and the construction gives the required equalities. If g∉S, the mark at the subgroup generated by g already differs.

An orbit Γ/D corresponds to the finite étale algebra E^D, and disjoint unions correspond to direct products of algebras. Use integral models given by products of rings of integers, which are finite étale after inverting the ramified primes. At an unramified prime p, reduction to F_(p^j) restricts the Γ-set to the cyclic permutation generated by Frob_p^j. Equivalently, the cycle lengths give the degrees of the factors of the reduced finite étale algebra. The abstract isomorphism of these algebras is therefore equivalent to membership of Frob_p^j in S. [Stacks Project on finite étale Galois sets](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03QR)

For unital associative algebras, an isotopy f(x)g(y)=h(xy) exists exactly when the algebras are isomorphic. Indeed a=f(1) and b=g(1) are units because their multiplication operators are invertible, and φ(x)=a⁻¹h(x)b⁻¹ is an algebra isomorphism. Conversely an algebra isomorphism supplies all three maps. In coordinates, independent invertible changes of the three modes of the multiplication tensor are precisely isotopies, with inverses or transposes depending on the choice of duals. This finishes the proof of item 1. The tensors are geometrically isomorphic because both algebras become products of the same number of copies of the algebraic closure. [MathProofs isotopy normalization](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/IsomorphismCrossoversRoundThree/proof-complexity.md)

A small test case gives density 2/3 on six-dimensional spaces. Let E be the S_3-splitting field of x³−2 and let S consist of the identity and transpositions. There is one excluded cyclic class H=A_3, so Z_H=3[Γ/A_3]−[Γ/1]. Thus take the multiplication tensors of Q(√−3)^3 and E. For unramified primes, a transposition splits both étale reductions into three quadratic factors, an identity splits both completely, and a 3-cycle distinguishes them.

## Completeness of the density spectrum

For any fixed rational tensor pair, its transporter is a scheme of finite type. If it has empty geometric generic fiber, its coordinate ring over Q is zero. Spreading the equations out and inverting one nonzero integer therefore gives empty fibers at every remaining prime. This includes tensor shape mismatches, which can be excluded directly.

If the geometric transporter is nonempty, it is a torsor under the tensor stabilizer. In characteristic zero the stabilizer is smooth. Invert finitely many primes so that the transporter components form a finite étale scheme and each fiber over a component is a torsor under a smooth geometrically connected group. At every good finite prime Lang's theorem makes a component with a rational point of the component scheme contain an actual rational transporter. Thus a modular yes algorithm is testing whether the Frobenius permutation on the finite geometric component set has a fixed point. Its yes set is closed under powers and contains the identity. By Chebotarev its density is positive and rational, namely the fraction of elements with a fixed point in a finite Galois group through which the component action factors. Over F_(p^j) the same description uses the jth power. [Lang](https://wstein.org/papers/bib/Lang-Algebraic_Groups_Over_Finite_Fields.pdf), [Chebotarev and local splitting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.01956)

Conversely let 0<a<b. Choose M=2^s with b²≤M<2b² and Γ=C_b×(C₂)^s. This group is realized by a cyclic cyclotomic subfield of degree b and s independent quadratic fields ramified at disjoint new odd primes. The cyclic field can be taken in Q(ζ_ℓ) for ℓ≡1 mod b. The intersection with the multiquadratic field is trivial because the degree-b field's nontrivial subfields ramify at ℓ and the multiquadratic field is unramified there.

Write aM=kb+t with 0≤t<b. Then k≥1 and k+t≤M, since M−k≥(b−a)M/b≥b. Choose k full C_b-fibers over (C₂)^s, including the fiber over the zero vector, and choose t more singleton points (1,h) over other vectors. The union has size aM. A power preserves a binary vector or sends it to zero. Inside a full fiber it stays in a full fiber, and a power of a singleton stays in that singleton or goes to (1,0). This proves power closure. Item 1 and Chebotarev give the desired density a/b, with tensor side at most |Γ|²<4b⁶. Density 1 is given by any equal pair or by the distinct local-genus pair below. Geometrically inequivalent tensors give density 0. The density assertions classify a property of fixed pairs. They do not bound the first distinguishing prime or compute the corresponding field from arbitrary input.

## An explicit exponential local genus

Take the biquadratic E=Q(√−3,√13), for which 3 is an everywhere-local norm and fails to be a rational norm. The previous explicit construction used a bilinear algebra D_c whose underlying space has the direct sum of a marked left identity e, idempotent lines Qa and Qb, and the four positive exterior powers of E. Right multiplication by e marks those summands by distinct nonzero scalars. Multiplication by a on the degree-one E copy is primitive-element multiplication. The product ab is c times a fixed generator of the top exterior power. Exterior multiplication connects all four exterior summands. This gives transporter N_E⁻¹(c′/c) from D_c to D_c′, since fixing e,a,b forces the map on E to commute with primitive-element multiplication and the top wedge requires determinant c′/c. The dimension is 18. [Earlier explicit norm-torsor construction](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/IsomorphismAndProofComplexityCrossovers/torsors.md), [Macedo–Newton on the Hasse norm principle](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004121000268)

For ε∈{0,1}^r take r such blocks with c_i=3^(ε_i) and one shared left identity. The right multiplication tags are pairwise distinct and nonzero across the entire algebra. Cross products between the nonidentity parts of different blocks are zero. The shared left identity is unique, since right multiplication by it is injective. Hence every isomorphism preserves every tagged block and every pair of marked idempotents. The transporter between two vectors of blocks is exactly the product of the individual norm transporters, functorially over fields. The new dimension is 1+17r.

Apply the previous nine-vertex construction to a bilinear algebra of dimension d. It gives an associative unital algebra of dimension 40+16d, whose multiplication tensor has transporter isomorphic to the original algebra transporter times a split rational factor. The tensor side here is 56+272r. It follows immediately that the displayed tensors are all locally isomorphic and pairwise rationally inequivalent. The split rational factors have rational points over all fields. All stabilizers are geometrically connected because the norm-one tori and the additional factors are connected.

To see that the family already accounts for the whole genus, use the rational form classification. For a rational tensor T with stabilizer H in the product of general linear groups, Hilbert 90 identifies the rational orbits in the geometric orbit with H¹(Q,H). The same holds over every completion. In the nine-vertex construction H projects with a section to the automorphism group J of the marked bilinear algebra. Its kernel has a split unipotent filtration and a reductive quotient that is a product of split tori and general linear groups. The section conjugations fix the quotient factors. This follows directly from the coordinates of the construction. The two units that occur in isotopy normalization have diagonal quotient (G_m)^9 and unipotent kernel. The inner frame normalizations come from 1 plus the radical. After fixing the frame the extra two main scalars and the tag scalars are split torus factors. The free tag maps have general linear diagonal blocks and additive off-diagonal blocks. The section acts as the specified automorphism on the copies of the bilinear algebra and does not change these quotient blocks. Thus every twist of the kernel by a J-cocycle also has trivial H¹ in characteristic zero, by unipotent cohomology and Hilbert 90. The split projection induces a bijection H¹(Q,H)→H¹(Q,J), compatible at all places. Here J is the r-fold product of norm-one tori. [Earlier transporter construction and coordinate description](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/IsomorphismAndProofComplexityCrossovers/torsors.md), [Galois cohomology and twisting](https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~jean-louis.colliot-thelene/BGgroup_book.pdf)

For this torus the cocharacter module is the augmentation ideal I in Z[C₂×C₂]. The augmentation sequence, restricted to a subgroup D, has a free permutation module in the middle, so H₁(D,I)≅H₂(D,Z). Every decomposition subgroup of E/Q is cyclic. This is automatic at unramified and archimedean places. At 3 the field Q(√13) splits, and at 13 the field Q(√−3) splits. Thus the local groups H₂(D,Z) vanish. The global group is H₂(C₂×C₂,Z)≅C₂. The Tate–Shafarevich cokernel formula now gives exactly 2^r rational orbits in the local genus. [Borovoi–Kaletha–Hinich, Corollary 8.3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.04120v4#S8.SS3)

One can impose a prescribed modular profile between two such families. Add one more marked summand to the shared-left-identity algebra and give it the multiplication table of A_X or A_Y from item 1. Give this summand a separate right-eigenvalue tag and zero cross products. Then its isomorphism condition is independent of the norm block conditions. At every good finite prime and in all its finite extensions the finite étale rank-four norms are surjective, so the outlying norm blocks place no restriction there. The resulting two families each contain 2^r rationally distinct tensors that are all locally isomorphic within their family. Cross-family modular equivalence has exactly the prescribed profile. For a proper S all cross-family pairs are rationally inequivalent. The claim of exact genus size 2^r above is for the construction without the added summand.

## Realizing every finite abelian group as a full local genus

For m≥2 choose ℓ≡1 mod m and take F_ℓ to be the cyclic degree-m subfield of Q(ζ_ℓ). Choose a different prime q splitting completely in the splitting field of x^m−ℓ and in Q(ζ_(mℓ)), using Chebotarev. Then q≡1 mod m and q≡1 mod ℓ, and ℓ is an mth power modulo q. Let F_q denote the cyclic degree-m subfield of Q(ζ_q) in this paragraph. The fields F_ℓ and F_q intersect trivially, by ramification. Their composite E has Galois group C_m². The prime q splits in F_ℓ, while ℓ splits in F_q because the kernel of the quotient (Z/qZ)^×→C_m consists of the mth powers. Consequently all decomposition groups in E/Q are cyclic, including at the two ramified places. By the same augmentation-sequence and homology argument above, the norm-one torus has Tate–Shafarevich group H₂(C_m²,Z)≅C_m. Standard existence theorems for Hasse-norm failures enter here through a direct cyclotomic construction. [Frei–Loughran–Newton](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.02518), [Borovoi–Kaletha–Hinich](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.04120v4#S8.SS3)

Here is a compact marked bilinear algebra realizing the norm-one torus for this E. Let n=m². Keep one left identity and two marked idempotents a,b. Use copies of the n-dimensional vector space E as registers, with a separate eigenspace tag from right multiplication by the left identity for each register and idempotent line. The first E copy has a·x=θx, where θ is a primitive element. This forces any isomorphism to act on that input register as x↦zx for z∈(E⊗K)^× over any field extension K/Q.

Choose generators σ,τ of C_m². For η equal to σ or τ and a register scalar w define P_j(w)=∏_(0≤k<j)η^k(w). Binary expansion computes P_m(w) from P_1(w)=w by
$$
P_{2j}(w)=P_j(w)\eta^j(P_j(w)),\qquad
P_{2j+1}(w)=P_{2j}(w)\eta^{2j}(w).
$$
Each step has a fresh output register. Implement a gate from input registers X,Y to output register Z and shift η^k by the bilinear product (x,y)↦xη^k(y) in Z. If the input register maps multiply by scalars u,v, multiplication compatibility, evaluated at y=1 and all x, uniquely forces the output map to multiply by uη^k(v). This is also sufficient. In the binary scheme each ordered pair of domain registers occurs at most once. The doubling gates use (P_j,P_j), while an odd step uses the newly made P_(2j) and the chain's input. The τ-chain starts at the fresh output of the σ-chain and uses fresh remaining registers. Thus these products define a bilinear algebra, with all unspecified products zero except the identity and tag actions already fixed.

The final register multiplier equals Norm_τ(Norm_σ(z))=N_(E/Q)(z). Require a·b=c·1 in that register. The isomorphism equation then forces N_(E/Q)(z)=c′/c, and no further restrictions arise. With c=c′=1 the algebra's automorphism group is exactly the norm-one torus. Only O(log m) registers are needed. The resulting bilinear algebra dimension is O(m² log m), and the field construction and all its operators are exact even if their coefficient heights are large.

For a product B=∏_i C_(m_i), use one shared-left-identity tagged sum of the base algebras for the factors. Apply the nine-vertex map and the same twisted-kernel cohomology calculation. The resulting tensor has geometrically connected stabilizer and full local genus in bijection with B, with identity the base rational orbit. The group structure comes from the explicit product of torus Tate–Shafarevich groups. The trivial group is obtained using a tensor with split connected stabilizer, such as a one-dimensional nonzero multiplication tensor. This proves the claimed side bound and the fourth result.

## Determinant-one padding

Add one unused coordinate in each of the three modes. Over each field considered here a GL transporter on the original spaces extends by one scalar in each new coordinate so all three determinants become one. Conversely, a padded transporter preserves the intrinsic mode supports, defined as the images of contraction from the other two dual modes. Restrict to these supports and extend to each original mode to get an ordinary transporter. For any tensor in the geometric orbit of the padded one, the support dimensions agree with the original padded support dimensions. Each mode retains a nonzero complement, so the determinant correction works for every GL transporter in that orbit. This also identifies the full local genera in the resulting SL problem. The result addresses these padded formats and does not give a determinant-sensitive reduction of arbitrary SL-TI to ordinary TI.

## Prior work and evidence

The Burnside-ring idempotents, Galois-set classification, Tate–Shafarevich calculation and Chebotarev and Lang theorems are established tools. The new deductions in this run are the prescription of whole modular TI profiles, the density spectrum, the explicit linear-side amplification of the local genus, and the quantitative group realization. Closest identified TI prior work includes Grochow–Qiao–Stange–Sun on tensor isomorphism over rings and the existing MathProofs Hasse and torsor examples. Nearby arithmetic prior work uses Frobenius to characterize primes at which a polynomial has a root. Priority for the stated TI consequences remains unestablished. These proofs are informal.
