# Tensor Frame Recovery

- [Raw coordinate recovery for weighted four-tensors](PROOFS.md) uses realigned flattening intertwiners to recover candidate frames. It gives the sharp counting exponent $2/3$ on the weighted cyclic template over fixed fields of order greater than two, plus $O(n)$ total dimension for weighted group-table supports in characteristic $p$ under the stated principal-block and coprimality conditions.
- [Compact invariants for arbitrary cyclic covariants](CYCLIC-QUOTIENT.md) reaches the ambient counting exponent even with nonunit payloads and repeated characteristic roots, conditional on computing modewise cyclic covariants.

The proofs are informal. Both are restricted advances toward Grochow–Qiao Question 7.8.
