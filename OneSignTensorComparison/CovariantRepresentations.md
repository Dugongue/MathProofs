# One ordinary comparison from an intrinsic Pfaffian covariant

Work over F = F_3 with explicit tensors. In the tested mode let U_T be the raw coordinate space of an input T. A transporter g has tested mode map g_U. We prove a sufficient criterion with one deterministic polynomial-size ordinary TI comparison and give a raw family with arbitrarily large determinant-relevant modules. Both flexible and rigid inputs occur in this family.

## An objectwise Pfaffian construction

Suppose that from T one can compute an explicit linked representation W_T of polynomial dimension. By linked we mean that a bounded-order tensor diagram forces each ordinary transporter to act on W_T by a determined map ρ(g), and that every transporter extends to this diagram. Require det ρ(g) = det g_U in the displayed source and target frames. The choice W_T = U_T works. For even-dimensional U_T another choice is W_T = Λ² U_T in its standard ordered coordinate frame, since det(Λ² g_U) = (det g_U)^(dim U_T−1). Attach the actual wedge bilinear map if using that choice, which forces the action on its spanned output.

Assume W_T has even dimension and has a polynomial-time computable space Q_T of alternating forms, with at least one nondegenerate form and dim Q_T = O(log L), where L is the explicit input length. Require covariance in the exact sense that the pullback along ρ(g) takes Q_S bijectively to Q_T for every ordinary transporter from T to S. Use the raw coordinate Pfaffian with the fixed order of coordinates on W_T. Write P_T for the finite, unordered set of actual forms B in Q_T with Pf(B)=1. This set is polynomially enumerable because Q_T contains at most 3^(dim Q_T) forms. Compute each Pfaffian by alternating elimination, tracking basis-change determinants. The identity Pf(h^t B h)=det(h) Pf(B) holds in characteristic three. For example it is a polynomial identity over the integers, proved in characteristic zero by top exterior multiplication and then specialized.

Mark Q_T by the actual evaluation map Q_T × W_T × W_T → F. Also mark P_T as an unordered family. Form the split coordinate algebra with one primitive idempotent for each element of P_T, and include a linear map taking that idempotent to its form in Q_T. Add one extra idempotent sent to the zero form, so this algebra is nonempty even when P_T is empty. Mark the algebra by its actual multiplication and unit. Its automorphisms permute primitive idempotents, and the evaluation marks require the permutation to match the actual forms. The zero idempotent label cannot mix with nonzero form labels. The scalar output of evaluation is pinned by its standard basis vector. Every genuine transporter with positive tested determinant extends, since its pullback preserves both Q and the Pfaffian values. Conversely, a negative determinant interchanges the Pfaffian +1 and −1 subsets. If one positive subset is nonempty, a negative map cannot pull the marked positive subsets onto one another. If both positive subsets are empty, then the promise of a nondegenerate form makes the negative subsets nonempty, so a negative map would force a positive form in the other space, a contradiction.

Thus the objectwise diagram map reflects and preserves exactly the one-sign equivalence relation. Its number of tensor components is fixed for W_T=U_T or W_T=Λ² U_T: the whole unordered family was encoded by one coordinate algebra and one linear map. Apply the fixed-signature ordinary tensor-system compiler to obtain one ordinary TI pair. There is no need for disjunction packing or any special construction for flexible inputs. [Ordinary tensor-system isomorphism compiler, Theorem 4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S4)

A source of Q_T is the invariant alternating form space of the tested projection of the commutator Lie algebra [Der(T),Der(T)]. Solve linear equations X^tB+BX=0 for a basis of that projection. The derivation equations are linear on the raw coefficients. The projection of the commutator algebra is transporter-equivariant by conjugation, which gives the required covariance. The same procedure works in a linked representation with an explicitly computable induced Lie action.

## A raw unbounded family where both flexible and rigid cases occur

Take U=F^(2m), m≥2, and any nondegenerate alternating form J. Consider the bilinear tensor

    T(x,y) = (xy, J(x,y)) ∈ Sym²(U) ⊕ F,

where xy is the symmetric product. Inputs can be independently rebased in all modes; no common basis or form is supplied to the algorithm. The tensor is concise. The symmetric products span Sym²(U), and subtracting the product in the opposite input order isolates the nonzero alternating coordinate since 2 is invertible.

A derivation is a triple (A,B,C) with C T(x,y)=T(Ax,y)+T(x,By). Compare this equation with swapped arguments. On a J-orthogonal pair x,y the two tensor evaluations coincide, and subtraction in the symmetric output gives

    ((A−B)x)y = x((A−B)y).

For independent x,y, the linear forms x,y in Sym(U) are coprime, so (A−B)x belongs to Fx. For every nonzero x choose y independent of x in its J-orthogonal hyperplane, which is possible in dimension at least four. Thus every line is an eigenline and A−B is a scalar. In the swapped equation on all pairs, the symmetric terms therefore cancel. The scalar output gives

    J(Ax,y)+J(x,Ay) = λ J(x,y)

for some λ. Conversely every A satisfying this equation, and every B differing from A by a scalar, gives a derivation. On the symmetric output use the induced infinitesimal symmetric-square action, adjusted by that scalar. On the alternating output use the conformal multiplier, adjusted by the same scalar.

The projected commutator algebra is precisely sp(U,J). The conformal symplectic algebra is sp(U,J) plus the scalar algebra, since 2 is invertible. Each map N_z(x)=J(z,x)z lies in sp(U,J) and is a commutator up to a factor 2. Take a hyperbolic pair (z,w) and use the diagonal map with weights +1 and −1 on that pair and zero on its orthogonal complement. Its bracket with N_z is 2N_z. The N_z span sp(U,J), because the identification with Sym²(U) via J sends the squares z² to these operators, and the squares span the symmetric square in odd characteristic.

A form B invariant under all N_z satisfies B(z,y)=0 whenever J(z,y)=0: choose x with J(z,x)=1 and evaluate the infinitesimal invariance on (x,y). Hence for each nonzero z the functional B(z,−) is a scalar multiple of J(z,−). Since J is nondegenerate and B is bilinear, these scalars coincide, by comparing independent z,z' and their sum. Consequently Q_T = FJ in the original coordinates. This is intrinsic and is recovered by the linear calculation even after arbitrary rebasings.

If m is even, the Pfaffian identity forces every autotopism to have positive tested determinant, because the multiplier on FJ contributes its m-th power. A negative reflection of one raw mode gives an ordinary-isomorphic pair with no positive transporter. If m is odd, scaling one vector of each of the m hyperbolic pairs by −1 is a similitude of multiplier −1 and determinant −1. Its simultaneous action in both input modes extends via the symmetric-square action and the multiplier on the alternating output. This gives a negative-sign autotopism directly. The same marked compiler applies to both parities.

All pair-adjoint algebras are scalar. For the two input modes this follows from comparing (Ax)y=x(By) on independent x,y as above, then using all pairs. For an output-matching relation T(Ax,y)=C T(x,y), swap independent J-orthogonal arguments to obtain the same coprime-linear-form conclusion for A. Spanning of the output then forces C to be the same scalar, and likewise for the other input. The intrinsic centroid is therefore scalar. Simultaneous symplectic actions on the inputs and their induced action on the output give tensor automorphisms. The symplectic group acts transitively on all nonzero vectors: extend a nonzero vector to a hyperbolic pair and complete to a symplectic basis. The same argument works over every extension of F. Thus any nonempty invariant projective list in either input mode contains the entire projective space.

The associative envelope of the derived algebra on the tested mode is the full matrix algebra with natural multiplicity one. Indeed N_z N_w is the rank-one map x↦J(z,w)J(w,x)z. For any nonzero z, the w with J(z,w) nonzero span U, so these products linearly generate all rank-one maps. The one-sign invariant thus includes an arbitrarily large determinant-relevant natural factor on the rigid members, and the test uses neither small projective lists nor a small exterior determinant space. ## A bound on uniform low-degree orientation marks

There is an elementary obstruction to one restricted style of universal marker over F_3. Suppose auxiliary actions are obtained from the natural U and its dual by equivariant subquotients of mixed tensor words. Give each word its total number of natural and dual slots as degree. Assume every fixed tensor relation, including its auxiliary input and output slots and any duals, has total degree less than n=dim U. Suppose the relations are fixed by the prescribed lifts of all determinant-one g, where those lifts are the actions induced by the stated words on the subquotients. Then a coordinate reflection fixes every relation under its corresponding induced action. Consequently these low-degree marks alone cannot distinguish the sign while accepting all determinant-one matrices.

In fact the full diagonal sign subgroup acts semisimply because its order is a power of two. Its weight characters are described by a binary vector of length n. Dual slots contribute the same binary character as primal slots. Every weight in a word of degree less than n has support of cardinality less than n, and subquotients introduce no new weights. The determinant-one diagonal sign subgroup is the kernel of the all-ones character. A weight that is trivial on this kernel is either zero or all-ones. The degree condition excludes all-ones, so every invariant relation has only weight zero. This proves the assertion for every coordinate reflection. The conclusion is scoped to the stated uniform lifts and degree bound. The covariant Pfaffian compiler uses input-dependent forms and explicitly computed numerical Pfaffians, so it falls outside those hypotheses.

The constructions and proofs in this note are informal. They provide a kernel reduction on the stated covariant class and handle both signs without globally enumerating transporters. Unrestricted one-sign TI still requires a universal compiler or a different criterion covering tensors without these alternating covariants. Priority for these exact tensor statements has not been established.
