# Ordinary tensor isomorphism for determinant tests from small lists of forms and operators

**Theorem.** Consider explicit 3-tensors over the field F of three elements. Test the determinant of the transporter in a designated mode U, leaving the other two mode maps unrestricted. Suppose a polynomial-time procedure computes on the designated mode a polynomial-size list of tuples of alternating forms, preserved under the induced action of all ordinary transporters. If one can choose from the source list a tuple whose successive radical restrictions leave a space of logarithmic dimension, the one-sign comparison reduces deterministically to one ordinary TI pair.

A second sufficient condition uses covariant polynomial-size lists of endomorphisms of the designated mode. It suffices to find one source endomorphism for which, in its rational Jordan decomposition, the multiplicity of every odd-length primary block type is logarithmic. Lengths and irreducible degrees themselves can be arbitrarily large. Every even primary length contributes a positive sign regardless of multiplicity.

The comparison in either theorem may use distinct source and target lists. A list is covariant if every ordinary transporter between the raw tensors takes the whole source list bijectively onto the full target list, by pushforward on forms or conjugation on operators. There is no requirement that an element of a list be fixed by all automorphisms, that the list be a single orbit, or that the input tensor be supplied with a transporter or an orientation mark. The reductions below work for flexible and rigid inputs under the same stated condition. These are informal theorems.

## Marked systems and assembly

A marked system keeps the original three-tensor and adds forms, maps, and auxiliary spaces in a mixed tensor diagram. A transporter must preserve all listed data. Linear inclusions and quotients encode its restrictions and induced maps. A bilinear form on a space is a map from two copies of that space to a fixed scalar line. An endomorphism gives a linear intertwining mark. The prior constant-signature packing represents polynomially many such marks by a constant number of tensors, using separately labelled summands and link maps. The ordinary mixed-system compiler therefore reduces each marked query to an ordinary 3-TI pair. The disjunction construction in this packet then produces a single ordinary TI pair for the union of polynomially many branches. [Uniform packing](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/SLAdjointMultiplicities/PRIOR-UNIFORM-PACKING.md) · [Chen, Grochow, Qiao, Tang and Zhang](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S4) · [Disjunction](Disjunction.md)

A determinant on a b-dimensional space is an ordinary mixed-system mark of size polynomial in 2^b. Include its full exterior algebra and the degree-one inclusion. Algebra compatibility forces the induced action on all products of degree-one vectors and thus on the top line. Mark the top line with the required multiplier by including the corresponding two volume vectors in the paired diagrams. In dimension zero the acting determinant is one. Determinants of tensors written in covariant instead of contravariant mode coordinates are reciprocal; their F-sign is unchanged.

## Successive alternating radicals

Fix one source tuple of forms α₁,…,α_t on U. Set U₀=U and U_i to the radical of the restriction of α_i to U_(i−1). Quotient U_(i−1) by U_i. The induced alternating form is nondegenerate, and its dimension is even. The quotient may have dimension zero. Test every target tuple β in the supplied list by an ordinary marked query, adding the requirement that the physical transporter takes each α_i to β_i. Construct the target radicals W_i by the same linear algebra and discard a branch if any paired filtration dimensions differ.

For a transporter that respects a branch, choose paired adapted frames of U and W and quotient frames obtained from them. Write Q_i and Q'_i for the alternating matrices on the two successive quotients in those frames. The induced quotient map g_i satisfies g_iᵗ Q'_i g_i=Q_i. The Pfaffian congruence identity therefore gives

    det(g_i) = Pf(Q_i)/Pf(Q'_i).

Both Pfaffians are nonzero, and each is computable by ordinary elimination for alternating forms. Empty quotients contribute one. For example, the elimination can choose a nonzero off-diagonal entry, put its two vectors first, and clear their pairings against the complement by a block-unitriangular change. The Pfaffian of the resulting block diagonal matrix is the pivot times the complement Pfaffian. Basis swaps contribute their known signs. This gives a polynomial-time procedure and uses the Pfaffian identity with integer coefficients, so no division by a factorial is needed in characteristic three.

The desired sign of the physical map is thus the product of these quotient contributions, the known ratio of the two full adapted-frame determinants, and the determinant of the map on U_t. Use the full exterior algebra only for U_t and its counterpart. Choosing the multiplier on that top line to cancel the known factors is exactly the determinant-one condition. Equivariance of the computed restrictions means that every genuine transporter preserving the tuple and satisfying the determinant condition extends to this marked system. Conversely every system transporter restricts to such a genuine transporter. The disjunction over β gives the asserted single pair. An arbitrary genuine transporter takes the chosen source tuple to a target tuple on the list, so the disjunction loses no witness.

In particular one alternating form of corank O(log L), for input length L, suffices. More generally a constant number of restrictions can leave a small final radical even when no individual form on the full space has large rank. One source of raw covariant lists is the subspace of alternating forms invariant under the designated projection of the full tensor derivation Lie algebra, its commutator subalgebra, or another computable covariant space of mode endomorphisms. Solve XᵗQ+QX=0 for all X in its computed basis. Conjugation of the operator space by every transporter forces the required covariance of the space of forms. A dimension bound O(log L) makes listing all its elements, or tuples of any fixed length, polynomial. A near-full-rank form and all candidate final radicals can then be found by testing the displayed finite lists. This establishes a raw polynomial-time recognizer for this sufficient case. A large dimension for the solution space may prevent this enumeration.

## Rational primary lengths of a marked operator

Choose a source endomorphism M from a raw covariant list and compare it in turn with each target endomorphism N in the other list. Mark the relation gM=Ng on the physical mode, so all further computations use one and the same transporter. Rational canonical bases and primary decompositions are computable over F. Compare the two module types over F[t], where t acts by the selected endomorphism. Discard a branch when these types differ. For each monic irreducible f and length ℓ in the decomposition, let E_f=F[t]/(f) and let m_(f,ℓ) be the number of chains of length ℓ with irreducible f. On the f-primary component V_f set

    Q_(f,ℓ) = ker(f(M)^ℓ) / (ker(f(M)^(ℓ−1)) + f(M) ker(f(M)^(ℓ+1)).

The denominator is contained in the numerator. This quotient is naturally an E_f-vector space of dimension m_(f,ℓ). On a single chain of length j<ℓ the first denominator term is already the whole kernel. When j>ℓ the second denominator term covers that kernel. When j=ℓ exactly one E_f-copy survives. These statements follow directly by taking powers of f in the module F[t]/(f^j). The induced map on Q_(f,ℓ) is E_f-linear because it commutes with t.

Use matched rational canonical frames from the two endpoints. The determinant of the physical g equals their known frame ratio times

    ∏_(f,ℓ) Norm_(E_f/F)(det_(E_f)(g_(f,ℓ)))^ℓ,

where g_(f,ℓ) is the map on the corresponding Q quotient in the induced frames. To prove the formula, use the decreasing filtration by powers of f on V_f. In f^r V_f / f^(r+1) V_f, a map of a chain of length ℓ into a longer chain of length j>ℓ has image divisible by f^(j−ℓ) even before multiplication by f^r. It is therefore zero on that layer. After ordering by chain lengths, the map on each layer is block triangular. Its diagonal block for length ℓ is the same matrix of leading E_f coefficients whenever r<ℓ. This block is precisely the map on Q_(f,ℓ), in the bases given by the corresponding bottom representatives of exact-length chains. As an F-linear map its determinant is the field norm of its E_f-determinant. Multiplication over the ℓ layers where it occurs proves the formula. The actual input coordinates contribute just the known frame ratio.

Over F, every even-length factor in the formula equals one. For each surviving odd ℓ, attach the exterior algebra of Q_(f,ℓ) over E_f to the marked diagram, including its actual kernel and quotient maps. Read the norm of its top-line scale by linking scalar copies via the explicit F-linear Frobenius operations and multiplying the conjugate scales. Alternatively compile the same top-line scale through the restriction-of-scalars norm diagram used in the earlier flags theorem. Multiply the resulting output scales in a fixed F-line, and mark the multiplier needed to cancel the frame ratio. This attaches no determinant gadget to the even lengths. The size of every exponential construction here is [E_f:F] times 2^(m_(f,ℓ)), rather than 2^([E_f:F]m_(f,ℓ)) or exponential in the length of the chain. For m_(f,ℓ)=O(log L) for every odd ℓ, the system has polynomial bit size. The original module and all auxiliary maps are retained, so feasibility is precisely a real raw tensor transporter with the desired sign. Again combine the target branches by ordinary TI disjunction. [Prior quotient determinant and norm diagrams](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/IntrinsicTensorSubspaces/Flags.md)

For an explicit source of endomorphism lists, take a computed covariant linear subspace of mode operators of dimension O(log L), for example a small center or projection of the full derivation algebra. All its field elements can be enumerated over F. If an endomorphism with all nonzero primary multiplicities on even lengths is found, its branches require only one endomorphism mark and a frame-ratio check. This case allows arbitrarily large primary components. Odd lengths are handled with the logarithmic-width condition above.

## Other marked bilinear relations

A nondegenerate bilinear pairing between two distinct marked spaces gives the product of their map determinants, by taking determinants of the paired congruence equation. A rectangular pairing gives the same product after dividing each side by its appropriate radical, and those quotient maps can be marked. Such relations can join either construction. If linear combinations of the available calibrated sign equations isolate the tested sign, then the other unresolved determinant factors can be discarded. If a short auxiliary list still leaves small factors, attach the exterior algebra to those factors. A symmetric form on a single space supplies only the square of its acting determinant, so that mark by itself does not distinguish the two signs in F. An accompanying endomorphism can supply the missing information through the rational primary lengths.

## Scope

Each result is a pair-to-pair many-one reduction on its stated raw-input family. Neither requires knowing in advance whether a tested sign is available from the stabilizer. Accordingly the same reduction applied to a tensor and its reflected copy tests flexibility on every covered tensor. The constructions allow arbitrary large marked quotients but need the stated recoverable lists and determinant widths. They do not establish that arbitrary tensors have these lists, and the unrestricted one-sign problem remains open. Publication priority for the combinations is unverified.
