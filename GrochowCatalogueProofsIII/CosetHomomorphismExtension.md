# GP-0380 · Coset-constrained homomorphism extension at index two

**Theorem.** For finite permutation-group inputs with subgroup generators and marked target cosets supplied explicitly by generators and representatives, homomorphism extendibility is NP-complete. This holds with source $G=C_2$, subgroup $M=1$ of index two, a single marked generator, and ambient target the full symmetric group. At index one it is decidable in polynomial time.

We first build a fixed group for an AND gate. In $D_8$, use reflection generators $\alpha,\beta$ with $\alpha^2=\beta^2=1$ and central commutator $\zeta$ of order two. Let $t$ generate $C_4$, and form the central product
$$
 J=(D_8\times C_4)/\langle(\zeta,t^2)\rangle.
$$
In $J$ the images of $\zeta$ and $t^2$ agree. The quotient by $\langle\zeta\rangle$ is $F_2^3$ with coordinates $(a,b,c)$ on the images of $\alpha,\beta,t$. Every lift of these coordinates squares to $\zeta^{ab+c}$, because $t$ is central, the two reflections have square one, and changing the central lift does not affect the square. Thus the equation for this component to square to one is $c=ab$. For a unary square check use a $C_4$ factor: in its order-two quotient a lift of the bit $u$ has square equal to the central involution to the power $u$.

Convert a 3-SAT instance to satisfiability of a Boolean circuit with binary AND and unary NOT gates. Include the bits of internal gate outputs among the Boolean variables. Let $P$ be a product of one $J$ for each AND gate, one $C_4$ for each NOT gate, and one additional $C_4$ for the designated output. Let $W$ be the elementary abelian central subgroup given by the distinguished involutions of the factors. Use the coordinate space $P/W$ to encode the following affine map of the Boolean variables:

- The three coordinates for $z=xy$ are $(x,y,z)$.
- The coordinate for $z=1+x$ is $1+x+z$.
- The designated output coordinate is $1+z$.

Write the affine map as $u\mapsto b+Au$. For each column of $A$, choose an explicit lift to $P$ from the standard words of the constant-size factors, and let $L$ be the subgroup generated by these lifts. Choose $g\in P$ lifting $b$. Since $P/W$ is elementary abelian and the quotient is a homomorphism, the values on the coset $gL$ are exactly $b+\operatorname{im}A$. The square of each member is determined by those coordinates because $W$ is central of exponent two. The three types of component checks show that $gL$ contains an element whose square is one if and only if the circuit has a satisfying assignment.

Give every constant-size factor its regular permutation action on its own disjoint block. The product embeds in $S_m$ with $m$ linear in the gate count, and the listed generators and representative of $gL$ are polynomial-size permutations. The marked generator of $C_2$ can be sent precisely to those elements whose square is one. This establishes the reduction with subgroup $1$ and a single target coset. It uses a square test inside an affinely specified coset, whereas the related portfolio reduction asks for an onto map to a fixed dihedral target. [Earlier dihedral epimorphism construction](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/d7366c4348bc03f0e93cb8674db191de32ba58bf/GrochowCatalogueProofs/DihedralEpimorphism.md)

For membership in NP in the full permutation-group model, add the subgroup generators and all marked generators to the supplied generating list for $G$. Guess images of the enlarged list. Test membership in the target and the prescribed cosets by polynomial-time permutation group membership. Test the defining relators using the polynomial-size straight-line presentation of a permutation group on a supplied generating list. Agreement with the fixed homomorphism on the supplied subgroup generators guarantees agreement on the whole subgroup. At index one the fixed homomorphism already determines the answer, and the coset tests can be evaluated directly; in the formulation where marks are allowed only outside $M$, there are then no such marks. [Homomorphism Extension, Proposition 8.1 and Theorem 8.8](https://arxiv.org/html/1802.08656#S8)
