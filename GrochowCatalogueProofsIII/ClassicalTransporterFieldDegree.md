# Exact transporter fields for rational orthogonal and unitary diagonal orbits

**GP-0147, lower bound and an exact family.** For every n≥2 there are rational tensors S,T of format (4n)×(4n)×(4n) with a real product-orthogonal transporter, such that the least number-field degree carrying either an orthogonal or a complex unitary transporter is exactly n!. In fact the lower bound holds even if the witness matrices are merely invertible. This proves factorial growth in the local dimension up to the constant factor in the format. Sharp worst-case upper bounds for arbitrary classical-group tensor orbits remain open here. This is a written proof.

## A rational model of an orthonormal idempotent frame

Choose a totally real degree-n number field E whose Galois closure L has group S_n. Such fields are obtained by choosing a monic polynomial with three square-free reductions of factorization types (n), (n−1,1), and (2,1,…,1), using distinct primes larger than n. For n=2 the type (n) already suffices. Choose the coefficients to satisfy those congruences while remaining at bounded distance from the corresponding coefficients of ∏_{i=1}^n(X−iM), for an integer M→∞. Rescaling X=MZ yields a polynomial converging coefficientwise to ∏_{i=1}^n(Z−i), so for large M all its roots are real and distinct. The n-cycle and (n−1)-cycle imply 2-transitivity, since the group is transitive and one point stabilizer acts transitively on the other n−1 points. Conjugates of the transposition then include every transposition. The reduced factorization types yield the indicated cycle types by the usual Frobenius factorization theorem. Thus the Galois group is S_n.

The rational form (x,y)↦Tr_{E/Q}(xy) is positive definite since all embeddings are real. Diagonalize it over Q. Every positive rational diagonal entry m/k with positive integers m,k is a sum of four rational squares, by expressing mk as a sum of four integer squares and dividing each square's argument by k. Consequently the trace space embeds by a rational isometry J into standard Q^{4n}.

Write e_1,…,e_n for the primitive idempotents of E⊗_Q L≅L^n. These form an orthonormal L-basis for the extended trace form. Set v_i=J(e_i)∈L^{4n}, and let u_i be the first n standard coordinate vectors in the ambient space. Define
```
S = Σ_i u_i⊗u_i⊗u_i,       T = Σ_i v_i⊗v_i⊗v_i.
```
All Galois automorphisms of L permute the primitive idempotents, so T has rational coefficients. The two families (u_i) and (v_i) are each orthonormal. By Witt extension over L, the isometry from span(u_i) to span(v_i) carrying u_i to v_i extends to an ambient orthogonal transformation over L. Since L is totally real, the resulting orthogonal transporter is also unitary under the standard complex Hermitian form.

## Every transporter recovers the whole splitting field

The rank-one lines in the matrix slice span of T over an algebraic overfield are exactly the lines spanned by v_i v_iᵗ. Indeed the slice span is spanned by the n such matrices, with independent left and right vectors, so the rank of a diagonal coefficient combination is the number of its nonzero coefficients. The same description holds for S. Any product-general-linear transporter over a number field K sends the individual rank-one slice lines of S to those of T, hence the image lines K v_i of all those rank-one matrices are individually defined over K. More explicitly, a nonzero matrix on each line is defined over K and a nonzero column gives a K-vector proportional to v_i.

There is a rational linear functional on the ambient space extending Tr_{E/Q}∘J^{-1} on the image of J. Its scalar extension has value 1 on every v_i, since multiplication by the primitive idempotent has trace 1 on the split algebra. Normalizing any K-rational vector on each of these lines by this functional shows that each v_i actually has coordinates in K. Thus each e_i is K-rational in the fixed rational basis of E. Let α be a generator of E. After extending scalars, the scalar by which α multiplies e_i is the corresponding root of its defining polynomial, and it belongs to K. All roots therefore lie in K, forcing L⊆K inside the chosen algebraic closure. Since [L:Q]=n!, this lower bound matches the L-witness.

The project previously obtained factorial degree for ordinary GL transporters of finite étale multiplication tensors using a coefficient field generated by transcendental parameters. The rational trace-form embedding above realizes the factorial requirement for actual standard-form O/U transporters of rational inputs.
