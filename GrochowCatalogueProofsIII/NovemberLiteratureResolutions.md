# Three November 2026 literature resolutions

These entries have primary-source written solutions. They are credited literature resolutions. Where an author provides a Lean check, distinguish the checked finite certificate from the whole theorem.

## GP-0112. The asymptotic subrank of Dicke tensors

Zhu, Liu, Zou and Nie prove that for all positive multiplicities d_1,…,d_s with k=Σ_i d_i, the asymptotic subrank of the coefficient-one Dicke tensor over C is 2^{H₂(p)}, where p_i=d_i/k. They prove the same limit for coordinate restrictions, and their finite-power upper bound is k·2^{NH₂(p)}. The result preserves all k tensor modes and expressly includes repeated species and s≥3. Their proof is a November 2026 manuscript available from the authors' site. [The Entropy Law for the Asymptotic Subrank of Dicke Tensors](https://katewenqizhu.github.io/assets/pdf/tensor-rank/2026-11/tr28-paper.pdf).

**Field extension.** In fact the coefficient-one statement holds over every field. The paper's lower bound constructs an induced matching in the unweighted support of every suitable tensor power. It depends only on the coordinate words and on an auxiliary hashing prime, which is unrelated to the coefficient field. Each surviving entry is 1 over every field, so the very same coordinate restriction gives the same subrank bound. For the upper bound let L_N consist of length-N words w whose product probability ∏_j p_{w_j} is at least 2^{-NH₂(p)}. Then |L_N|≤2^{NH₂(p)}. On each support edge of the Kronecker power, the product of the k word probabilities is 2^{-NkH₂(p)} because each column has multiplicities d_i. Thus the support is covered by the slices indexed by L_N in each of its k modes. This gives slice rank at most k|L_N| over any coefficient field. Subrank is bounded by slice rank, since slice rank does not increase under independent linear maps and a diagonal tensor with nonzero diagonal has slice rank equal to its length over any field. For the diagonal fact, it suffices to pass to an infinite extension, which can only decrease slice rank. If r_j slices are used on mode j of a length-D diagonal, they give annihilator subspaces U_j of codimension at most r_j in the dual coordinate spaces, and the diagonal form vanishes on their product. For subspaces A,B of a D-dimensional coordinate space over an infinite field, the span of the coordinatewise products has dimension at least dim A + dim B − D. Choose one vector in A with support the union of all supports in A, using that the field is infinite. This support has at least dim A coordinates, and multiplying vectors of B by that fixed vector kills a subspace of dimension at most D−dim A. Iterate the bound for the first k−1 spaces U_j and take the dot pairing with U_k. Vanishing is impossible when Σ_j r_j<D. This proves the diagonal fact and therefore the field-independent conclusion on taking Nth roots. This uses the tensor with coefficient one on each support word. This convention is relevant in small characteristic, where translating from a symmetrized polynomial can introduce zero multinomial scalars.

## GP-0114. Reducibility of an asymptotic-rank sublevel

Zhu, Chen and Nie give a counterexample in C³⊗C³⊗C³⊗C² at threshold 62^{1/3}. Their first witness x is the tensor for the six permutations of three symbols with a fixed fourth coordinate. Their second witness y is the two-term diagonal tensor, so the fourth flattening rank of y is two. In an ambient Zariski-open neighborhood of x, the flattening equations of the sublevel force fourth flattening rank at most one. The sublevel is consequently the union of two proper closed subsets, the fourth-rank-one locus within it and the complement of that open neighborhood within it. The proof answers the all-orders question negatively. Its counterexample has order four. The manuscript reports Lean checks for finite algebraic identities and geometry, while the asymptotic spectrum and Zariski argument are presented in prose. [Asymptotic Tensor-Rank Sublevels Need Not Be Irreducible, Theorem 1.1](https://katewenqizhu.github.io/assets/pdf/tensor-rank/2026-11/tr23-paper.pdf).

## GP-0197. Oeding's Cohen–Macaulayness question

Zhu and Nie give a negative answer even for three Segre factors. Their Theorem 1.1 gives failure of Serre S2 for the affine cone of the complex secant variety in dimensions a×b×c, at rank r, whenever r≥5, a,b≥3, a+b≥r+2 and c≥r. In particular (a,b,c,r)=(3,4,5,5) is a counterexample to the universal Cohen–Macaulayness claim. The theorem gives an explicit integer witness of ordinary rank and border rank r. The manuscript reports Lean checks on finite witness calculations. The algebraic geometry of the infinite family is proved in prose. The same family consists of nonnormal varieties by Serre's criterion and thus supplies negative examples for GP-0224 and GP-0225, whose unrestricted classification questions remain open. [Secant Varieties of Segre Products Need Not Be Cohen–Macaulay, Theorem 1.1](https://katewenqizhu.github.io/assets/pdf/tensor-rank/2026-11/tr25-paper.pdf).
