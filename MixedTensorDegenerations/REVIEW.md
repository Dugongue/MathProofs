# Independent review

The directional degeneration proof and its smooth comparison received two independent mathematical reviews. One reviewer independently derived the path-algebra recovery argument before seeing the assembled proof. The reviews checked the top-degree relation, the commutator ideal and its constant-rank powers, the idempotent and arrow frame chart, the unital normalization of the bare tensor, and the treatment of dual slots and relation frames. The final chart uses the invariant rank-open locus, so all changes of basis are admitted, and the length bound covers the empty signature.

The counting proofs were reviewed using explicit block multiplication and independent checks of the cover and shear factors. In the shear step the corrections sum linearly, and their overlaps are counted by the single image denominator. The support proof was checked by constructing a compatible ambient extension. The spectral proof was checked by verifying the field-factor marks, one-occurrence Hilbert 90 step, and remaining congruence equations. The Polynomial Calculus proof was independently checked for degree and size directions and for both good-prime conditions of the original FGS argument.
