# What collapses in the four determinant tiers

Work over the field of three elements. The chain is ordinary tensor isomorphism, one-sign tensor isomorphism, two-sign tensor isomorphism, and special-linear tensor isomorphism. The forward arrows are deterministic polynomial-time many-one reductions. The signed three already have equal polynomial-time Turing closure by a constant number of sign probes. This packet does not prove or disprove an unrestricted reverse many-one arrow.

| Result | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Retain all three signs in one three-tensor format | A fixed finite-field tensor-system construction compiles polynomially many bounded-order marks while passing up to three designated determinants through to corresponding physical modes. If any one of the three desired signs can be enforced by an exact ordinary lock, the three-sign comparison reduces to one two-sign pair. The two-sign to one-sign consequence uses the earlier one-sign compiler. The remaining live modes may be large. [Proof](RetainedSigns.md) |
| A kernel collapse at the middle gap forces all four tiers to collapse for many-one reductions | If an independently computed object map reduces two signs to one, pack two one-sign inputs on separate axes and use three objects. A negative comparison generates three distinct one-sign classes, which cannot all lie in one ordinary orbit. Two ordinary queries are packed into one. The analogous five-object result for the upper gap was proved earlier. [Proof](KernelCollapse.md) |
| Exact cost of affine sign probes | Two-sign to one-sign takes exactly three probes. Three-sign to two-sign takes exactly three, and three-sign to one-sign takes seven. These minima hold with adaptive and affine probes. The hard profiles are realized by actual tensor pairs. Two randomized two-sign probes decide the three-sign test with one-sided false acceptance at most 2/7. [Proof](ProfileQueries.md) |
| Exponential dimension for exact lifting across every adjacent gap | There is a concise balanced family where every sufficiently small bounded-order typed gadget lifting all allowed source maps must pass a forbidden source tuple if it has fewer sign tests. An objectwise reduction on a maximally split orbit also has to send that orbit to at least two ordinary output orbits for each removed sign. These statements concern the specified construction models. [Proof](ExactLifts.md) |

The retained-sign construction synchronizes the whole marked system inside one algebra and linked left and right modules. The algebra and module determinants recover the three separate characters of the same original transporter after the gauges cancel. Thus the recent Pfaffian and related locks have a direct single-instance consequence for the highest rung as well as the middle rung. The remaining full decision problem requires an exact lock or another construction that preserves the existence of a witness on arbitrary raw inputs.

All proofs are informal. Primary sources and prior MathProofs constructions are named inside each note. Publication priority for the exact formulations remains unverified. [Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1)
