# Eleven results on permanents, group actions, and numerical linear algebra

1. **The first obstruction degree for symmetric permanent projection is \(p\).** In characteristic \(p\), every matrix-invariant polynomial of degree less than \(p\) has a symmetric projection using variables and constants. For \(n>p\), the homogeneous polynomial \(e_p\) of the \(n^2\) entries has no symmetric affine projection of any dimension. [Proof](complexity.md#4-the-first-degree-obstructing-symmetric-permanent-projection-is-p)
2. **Every field has a nonzero-entry \(4\times6\) matrix whose maximal permanents all vanish.** Explicit examples cover all characteristics and answer Kirkup's question for fields without a square root of \(-1\). [Proof](complexity.md#2-dense-four-by-six-matrices-with-all-maximal-permanents-zero-over-every-field)
3. **Sharp square-zero annihilator classification.** An explicit product parametrizes common annihilators in every degree of the commutative square-zero algebra. It classifies equality in both bounds in Yu's Exercise A5. [Proof](complexity.md#3-equality-in-the-square-zero-annihilator-bounds)
4. **The half-size permanental ideal has the minimum circuit-reduction degree of VP.** Its unrestricted linear-order permanent extraction assertion is equivalent to \(\mathsf{VP}=\mathsf{VNP}\), so that assertion remains open. The constant-depth division-free version is false by the permanent lower bound and Kirkup's monomial. [Proof](complexity.md#1-the-half-size-permanental-ideal-has-the-minimum-circuit-reduction-degree-of-vp)
5. **Exact characteristic-two moment cutoffs for every even polygon.** For the unnormalized group-sum tensors, the cutoff is four when even \(n\ge6\), five at \(n=4\), and infinite at \(n=2\) because every tensor vanishes. [Proof](invariants.md#sharp-characteristic-two-tensor-cutoffs-for-every-even-polygon)
6. **One generic real Gram graph determines every finite group in its regular representation.** The proof also applies to any faithful real orthogonal representation whose symmetric operators \(g+g^{-1}\) distinguish inverse pairs, even against arbitrary competing generic representations. [Proof](invariants.md#one-generic-real-gram-graph-recovers-groups-whose-inverse-pairs-are-distinguished)
7. **Finite linear groups can be recovered without an invariant inner product.** Over an infinite field, \(d+1\) generic orbit sets determine any finite subgroup of \(\mathrm{GL}_d\), and \(d\) suffice for nonscalar groups. The general \(d+1\) bound is sharp over \(\mathbb R\) or \(\mathbb C\) in the stated competing-action model. [Proof](invariants.md#recovering-finite-linear-groups-without-an-invariant-inner-product)
8. **Abelian permutation-group conjugacy has deterministic polynomial search-to-decision.** Characteristic prime tags enforce prescribed orbit matches for arbitrary groups. A finite-module isomorphism algorithm then returns an actual conjugating permutation for abelian groups of unrestricted rank and exponents. [Proof](groups.md#1-orbit-marked-conjugacy-and-conjugacy-search-for-abelian-permutation-groups)
9. **Minimum faithful permutation actions can be constructed in polynomial Cayley-table time for every odd-order nilpotent group.** The construction also handles all nilpotent groups whose Sylow \(2\)-subgroup has elementary abelian central quotient, with unbounded class and exponent in the odd Sylows. [Odd-prime proof](groups.md#3-removing-the-class-bound-on-odd-sylow-subgroups) · [Binary case](groups.md#2-minimum-faithful-permutation-representations-of-class-two-groups)
10. **Factor-one Green-operator partition selection.** For separable layered Green matrices, dynamic programming returns the exact Frobenius-optimal longitudinal hierarchical partition in the prescribed class, including a leaf budget. The algorithm computes all cut costs without forming the dense Green matrix. [Proof](green-partition.md)
11. **A sharp randomized-SVD error scale in the Gaussian mixture model.** At the least feasible width the worst-case fixed-confidence squared excess is of order \((\varepsilon+\delta)^2/\log(1/\delta)\) at target rank one. The untruncated range finder can miss \(1+O(\varepsilon)\) for every \(\delta=\varepsilon^\gamma\) with \(0<\gamma<1/2\). [Proof](gaussian-sketch.md)
