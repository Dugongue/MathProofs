# Isomorphism invariant zoo

This catalog adds twenty constructions to the early Rune, Ley, Sigil, Ward and Trellis work. Rune isolates the information that survives a change of gauge. Ley uses incidence to expose hidden coordinates. Sigil records an orbit under the actual allowed action. Ward and Trellis retain which observations share an index. These are design sources for the constructions below. Each chapter gives the mathematical definition, the precise permitted action, a cost bound or scope, a use or separating example, and literature antecedents. The proofs are informal and priority is unverified.

The most immediate TI applications are the exact principal determinant test, the extraction of raw contraction flags, and the apolar pin on the stated inputs. The compact permutation-group epimorphism result and the output-sensitive code reduction have independent complexity applications. Several entries deliberately adapt classical data such as Smith forms, tables of marks and apolar algebras. The contributions are the exact transfers, joint records, and proved consequences described in the individual entries.
