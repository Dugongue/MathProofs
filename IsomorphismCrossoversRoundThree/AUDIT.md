# Four isomorphism and complexity crossovers

These are AI-assisted informal proofs. The statements were compared to the cited primary sources, and the proofs were reviewed informally by a second worker. Publication priority for the proposed contributions is unestablished.

| Result | Exact scope and contribution |
| --- | --- |
| [Cayley group membership](group-membership.md) | Polynomial-size logspace-uniform Boolean circuits with depth $o(\log n)$ for subgroup membership in arbitrary Cayley tables. Depth $O(\log\log n)$ holds unconditionally for generated subgroups with bounded-rank nonabelian composition factors and conditionally in general under Babai's simple-group diameter conjecture. This addresses the bounded-rank case of Stober–Thumm–Weiß Question 39(2). |
| [Finite-field tensor descent](tensors.md) | A deterministic nonadaptive reduction from any 3-tensor isomorphism instance in a fixed characteristic to polynomially many extension-field instances of exactly the same format. All queries are literal scalar extensions. Two coprime extension degrees alone can both give false positives. The comparison concerns the direction from a smaller field to tunably larger fields of the same characteristic. |
| [Polynomial Calculus for Grigoriev multiplication tensors](proof-complexity.md) | Absolute constant-degree simulations in both directions between graph isomorphism and the bare multiplication-tensor encoding through Grigoriev's associative algebras. Ordinary Polynomial Calculus suffices in every field. This is a concrete variant of Galesi–Grochow–Pitassi–She Question 7.5. The precise compiler cited in that question has not been identified with this construction. |
| [Special-linear orbit closure intersection for pencils](geometry.md) | An efficient algebraic reduction to one fixed mixed-tensor signature for all $2\times m\times n$ formats, plus a deterministic polynomial bit-time decision algorithm on rational inputs. This covers the one-mode-dimension-two case of Lysikov–Walter Question I.13. Pervouchine established the pencil invariant classifications. The proposed contribution is the linear-side bounded-arity compiler for binary forms and its application to the classical classification. |

The sublogarithmic group bound also lifts to membership for every efficiently compressible finite-semigroup pseudovariety by Stober–Thumm–Weiß Theorem 31. The group theorem combines the short normal generators of Eberhard–Maini–Sabatini–Tracey with the solvable-group program of Stober–Thumm–Weiß and configuration reachability in Fleischer's circuit construction. The finite-field theorem combines Lang's theorem with the prime-cycle congruence on a finite component torsor and the affine Bézout bound. The proof-complexity result makes the explicitly invertible isotopy-to-isomorphism normalization degree-controlled. The pencil proof uses polynomial maps that implement an addition chain in symmetric powers, with closed marker orbits to preserve closure intersection.

Exact finite controls checked the tensor component-action identity and étale examples, the unital isotopy normalization on relabeled graph algebras in small fields, and the pencil determinant and symmetric-power identities on small cases. These are supplementary controls. The proofs justify the general statements. No Lean certification is claimed.

## Published questions

- [Stober–Thumm–Weiß Question 39(2)](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.04747v2#S12) asks for bounded-width polylogarithmic straight-line programs for efficiently compressible pseudovarieties. The group theorem gives an unconditional answer when their groups have bounded-rank nonabelian composition factors, along with a general conditional implication.
- [Galesi–Grochow–Pitassi–She Question 7.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S7.SS5) asks which listed graph-to-tensor reductions are low-degree equivalent. The bare-multiplication route yields a bidirectional comparison to the graph system with its own explicit tensor encoding.
- [Lysikov–Walter Question I.13](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04639v2) concerns special-linear orbit closure intersection in general. The pencil result covers all dimensions of the two large modes, with fixed dimension two in the other.
