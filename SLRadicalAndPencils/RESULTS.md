# Special-linear tensor isomorphism beyond étale multiplication

The strongest reduction here is deterministic and many-one over the same fixed finite field. It compiles special-linear isomorphism of a class of associative multiplication isotopes into one ordinary tensor comparison. The class includes algebras with arbitrarily long Jacobson-radical filtrations, different residue matrix sizes, and arbitrarily large determinant-invisible corners.

| Result | Scope | Reduction or algorithm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Matrix corners and radical grades](PeirceReduction.md) | Prescribed determinants for associative multiplication isotopes. The exponent of a corner is the product of its two residue matrix sizes. Only grades visible after genuine determinant corrections must admit small exterior constructions and a polynomial matching search. | One deterministic ordinary TI query over the same fixed field. A polynomial TI oracle reduction removes the bound on visible matchings. |
| [A TI-complete nonsemisimple class](PeirceReduction.md#a-recognizable-ti-complete-class-with-cube-zero-radical) | Raw multiplication isotopes with cube-zero radical and each residue matrix size divisible by $q-1$. | Both deterministic many-one directions, including ordinary TI hardness on this recognizable class. |
| [Matrix stabilization and square-zero radicals](RadicalMultiplication.md) | Arbitrary radical length on the matrix-compression locus. Every multiplication isotope with square-zero radical admits exact determinant-profile computation. | One ordinary query under the compression criterion. A polynomial colored-graph-isomorphism oracle reduction covers all square-zero radicals. The constructive proof specifies the field and randomness bounds. |
| [Two-slice tensors](MatrixPencils.md) | All $2\times m\times n$ tensors over explicitly represented finite fields, including singular, rectangular and zero pencils. Prescribed determinants on all three modes. | Deterministic polynomial bit time, including dependence polynomial in $\log q$. [Independent treatment of the exceptional root configurations](ExceptionalPencils.md). |
| [Fixed-width projective rank frames](FiniteRankFrames.md) | Any fixed number of slices with a finite geometric rank locus containing a projective frame. The first rank-drop locus can have positive dimension. | Deterministic polynomial bit time over explicitly represented finite fields. |
| [Variable-width rational rank frames](RankFrameOracle.md) | The number of slices and the field may vary. A complete rational rank locus containing a projective frame must be supplied or computable. The proof gives an explicit unbounded-width family that computes its own list. | Deterministic polynomial Turing reduction to ordinary TI over the same field. |

These are steps in the reverse direction of [Chen–Grochow–Qiao–Tang–Zhang, Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2). In the other direction, adding an unused line to each tensor mode lets ordinary transporters be rescaled to determinant one on the new lines. An isomorphism of the padded tensors preserves the three minimal supports. Restrict there and extend along any complements to recover ordinary isomorphism of the original tensors.

The reverse arguments are unconditional. The tensor-system compiler, associative-isotope normalization, module isomorphism and the determinant-line construction over fields are reused with the hypotheses given in the chapters. The new corner criterion uses the radical grades and their repetition exponents. The pencil algorithm avoids enumeration in $q$ on large fields. A previous fixed-field width-two result allowed a running time polynomial in $q$. The unrestricted reverse inclusion remains open here. Publication priority is unverified.
