# Four classification theorems for Yang–Baxter operators

These are informal proofs. The complexity assertions use the input fields and equivalence relations specified in each note. Publication priority is unverified.

1. [Directed orbit closure](Degenerations.md). Ordinary tensor degeneration embeds in the directed local degeneration order of involutive complex operators with flip-equivalent strand representations. This gives NP-hardness for rational descriptions and quadratic-length strict chains in a single representation class.
2. [Unitary gates over finite fields](FiniteUnitary.md). Over each fixed quadratic finite field, ordinary tensor isomorphism is equivalent on a constructed family to locally isometric classification of Hermitian-isometric involutions whose strand representations are isometrically conjugate to flips.
3. [Complex unitary gates in a hidden basis](HiddenBasisQueries.md). The exact number of channel queries needed for positive distinguishing advantage on graph sign gates equals half the minimum distinguishing graph-probe edge count, rounded up. One cycle of length twice t versus two cycles of length t gives a linear sharp threshold.
4. [Graph-equivalent raw operators](GraphEquivalence.md). Bounded individual slice-block dimension gives a polynomially recognizable GI-complete family over fixed finite fields, even with unbounded total carrier. Explicit graph instances also transfer automorphism counts.
