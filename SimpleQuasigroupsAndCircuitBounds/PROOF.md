# Simple quasigroup generators, Cayley circuit bounds, and graph multiplicities

These are AI-assisted informal proofs.

| Proof | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Simple quasigroups with many generators](SimpleQuasigroupsWithManyGenerators.md) | Answers Grochow–Kardeş–Levet's bounded-generation question in the negative. For every $m\ge 2$, an explicit simple loop of order $8^m$ requires exactly $2m$ generators. |
| [Depth-three Cayley circuit bounds](DepthThreeCayleyCircuits.md) | Both alternating depth-three gate orders require $2^{\Omega(n^\varepsilon)}$ gates at output fan-in at most $n^{1/2-\varepsilon}$. Two promises used in the depth-two lower bounds admit polynomial-size depth-three circuits. Unrestricted Question 7.2 remains open. |
| [Low-degree graph multiplicities](GraphSymmetries.md) | Computes exact multiplicity obstructions in $2^{O(d\log(d+2))}n^{O(1+d/\sqrt{\log(d+2)})}$ bit time. The same bound gives a compact separating-module witness. Explicit monomial-list output has a different cost. |
| [Modular group algebra factorization](GroupAlgebraExtension.md) | Proves the central-Frattini case of Carlson–Kovács over arbitrary coefficient fields of characteristic $p$, including unbounded Frattini exponent. |
| [Depth-two Cayley lower bound](GroupIsomorphismCNF.md) | Supporting proof of the earlier partial-table and random-relabeling bound used in the depth-three result. |
