# One-instance TI reductions with two scalar centroids

The main comparisons are over the original field \(\mathbb F_3\), with scalar centroid both for the input bimap and its full-support dual relation bimap.

- [Symplectic determinant record](SymplecticDeterminantRecord.md). Recover an intrinsic symplectic factor and read the sign from its similitude multiplier and a small exterior relation space. There are unbounded correction classes without a residual kernel bound.
- [Multicolor graph records](MulticolorDeterminantRecords.md). Recover hidden signed graph colors, and multiply their scalar actions with a triangular matrix algebra. The earlier one-color graph family also has two scalar centroids.
- [Cube kernel and first syzygies](CubeKernelSyzygies.md). Recover the old variables through the intrinsic cube map. First syzygies then give an exact sign record, including large sign-rigid examples with growing complement.
- [Cartesian graph orientation](CartesianGraphOrientation.md). Read the sign from intrinsic product frames and at most six edge-layer branches. The class contains a graph-isomorphism-hard subfamily.

The four results are written proofs. The unrestricted reduction remains open. The supporting parity identities in the symplectic record passed an independent Lean audit. [Lean source](SymplecticOrientation.lean) · [Audit](SymplecticOrientationAudit.json)
