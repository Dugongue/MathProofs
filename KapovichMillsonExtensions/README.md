# Extensions of Kapovich–Millson universality

These proofs start from the hyperbolic perfect-kernel construction for Question 8.2 and address further questions about the topology of the source and the algebraic structure of its representation schemes. [Kapovich–Millson, *On representation varieties of 3-manifold groups*](https://msp.org/gt/2017/21-4/gt-v21-n4-p01-p.pdf)

| Manuscript | Result |
| --- | --- |
| [Derived hyperbolic transfer](DerivedHyperbolicTransfer.md) | Every closed connected 3-manifold admits a hyperbolic replacement of the same orientability type with an equivalence on chains with all pulled-back local coefficients. The comparison identifies the entire derived Betti stack with an open-and-closed derived substack, preserving the transgressed shifted symplectic form in the oriented case. |
| [Projective-plane cone points](TwoConePoints.md) | Problem 4.3 has a negative answer. At least six cone points are needed for \(\mathbb Z^4\), and the necessary number is unbounded over finitely presented groups. A hyperbolic realization preserves any positive count already attainable topologically. The minimum for \(\mathbb Z^n\) is \((n-1)(n-2)\) when \(n=3\) or \(n\ge5\). |
| [Exact algebraic charts](ExactChart.md) | Arbitrary pointed affine schemes over \(\mathbb Q\) occur in exact Zariski product charts in closed hyperbolic \(\mathrm{SL}_2\)-representation schemes at representations with Zariski-dense image. The unrestricted dense-\(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) chart problem in Remark 1.5 remains open. |
| [Knot universality obstruction](KnotUniversality.md) | A three-generator square-zero germ, even with an arbitrary smooth factor, is excluded from the natural complex \(\mathrm{SL}_2\) character and representation schemes of knots in \(S^3\). This gives a negative answer to the knot-complement universality question in the companion slides. |

The manuscripts contain informal proofs. They rely on the cited results in low-dimensional topology, representation theory and deformation theory. The status of external review and priority remains provisional.
