# Four invariant transfers in isomorphism

These are four informal proof documents. The early invariant notes supply methods of organizing the proofs. Each document specifies the exact new mathematical step.

| Result | Transfer from the early notes | New statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Everywhere local cubic equivalence](Geometry.md) | **Ley** treated incidence points as intrinsic data. **Ward** and **Trellis** retained individual labels. | Hessian rank-one lines recover the primitive idempotents with their Galois action. Two explicit smooth integral cubics are equivalent over the reals and modulo every prime power and inequivalent over the rationals. This answers Agrawal–Saxena §6 negatively. In dimension $6m$, the same construction gives $2^m$ pairwise distinct smooth companions whose tensors are equivalent in all three modes by special orthogonal maps over every completion. |
| [Parallel subgroup descent](Groups.md) | **Ward** and **Trellis** retain the relation at each index. | Keep the orbit of each simple factor at each descent step and the evaluated value of every local group-program gate. This gives uniform deterministic NC for entire normal-closure, relative-commutator and exponent-central towers in arbitrary permutation-group quotients. |
| [Switching isomorphism with a shared phase unit](Phase.md) | **Rune** descends to the effective modulus. **Sigil** retains the projective switching orbit. **Ward** keeps overlapping congruences attached to their components. | A deterministic polynomial-time search algorithm handles disconnected bounded-degree gain graphs with an arbitrary binary modulus and one common unit. Smooth-order unit equations across different composite moduli are solved by gcd refinement without factoring the moduli. |
| [Determinant-constrained monomial tensor isomorphism](Determinant.md) | **Rune** retains each coefficient’s effective precision. **Sigil** motivates separating coordinate permutations from scalings. **Trellis** keeps the exponent constraints coupled across coordinates. | Over finite fields and explicit finite chain rings, arbitrary-support monomial 3-tensor equivalence with prescribed mode determinants polynomial-time Turing reduces to ordinary 3-tensor isomorphism. The input bases are supplied. |

The cubic counterexample gives a full answer to the stated Agrawal–Saxena question. Its arithmetic ingredients are classical, and priority for the cubic construction and the other algorithms remains unverified. The determinant theorem concerns monomial transporters and leaves the unrestricted special-linear tensor-isomorphism problem open. The group theorems concern the specified series and leave the general intermediate-generator problem open.
