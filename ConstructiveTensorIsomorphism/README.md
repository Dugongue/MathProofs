# Constructive tensor isomorphism and sparse refutations

Four results build on the deterministic average-case tensor algorithms. The factoring premise used below is that complete univariate factorization over all explicitly presented \(\mathbb F_q\) has deterministic bit complexity \(r^{O(\log(r+1))}(\log q)^{O(1)}\) in degree \(r\). [Preceding algorithms](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/tree/main/DeterministicAverageCaseTI).

1. [Sparse Polynomial Calculus for spectral tensors](Certificates.md). Every negative comparison from the connected spectral class of ordinary order-\(d\) tensors has a literal Polynomial Calculus refutation of degree at most \(8d\) and polynomial expanded size in the dense tensor length. There is also a sparse Nullstellensatz certificate of total-product degree \(O(d)\). The proofs need neither a factoring premise nor field equations. A witness-preserving compilation gives ordinary three-tensor families with polynomial sparse size and degree \(O(\log(r+1))\) in ordinary or border rank \(r\). For order-four inputs the compiled degree is constant. The ordinary three-tensor family has the induced source distribution from the even-order compilation. These are special cases of [Grochow and coauthors' degree and size questions](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S7).

2. [Deterministic bounded-mode search through the determinant quotient](Search.md). For \(a\times b\times k\) ordinary tensors with \(k\) fixed, the factoring premise gives deterministic quasipolynomial construction of all three witness matrices when the connected projected source stabilizer contains the determinant kernel of its matrix span. This covers nonsplit tori without a supplied splitting or basis, as well as some nonabelian groups. The preceding unrestricted bounded-mode result gives decision and extension-degree profiles, while the new search result requires the displayed stabilizer condition.

3. [Transporters with disconnected spectral support](Spectral.md). For squarefree equivariant mode covariants, the factoring premise gives deterministic quasipolynomial witness recovery whenever the relevant prime divisors of the support lattice torsion are polynomially bounded. This includes saturated support lattices and a parity family with two overlap components per mode. The algorithm also handles prescribed determinants by adding determinant rows. Exact counting works on the full spectral class under the factoring premise with polynomially bounded factor degrees, regardless of the sizes of the torsion primes. The construction works in local factor fields whose degrees are polynomial in the dense input size.

4. [Exact tensor lifting over finite local rings](Lifting.md). A residue Lie-rank condition gives one lift class up to scalars or a linear obstruction at every precision, with doubling corrections. It gives unconditional witnesses for the connected even-order residue class, and applies to supplied orthogonal residue witnesses under the stated added rank tests. Prescribed determinants can be decided and counted even with wild principal units. Under the factoring premise, the determinant step needs only residue-field root calls of prime degree bounded by the largest mode size. The proof also removes the previous tame restriction for simultaneous powered norms.

These are written mathematical proofs. [Averaging.lean](Averaging.lean) is a separately kernel-checked identity for the equivariant integer-matrix construction. Its axiom audit reports \(\texttt{propext}\), \(\texttt{Classical.choice}\), and \(\texttt{Quot.sound}\). The full algorithmic and proof-complexity theorems are outside that Lean audit.
