# Inversion in algebraic proofs of tensor nonisomorphism

These AI-assisted informal proofs extend the proof-line degree collapse for Polynomial Calculus with Inversion (PC+INV). The standard proof-line measure and the sizes of the matrix annotations are addressed explicitly.

| Result | Exact scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Exponential separation on concise multiplication tensors](concise-separation.md) | In each fixed positive characteristic, ordinary PC needs degree linear in the side length and exponentially many monomial occurrences on explicit split semisimple multiplication tensors. The same systems admit polynomial-size degree-five PC+INV refutations with one inversion. Every matrix entry of that inversion is an original variable or zero. |
| [Rank-sensitive simulation](rank-sensitive.md) | If the symbolic rank of $AB-I$ is $k$ and the sum of the two matrix-entry degree bounds is $d$, ordinary PC simulates an inversion in degree at most $(k+1)d$. Capping $k,d$ gives a polynomial sparse-size simulation. A related restricted ordered branching-program certificate model has deterministic verification. |
| [Sharpness and nonlinear pullbacks](bounded-rule.md) | Single inversion examples attain order $kd$ over every field. More generally, the minimum PC and Nullstellensatz degrees are preserved as weighted degrees under polynomial substitutions whose top homogeneous coordinate forms are a regular sequence. |
| [Transfer to associative, alternating and group equations](transfer.md) | Constant-degree algebraic interpretations transfer ordinary-PC lower bounds to restricted associative algebras, alternating trilinear forms, and the succinct isomorphism equations for special groups. The group equations use exponent $p$ for odd $p$ and exponent four for $p=2$. |
| [Exponential separation on succinct special groups](special-group-separation.md) | Explicit equal-order special groups in each fixed prime characteristic give exponential sparse ordinary-PC lower bounds and polynomial-size PC+INV proofs using one inversion with variable-or-zero entries. A Pfaffian argument derives the obstruction already over the algebraic closure. |
| [Eliminating a linear form from IPS certificates](geometric-ips.md) | Over a field containing $\mathbb F_q$, with all $x_i^q-x_i$ included as axioms, an IPS refutation of bounded degree in one supplied linear form in the original variables gives a Geometric IPS circuit of polynomial size in that degree and the original representation. |

The concise separation uses the earlier rectangle restriction for its lower bound. Its upper bound obtains a rectangular inverse from the standard tensor action equations: the output map of an isotopy must carry the matrix-factor subspace into the matrix-factor subspace. The polynomial derivation eliminates scalar target components. This makes the final inversion an explicit rank contradiction with polynomial total written size.

The rank-sensitive and sharpness arguments use different mechanisms. Vanishing large minors truncates the adjugate in the first. For the second, a free-module basis separates degrees after a nonlinear substitution, so projecting proof lines back to their coefficients preserves the degree filtration. The Geometric IPS argument uses the recurrence for linearized polynomials to avoid enumerating an additive orbit and evaluates its reciprocal polynomial on a companion matrix of side only the supplied degree bound. The field equations remain essential in this argument.

The closest primary sources and reused MathProofs results are cited in each proof. The scope of the original conjecture and the inversion inference was checked against the published definitions. Two independent workers checked the upper proof and the matching projection and restriction in the concise separation. The group statements refer to structure-constant equations and measure dimension on the succinct presentation scale. The general Geometric IPS simulation question remains open.

To replay the concise checks, run `python check_concise.py`. This checks the projected tensor action symbolically on five shapes, the scalar cancellation identities on 7,500 exact finite evaluations, and five instances of the selected padded inversion entry. Run `python check_group.py` for 722 block coefficient tuples and 750 decomposable Pfaffian evaluations. The companion degree-transfer note records a finite degree-control calculation. The finite checks support the symbolic arguments. All statements are informal and publication priority for the new claims is unestablished.
