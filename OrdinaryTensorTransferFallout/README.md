# Sharp proof bounds and arithmetic in ordinary tensor isomorphism

Four written proofs apply all-characteristic label erasure to the literal ordinary three-tensor equations. These placements are proposed for a Grochow briefing. The size parameter is the tensor side unless specified otherwise.

| Proposed placement | Result | Exact scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New #1 candidate** | [Sparse proof preservation at every degree](Mixed.md) | The ordered-path compiler transfers expanded PC in both directions at arbitrary degree, with constant-factor degree and sparse-size overhead plus polynomial preparation. The decoder for each block entry is one monomial in three literal tensor witness entries. Over each fixed odd $\mathbb F_q$, explicit ordinary tensors of rank $\Theta(N)$ have tight expanded PC size $N^{\Theta_q(N)}$ with field equations, versus polynomial-size Hilbert-like IPS. The broader compiled class of arbitrarily long lists of forms and operators has ordinary PC degree $O(qn)$ and small IPS with field equations, and constant raw degree for geometric nonisomorphism in characteristic zero. |
| **Top 5** | [One ordinary $0,1$ family hard over every field](Degree.md) | The very same pair is geometrically nonisomorphic in every characteristic, has rank $\Theta(N)$, needs raw PC degree $\Theta(N)$ and expanded size $2^{\Omega(N)}$, and has polynomial-size Hilbert-like IPS. A second family specifies exactly which characteristics admit witnesses and controls the negative proof resources at each selected prime, with a sharp $N^{\Theta(N^{1-\epsilon})}$ sparse regime for fixed $0<\epsilon<1$. |
| **Top 5** | [Exponential arithmetic thresholds and stabilizer component orders](Arithmetic.md) | A sparse ordinary pair over fixed $\mathbb F_7$, of side exactly $30k+64$, is isomorphic exactly in extension degrees divisible by $3^k$. Equal-characteristic congruence precision is also exponential, with an exact Greenberg lifting function. Every geometric stabilizer component element of any ordinary three-tensor has an exponential order bound in the sum of the mode dimensions, and the scale is attained. The same scalar network viewpoint gives linear raw PC degree, an exponential expanded PC upper bound, and polynomial-size IPS for arbitrary scalar-block outputs. |
| **Top 10–15** | [Short raw certificates with all three modes growing](Certificates.md) | Ordinary tensors encoding projective diagrams have polynomial-size Hilbert-like IPS and polynomial raw PC degree whenever each connected component has a bounded number of independent cycles. For one cycle, the extension fields accepting the original pair are determined exactly by selected irreducible factors of one Hom minor. This test gives polynomial-time search from the diagram presentations over $\mathbb Q$, and randomized polynomial-time search over finite fields. |

The first theorem gives a sharp size gap for the finite-field version of Question 7.2 and transfers the matrix results of Question 7.3 to ordinary TI. The all-field theorem gives raw lower bounds for Questions 7.1 and 7.2. The last theorem gives a raw polynomial upper bound on a structural class with all mode dimensions unbounded. General polynomial upper bounds in rank for raw ordinary TI and the full raw sparse-size problem remain open in this work. [Galesi–Grochow–Pitassi–She, §7](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S7)

The arithmetic proof gives a quantitative conclusion for base changes of a fixed ordinary TI instance, part of the across-fields question raised by Grochow and Qiao. It bounds the first accepting degree from above when the geometric component group is constant. [Grochow–Qiao, §10.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1907.00309#S10.SS2) · [TI over commutative rings](https://doi.org/10.1145/3717823.3718286)

The four manuscripts are written proofs. Lean independently checked the [corner projection and scalar normalization identities](TiedTransport.lean). The finite controls covered the mixed actions, arithmetic counts, field switches, and group-ring coefficients. The [block-erasure proof](Background/MarkerProof.md), [exact transporter proof](Background/ArithmeticProof.md), [quiver compiler](Background/DiagramProof.md), and [circuit transfer](Background/LabelProof.md) give the construction and bounds reused here. Priority for the transfers and the new quantitative conclusions remains provisional.
