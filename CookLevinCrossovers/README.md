# Eighteen results from the block Cook–Levin crossover

This package applies the epoch decomposition, low-incidence circuit constraints, and exact tensor contraction used by [Carmen Strassle and Bernardo Subercaseaux](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11832) to results in topology, arithmetic geometry, algebraic complexity, matrix completion, and quiver representations. The cited paper's deterministic simulation of nondeterminism is an input. The proofs here give exact geometric realizations, new algebraic classifications, stronger reductions, and algorithms in geometric parameters. All manuscripts are informal.

## The ten strongest results

The rank ranges are proposed positions against the existing portfolio. They reflect the scope of each statement and the strength of the advance over the earlier result. They are judgments, and priority remains provisional.

## Eight further proved statements

11. A bounded-time computation's accepting paths biject with the rational points of a single smooth geometrically integral affine hypersurface, with ambient dimension and degree both \(O(t)\). The circuit description can be constructed in \(2^{O(\sqrt t)}\operatorname{poly}(t)\) time. [Proof](AffineTransfer.md#1-one-linear-degree-smooth-hypersurface-for-bounded-time-computation)
12. A sparse triple cup form in a supplied basis gives an intrinsic \(2^{O(h/\sqrt{\log h})}\) exact unmarked Heisenberg counting algorithm, where \(h\) is the relevant first Betti number. This includes characteristic-two torsion contributions from repeated indices. [Proof](UnmarkedHeisenbergCounting.md#2-an-intrinsic-algorithm-when-the-cup-form-is-sparse)
13. Weighted accepting-path polynomials transfer to sparse pinned cut polynomials and fixed-group multiplication polynomials while preserving a linear total interface. [Proof](CircuitTransfer.md#3-weighted-block-cooklevin-via-sparse-cut-polynomials)
14. Anchored rank-two completions with nonzero incident entries are exactly a projective coloring count with explicit cycle constraints and multiplicative factor. Over \(\mathbb F_2\) this is an exact proper three-coloring count. [Proof](CompletionAndQuivers.md#2-completion-as-a-projective-coloring-with-cycle-constraints)
15. Quiver subrepresentations can be counted exactly with a \(2^{O(B/\sqrt{\log B})}\) exponent in the number \(B\) of basis or annihilator bits, when the stated local scopes have linear incidence and bounded maximum arity. Dense maps and arbitrary parallel-arrow multiplicity are allowed. [Proof](CompletionAndQuivers.md#3-exact-counting-of-subrepresentations-of-a-quiver)
16. One can encode the epochs of a nondeterministic computation into marked hyperbolic three-manifold instances, with \(O(t)\) Boolean interface variables and \(O(t\log b)\) first Betti number at block size \(b\). Counts on the supplied reduction family use the interface size. [Proof](GeometryTransfer.md#3-a-topological-form-of-generalized-cooklevin)
17. A hypothetical faster unmarked Heisenberg counter on triangulated hyperbolic manifolds transfers to nondeterministic time simulation with an explicit iterated-logarithm loss. [Proof](UnmarkedHeisenbergCounting.md#3-a-transfer-from-unmarked-manifold-counters-to-nondeterministic-simulation)
18. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), every prescribed set in \(m\) base coordinates, \(m\ge3\), has a derivative-one hypersurface realization of degree at most \(m+2\). For odd-size sets this degree is sharp among such equations with last coordinate zero on the lifted points. [Proof](AffineTransfer.md#2-explicit-finite-field-smoothing-and-finite-zeta-prefixes)

## Dependencies and verification

Galindo already implies general unmarked counting hardness under Turing reductions. The new claim is the explicit single-query decoder and linear first Betti number. De Rugy-Altherre obtained rational-field cut hardness. The field classification here concerns ordinary unpowered cuts, and the intermediate status over the three exceptions assumes the modular counting circuit separation stated in the manuscript. [Galindo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06984v2) · [De Rugy-Altherre](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.7641)

The subexponential running-time bounds use the cited exact counting algorithms. The global-field smoothing uses Poonen's singleton-point curves, and the efficient topological construction uses the fixed-exponent Heegaard twist argument. The displayed oracle implication in item 17 is conditional on the hypothesized algorithm. Mathematical review and exact finite computations checked the rank-selection gadget, small-field phase reductions, finite-field smoothing identities, and 393 projective completion instances over three fields. The full statements have informal proofs. [Strassle–Subercaseaux](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11832) · [Poonen](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3965) · [Ennes–Maria](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02814)
