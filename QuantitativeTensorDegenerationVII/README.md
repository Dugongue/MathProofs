# Quantitative tensor degeneration VII

- [Regular multiplication pencils](NonsemisimplePencils.md). Exact order-two criterion for regular pencils over algebraically closed fields of characteristic different from two. Additivity for length-at-most-two Jordan blocks and an explicit family over every field with dimension \(7^r\), exact order \(3r\), and Loewy degree \(2r\).
- [Cancellation of tensor catalysts](TensorCatalystBridge.md). Exact three-cap cancellation for concise scalar-paired-adjoint catalysts with scalar-center outer algebras, extended to semisimple paired adjoints for thin pairs.
- [Face-incidence threshold](FaceIncidenceAlgorithms.md). Polynomial-time cap optimization and a compact optimal witness at incidence two. NP-completeness at incidence three at a fixed cap on promised degenerating rank-three flag pairs.
- [Exact cubic transporter schemes](CubicTransporterSchemes.md). Realization of integer unit equations by sparse cubic equivalence over every commutative ring, with explicit transporter factors and prescribed determinants.
