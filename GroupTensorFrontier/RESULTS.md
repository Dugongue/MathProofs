# Minimum permutation degree, determinant kernels, and tensor descent

Four informal theorems. The reductions use explicit dense tensors or explicit permutation generators, as specified in each proof.

1. **Minimum faithful permutation degree is NP-complete.** This answers Kantor and Luks's Size of Representation Domain problem (§13, Problem 1) in the quotient-of-permutation-groups model. The hardness already holds with trivial quotient kernel, for class-two exponent-$p^2$ groups with displayed orbit sizes at most $p^3$, for every fixed odd $p$. At orbit size at most $p^2$, minimum faithful degree of these $p$-groups is computable in polynomial time, while ordinary abstract isomorphism already has exactly the degree of 3-TI over $\mathbb F_p$. The hard group isomorphism instances can have exponent $p$ and come with actions of minimum faithful degree. The $p^3$-orbit hardness also gives every fixed approximation factor from above below $2p/(p+1)$. [Minimum degree proof](GroupTransfer.md) · [Exponent-prime TI equivalence](ExponentPrime.md) · [Kantor and Luks, p. 532](https://pages.uoregon.edu/kantor/PAPERS/quotient.pdf#page=9)
2. **Determinant-preserving compression of arbitrary raw tensors.** Over each fixed finite field, reduce to a single tensor pair of side $O_q(r+w+1)$, where $r$ is the sum of the two sides of one copy of each distinct fixed-label indecomposable and $w$ is the compact label dimension. It preserves every attainable determinant triple, so the same compression works for ordinary, one-sign, two-sign and special-linear TI. The proof trims multiplicities modulo $q-1$ and encodes their markers by a geometrically recoverable pencil. [Proof](SignedKernel.md)
3. **Exponential extension degree for ordinary finite-field TI.** Over any fixed finite field, side $O(k)$ suffices for a pair whose admissible degrees are exactly the multiples of $\ell^k$, for a fixed auxiliary odd prime $\ell$ different from the characteristic. Binary threshold testing on geometrically equivalent pairs is NP-hard, with a stretched-exponential approximation gap. Computing whether one specified binary degree works remains polynomial on the canonical family used in the reduction. [Proof](FiniteFieldDescent.md)
4. **Determinants for local algebras with unrestricted residue degree.** For raw multiplication tensors over $\mathbb F_3$ with a one-dimensional radical square over the residue field, compute the entire determinant profile and witnesses in deterministic polynomial time. Mixed Frobenius types and singular products are allowed. A second theorem covers unbounded radical squares when the tangent pieces can be separated by their interacting types and each pair lands on one output line. [Proof](TwistedResidue.md)

The group classification uses the published general NP upper bound for the quotient model and a new lower bound with trivial quotient kernel. The other results concern exact scope stated above. In particular the determinant kernel preserves the restrictions in its output, and the signed isomorphism algorithm gives one-sign absorption when either conjunct is in its stated family. These are informal mathematical proofs with finite arithmetic and rank controls on selected identities. External review and publication priority remain pending. [Levet, Srivastava and Thakkar on the existing NP upper bound](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.16039v4#S1)
