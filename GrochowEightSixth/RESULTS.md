# Ten answers on invariant recovery, symmetric computation, and numerical linear algebra

## Results

1. **Cubic dihedral invariants over other fields.** For the natural action on an \(n\)-gon in characteristic prime to \(2n\), cubic polynomial invariants generically separate geometric orbits for every \(n\) except \(5\). The five-point cubic fiber has two dihedral orbits over both the complex and the real signals, and a quartic splits them. This gives a five-point correction to Edidin–Katz Theorem 2.5 and answers §5.2. [Proof](invariants-dihedral.md#polynomial-invariants-over-other-fields)
2. **Cubic unitary recovery.** The first three unitary moments generically recover complex signals on every regular \(n\)-gon, including the five-point case. This answers Edidin–Katz §5.1. [Proof](invariants-dihedral.md#unitary-cubic-recovery-on-complex-vectors)
3. **Orthogonal bispectrum reconstruction.** For the real bandlimited \(O(2)\) model with consecutive frequencies \(1,\ldots,k\) and \(k\ge 4\), reconstruction takes \(O(k)\) local tests involving a bounded number of quadratic roots. Rational invariant data admit exact polynomial-time reconstruction with arithmetic-expression output. This answers Edidin–Katz Remark 3.13. [Proof](invariants-dihedral.md#polynomial-time-reconstruction-from-the-orthogonal-bispectrum)
4. **Symmetric determinantal factorization over \(\mathbb F_2\).** A two-variable polynomial with a symmetric determinantal representation defeats all four square specializations in the Grenet–Monteil–Thomassé §7 factorization conjecture. [Proof](complexity.md#a-counterexample-to-the-characteristic-two-symmetric-determinantal-factorization-conjecture)
5. **Matrix-symmetric circuit classes in finite characteristic.** An explicit automorphism-counting family belongs to matrix-symmetric VNP and requires superpolynomial orbits in matrix-symmetric VP over every field, including characteristic two, under the independent row-and-column action. [Proof](complexity.md#matrix-symmetric-vnp-is-strictly-larger-than-matrix-symmetric-vp-in-every-characteristic)
6. **Algebraic and unitary moments.** The two kinds of moment admit strict differences in separating power in both directions. Unitary moments can save arbitrarily many degrees on explicit strata. Algebraic moments can save at most two degrees, and this bound is attained. This answers Edidin–Katz Remark II.7. [Proof](invariants.md#algebraic-and-unitary-moments-distinguish-different-complex-vectors)
7. **Modular cyclic spanning degree.** The largest Jordan block gives an exact formula for a cyclic \(p\)-group and a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm in the generator-matrix encoding. A separate algorithm covers mixed cyclic order in time polynomial in the group order and supplied block and character data. [Proof](invariants.md#a-closed-formula-for-the-modular-cyclic-spanning-degree)
8. **Spanning degree from succinct sign generators.** Computing the spanning degree of a diagonal elementary abelian \(2\)-group action specified by generators is polynomial-time equivalent to computing the covering radius of a binary linear code, with an equality for each instance. Its threshold version is complete for the second universal level of the polynomial hierarchy. [Proof](invariants.md#the-spanning-degree-in-a-succinct-encoding-is-a-covering-radius-problem)
9. **Least-squares confidence under zero-failure subspace injection.** A sharp dimension-unrestricted tail law gives a positive failure probability for every fixed \(1+C\varepsilon\) relative-error guarantee, even when the injection failure is zero. This settles the least-squares question about whether small injection failure suffices for high-confidence near-relative accuracy. [Proof](osi-confidence.md)
10. **GMRES with clustered transport channels.** A one-way coupled upwind model with a per-channel sweep has quantitative GMRES contraction in a number of steps equal to the number of channels, independently of spatial resolution. The norm estimate also covers defective and perturbed matrices. This gives a model requested in Workshop Problem 2.13. [Proof](upwind-gmres.md#gmres-for-one-way-coupled-transport-channels)

## Further results

- **Bounded central quotient.** Deterministic polynomial-time exact epimorphism counting and witness extraction for Cayley-table nilpotent targets of fixed class whose central quotient has bounded generating rank. The center may have arbitrary rank. [Proof](groups.md)
- **Modular norm replacement.** For an indecomposable \(n\)-dimensional \(C_p\)-module with \(p>3(n-1)\), an explicit transfer gives a lower-degree replacement for the degree-\(p\) norm when \(n\ge 4\). [Proof](invariants.md#replacing-the-high-degree-norm-in-modular-cyclic-field-generators)
- **GMRES convergence transition.** For the specified tridiagonal upwind discretization and inflow right-hand side, an exact residual law gives the crossover from algebraic to geometric decay. This supplies a one-dimensional model for Workshop Problem 2.14. [Proof](upwind-gmres.md#the-gmres-transition-in-an-upwind-model-with-reaction)
