# Which routes to special-linear tensor isomorphism can be ruled out?

These four results give unconditional obstructions to specific routes for reducing special-linear tensor isomorphism to ordinary tensor isomorphism. The exact exclusions and the constructions that escape them are in the linked proofs.

| Route | Proven exclusion | What survives this exclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Natural determinant-line diagrams](NaturalDeterminantGadgets.md) | With balanced representation weights, fixed orientation marks and equivariant data polynomials of degree at most $D<n$, a diagram capable of enforcing the determinant of an $n$-dimensional mode needs a slot with at least $2^{n-D}$ dense coordinates. The bound allows unbounded arity. For structural slots at arity $m$, the sharp largest-role bound is $\binom{n}{\lceil n/m\rceil}$ when the index is within the stated range. There is also a sparse weight-basis lower bound under the stated characteristic and width hypotheses. | Coupled data-dependent constraints outside the natural model, high-degree covariants, and succinct encodings outside the stated sparse model. |
| [Homogeneous maps and rank probes](HomogeneousMaps.md) | Any single homogeneous objectwise map collapses scalar multiples, even at unbounded degree and output dimension. For homogeneous blocks, an exact incidence-lattice test detects when the output block scalars absorb input scaling. Rank-only queries on the scalar pair are indistinguishable. | Mixed-degree coupling across shared blocks. A four-block bipartite cycle is the smallest one-label support that can defeat scalar absorption. This is a local test for a gadget, and the cycle alone gives no tensor-isomorphism reduction. |
| [Amplification and radical grades](AlgebraObstructions.md) | A fixed schedule of matrix amplifications reflects special-linear equivalence of all multiplication isotopes exactly when $\gcd(q-1,d_1,\ldots,d_s)=1$. Identical ordered grades, radical flags, radical-annihilator subspaces, unit determinants and calibrations can coexist with opposite special-linear answers. The examples agree modulo arbitrarily deep radical powers. Over odd fields the failure first occurs at radical length four. | A determinant-faithful encoding that retains filtered products. The Peirce approach based on unabridged multiplication can use grades to compute a character of a bona fide algebra map. |
| [Opaque transporter queries](OpaqueTransporters.md) | Some $2\times 12\times 12$ pencils over $\mathbb F_q$ require $\Theta(q)$ queries in the stated opaque model. This holds with exact all-mode vector-pin counts, a known witness, contraction ranks and comparisons with separately specified explicit tensors. | Read tensor entries, construct instances using those entries, or use an oracle with stronger witness access. The lower bound is for a family of varying fields. |

## Where to focus a gadget search

Within shared-block gadgets, keep candidate supports on which the active degrees have a nonzero obstruction in the incidence quotient modulo $q-1$. Reject disjoint padding, independent homogeneous outputs and acyclic bipartite supports before attempting transporter recovery. The four-block example supplies a minimal control for breaking scalar absorption. A transporter-preserving construction in that fragment would pass the control, force every output transporter to induce a determinant-one source transporter and let every determinant-one source transporter extend. If the construction passes through associative algebras, use the full filtered multiplication or prove that the replacement preserves the determinant coset on the counterexample families.

The unrestricted reverse reduction remains [Chen–Grochow–Qiao–Tang–Zhang, Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5). These results are independent of the Riemann hypotheses. [Verification](Verification.json).
