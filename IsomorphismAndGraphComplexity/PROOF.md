# Isomorphism and graph complexity

AI-assisted informal proofs with internal mathematical checks. Publication priority remains provisional.

1. [Abelian group isomorphism DNF lower bound](AbelianGroupIsomorphismDNF.md). Answers Grochow, Kardeş and Levet's Question 7.3 on valid Cayley tables. A quasipolynomial number of DNF terms is required, even if every input literal can be an affine parity and one table is fixed.
2. [Occurrence under symmetric-group padding](SymmetricGroupPadding.md). Gives a counterexample to the unrestricted converse asked after Grochow–Urisman's Lemma 6.11(2). Proves the converse for all subgroups of $S_k$ when the padding has at least $k-1$ points, including their graph application.
3. [Extension-field codes to lattices](ExtensionFieldCodeToLattice.md). Answers the non-prime-field reduction question of Cheraghchi–Shagrithaya–Veliche and Horlemann et al. for every fixed finite field and uniformly when the field order is polynomially bounded. Uniform running time is polynomial in the field order $q$.
4. [Monophonic position complexity](MonophonicPositionComplexity.md). Sharpens the question of Thomas et al. to an unconditional $\Theta_2^{\mathrm P}$-completeness classification, even at diameter two. NP-completeness is equivalent to $\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}$.

[Additional result on evolution algebras](EvolutionAlgebraAlgorithms.md). Addresses the algorithmic clauses of Kaygorodov–Martín González–Viruel's Question 50. This includes deterministic polynomial-bit recognition over rationals and explicit finite fields, plus graph-isomorphism equivalence for perfect evolution algebras over the rationals and each fixed finite field. The general variable-field isomorphism bound and the low-dimensional certification question remain open in this work.
