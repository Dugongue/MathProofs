# Recovering the two determinant rulings over the coefficient field

**Theorem.** Let $P$ be a homogeneous degree-$n$ form on $n^2$ variables over any field $k$, with $n\ge2$, geometrically equivalent to the determinant. Its second Hasse coefficient space determines an intrinsic quadratic étale $k$-algebra $C(P)$ by deterministic linear algebra. If $C(P)$ splits, the same construction gives two central simple algebras of degree $n$, with opposite Brauer classes. The form is equivalent to the split determinant over $k$ precisely when $C(P)$ and either algebra split. This criterion commutes with field extension. In particular the reduced norm forms of two degree-$n$ central simple algebras are linearly equivalent exactly when the algebras are isomorphic or opposite.

For number fields this yields a Hasse principle for equivalence to the split determinant. In each fixed degree $n\ge3$ there are unbounded everywhere-locally-equivalent families of geometric determinant forms. Over $\mathbb Q$ these examples can be made integral and equivalent over every $\mathbb Z_p$ and over $\mathbb R$, while remaining pairwise inequivalent over $\mathbb Q$. [Grochow, Question VII.2](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~billey/classes/lie/bulletins/grochow.2012.pdf#page=10)

## Recovery in every characteristic

Let $V=k^{n^2}$. Let $Q(P)\subset\operatorname{Sym}^2(V^*)$ be the coefficient span in $x$ of the part of $P(x+v)$ of degree two in $v$. For the determinant it is the span of the $2\times2$ minors by Laplace expansion. This formal coefficient extraction commutes with scalar extension in all characteristics. Let $\mathfrak a\subset\operatorname{End}(V)$ consist of all $A$ for which $q(v)\mapsto[t]q(v+tAv)$ preserves $Q(P)$. It is a Lie algebra by the commutator identity for derivations, and its defining linear equations commute with scalar extension. In split determinant coordinates it is the space $\operatorname{End}(U)\otimes I+I\otimes\operatorname{End}(W)$ for $V=U\otimes W$, with both factors of dimension $n$. The companion proof identifies this space by the tangent space to the rank-one cone. [Second Hasse coefficients and the tangent computation](SparseDeterminantToTI.md)

Put $H=\mathfrak a/kI$. Define a bilinear map
$$
\phi:H\times H\longrightarrow \operatorname{End}(V)/\mathfrak a,\qquad
\phi(\overline A,\overline B)=\overline{AB}.
$$
It is well defined because changing either lift by a scalar matrix changes the product by an element of $\mathfrak a$. It is symmetric since $\mathfrak a$ is closed under commutators. Write $J$ for the linear span of its image. Over split coordinates put $U_0=\operatorname{End}(U)/kI$ and $W_0=\operatorname{End}(W)/kI$. Then $H=U_0\oplus W_0$, the quotient $\operatorname{End}(V)/\mathfrak a$ is $U_0\otimes W_0=J$, and expanding a product gives
$$
\phi((u,w),(u',w'))=u\otimes w'+u'\otimes w .
$$

Solve the linear equations
$$
\phi(Dx,y)+\phi(x,Dy)=E\phi(x,y)\qquad(x,y\in H)
$$
for $D\in\operatorname{End}(H)$ and $E\in\operatorname{End}(J)$. Let $\mathcal D$ be the projection of the solution space to $D$. The computation and the projection commute with scalar extension. In split coordinates any block diagonal $D$ satisfies the equation with $E=D_U\otimes I+I\otimes D_W$. Conversely for $x,y\in U_0$ the right side is zero and the left side is $y\otimes D_{WU}x+x\otimes D_{WU}y$, where $D_{WU}$ is the off-diagonal block. Given a nonzero $x$, choose $y$ independent of it and apply a functional in the first factor vanishing on $x$ with value one on $y$. This forces $D_{WU}x=0$, also in characteristic two. The other off-diagonal block vanishes by the same calculation on $W_0$. Thus geometrically $\mathcal D=\operatorname{End}(U_0)\times\operatorname{End}(W_0)$. In particular $\mathcal D$ is an associative unital algebra by descent of closure under composition. Its center $C(P)$ is quadratic étale, since its geometric center is two copies of the field. Every step of the construction commutes with linear equivalence of forms.

Over a field $L$ where $C(P)$ splits, apply its two primitive idempotents to $H_L$. The preimages of their image spaces under $\mathfrak a_L\to H_L$ are unital subalgebras $A_1,A_2\subset\operatorname{End}_L(V_L)$, since geometrically they are the separate full matrix action algebras. They are central simple, commute, intersect in the scalars, and multiplication identifies $A_1\otimes_L A_2$ with the full endomorphism algebra. These statements follow by checking them over an algebraic closure. In particular the Brauer classes are opposite.

If $P_L$ is equivalent to the split determinant, both algebras and the center split. Conversely if the center and one algebra split, both algebras split. The action of $A_1=M_n(L)$ on $V_L$ is a sum of $n$ copies of its standard simple module by dimension. Its commuting algebra is $A_2$, by containment and dimension. Choose these coordinates so their sum is the standard operator space. Over an algebraic closure, the unique minor submodule of the required dimension with this infinitesimal stabilizer is the standard space $Q_0$, by the minor-module calculation in the companion proof in all characteristics. Thus $Q(P)_L=Q_0$ in these coordinates by linear descent. The higher Hasse coefficients vanish modulo $Q(P)$ over $L$, since they do over the geometric field. It follows that $P_L$ is formally affine on rank-one lines. The companion multilinearity and alternation argument now gives $P_L=c\det$, with $c\ne0$. Rescale one row.

For $P=\operatorname{Nrd}_A$ on a central simple algebra $A$, its left and right multiplications give the two recovered spaces, as can be checked after splitting $A$. In particular the two recovered algebras are $A,A^{\mathrm{op}}$, so a linear equivalence of reduced norms induces an isomorphism or anti-isomorphism. Conversely either type of algebra map preserves the reduced norm, as is seen after splitting. Affine equivalence of homogeneous forms also implies linear equivalence by taking their leading parts.

## Local–global consequences

Let $k$ be a number field. Suppose $P$ is equivalent to the split determinant over all completions $k_v$. Equivalence over one completion gives geometric equivalence over $k$, since the transporter equations are defined over $k$ and are geometrically nonempty. The quadratic étale algebra $C(P)$ splits everywhere locally and hence over $k$, because a nontrivial quadratic extension has an inert place. Both central simple factors then split at every place. Albert–Brauer–Hasse–Noether gives their global splitting, so $P$ is globally equivalent to the split determinant. [Milne, Class Field Theory, Brauer invariants](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf)

For failure inside one geometric orbit, fix $n\ge3$ and choose $2r$ distinct finite places in ordered pairs, with $r\ge2$. At each pair assign the two invariants $(1/n,-1/n)$ in either order and assign zero at the other places. For each assignment the sum vanishes. Albert–Brauer–Hasse–Noether and period equals index give a degree-$n$ division algebra with these invariants. At each completion any two such algebras are isomorphic or opposite, so all their reduced norms are locally equivalent. Globally the classes are distinct up to the single simultaneous reversal of all signs, and the recovered ruling algebras distinguish the reduced norms up to exactly that relation. Thus there are $2^{r-1}$ pairwise inequivalent degree-$n$ forms in $n^2$ variables of one geometric type, all equivalent at every completion.

Over $\mathbb Q$ choose a maximal $\mathbb Z$-order in each algebra and write its reduced norm in an integral basis. These norms have integral coefficients. Indeed the left regular characteristic polynomial of the generic element is in $\mathbb Z[x_1,\ldots,x_{n^2}][t]$ and is the $n$-th power of its reduced characteristic polynomial. Each root is integral over the polynomial ring, so the reduced coefficients are integral and lie in that ring by integral closedness. The completion of a maximal order is maximal. Over a nonarchimedean local field every maximal order in a central simple algebra is conjugate to a matrix order over the unique maximal order of the local division representative. Thus each local isomorphism or anti-isomorphism can be adjusted by conjugacy to identify the completed orders. The norm forms are equivalent by a matrix over $\mathbb Z_p$ at every prime, and by a real matrix since the archimedean invariants are zero. In particular they are equivalent modulo every positive integer by the Chinese remainder theorem. In degree three this gives irreducible cubic forms in nine variables, all geometrically the generic determinant. [Reiner, Maximal Orders](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780198526735.001.0001)

This is a written argument. The characteristic-independent linear ruling recovery and the fixed-degree arithmetic families are the additional results. The classical Brauer group and the classification of maximal local orders are standard inputs.
