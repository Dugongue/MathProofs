# Determinantal methods for tensor isomorphism

Four proof tracks connect optimal representations, determinantal tangent calculations, and cokernel arithmetic to ordinary tensor isomorphism. The main results have written proofs and provisional publication priority.

1. **A fixed determinant can hide all of Tensor Isomorphism.** The same optimal uniform quadratic family contains coefficient tensors encoding arbitrary ordinary three-tensor isomorphism over every field. Every affine matrix substitution in every size gives the same cokernel, determinant, and determinantal ideals across the family. Recovery uses the homogeneous boundary factors and the affine hyperbolic quadratic part. A separate remark records what the known support-compression argument establishes for fixed border rank. [Proof](OptimalUniformTensorIsomorphism.md)

2. **Sparse determinant equivalence reduces to one ordinary three-tensor comparison.** The reduction has side $O(n^2)$ for the order-$n$ determinant, works in every characteristic and commutes with all nonzero coefficient-algebra extensions. A separate rational black-box theorem characterizes the decision problem by PIT and central-simple-algebra splitting, with one determinant query for their conjunction. The two recovered rank-one rulings give the exact Brauer obstruction and yield unbounded integral genera of degree-three determinant forms. [Reduction](SparseDeterminantToTI.md) · [Rulings and arithmetic](DeterminantRulings.md) · [Original determinant question](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.2012)

3. **Cokernels give arithmetic tensor invariants.** Over finite fields of characteristic two, a commutative pair endomorphism algebra suffices for deterministic polynomial-time ordered symmetric tuple isometry and witness recovery, even for repeated determinant factors. Picard torsion gives unbounded genera of unmarked $3\times8\times8$ integer tensors within a fixed irreducible ternary determinant in each family. [Proofs](CokernelArithmetic.md) · [Part V, §1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00735v1#S1.SS4)

4. **Recognition and certificates for geometric direct sums of matrix multiplication.** Linear equations recognize the class over every field, including nonsplit centers. Brauer and center data give an exact classification under field extension, and finite-field comparison is deterministic polynomial bit time without factoring. The same adjoint reconstruction yields constant PC degree and polynomial sparse size in characteristic zero, linear degree in border rank with matching positive-characteristic examples, and polynomial-size circuit IPS over every field. [Classification](AdjointBrauer.md) · [Proof complexity](SemisimpleAdjointPC.md) · [Published proof-complexity questions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19320)

The existing higher-order universality theorem remains the proposed first item in the Grochow ranking. Its unrestricted scope is stronger than the new answers for specific classes. The earlier sparse geometric determinant decision and fixed-parameter border-rank implications are credited in the proofs. The unrestricted rank-dependent degree, sparse size, and characteristic-two tuple-isometry questions remain open.
