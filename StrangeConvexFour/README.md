# Four unexpected bridges from parametric discrete convexity

1. [Toric vector bundles and tensor instability](Geometry.md). A fixed bundle on a smooth projective toric surface realizes every rational principal-price filtration of a loopless quiver as an ordinary Harder–Narasimhan filtration. Vary only the ample polarization. Two bundles with the same equivariant Chern classes recover different exact tensor instability scores.
2. [Dimer occupation numbers and tensor orbits](Dimers.md). On strictly balanceable rectangular slice supports with planar pair graphs, two vectors of dimer occupation means, one partition-mass product, and local cross ratios classify the weighted positive diagonal orbit. The uniform determinant-one action requires at most two further power tests. A marked partition function also records the discrete-convex transportation cost profile.
3. [Graph sandpiles and arithmetic tensor transport](Sandpile.md). Encode a reduced graph Laplacian using three-way tensor coordinates. Its critical group gives the torsion character group of the diagonal stabilizer and classifies Laurent monomial twists. A minimum cut normalizes the chip-firing class, and one parametric cut gives a nested period of firings.
4. [Persistent homology from priced minors](Persistence.md). Priced augmented boundary minors recover all filtration grades up to componentwise translations. Three further priced minima give each multipersistence rank. Over the two-element field the data also reconstruct the based complex.

The four main proofs are in prose. An independent Lean kernel and axiom audit checked the [least-optimizer monotonicity lemma](MonotoneCut.lean) used in the sandpile proof. The notes include finite controls, exact scope, and primary-source comparisons. Publication priority remains unconfirmed.
