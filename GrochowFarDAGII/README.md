# Four further bridges to Grochow's work

These proposed placements are editorial estimates against the thirty-result research briefing. The source branches are Honda formal groups and Frobenius quotients, Galois categories and Mackey restriction, weighted automata and Hankel realizations, and conditional relative entropy in statistical testing.

| Suggested placement | Result | What the proof establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3–7 | [Form equivalence through an intrinsic extra variable](Tensor.md) | Fixed-degree homogeneous form equivalence reduces to unmarked commutative algebra isomorphism over every field, answering Agrawal–Saxena's all-fields question for cubics. The construction extends to reduced base rings in at least two variables and yields cubic TI-completeness in characteristic three. |
| 7–12 | [Tensor isomorphism over every intermediate field](GaloisMarks.md) | Prescribed subgroup-compatible partitions of any finite family are realized by ordinary and determinant-one tensor isomorphism. The proof gives sharp dimensions for the elementary abelian all-proper-subgroups pattern and rational examples that agree at every completion but first agree globally in degree \(2^{r-1}\). |
| 9–15 | [Tensor isomorphism in positive stochastic sources](Optimization.md) | Exact rational alphabet recoding is TI-complete for sources with fixed positive hidden dynamics. Every histogram agrees with independent uniform emission, and every channel with at most three outputs is independent across time. Four outputs can expose a nonzero encoded tensor. |
| 15–21 | [Sharp path-law bounds for Markov compression](Topology.md) | Projective contraction and near-lumpability forward and backward imply a quadratic-in-each-defect KL bound on the full coarse path. A four-state family proves the product rate sharp. Finite memory gives a further geometric decay bound. |
