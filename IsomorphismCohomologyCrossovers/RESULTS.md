# Tensor, group and algebra isomorphism crossovers

All proofs in this folder are informal and AI-assisted. Publication priority is unverified.

| Result | Scope and proof |
| --- | --- |
| Tensor-isomorphism hardness in global dimension two | A one-point extension of a Kronecker algebra converts the input into a split basic algebra with three simple modules. The Lie bracket on its first Hochschild cohomology recovers the encoded algebra. This strengthens the earlier global-dimension-three hardness theorem. [Proof](geometry.md) |
| Linear-length reductions over commutative rings | The five free-module actions on three-way arrays reduce to one another with linear side lengths over every commutative unital ring. The same construction compiles fixed-type tensor systems and gives linear-side marked decision queries for search and counting over finite rings. [Proof](LinearLengthRings.md) |
| Ordinary Polynomial Calculus across graph-to-tensor encodings | The Grochow–Qiao graph-to-alternating-space construction and its bare tensor presentation admit constant-degree reductions in both directions with the graph equations. A second padding uses $9n$ vertices and $m+10n$ edge slices. Galesi–Grochow–Pitassi–She Open Question 7.5 also includes other reductions. [Proof](proof-systems.md) |
| Matrix congruence in characteristic two | Geometric matrix noncongruence admits ordinary Polynomial Calculus refutations of degree at most $\max(3,2n)$, with degree three once the indecomposable multiplicities match. This answers the characteristic-two degree part of Galesi–Grochow–Pitassi–She Open Question 7.3. [Proof](char2-matrix-congruence.md) |
| Exact group epimorphism counting | Polynomial-time counting and search from arbitrary Cayley-table groups onto an abelian Hall extension whose abelian complement has homocyclic Sylows. The proof also treats split complement maps between abelian Hall extensions with general abelian complements. This gives restricted answers to Grochow–Srivastava–Thakkar Open Questions 2 and 3(4). [Proof](groups.md) |
