# Exact degree of the weak rank principle

Let \(K\) be any field, \(A\) a fixed \(m\times n\) matrix of rank \(r>b\ge1\), \(U\) an \(m\times b\) matrix of variables, and \(V\) a \(b\times n\) matrix of variables. The ordinary PC refutation degree of \(UV=A\) is exactly \(b+2\). The result also holds over \(\mathbb F_Q\), \(Q>2\), with the field equations. Galesi–Grochow–Pitassi–She proved a lower bound \(b/2+1\) for the identity case in their rank-principle argument. [GGPS, §3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S3.SS3)

Constant row and column basis changes on \(A\) induce invertible linear substitutions on the two variable matrices. Any such substitution respects PC degree. With field equations and constants in \(\mathbb F_Q\), the field equations transform by linear combinations by Frobenius. Normalize \(A\) to a leading identity block of size \(r\) with zeros elsewhere. The leading \(r\times r\) equations are the one-sided identity system, proving the upper transfer. Conversely, replace the outer rows of \(U\) and columns of \(V\) by zero. The only remaining equations are the same identity system, proving the lower transfer. The descending-minor derivation gives degree at most \(b+2\). [Descending-minor derivation](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/GrochowEightFollowup/complexity.md)

For characteristic \(p>0\), choose a \(p\)-power \(q\) with \(b+q\ge r\) and \(2q\ge b+2\). The one-sided identity system of outer size \(r\) is a subsystem of the first inverse family in the two-sided system of outer size \(b+q\), by taking the same \(r\) indices for \(U\)'s rows and \(V\)'s columns. A refutation would transfer with no increase in degree. The two-sided theorem gives the lower bound \(b+2\), also with the allowed field equations. [Exact two-sided degree](rectangular-inverses.md)

In characteristic zero, the identity equations have rational coefficients. In a fixed degree cap, the ordinary PC proof subspace is computed by repeatedly taking sums of subspaces and images of their intersections with the one-lower-degree space. Scalar extension from \(\mathbb Q\) commutes with these finite-dimensional operations. A proof over \(K\) of degree at most \(b+1\) would therefore give such a proof over \(\mathbb Q\). Reduce its finitely many coefficients modulo any prime avoiding their denominators. Every inference and the last line \(1\) then survive, contradicting the positive-characteristic lower bound. [Scalar extension for bounded PC degree](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.07280v1#S2.SS2)

Consequently, dropping \(VU=I_b\) can raise the exact minimum from 2 to \(b+2\) whenever the original gap \(a-b\) is coprime to the characteristic. The one-sided formula does not require a restriction on the gap.
