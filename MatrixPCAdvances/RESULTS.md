# Four Polynomial Calculus results for matrices

1. [Exact worst-case conjugacy degree](quiver-conjugacy.md). For a single matrix, an explicit formula gives every positive dimension in characteristic \(p>0\), with normalized liminf \(2/(p+4)\) and limsup \(2/5\). The same proof gives a per-instance characterization for labelled quiver representations. This answers the degree part of GGPS Open Question 7.3 for positive-characteristic conjugacy, including with the field equations over finite fields of size greater than two.
2. [Exact weak rank principle](weak-rank.md). For a fixed matrix \(A\) of rank greater than the inner dimension \(b\ge1\), the ordinary PC degree of \(UV=A\) is exactly \(b+2\) over every field. The finite-field result with field equations applies when the field has size greater than two. This sharpens the GGPS degree lower bound.
3. [Exact two-sided rectangular inverse degree](rectangular-inverses.md). For positive unequal sides \(a>b\) in characteristic \(p\), the ordinary PC degree is \(\min(2p^{v_p(a-b)},b+2)\). The same formula holds with the field equations over finite fields of size greater than two.
4. [Finite-field arithmetic noncongruence](finite-field-congruence.md). Over every fixed odd finite field, labelled tuple noncongruence with field equations has sharp linear worst-case degree and admits an exponential sparse-size lower bound already for one nonsingular symmetric matrix on each side. The examples are congruent over the algebraic closure. This addresses the finite-field degree and size-lower-bound parts of GGPS Open Question 7.3.

The four main statements have written proofs. [Lean](MatrixPCAdvances.lean) checks subsidiary inequalities and the finite-field exponent step.
