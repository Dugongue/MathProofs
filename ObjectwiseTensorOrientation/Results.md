# Objectwise two-sign reductions from intrinsic fields, operators and slices

Work over the field of three elements. An objectwise reduction assigns a single output tensor f(T) to each raw tensor. It uses one ordinary coordinate convention inside the output and tests the determinant of its first physical mode. The map must preserve the two-sign comparison for every pair in its source class.

| Result | What it supplies |
|---|---|
| Objectwise signed tensor-system compiler | Polynomially many bounded-order marks are synchronized in a layered algebra. Even right-ideal dimensions cancel the unit multiplication determinant. The first physical sign of the multiplication tensor is exactly the sign of the designated original role. Signature tags prevent comparisons between unrelated input formats. [Proof](RetainedKernel.md) |
| A field of alternating forms | Recover a coefficient field and its rank-one form module by deterministic linear algebra. The Pfaffian sign is the field norm of one actual semilinear multiplier, with an explicit basis calibration. Ordinary marks compute the norm without enumerating the field. The pencil dimension can be linear in the tested mode. [Proof](FieldPencils.md) |
| Products of coefficient fields | Recover the blocks from the center of the left adjoint algebra. Even half-ranks remove their norm characters, leaving an explicit product of raw Pfaffian ratios. Odd half-ranks can also be retained when the number of repeated blocks of each odd type is bounded. The other tested mode remains arbitrary. [Proof](ProductPencils.md) |
| Small determinant lines from slices | A covariant list containing a slice with small kernel and cokernel gives a short determinant line using two exact sequences. Its calibrated vector tests the product of the two matrix-mode signs. The objectwise compiler retains one of those signs on the same transporter. [Proof](SliceDetLines.md) |
| Primary chain orientations | The operator construction marks the induced determinants on all the odd-length multiplicity spaces of a covariant list, with even lengths contributing nothing. A sum of calibrated vectors across the unordered list enforces a given tested sign objectwise. [Proof](RetainedKernel.md) |

The compiler proves that an exact ordinary tensor lock for one sign is sufficient for an objectwise two-sign to one-sign reduction on the lock's domain. It no longer requires first applying a pair-dependent sign projection. Deterministic covariant tests allow the branches to be combined by choosing the first applicable test and tagging its output signature. Ordinary-isomorphic inputs agree on the applicable tests. Hence this gives one object map on the union of the covered branches.

The field and slice reductions do not decide how to produce suitable covariant input data on arbitrary tensors. The unrestricted objectwise comparison remains open. By the previous three-object implication, closing it would also give all four deterministic polynomial many-one equivalences. [Kernel-collapse implication](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/TensorSignChain/KernelCollapse.md) · [Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1)

All proofs are informal. The underlying algebra, determinant identities and tensor-system compilation are credited in the individual proofs. Publication priority for the precise reductions is unverified.
