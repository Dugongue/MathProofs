# Product-sign marks from large-rank tensor slices

Work over the field F of three elements. Write the raw tensor in bilinear form T:U×V→W. We test the two determinant coordinates on V and W. Dualization of a tensor mode reciprocates its determinant, which leaves signs unchanged. Physical modes can be permuted.

**Theorem.** Suppose a polynomial-time objectwise rule returns a polynomial-size set of marked labels X_T⊂U. Require that the U-mode map of every ordinary transporter sends X_T bijectively to the corresponding set on the other input. Let L denote the explicit input size. Suppose X_T contains a label whose slice M_x:V→W, y↦T(x,y), has kernel and cokernel dimensions at most b=O(log(2+L)). On this class there is a polynomial kernel reduction from two-sign TI to one-sign TI. No reference transporter or stabilizer generators are supplied. The criterion permits singular slices, unequal matrix sides, and unbounded label dimension. It also gives one ordinary TI comparison for the product-sign condition alone.

Use a common format-dependent bound b and filter the list by the two dimension tests. Ranks are preserved by transported labels, so the new list remains intrinsic. It is nonempty on the promised domain. Every ordinary-positive pair then has matching list sizes and slice ranks.

## Move the raw relative volume into small spaces

For each retained label x write K_x=ker M_x and C_x=coker M_x. With det(0)=F, the exact sequences

    0 → K_x → V → im M_x → 0,
    0 → im M_x → W → C_x → 0

give a natural isomorphism

    (det K_x)* ⊗ det C_x  ≅  (det V)* ⊗ det W.

Here the first sequence uses the actual map M_x. The image determinant and its dual contract out. Fix the written volume bases on V,W. Let ω_x on the left be the preimage of the raw relative volume vol(V)*⊗vol(W). This is an explicit nonzero vector. Compute it by Gaussian elimination, using a kernel frame and lifts of an image basis for the first sequence, and that image basis with a cokernel frame for the second. The change-of-frame determinants give its single scalar in the resulting small exterior bases.

If an ordinary transporter is (a,b_V,b_W) from T to S, then M_(ax) b_V=b_W M_x. It induces compatible maps on these two exact sequences. Therefore the map on the small determinant line sends

    ω_x  ↦  (det b_W / det b_V) ω_(ax).

This formula is exact in the original coordinate frames. It includes singular M_x. The ratio encodes the product sign over F.

## Turn the relative volumes into ordinary marks

The following construction uses the same object map on each input.

* Keep the actual raw tensor. Add the split coordinate algebra D=F^(X_T), with its multiplication and unit, and the map e_x↦x into U. Algebra isomorphisms permute the primitive idempotents and this map enforces their actual label correspondences.
* Include the split D-modules formed by the direct sums of all K_x and all C_x. Include their inclusion and quotient maps, using the D idempotent to select the relevant summand. The kernel inclusion can be a single linear map to V whose restriction to each labeled summand is the corresponding inclusion. The quotient is a bilinear map D×W to the direct sum. These force the actual induced maps on the summands.
* Include the products of unital exterior algebras of the K_x and C_x with their D-actions and the degree-one inclusions. Include the duals of the kernel exterior spaces via the fixed scalar evaluation pairing. The actual induced maps on the exterior spaces are forced by multiplication and the degree-one inclusions, even if a summand has dimension zero.
* Use their tensor product over D to store the pairs of dual kernel and cokernel exterior factors in one space. Concretely use the direct sum of the indicated tensor products and its bilinear map from the factor spaces, zero between different label summands. This is generated by the images of the bilinear map, so the induced map on it is forced. Mark the single vector Σ_x ω_x in its top components.

Compatibility with the marked vector is equivalent to det b_W / det b_V=1. In one direction an accepted comparison induces an actual raw transporter matching all its labels and small spaces. Every component ω_x is nonzero, so at least one forces the ratio to one. In the other direction any transporter with this ratio permutes the labels and induces the stipulated maps on every added space, preserving the distinguished vector. There is no independent map on a determinant line that can change its scalar.

Every component has bounded arity and the size is polynomial in L, the label list size, and 2^b. Kernel and cokernel exterior algebras have dimension at most 2^b apiece. The fixed-component or uniformly packed mixed tensor compiler gives one ordinary TI comparison for this marked relation. For the two-sign result, apply the retained-sign compiler to this very diagram and retain det b_V as the one physical sign. The mark enforces equality of the two original signs. Hence positive retained sign is equivalent to both original signs being positive. This composition needs the determinant-aware compiler, because merely applying an ordinary compiler to a marked diagram would lose the second sign. ## A raw family with no invertible slice and a growing label dimension

Let U=F^r, with r≥3. Take a connected simple graph on vertices 1,…,r and use these linear forms on U:

    x_i for each vertex i,
    x_i+x_j for each edge ij,
    x_1−x_2 once.

Use mutually distinct forms up to scalars, as holds for this list over F. Give the vertex forms multiplicity 2r, the edge forms multiplicity 2r+2, and the last form multiplicity two. Write V and W as the corresponding direct sums of multiplicity blocks. The slice M_x acts on the block for a form ℓ by ℓ(x) times the identity. Independently rebase all three modes before receiving the raw inputs.

A slice is within rank two of full rank exactly when every heavy form is nonzero on its label. The vertex constraints make every coordinate ±1. Each edge constraint x_i+x_j≠0 then forces x_i=x_j. Connectedness makes all coordinates equal. The two qualifying labels are the all-ones vector and its negative. The form x_1−x_2 vanishes on both. Their kernel and cokernel dimensions are exactly two. All other slices lose rank in a heavy block of dimension greater than two. In particular no invertible slice exists.

These labels can be found in raw coordinates in polynomial time. Compute by linear equations the algebra of pairs (A,B) of endomorphisms of V,W that satisfy M_x A=B M_x for every label x. In the hidden block coordinates the off-diagonal equations read ℓ_i A_ij=ℓ_j B_ij. Two distinct form lines force both off-diagonal blocks to vanish. At i=j the equation identifies the two diagonal blocks. Thus the algebra is the product of full matrix algebras on the multiplicity blocks. Compute its center by commutation equations. Its minimal joint eigenspaces on V and W, with labels given by the same action of the center, are exactly the displayed blocks. These can be computed by refining the joint eigenspaces of a linear basis of the center, using the three scalar eigenvalues over F. The corresponding block coefficient line ℓ⊂U* is extracted from any nonzero coefficient of its raw block slice map. Rank one of that coefficient map and invertibility of a matrix generator verify the claimed block presentation.

The three multiplicities distinguish vertex, edge, and light form lines without choosing a tensor-invariant ordering of the vertices. Choose any nonzero representatives of the vertex lines. They are a basis of U*. In their coordinates each edge form has two nonzero coefficients. For nonzero vertex values the edge nonvanishing constraint forces the two coefficient-scaled values to coincide, since F has exactly two nonzero elements. Propagate these ratios along a spanning tree and check every remaining edge and the light block rank. This recovers exactly the two high-rank labels, regardless of raw coordinate choices. It is also possible to verify this criterion for any recovered presentation with these checked basis, incidence, and rank properties. The output list consists of all labels with the specified rank threshold, so its covariance holds against every actual transporter.

For these examples the product sign of the two matrix modes is rigid, since a transporter between copies of the standard tensor permutes the multiplicity blocks and acts on each with two maps differing by one scalar. In more detail, the equation on a matched block is ℓ'(a x) b_V=ℓ(x)b_W. Choosing a label where ℓ(x) is nonzero shows that b_W is a nonzero scalar times b_V in the standard block frames. All block widths are even, so these scalar differences make no determinant difference and the block permutations contribute the same sign in both modes. Individually the two signs can flip simultaneously, by reflecting one multiplicity block in both modes. Thus testing the product sign has actual content. Rebasing just one matrix mode by a reflection gives an ordinarily isomorphic pair whose relative product sign is negative. The extraction and determinant-line theorem does not use rigidity and works for any intrinsic high-rank label list satisfying its hypotheses.

The proofs are informal. The unrestricted two-sign comparison remains open. This criterion gives a certified class on which large map determinants are eliminated using only small kernels and cokernels.
