# Objectwise compilation of one retained sign and operator orientation marks

Work over the field F with three elements. We first make an existing signed mixed-system compiler objectwise. We then prove an objectwise two-sign to one-sign reduction for raw tensors admitting suitable covariant lists of endomorphisms. Every constructed object depends only on its own input. Its possible isomorphisms always use one shared transporter for the two original restrictions.

## Keep one specified determinant through an object map

**Compiler theorem.** Input objects are labelled tensor systems of bounded order at most three with a distinguished linear role a. The signature, which includes the dimensions, the roles of the slots and their covariances, is part of the explicit input. Two objects of different signatures are considered inequivalent. There is a polynomial object map sending such a system X to a three-tensor R_X, so that the first-mode determinant profile of R_X to R_Y equals the role-a determinant profile of simultaneous system maps X to Y. This includes the empty profile.

Use the earlier signed compiler up to its layered associative algebra construction. Convert tensors to bilinear maps, including the dual evaluation pairings and fixed scalar marks. Pad even-dimensional roles to odd dimensions by a fixed scalar direction as in that compiler. This preserves the exact original role determinant. Keep its common output corner, probe products and projection corners unchanged. For any algebra isomorphism φ the proof there extracts a system isomorphism with determinant on the designated role a equal to det(φ). Every system transporter extends to an algebra isomorphism. [Prior layered algebra and its determinant calculation](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/TensorDeterminantIntervals/SignedCompiler.md)

Modify the diagonal corner tag of each vertex v. Its ring remains F[t_v]/(t_v^(N_v)), with positive-degree diagonal elements annihilating all cross corners. Take N_v from residues 0 or 1 modulo 4, choosing the parity that makes dim(e_v R) even. Here e_v R is the right ideal, whose dimension is N_v plus the sum of the dimensions of all outgoing cross corners from v. Pick distinct positive N_v in disjoint four-element intervals ordered by the labelled vertex order. The off-diagonal dimensions and vertex order are determined by the signature. Thus the tags are objectwise, and two objects with the same signature use corresponding tags. The earlier calculation of the algebra isomorphism determinant still applies. Its only use of the tag residue was to make the determinant of a local corner map positive. That determinant equals ρ^(N_v(N_v−1)/2), where ρ is the leading coefficient at t_v. The exponent is even for both new residue classes. The new tags still distinguish vertices and leave all other corner computations unchanged.

For a unit u of R, write its residue at vertex v as u_v∈F×. The element ū=Σ_v u_v e_v is a unit lifting this residue and acts by the scalar u_v on the right ideal e_v R under left multiplication. The remaining factor belongs to 1+J for the nilpotent radical J. Its left multiplication is unipotent, since the left multiplication of a nilpotent radical element is nilpotent. Hence

    det_F(L_u) = ∏_v u_v^dim(e_v R) = 1.

This calculation uses the dimensions of the right ideals e_v R, as left multiplication acts by u_v on elements whose first vertex is v.

One can encode all signature information by a separate determinant-neutral algebra factor, to make the compiler apply to variable signatures across raw objects. Choose a fixed injective binary encoding of the signature, including the designated role and any additional structural tags. For its h bits bit_i, adjoin the direct product of the local algebras F[s_i]/(s_i^(8i+4bit_i)) for 1≤i≤h. Their dimensions are distinct positive multiples of four. The multiset of these dimensions determines the bit string. These factors cannot be confused with R. The layered algebra R is directly indecomposable and has at least two vertices in its semisimple quotient. In fact every vertex is connected to the shared O,P vertices by the graph of nonzero cross corners. A central idempotent has equal residue at the two ends of every such edge, since its commuting action on the corresponding corner equates the residues. This can be checked after aligning its quotient idempotents, or in the lowest nonzero radical layer of the corner. Hence its residue is constant and the idempotent is zero or one. The signature factors are each local with one quotient vertex. Automorphisms of each signature factor and left multiplication by each of its units have positive determinant, by the same local dimension and exponent calculations. This direct product adds polynomial dimension and leaves both determinant conclusions intact. Write A_X for the resulting algebra.

Output the multiplication tensor of A_X and test its first physical mode. Any ordinary transporter of multiplication tensors of unital associative algebras is an isotopy of the form

    a=L_u φ,     b=R_v φ,     c=L_u R_v φ

for units u,v in the target and an algebra isomorphism φ. To see this, put u=a(1), v=b(1) and evaluate a(x)b(y)=c(xy) at each unit. Then c(x)=a(x)v=u b(x). The multiplication maps by u and v are surjective and therefore invertible, so u and v are units. Cancellation now gives φ=L_(u^(-1))a=R_(v^(-1))b and its multiplicativity. Here det(a)=det(φ) by the even right-ideal adjustment. Conversely an algebra isomorphism gives an isotopy by taking both units equal to one. The signature factors protect mismatched inputs, and the determinant of φ on the layered factor reads exactly the original designated role. The usual dual interpretation of tensor inputs inverts their determinants, which leaves signs unchanged over F. This completes the objectwise compiler using the first physical sign directly, without a sign projection on the output pair.

## A raw covariant operator list locks a second determinant

**Reduction theorem.** Let X be a raw three-tensor with two specified tested modes U and V. Suppose each X in an intrinsic source family admits a polynomial-time procedure returning a polynomial-size nonempty covariant set of endomorphisms of V. Covariant means every ordinary transporter from X to X' conjugates the two sets bijectively by its V-mode map. Assume at least one endomorphism in each set has all odd-length rational primary multiplicities at most O(log L), where L is the explicit tensor length. The cutoff can be fixed as a polynomial-time function of the common format bound. Then two-sign TI on this family has a polynomial-time kernel reduction to one-sign TI. Neither the V dimension, the primary lengths nor the primary residue-field degrees are bounded by the cutoff.

Retain every operator satisfying the cutoff. The filtered set is still intrinsic because the condition is conjugacy invariant. Let J_X index its distinct elements. For M∈J_X and a monic irreducible f over F, work on the f-primary part V_f of V. Define its length-ℓ quotient

    Q_(M,f,ℓ) = ker f(M)^ℓ / (ker f(M)^(ℓ−1) + f(M) ker f(M)^(ℓ+1))

on V_f. It is naturally a vector space over E_f=F[t]/(f). The action of t comes from M, and f acts by zero. Its dimension over E_f is the multiplicity of the primary chain F[t]/(f^ℓ). Gaussian elimination and rational canonical forms recover all of these data in polynomial time over the fixed field. If a pair (f,ℓ) is absent at one M, use the zero quotient there.

For source M and target N conjugate by a map g, use matched rational canonical frames in fixed orders on polynomials, lengths and equal chain slots. Write c_M and c_N for the determinants of those chosen full frames in the original physical coordinates. Denote by b_(f,ℓ) the induced E_f-linear map on the two quotient spaces in their resulting chain frames. The determinant is

    det_F(g) = (c_N/c_M) · ∏_(f,ℓ) Norm_(E_f/F)(det_(E_f)(b_(f,ℓ)))^ℓ .

For verification, filter V_f by powers of f(M). On layer f^r V_f/f^(r+1)V_f, a map from a chain of length ℓ to a longer chain has zero leading coefficient, so the matrix on that layer is triangular in the length order. Its diagonal map for length ℓ is the induced b_(f,ℓ), in chain coordinates. That matrix occurs on exactly ℓ layers. The field norm computes the determinant of each E_f-linear diagonal block on the F underlying space. The displayed frame ratio converts back to the original coordinates. This is the earlier rational-primary determinant calculation. Over F only the odd lengths contribute to the product, even when even-length blocks have arbitrarily large multiplicities. [Rational primary determinant calculation](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/OneSignTensorComparison/AlternatingForms.md)

## Encode all marks together

Construct one bounded-order diagram for X. Keep the raw tensor. Add the split label algebra D=F^(J_X) with its unit and multiplication, and add the map D×V→V taking (e_M,x) to Mx, where e_M is the primitive idempotent indexed by M. This forces every induced permutation of D to be the actual conjugacy correspondence of the marked operators under the shared map on V. There are no fixed individual labels on the operators. A genuine raw transporter acts on the entire set.

Use the union of the polynomial-length sets of pairs (f,ℓ) occurring in these operators, in a deterministic order. This ordered index set is the same on ordinarily equivalent tensors. For each odd length in this set use a role that is the direct sum of the quotient spaces Q_(M,f,ℓ) over M, with its D-idempotent actions. Include the actual kernels, inclusions and quotient maps so that the induced maps on these factors are exactly b_(f,ℓ). To tie the inclusions to the original V map, one can use D⊗_F V with the linked bilinear map (d,x)↦d⊗x, and embed the direct sum of the kernel subspaces in the corresponding components of this space. Multiplication by the indicated t in E_f can be a fixed linear operator on each such quotient role, enforcing E_f-linearity. The actual quotient maps commute with this designated action. Collect each kind of marked component in its direct sum with D-action, so the same permutation of marks acts throughout.

For the odd lengths, attach the product over M of the exterior algebras over E_f of these quotient spaces. The diagonal D-action separates the factors. Include the degree-one maps, the E_f-scalar actions, and a marked inclusion of their top exterior lines. Algebra multiplication then forces the action in each top line to be det_(E_f)(b_(f,ℓ)) in the indicated generator frames. A zero quotient contributes its degree zero unit line with action one. Each such algebra has dimension at most 2^(cutoff) over E_f. This is where the multiplicity bound is used.

Each top E_f-line can be converted to an F-line whose scale is the norm of the E_f-scale. Explicitly attach a network of coordinate line multiplications over E_f that raises the scale to (3^[E_f:F]−1)/2, using its binary exponentiation circuit. A coordinate multiplication is a specified bilinear map between lines with the chosen bases. A shared input can be used twice or linked to a second copy by a linear identity. All multipliers of line maps are therefore forced by multiplication. The result is in F×; include a marked F-subline to read it. Carry out these multiplications separately in the D-components, using an explicit componentwise bilinear map. The fixed E_f-actions are retained on all E_f roles. The exponent bit lengths are O([E_f:F]), so the number of line multiplication maps stays polynomial. Every genuine conjugacy transporter extends to these roles, since its forced line scales are actual powers and norms.

Multiply the resulting F-line scales of the odd-length factors, component by component with the same D labels, using coordinate products in the fixed order of pairs (f,ℓ). Let the terminal role be the direct sum of the one-dimensional outcome lines indexed by M. Its basis in component M is denoted w_M, with the chosen product frame. If no odd length occurs, take that terminal role to be D with its unit coordinate frames, giving scale one. Include the single marked vector

    Σ_(M∈J_X) c_M^(-1) w_M.

When the induced permutation takes M to N and the actual physical transporter in V is g, it takes c_M^(-1)w_M to det_F(g)·c_N^(-1)w_N, by the determinant formula. Thus preserving the marked sum forces det_F(g)=1, since the mark set is nonempty and every component of the sum is nonzero. Conversely a raw positive V-map induces the exact mark permutation and all the actual quotient, exterior and line actions, preserving the sum. This locks the second determinant over the whole tensor, with no chosen reference transporter and no pair-dependent selection of a single M.

Every constituent map is linear or bilinear, and all coefficient tables, ranks, field extensions and line multiplication diagrams have polynomial size. Include the sorted list of polynomial and length types, labelled role dimensions and raw format as part of the structural signature. A genuine transporter preserves the whole marked set and hence all these types and dimensions. Across two unrelated signatures the compiler tags prevent spurious equivalence. Designate the original mode U as the one retained determinant role and apply the objectwise compiler. A positive ordinary V-lock and a positive physical U-sign must hold on the same raw transporter. This proves the stated kernel reduction.

The cutoff is automatic on appropriate small computed operator spaces, for example when a polynomial-order list is available and an operator among them has no odd-length primary chains at all. A logarithmic-dimensional covariant linear space of mode endomorphisms can be listed over F, using its explicitly computable basis without any need for the basis itself to be intrinsic. Recoverability of such lists, and the presence of an admissible operator, are substantive source-class hypotheses. These constructions are informal and give no unrestricted two-sign to one-sign collapse.
