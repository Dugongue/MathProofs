# Four consequences of the extremal Cauchy permanent construction

1. **The permanent's singular locus is larger than the proposed partition union.** Conjecture 4.27 of Boralevi, Carlini, Michałek and Ventura fails in every size from four onward. The critical scheme has explicit smooth open subsets of components in different dimensions in each such size. For every size $n\ge6$ there is a totally nonsingular real Cauchy witness of permanental rank exactly $n-2$ outside every proposed row and column partition locus. [Proof](geometry.md)
2. **Exact one-row localization for maximal permanental ideals.** For the generic last row and a product $D_S$ of distinct entries in that row, $I_r:D_S=I_{r-1}$ exactly when $|S|\ge2r-1$ and the characteristic is zero or greater than $r$. Even saturation fails outside that range. The proof also gives sharp monomial membership criteria for nested row supports and squarefree column degrees. [Proof](complexity.md)
3. **Cauchy permanental rank diverges across characteristics.** A characteristic-zero square Cauchy matrix has deficiency $\delta$ satisfying $\delta(\delta+1)\le n$. In each fixed odd characteristic $p$, square examples with $n=p^k$ have exact deficiency $n/p$. Every odd prime power $n$ also admits the sharp width-$2n-2$ example over $\mathbb F_{n^3}$. [Proof](algebra.md)
4. **Any real extremal column set can be prescribed, and rational realization is arithmetic.** A Wronski fiber classifies all valid Cauchy rows. For three rows and four prescribed rational columns, the existence of rational row parameters is equivalent to positive Mordell–Weil rank on one of at most two explicit elliptic curves. The same construction gives an exact finite-field count in characteristic greater than three. [Proof](wronski.md)

The main results have paper proofs.
