# Proofs recovered from the Open Problem Archive

| Result | Scope | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Trace-indistinguishable matrix triples](P2750.md) | Answers TheoremDB P2750 as stated. The largest trace fiber over $\mathbb F_5$ has 32 conjugacy classes. The proof also determines the maximum over every odd finite field. | Informal proof and independently run [exhaustive checker](check_p2750.py). |
| [Symplectic tensor isomorphism for five actions and fixed arity](SymplecticActions.md) | Extends the plain-action equivalence already in MathProofs and gives symplectic counterparts to Chen et al., Theorems 1.6 and 1.7. | Informal proof with cited tensor-system reductions. |
| [Special-linear isomorphism of matrix pencils](SpecialLinearPencils.md) | Deterministic polynomial-time decision over each fixed finite field for any fixed number of slices, extending the earlier field-three computation. A restricted case of Chen et al., Open Question 1.9. | Informal proof with cited module algorithms. |
| [Tensor isomorphism hardness in concise critical formats](CriticalFormatCastling.md) | Polynomial-time reductions from ordinary TI to concise ordinary TI and to concise SL-TI in formats with $\Delta=0$ or $\Delta=4$. | Informal proof using the archived sentinel construction and existing centroid machinery. |
| [Finite-ring rank-threshold capacity](RankThresholdCapacity.md) | Exact classical and entanglement-assisted zero-error capacity for the specified finite-ring channels. | Informal proof. The archive gives no verified exact published open-problem source for this formulation. |

Publication priority has not been established for these notes. The P2750 computation checks the finite acceptance conditions independently of the archived certificate files.
