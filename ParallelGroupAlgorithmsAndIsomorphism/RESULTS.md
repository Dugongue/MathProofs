# Parallel group algorithms and isomorphism proofs

These are AI-assisted informal proofs and applications of earlier theorems. Publication priority for the new arguments has not been established.

| Proof | Result |
| --- | --- |
| [Solvable radicals of quotient permutation groups](QuotientRadicalNC.md) | Deterministic uniform NC computes the solvable radical of arbitrary $G/K$ and gives an explicit faithful permutation action of its radical quotient. This answers Levet–Srivastava–Thakkar Problems 12.5, 12.4 as stated for Fitting-free quotients, and 12.1 on their minimum faithful degree. The minimum faithful representation can also be constructed in NC using the earlier transversal argument. [Questions and conventions](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.16039v4#S12) |
| [Socles of arbitrary permutation groups](PermutationGroupSocleNC.md) | Deterministic uniform NC computes the entire socle of an arbitrary permutation group given by generators. This answers Levet's main Shonan question, including the abelian part, and the ordinary permutation-group case discussed after Problem 12.4. [Shonan Meeting 246, printed p. 15](https://shonan.nii.ac.jp/docs/No.246.pdf#page=16) |
| [Monomial witnesses in code equivalence](MonomialWitnessModels.md) | A single affine-linear system cannot express the monomial constraint of Horlemann et al. in general. Exponentially many branches can be necessary under the original code equations and invertibility promise. A quadratic model with $O(n^2)$ equations is optimal in order among constant-degree models with a unique auxiliary witness. [Survey, §6.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12870v2#S6.SS6) |
| [Isomorphism of flat genus-two quotients](FlatBlockQuotientIsomorphism.md) | Proves Schrock's Conjecture 4.4.2.5 and the pointwise stabilizer conjecture in §4.5. Gives deterministic polynomial-time isomorphism testing from compact commutator data and group orders for arbitrary normal quotients of single flat blocks, including the decision task of Conjecture 4.4.4.5. [Schrock, §§4.4–4.5](https://math.colorado.edu/~tysc3584/Tyler_Schrock-PhD_Thesis.pdf#page=114) |
| [Fixed-format tensor isomorphism](FixedFormatTensorIso.md) | Kayal's published algorithm directly yields deterministic $\operatorname{poly}(\log q)$ decision for $3\times3\times3$ TI and every other fixed format over the explicitly presented field $\mathbb F_q$. Answers Qiao's Shonan question as a consequence of prior work. [Shonan Meeting 246, printed p. 14](https://shonan.nii.ac.jp/docs/No.246.pdf#page=15) |
| [No singular tuple over the input field](TensorSampler.md) | A zero-error polynomial-expected-time sampler works in every format for Narayanan's finite-field quantifier in (2.2). The geometric hyperdeterminant question has a different quantifier. The construction and a singularity density estimate are proved with the finite-field quantifier. [Narayanan, MFCS 2025, §§1–2](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol345-mfcs2025/html/LIPIcs.MFCS.2025.78/LIPIcs.MFCS.2025.78.html) |

The Fitting-free hypothesis in numbered Problem 12.4 refers to the quotient group itself. The broader socle question for arbitrary quotient inputs still has an abelian part to resolve. The monomial result addresses exact representation of witnesses.
