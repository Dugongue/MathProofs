# One symmetric cubic and one alternating three-form are TOCI-complete

**Theorem.** Over \(\mathbb C\), orbit-closure intersection under a single \(\operatorname{Sp}(W)\) is TOCI-complete on each of \(\operatorname{Sym}^3 W\) and \(\bigwedge^3 W\). There is an explicit rational affine reduction from the independent action on \(W_1\otimes W_2\otimes W_3\). Writing \(m=\max_i\dim W_i\), its target dimension is \(O(m\sum_i\dim W_i)\). The same statement for three-forms in \(\bigwedge^3 W^*\) follows from symplectic duality.

The proof uses the independent-action order-three threshold and the Kempf–Ness minimum-norm theorem. The orthogonal symmetric-cubic result uses separate quadratic markers and an orthogonal Laplacian. The construction here recovers the three source spaces from the cubic tensor at a minimum-norm representative and works with both full slot symmetries. [Independent-action hardness](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/SymplecticTensorFusions/OrbitClosures.md), [orthogonal cubic result](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/SymmetricCubicOCI/Proof.md), [Kempf–Ness](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0066647).

**The affine map.** Put each \(W_i\) in standard symplectic coordinates, with orthonormal basis for its Hermitian norm. Call its initial copy \(C_i\). Write \(n_i=\dim W_i\), take \(b=m+2\) arms for each \(i\), and index all \(3b\) arms by \(e\). For arm \(e\) at \(C_i\), make three new symplectic copies \(A_e,B_e,D_e\) of \(W_i\) and a symplectic plane \(L_e=\langle z_e,w_e\rangle\) with an orthonormal standard pair. All named blocks are mutually symplectically orthogonal and Hermitian orthogonal in \(W\). Let \(Q_{PQ}\) between identified copies \(P,Q\) of the same symplectic space be its coevaluation tensor, of coefficient matrix the inverse of the standard symplectic matrix. It is fixed under simultaneous symplectic action on its two copies, has both partial Gram matrices equal to identity, and has squared norm \(n_i\).

Number the arms \(j=1,\ldots,3b\) and take \(c_e=1+j/(10m(3b+1))\). The \(c_e\) are distinct and satisfy \(1<c_e<1+1/(10m)\), whence \(n_i c_e^2<n_i+1\). Let \(S_\pm\) be the sum over the six slot permutations, with all coefficients \(+1\) in the symmetric case and permutation signs in the alternating case. Define
\[
F_\pm(T)= S_\pm(T)+\sum_{e\text{ at }i} c_e\,
 S_\pm(Q_{C_i A_e}\otimes z_e + Q_{B_e D_e}\otimes w_e),
\]
where the data term places the three factors in the \(C_i\). Each triple uses distinct named blocks, so symmetrization or alternation is injective on that three-block component. The group \(\operatorname{Sp}(W_i)\) acts simultaneously on \(C_i\) and all copies on its arms, and trivially on marker planes. Thus \(F_\pm\) is equivariant for the block representation of the source group in \(\operatorname{Sp}(W)\). In particular it preserves orbit-closure intersection. It is affine in the data, and all dimensions and coefficient bit lengths are polynomial.

**The Gram calculation.** For a cubic tensor \(Z\subset W^{\otimes3}\), let \(G(Z)\) be the partial Hermitian Gram in the first slot. In a symmetric or alternating cubic the three partial Grams are identical. Two distinct support triples among those displayed in the formula share at most one named block. Thus no cross term between distinct triples contributes to this partial Gram, because the contracted slots would need two shared blocks. In one symmetrized or alternated triple, each named block occurs in the first slot in two permutations, which also have disjoint contracted support. Its contribution is twice the appropriate partial Gram before symmetrization, and the signs disappear. Consequently the Gram of \(F_\pm(T)\) is block diagonal, with the following restrictions.

- On \(C_i\) it is \(2\sum_{e\text{ at }i} c_e^2 I+2G_i(T)\), where \(G_i(T)\succeq 0\) is the \(i\)-th partial Gram of \(T\).
- On each \(A_e,B_e,D_e\) it is \(2c_e^2 I\).
- On \(L_e\) it is \(2n_i c_e^2 I\). The two terms use equal coefficient norms and orthogonal contracted blocks.

The Gram spectrum on \(C=\bigoplus_i C_i\) is strictly above \(2(m+1)\), as \(b=m+2\). All auxiliary eigenvalues are strictly below \(2(m+1)\). All plane eigenvalues are distinct: for unequal \(n_i\), the intervals \((2n_i,2(n_i+1))\) are disjoint, and for equal \(n_i\) the \(c_e\) differ. Plane eigenvalues exceed all leaf and mirror eigenvalues, since \(n_i\ge 2\) and \(c_e<1+1/(10m)\). The Gram therefore determines \(C\) and every \(L_e\) intrinsically under unitary transport.

With \(P_C,P_{L_e}\) their Hermitian projections, \((P_C\otimes 1\otimes P_{L_e})F_\pm(T)\) has first-mode support exactly \(C_i\). The payload has no marker, the mirror term has no central block, and only the term \(Q_{C_i A_e}\otimes z_e\) with its displayed slot order survives. Its first-mode support is \(C_i\) by nondegeneracy. Thus every unitary transporter of two such cubics carries each \(C_i\) to its corresponding \(C_i\).

**Reflection through closed orbits.** Replace each source tensor by a minimum-norm vector in the unique closed orbit in its closure. A source vector \(T_0\) so chosen has zero moment in each independent factor: \(\operatorname{tr}(H_i G_i(T_0))=0\) for every Hermitian \(H_i\) in the complex symplectic Lie algebra. At \(F_\pm(T_0)\), the ambient diagonal group moment is also zero. For a Hermitian ambient symplectic Lie algebra element, its diagonal block in each symplectic block is in that block's symplectic Lie algebra and has trace zero. In the Gram formula all scalar blocks therefore give zero moment, and the only non-scalar terms pair with the three source moments. Off-diagonal Lie algebra blocks pair to zero with the block-diagonal Gram. The ambient cubic orbit is closed by Kempf–Ness.

If the two output orbit closures meet, the padded minimum-norm representatives are in their common closed orbit, since equivariance put one closed orbit into each closure. Two minimum-norm representatives in a common complex orbit are conjugate under the compact, unitary symplectic group. The Gram argument makes such a transporter preserve every \(C_i\), and its restrictions are symplectic. Reading the component in \(C_1\otimes C_2\otimes C_3\) recovers a source transporter between the two closed representatives. This proves reflection. Membership follows by the equivariant closed embeddings of the two symmetry types into ordered cubic tensors and the single-group membership argument in the [tuple theorem](tuple-threshold.md).
