# Priority audit for all-fields cubic equivalence

The broad polynomial-time classification was already obtainable from earlier published results. The direct unmarked algebra with $z^d=f$ retains a distinct claim. This audit found a close truncated-algebra predecessor and a prior abstract route, but did not locate the same one-extra-variable construction with recovery of its line from degree-two multiplication.

## Prior direct and abstract proofs

- **Agrawal–Saxena, 2006.** Theorem 2.3 of the journal paper uses $F[x]/(f,\mathfrak m^{d+1})$ and assumes every scalar has a $d$th root. This already applies to cubics over every finite field of characteristic three. Theorem 3.10 supplies the opposite direction from commutative algebra isomorphism over any field. The original quotient records the line of the form. One can see the lost arithmetic with $x^3$ and $2x^3$ over $\mathbb F_7$, which produce the same ideal and lie in different formal cubic orbits. [Journal paper](https://www.cse.iitk.ac.in/%75sers/nitin/papers/cubic-forms.pdf)
- **Futorny–Grochow–Sergeichuk, 2019, and Grochow–Qiao, 2023.** The fixed tensor-system compiler works over arbitrary fields. Give it the ordered spaces $F,V,\operatorname{Sym}^2 V,\operatorname{Sym}^3 V$, the scalar multiplication on $F$, the maps $V\times V\to\operatorname{Sym}^2 V$ and $V\times\operatorname{Sym}^2 V\to\operatorname{Sym}^3 V$, and $F\to\operatorname{Sym}^3 V$ sending $1$ to $f$. A transporter fixes the scalar line pointwise and acts on the two symmetric powers by the induced actions, since monomial products span them. Thus this is already a same-field ordinary three-tensor reduction, with the standard commutative unital algebra hardness and completeness constructions giving the abstract classification. The same argument handles every fixed degree using a fixed number of multiplication maps. The project already recorded this route in its earlier work on raising form degree. [Tensor-system theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.09219) · [Part I, Theorem B and Section 7.1](https://doi.org/10.1137/21M1441110) · [Earlier project proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/FormDegreeRaising/Proof.md)
- **Grochow–Qiao–Stange–Sun, Part V, October 2026.** Theorem 3.7 explicitly lists the orbit problem on the third symmetric power alongside ordinary three-tensor isomorphism for rings with the diagonal-full property, which includes every field by Corollary 3.5. Taken with the paper's definition of symmetric powers, the statement covers formal cubics. The abstract tensor-system proof above supplies the field reduction independently of any interpretation of symmetric array coordinates in small characteristic. [Part V, Sections 2.1, 3.2 and 3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00735v1#S3.SS3)

## What to present

The earlier top-30 briefing should lead with the **intrinsic algebra model**, rather than priority for the all-fields complexity class equality. The direct proof uses one extra algebra generator, has dimension $\binom{n+d}{d}+d-1$, and extracts a literal form transporter from an arbitrary isomorphism of the unmarked algebras. The input field need not be perfect. That particular presentation gives access to augmentation filtrations, automorphism schemes, multiplication tensors and arithmetic conditions under extension. The four proofs in this packet use that access in expressly stated ways. The finite characteristic-three decision exponent has a separate prior-art audit. [Direct construction](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/GrochowFarDAGII/Tensor.md) · [Algorithm priority audit](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/main/GrochowPureCube/Priority.md)

The searches included the primary papers above, their characteristic assumptions, and targeted searches for root-independent truncated-algebra encodings and recovery of an auxiliary line. The abstract consequence and the explicit Part V statement settle adoption of the broad claim as prior scope. Priority for the particular presentation and the new combinations still needs external review.
