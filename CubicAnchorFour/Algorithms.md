# Prime-field multiplication tensors that preserve cubic transporters

Let $K=\mathbb F_q$, $q=p^e$, be explicitly represented over $k=\mathbb F_p$. For $e>1$, use the generator $\theta$ in the supplied polynomial presentation of $K$. No search for a primitive multiplicative element is needed. A standard graded local $K$-algebra here is a finite-dimensional commutative unital algebra $A=\bigoplus_{j\ge0}A_j$, generated by $A_1$ with $A_0=K$. Fix an upper bound $d\ge3$ on its positive degrees.

**Theorem.** There is an explicit standard graded local $K$-algebra $B(A,\theta)$, of dimension $\dim_K A+4d+26$, with the following properties.

- The ordinary $k$-linear multiplication tensors of $B(A,\theta)$ and $B(A',\theta)$ are isomorphic exactly when $A$ and $A'$ are $K$-linearly isomorphic as graded algebras. Every multiplication-tensor transporter determines a graded $K$-isomorphism of the data algebras, and every such isomorphism lifts.
- Before or after normalizing a transporter by evaluating its two input units, its three maps are $K$-linear. On standard based input the construction has deterministic polynomial bit complexity in $e,\log p,\dim_K A,d$. Each tensor side has length $e(\dim_K A+4d+26)$. In particular it has $O(en)$ side length on linear-size cubic trilinear carriers.
- For the standard formal cubic jet $A_f$, with $n\ge2$ variables, the projection from ordinary $k$-tensor transporters to $K$-form transporters has the explicit constant fiber size in Section 4. In characteristic three with $n\equiv3\bmod4$, each of the three $k$-determinants of such a transporter equals the field norm of the determinant of the recovered form map. In particular the same construction simulates exact norm-one form equivalence by literal special-linear three-tensor equivalence over $\mathbb F_3$.

For $e=1$ ordinary transport uses $A$ itself. The tagged statements below assume $e>1$.

## 1. The algebra that fixes the coefficient field

Put $D=d+1$. Use four generators $u_0,\ldots,u_3$ and groups $W_i$ of respectively $r_i=0,2,4,6$ degree-one marker vectors. Include $u_i^j$ in each degree $1\le j<D$. In degree two also include a copy $E_i$ of $W_i$ defined by $u_iw=e(w)$ for $w\in W_i$. The $E_i$ are in the socle, meaning they annihilate all positive degrees. All products between distinct arms vanish, as do products of two marker vectors and products $u_i^jw$ for $j\ge2$. The top degree is spanned by $s,t$ with the four powers
$$
 u_0^D=s,\qquad u_1^D=t,\qquad u_2^D=s+t,\qquad u_3^D=s+\theta t.
 \tag{1}
$$
One obtains the resulting algebra $T_\theta$ as the fiber product over $K$ of four augmented truncated algebras in the $u_i,W_i$. Impose the two linear relations (1) among their top socle elements. This quotient proves associativity and gives the listed basis. Set $B=A\times_K T_\theta$ via the augmentations. The common constant term is $K$. All mixed products between the two positive ideals are zero. The extra dimension is $4(D-1)+2\sum_i r_i+2=4d+26$.

The maximal ideal $J$ of $B$ is its positive part. The coefficient subfield $K\subseteq B$ is intrinsic after restriction to $k$ because it is precisely the set of roots of $X^q-X$ in $B$. In fact write a root as $a+v$ with $a\in K$ and $v\in J$. Then $v^q=v$, which forces $v=0$ by nilpotence and iteration. Consequently a $k$-algebra isomorphism of the tagged objects is semilinear on their coefficient fields, with a single $\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(K/k)$.

The powers of $J$ recover the associated grading of $B$. In its degree-one piece $B_1$ take the kernel $C$ of the contraction of the $D$-fold product,
$$
 C=\{v\in B_1:v x_2\cdots x_D=0\text{ for all }x_2,\ldots,x_D\in B_1\}
   = A_1\oplus\bigoplus_i W_i .
$$
This equality follows by testing a putative kernel vector against $D-1$ copies of one $u_i$. On $B_1/C$, the rank-one contractions into $B_D$ give exactly the four lines of the $u_i$. Indeed the image of a contraction is the span of the nonzero top powers of the axes in its support. The four lines in (1) are pairwise distinct since $\theta\ne0,1$. Thus a support of size at least two gives rank two. To distinguish the four lines intrinsically, take the minimum rank of degree-two multiplication by any lift of a nonzero vector on the line. On the $i$-th line the minimum is $r_i+1$. Its own arm contributes $u_i^2$ and $E_i$ to the image. The coefficient on $u_i$ is nonzero, so adding markers in its arm changes this image only by a triangular operation. Other arms and the data products contribute independently in degree two, and the lift with no other components attains the minimum. These distinct integers order all four lines.

An induced semilinear map on $B_D$ must therefore preserve all four labeled lines in (1). Its action on the first two lines is $s\mapsto a s$, $t\mapsto b t$. The third forces $a=b$, and the fourth then gives $\sigma(\theta)=\theta$. Thus $\sigma=1$.

Finally the degree-one data space itself is recovered inside $C$. In degree two let $C^2$ denote the span of products of two vectors in $C$. The bilinear map
$$
 C\times (B_1/C)\longrightarrow B_2/C^2
$$
has left radical exactly $A_1$. Every vector in $A_1$ multiplies a tag generator to zero and contributes only $C^2$ on other lifts. A nonzero marker is detected by its own $u_i$ using the independent direction in $E_i$. Since $A_1$ generates $A$, this recovers the entire graded data algebra from the unmarked prime-field algebra isomorphism. Conversely a graded $K$-isomorphism of data algebras extends by the identity on the tag.

To pass from multiplication tensors to algebra isomorphisms, use input-input-output coordinates $H(xy)=P(x)Q(y)$. Evaluating on the units gives $P=L_a\phi$, $Q=L_b\phi$, $H=L_{ab}\phi$ for a unique unital algebra isomorphism $\phi$ and units $a,b$ in the target. Indeed $a=P(1)$ and $b=Q(1)$ have invertible multiplication operators by these same equations. The converse is immediate by multiplication. Since $\phi$ and these multiplication operators are $K$-linear, the three maps themselves are $K$-linear. To write the prime tensor, expand products in the power basis of $K$. All products and basis operations are polynomial bit time. The restriction only multiplies the dimension by $e$.

**Linear-length TI corollary.** Given an ordinary three-tensor pair over $K$ of total side length $n_0$, one can use the theorem to produce ordinary multiplication tensors over $k$ of side length $O(e n_0)$ preserving the decision. Use the Grochow–Qiao IV linear-length reduction to symmetric trilinear-form isometry, and quotient out each form's contraction radical. Unequal original or quotient dimensions reject, and a linear isometry of concise quotients lifts through radicals of equal dimension.

For a concise symmetric trilinear $T$ on $X$, use data degree one $X\oplus Kz\oplus Kw$, degree two $X^*\oplus Kz^2\oplus K\varepsilon$, and degree three $Ks_0$. Define $xx'=T(x,x',-)$, $x\ell=\ell(x)s_0$, $zw=\varepsilon$, and $z^3=s_0$, with the unlisted positive products zero and $z^2$ the square of $z$. Associativity follows from symmetry of $T$ on $X^3$ and the truncated arm relations. In degree two the annihilator of degree one is $K\varepsilon$. Reducing the degree-two product modulo that line gives degree-one radical $Kw$. The original annihilator of $Kw$ is $X\oplus Kw$, and the reduced multiplication recovers $Kz$ in the quotient by $Kw$ as the annihilator of $X$. Thus any graded data isomorphism induces $z\mapsto\lambda z$ and $S:X\to X'$ in that quotient. Triple products give $T'(Sx,Sy,Sv)=\lambda^3T(x,y,v)$, yielding the isometry $\lambda^{-1}S$. Each isometry extends by inverse duality and the identity on the attached arm. Zero concise dimension has the same marker construction with $X=0$. The data dimension is $2\dim X+6$, proving the asserted linear length after adding the field tag. The ordinary decision reduction is compatible with earlier fixed-characteristic field independence. Its additional property is that the target tensors are unmarked multiplications of local algebras. [Grochow–Qiao IV, Theorem 1.10 and Section 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.16317)

## 2. Specialization to the anchored cubic

For a formal cubic $f\in\operatorname{Sym}^3 V$, $\dim V=n\ge2$, use
$$
 A_f=\operatorname{Sym}(V\oplus Kz)/
          \big((V,z)^4,\ zV,\ z^3-f\big).
 \tag{2}
$$
Let $D_2=\dim\operatorname{Sym}^2 V$ and $D_3=\dim\operatorname{Sym}^3 V$. In degrees one, two and three the pieces are $V\oplus Kz,\operatorname{Sym}^2V\oplus Kz^2,\operatorname{Sym}^3V$, respectively. Hence $A_f$ has dimension $a_f=\binom{n+3}{3}+2$, and $N=\dim_K B(A_f,\theta)=a_f+38$.

In degree one of $A_f$ the unique line with nonzero rank-one multiplication into degree two is $Kz$. Indeed a nonzero element of $V$ multiplies $V$ injectively into $\operatorname{Sym}^2V$, and $z^2$ is independent of this image. Its annihilator in degree one is $V$. A graded isomorphism therefore uses $S\in\mathrm{GL}(V)$ and $z\mapsto\lambda z$, and (2) yields $(\operatorname{Sym}^3 S)f=\lambda^3g$. Put $h=\lambda^{-1}S$; then $(\operatorname{Sym}^3h)f=g$. Each such $h$, with any $\lambda\in K^\times$, conversely gives a graded lift. This is valid in every characteristic, including characteristic three. The original jet has no common degree-one radical in its degree-two product, even when $f=0$.

## 3. Determinants and a strict orbit split

For any graded tagged isomorphism on the jets, each $u_i$ maps to $a_i u_i+w_i$ with $w_i\in W_i$, and $W_i$ maps by $G_i\in\mathrm{GL}(W_i)$. To justify the shape, an image of $u_i$ has the minimum degree-two multiplication rank $r_i+1$. Thus it has no components in the data, since that degree-one product has zero common radical, and has no marker from another arm. A marker image lies in $C$, and its product with $C$ is zero, ruling out any data component. The relations with the other named axes exclude all other marker groups. The only constraints are $a_i^4=b$ for a common $b$, from (1), with each $w_i,G_i$ free. Any graded iso on the data and choices of these parameters extend, and there are no other graded choices.

The contribution of the tag to the $K$-determinant is
$$
 b^2 \prod_{i=0}^3 a_i^{6+r_i}(\det G_i)^2.
 \tag{3}
$$
The summands are the weights from $u_i,u_i^2,u_i^3$, the action $G_i$ on $W_i$, the action $a_iG_i$ on $E_i$, and the common action $b$ on the top two coordinates. All exponents in (3) are even. In the data branch, summing the symmetric-power determinant exponents gives $E=\binom{n+3}{2}$. The extra $z,z^2$ weights contribute $\lambda^3$, so the data determinant is
$$
 (\det h)^E\,\lambda^{nE+3}.
$$
These formulas also determine filtered determinants, by triangularity relative to the filtration. The determinant of restricting a $K$-linear map to $k$ is the field norm of its $K$-determinant, as can be seen after splitting the separable scalar algebra. If $k=\mathbb F_3$ and $n\equiv3\bmod4$, then $E$ is odd, $nE+3$ is even, and $N$ is even. Thus the $k$-determinant of a tagged algebra transporter is $N_{K/k}(\det h)$. A unit of scalar residue $u$ has $k$-regular determinant $N_{K/k}(u)^N=1$ by filtering its multiplication. Inverse-dual conventions for the tensor input legs do not change signs. This proves the claimed determinant triple.

For a strict example take $K=\mathbb F_9$, $f=xyz$, and $g=\xi xyz$ for $\xi$ a nonsquare. Unique factorization of linear forms shows that every automorphism of $f$ is monomial, with the three nonzero entries having product $1$. Its determinant is therefore $\pm1$, both of norm $1$ in this extension. The diagonal substitution with entries $\xi,1,1$ has determinant of norm $-1$ and transports $f$ to $g$. Every transporter has this norm. The resulting prime-field multiplication tensors are in the same ordinary orbit and different special-linear orbits.

A separate check shows why the coefficient field needs to be fixed before applying the norm argument. For $K=\mathbb F_{3^e}$ with $e>4$ and $\theta$ generating $K$, the quartics $xy(x-y)(x-\theta y)$ and $xy(x-y)(x-\theta^3y)$ are Frobenius conjugates. They are not linearly equivalent over $K$. Such an equivalence would put $\theta^3$ among the six cross-ratio transforms of $\theta$, all fractional-linear with prime-field coefficients, contrary to the minimal polynomial degree of $\theta$. The untagged jets of these forms become isomorphic after scalar restriction by entrywise Frobenius, while the tagged restriction reflects the negative answer.

## 4. Exact cubic transporter counts

Let $t(f,g)$ be the number of exact $K$-linear form transporters, and set $\eta=1$ if $g=0$ and $0$ otherwise. Define
$$
 e_A=nD_2+(n+1)D_3+1+n\eta,\qquad
 \Gamma=(q-1)\gcd(4,q-1)^3\,q^{12}
              \prod_{i=0}^3|\operatorname{GL}(r_i,K)|.
$$
Then the number of ordinary $k$-linear multiplication-tensor transporters is
$$
 t(f,g)(q-1)\,q^{e_A}\,
 \Gamma\,q^{244+14(n+1)+16(D_3+\eta)}\,
 (q-1)^2 q^{2(N-1)}.
 \tag{4}
$$
Under the characteristic-three and rank hypotheses of Section 3, the determinant-one count uses the same factor with $t(f,g)$ replaced by the number of form transporters of norm-one determinant.

Here are the factors in (4). The number of choices for a graded tag map is $\Gamma$. There are $q^{12}$ free $w_i$, independent $G_i$, and $(q-1)\gcd(4,q-1)^3$ quadruples $(a_i)$ with equal fourth power. Each graded data isomorphism has $q^{e_A}$ possible filtration lifts in the original jet. To see this, the quadratic corrections on $x\in V$ and $z$ are $Q(x)+\ell(x)z^2$ and $B+bz^2$. The only nonautomatic relation is $xB+\ell(x)g=0$. If $B\ne0$, multiplication by $B$ embeds the $n$-dimensional space $V$ into degree three, so its image cannot be contained in $Kg$. Thus $B=0$ and $\ell$ is free precisely when $g=0$. The remaining quadratic corrections and all cubic corrections are free.

The degree-raising kernel for the tag alone has order $q^{244}$. In a map inducing the identity on the grading, each $u_i$ can acquire independent $u_i^2,u_i^3$ components and an arbitrary vector in the $14$-dimensional tag socle $S=\bigoplus_i E_i\oplus\langle s,t\rangle$. Each marker in $W_i$ can acquire an arbitrary multiple of $u_i^3$ and a vector in $S$. For necessity, products $u_i u_j=0$ with $i\ne j$ first eliminate the off-arm quadratic coefficients at degree three. Their degree-four equations then eliminate off-arm cubic coefficients since the two top lines are independent. The same argument for $u_jw_i=0$ removes off-arm corrections to the marker. Finally $u_i^2w_i=0$ removes its own quadratic correction. These choices preserve all defining relations, with the $E_i$ images determined by $u_iw_i$. Their total number is $q^{4(14+2)+12(14+1)}=q^{244}$.

Across the two branches, a filtration lift inducing the graded identity can add arbitrary tag-socle terms to data generators. In fact multiplying by the four $u_i$ first kills any $u_i^2$ terms in such corrections at degree three, and then kills any $u_i^3$ terms at degree four. Conversely it can add arbitrary terms from the degree-at-least-two data socle to tag generators. Multiplying these corrections by data degree-one elements is the only mixed data condition, because products of two corrections in the data have degree at least four and vanish. This part of the data socle has dimension $D_3+\eta$: all degree three is in the socle, and $z^2$ joins it exactly for the zero cubic. These independent shifts give $q^{14(n+1)+16(D_3+\eta)}$. They preserve all relations because the two positive branches multiply to zero. Finally the two units in the tensor normalization supply $(q-1)^2q^{2(N-1)}$. These arguments describe the complete fiber even if additional filtered maps fail to preserve the displayed direct-sum bases.

Running `python Algorithms.py` checks 59,319 basis associativity relations over the field of nine elements, all 6,561 support vectors for the rank-one contraction test, and all 512 common-fourth-power choices in the scalar determinant formula. It also exhausts the top two-dimensional group to test that the ordered lines admit eight linear maps and no maps with the nontrivial Frobenius action.

## Relation to prior work

Agrawal and Saxena use truncated algebras to encode formal forms. Fiber products and identifications in the socle occur in the connected-sum construction of Ananthnarayan, Avramov, and Moore. The four named top powers here use that operation to fix a coefficient generator inside an unmarked algebra, while intrinsic tangent multiplication recovers the data. [Agrawal–Saxena, *On the Complexity of Cubic Forms*](https://www.cse.iitk.ac.in/%75sers/nitin/papers/cubic-forms.pdf) · [Ananthnarayan–Avramov–Moore, *Connected Sums of Gorenstein Local Rings*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.1304)

The fixed-characteristic field-independence construction already uses prime-field restriction with marked scalar multiplication matrices. This result gives a different restriction for multiplication tensors of anchored forms and standard graded local algebras. It dispenses with marked tensors and the general tensor-system compiler on those inputs. The determinant norm and exact cubic transporter fibers are additional data. Grochow and Qiao's field-comparison question gives the complexity setting, and the result above retains more structure than a decision reduction. The proof is mathematical prose. Priority for the tagged determinant and counting combination remains open. [Grochow–Qiao, *Tensor Isomorphism-Completeness*, Open Question 7.6](https://doi.org/10.1137/21M1441110) · [Grochow–Qiao–Stange–Sun, Part V over commutative rings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00735)
