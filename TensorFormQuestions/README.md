# Four questions around tensor and cubic-form isomorphism

This package records informal proofs and a literature correction. It does not claim four new broadly awaited breakthroughs. Publication priority for the new combinations is provisional.

| Target | Result and scope |
|---|---|
| Agrawal–Saxena algebra-to-cubic reduction over a commutative ring | A coefficient-copying reduction for finite-free commutative associative unital algebras in a supplied basis. This specifies the input model for the ring question. |
| Agrawal–Saxena local-to-global question for cubic forms | A negative answer using explicit six-variable smooth integral cubics equivalent over the reals and modulo every positive integer, yet inequivalent over the rationals. |
| Grochow–Qiao Open Question 7.8 on the size of arity reduction | An all-field upper bound poly(d) n^ceil(d/3), and a matching exponent lower bound for algebraically equivariant polynomial reductions. This settles the optimal exponent in that model. The nonequivariant fixed-d reading of the original question remains open. |
| Decidability of rational tensor and form equivalence | A credited classical consequence of Sarkisyan and Kneser–Harder–Chernousov, with the cocycle reduction written out. This is not claimed as our discovery. |

The first two proofs are in [Ring and local-to-global results](RingAndLocalGlobal.md). The precise size theorem and its model are in [Arity reduction](ArityReduction.md). The terminating classical algorithm is explained in [Rational decidability](RationalDecidability.md).

The ring reduction is based on the cubic q_A(x,y,phi)=phi(xy). Its mixed polarization has centroid exactly A over every commutative base ring. The local-to-global example uses the classical six-point Klein-four Gassmann relation, specialized to quadratic fields generated by square roots of 13 and 17. All ingredients and prior authors are credited in the proofs.

The arity construction combines product trees, logarithmically many binary projection markers, and the published linear-length partition-removal theorem. The lower bound uses weights under products of special-linear diagonal tori. Its equivariance hypothesis is explicit.

All mathematical proofs here are informal. No new Lean certificate is included. The unrestricted polynomial-time reduction from SL-TI to ordinary TI remains open.
