# Top 30 research register — 27 November 2026

This register follows the numbering of the 27 November research handoff. It records the scope of each result and the verification available for it. A `written proof` or `internal audit` label describes the evidence reported by the project. `Lean` identifies an explicitly documented formal artifact. Claims that depend on a recent external result retain that dependency. The [previous portfolio ranking](README.md) uses a different order.

### 01. Extension and lifting trichotomy (Theorem T)

The program asks for a P / NP-complete / computably enumerable complete classification of bounded-dimensional relative extension, coherent diagram lifting and related arithmetic problems. The global theorem remains open. Written results include a mixed quadratic diagram trichotomy and a mixed three-stage trichotomy. The handoff's stronger results on virtually nilpotent fundamental groups and general coherent templates have separate outstanding audit and algorithmic obligations. Some proposed hardness branches use Koymans–Pagano, Garreta–Miasnikov–Ovchinnikov or the rational Hilbert-tenth premise below. [Two-stage mixed theorem](../MixedPostnikovTrichotomy/README.md) · [Three-stage theorem](../HigherPostnikovTrichotomy/README.md) · [Bilinear arithmetic](ManinExtensions.md)

### 02. Planar tensor-price bridge and partial-transpose rank

The project derives a planar shortening obstruction from spectral flatness and uses it for row-ordered noncommutative permanent and determinant bounds, a complex sum-of-squares bound, and a partial-transpose rank lower bound of the form $n^{\sqrt d-o(1)}$. The glue and several consequences passed an internal audit with fixes. Exact partial-transpose rank growth remains unaudited. The deductions rely on the OpenAI Euclidean spectrum and flatness results. Liu's later version claims a sharper ordered-circuit bound, so the earlier exponent should be read in its historical scope. [Liu v1](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00994v1) · [Liu v2](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00994v2) · [Euclidean spectrum preprint](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/Euclidean-geometry-in-the-asymptotic-spectrum-of-tensors-November-24-2026)

### 03. Domination and rational tensor isomorphism

For the equal-second-Betti-number slice of simply connected six-manifolds in the stated Wall-system input model, the written reductions identify nonzero-degree domination with tensor isomorphism over $\mathbb Q$ under polynomial-time reductions. The equivalence is internally audited. The decidability reading of the slice uses separate unrefereed rational tensor-isomorphism work. The undecidability claim for the full domination problem uses the rational Hilbert-tenth premise.

### 04. Poonen's dimension-reduction question

For a fixed number field and $n$, a rational-point existence oracle for smooth projective geometrically integral varieties of dimension exactly $n+2$ decides rational-point existence for varieties of dimension at most $n$ and computes the points when finite. The norm-form pencil construction is an unconditional written proof with internal citation fixes. Poonen's journal numbering places this as question (i) of §12. [Proof and source question](../PoonenDimensionReduction/README.md)

### 05. Rational Diophantine hardness of PL embeddability

The handoff records a reduction from rational Hilbert-tenth to PL embeddability of $k$-complexes in $\mathbb R^d$ for $d\ge8$ and $\lceil(2d-1)/3\rceil\le k\le d$, with repairs to the algebraic encoding and the base dimension. The claimed completeness consequence uses the rational Hilbert-tenth premise. A separate almost-embeddability claim was withdrawn. Status is a written reduction with internal review and repairs.

### 06. Rational points on smooth affine hypersurfaces

For each fixed number field the existence problem for smooth geometrically integral affine hypersurfaces is computably many-one equivalent to Hilbert's tenth problem over that field. The uniform number-field statement is a Turing reduction. The written argument also gives a degree-eight restriction over $\mathbb Q$. Its undecidability reading over $\mathbb Q$ uses the rational Hilbert-tenth premise. [Proof](../RationalPointDecision/PROOF.md)

### 07. Binary rank-two matrix completion

Partial matrices with specified entries in $\{0,1\}$ and completion rank at most two express the existential theory of the field, in the scope of the stated reductions. A pinned identity minor forces exact rank two on accepting instances. The package records finite-field NP-completeness and a separate scheme universality result. The rational undecidability consequence uses the rational Hilbert-tenth premise. [Decision proof](../RankTwoAndRingUniversality/RankTwoCompletion.md) · [Geometric universality](../RankTwoGeometricUniversality/PROOF.md)

### 08. Exact partition rank of the determinant

For the column determinant over every field the written result gives partition rank $n-1$ for even $n\ge4$ and $n$ for odd $n$ or $n=2$. The internal audit records a repaired Chow-ring injectivity step. The exact permanent-rank comparison has separate prior-art attribution. [Proof](../GrochowOpenProblemSolutions2/DETERMINANT-PARTITION-RANK.md)

### 09. Fortnow–Grochow canonization and kernels

The audited written strand concerns the relationship of canonization, complete invariants, and P versus NP, with an oracle-relative formulation. The complex tensor-isomorphism in AM strand is a separate ledger claim invoking a Dedekind-GRH input. The handoff could not locate a dedicated proof or audit for that strand. [Canonization proof](../CanonicalFormsAndPNP/PROOF.md)

### 10. Orthogonal tensor invariants and Mulmuley's conjecture

The handoff records a barrier to succinct invariant generators for the stated $O_N^3$ action, with a Luna-slice transfer of the grid circuit lower bound. Theorem O has a written proof and internal audit fixes and uses the external OpenAI grid bound. Several related refutation claims overlap earlier work. Priority and scope should be assessed separately from the transfer.

### 11. Special-linear tensor invariants (SL³ Theorem S)

The companion result concerns generators or succinct encodings for the invariant ring of $SL_N^3$ acting on one cubic tensor. It uses the OpenAI grid lower bound and a stabilizer construction. The internal audit requires a component-group argument and distinguishes this claim from the question of finding a single easy invariant. An all-edges-swapped stabilizer case remains a hand step identified in the handoff. This is the special-linear result, distinct from the Levi-transfer theorem sharing its name.

### 12. Halting encoded by a fixed tensor network

In the audited core construction, varying only the boundary of a fixed commuting matrix-product family makes halting equivalent to endpoint entanglement at some chain length. Every state is positive definite and every proper marginal is maximally mixed. Separate extensions about quantifier patterns over lengths remain unaudited. [Written construction](../TensorHaltingThresholds/Network.md)

### 13. Generic tensor non-isomorphism proof degree

The handoff records a written $O(n^{3/2})$ degree bound for Nullstellensatz refutations for generic pairs of cubic tensors, without field equations. The source report was located. The handoff and secondary ranking disagree on audit status, and no dedicated audit was identified. The worst-case bound remains open.

### 14. Algebraic circuit dichotomies and hardness transfers

The bundle records p-projection completeness results for the three-dimensional permanent, Cayley hyperdeterminant and mixed discriminant, with characteristic-two constructions and several subclass dichotomies. Internal audits reported mathematical passes with priority or wording fixes. Assertions that a VNP-complete family lies outside VP depend on the separate VP versus VNP input. [External separation manuscript](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00954v1)

### 15. Explicit bounds from VP versus VNP work

This is a conditional transfer of spectral flatness and the Anthropic separation framework to explicit permanent and Hamiltonian-cycle bounds. The internal audit corrected the distinction between the first input size for which a bound is stated and the size at which the lower bound dominates a chosen polynomial. The bridge is written and has no claimed independent Lean replay. [External separation manuscript](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00954v1) · [Spectral flatness source](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/Euclidean-geometry-in-the-asymptotic-spectrum-of-tensors-November-24-2026)

### 16. Finite-Fourier transfer to binary circuits

Restriction of scalars from $\mathbb F_4$ to $\mathbb F_2$ transfers the cited finite-Fourier lower bounds to explicit XOR-circuit and rigidity instances. The transfer passed internal review with minor fixes and finite checks. The superlinear lower bounds rely on the external finite-Fourier and Euclidean-spectrum inputs.

### 17. Bounds for the Hrubeš parity exponent

The handoff records a lower exponent of $\varphi^{-1}$ from spectral flatness and an upper exponent of $2-\log_2(1+\sqrt2)\approx0.7284$ from a symplectic covering construction. The lower bound is conditional on the OpenAI flatness theorem. The upper construction has its own field restrictions. Both arguments passed internal review with the stated fixes.

### 18. Rational separability and rational Hilbert-tenth

The written reductions identify rational product-vector separability of rational PSD matrices with rational Hilbert-tenth under computable many-one reductions. The hardness direction produces matrices promised separable over the reals. The internal audit supports the computable reduction. A polynomial-time bound remains a sketch, and undecidability uses the rational Hilbert-tenth premise.

### 19. Average-case orthogonal and unitary tensor isomorphism in Lean

The formal artifact covers orthogonal and Hermitian unitary actions over the stated finite fields and rectangular formats. It gives a zero-error expected-polynomial-time solver on the certified class. The checked-in replay reports 35,192 constants accepted. Li–Li–Qiao–Tao–Wang v2 gives overlapping odd-characteristic cubic orthogonal results with stronger success guarantees, so any priority claim needs the exact version and scope. [Lean theorem](../Problem18/Problem18.lean) · [Kernel replay](../Problem18/kernel-replay.log) · [Original paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00591v1) · [Revised paper v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00591v2)

### 20. Characteristic-two matrix tuple isometry and Witt lifting

The handoff reports a written lifting criterion and a polynomial-time claim for matrix tuple isometry over $\mathbb F_{2^e}$. The characteristic-two pipeline was internally audited and compared with brute force on 120,759 small instance pairs. The recorded tests use a randomized subroutine, so the general deterministic implementation and stabilizer generation have separate outstanding obligations. The extension to higher powers of two in the coefficient ring is informal.

### 21. Consequences of the proposed matrix multiplication exponent 9/4

The collection proposes consequences for asymptotic tensor rank, spectrum points, and arithmetic circuit bounds using the OpenAI 9/4 and Euclidean-price manuscripts. The consequences are unaudited. The handoff flags a border-rank comparison that needs reconciliation before claims using it are relied on. [Matrix multiplication preprint](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/The-matrix-multiplication-exponent-is-nine-fourths-November-24-2026)

### 22. Tensor isomorphism for classical groups

This entry records the symplectic equivalence to ordinary tensor isomorphism, unitary-to-ordinary reductions in fixed characteristic, and padding for forms of sufficiently large Witt index. Internal review treats the symplectic and unitary branches separately. Only the former has the stronger formal evidence reported in the handoff. Definite real orthogonal and complex unitary cases are outside the stated closure.

### 23. Lazard threshold and magic-square hardness

Below the least prime divisor of the modulus, an operator solution with nilpotency class below that prime gives a classical solution. For the fixed-group problem, admitting the specified magic-square solution gives NP-completeness. The handoff reports internal audits and finite checks. A general dichotomy for finite groups with a distinguished central element remains open.

### 24. Deciding pre-injectivity of group cellular automata

The public written proof gives decidability for a finite alphabet group, a group subshift on $\mathbb Z^d$ given by finite forbidden patterns, and a group cellular automaton given by a local rule. The decision reduces to rank tests over rational function fields. [Proof and Béaur–Kari question](../GroupCAPreinjectivity/README.md)

### 25. Diagonal-functional promise CSPs and cyclic rounding

The internal audit supports the diagonal-functional subclass of Nakajima–Živný Problem 63, and the characterization of the stated cyclic targets with size $3^p$ or $2\cdot3^p$ for the AIP rounding test. The argument includes explicit rounding. The broader multiple-successor case remains open. The handoff credits the contributing ChatGPT research and records the overlap with earlier rainbow-free work. [Relevant PCSP literature](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/Stanislav.Zivny/publications/ckknz24lics-preprint.pdf)

### 26. Restricted integral solvability beyond étale–Brauer–Manin

The located theorem concerns any fixed totally real number field $K$ and prescribed connectivity bound. It gives a computable family of smooth affine $\mathcal O_K$-schemes, cut out by two equations of degree at most four, with computably enumerable complete integral-point existence. Local integral points exist at every finite place, and the generic-fiber adèles survive the stated étale–Brauer–Manin test. The proof cites Denef for the totally real integral input. Extrapolation to a separate rational-points claim requires its own statement and arithmetic premise.

### 27. Polynomial-time co-H-space recognition

For fixed $c\ge2$, the public written algorithm recognizes co-H-spaces among pointed $(c-1)$-connected finite complexes of dimension at most $4c-2$. Its complexity uses the stated effective Moore–Postnikov and stable-section computation model. [Proof](../CoHSpaceRecognition/README.md)

### 28. NAE promise CSP hardness and loop conditions

The handoff records hardness for $(R^*,\mathrm{NAE}_3[3])$ and a short loop-condition treatment of the Krokhin–Vagnozzi $(\mathrm{LO}_2,\mathrm{LO}_3)$ result. The hardness argument uses the cited external coloring preprint. An internal audit reverified the SAT certificates, while regeneration of one instance from its construction remains outstanding. [Coloring preprint](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.01095v1) · [Krokhin–Vagnozzi](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2026.184)

### 29. Tame and wild quiver decision problems

The audited core covers the stated finite-field hardness for quivers containing the specified wild subquiver and reductions for embedding and subrepresentation existence. General wild quivers and the Euclidean algorithm over number fields remain sketches. The rational undecidability reading uses the rational Hilbert-tenth premise. The orbit-degeneration transfers belong to the unaudited part of the entry.

### 30. Stiefel and Kervaire extension table

The internally audited calculation treats extension of independent vector fields on the unit tangent bundle of $S^{m+1}$ at source dimension $2m$. The listed polynomial-time values through $m=63$ are $1,3,7,15,31,63$. The statement at $m=127$ uses a choice of a Kervaire invariant one element $\theta_6$ of order two. The existence of a dimension-126 Kervaire invariant one class is a separate assertion from this order-two condition. [Lin–Wang–Xu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10879)

## Shared premises

- **Rational Hilbert-tenth premise.** Several entries prove reductions first and infer undecidability using an existential definition of $\mathbb Z$ in $\mathbb Q$. The handoff cites the [Bhargava–Burungale preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16130). The conditional consequences in this register use that premise explicitly.
- **Integral number-field decisions.** The [Koymans–Pagano theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01768) concerns rings finitely generated over $\mathbb Z$. The arguments using this theorem identify it separately from the rational Hilbert-tenth premise.
- **Recent circuit and tensor inputs.** The [OpenAI research index](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/history.md#november-24-2026) and the [Anthropic separation manuscript](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00954v1) are external dependencies where named. Their use in a deduction does not independently audit an upstream proof.
