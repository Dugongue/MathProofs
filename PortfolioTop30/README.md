# The 30 most significant MathProof discoveries

This ranking covers the full public [MathProofs snapshot](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/tree/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27). The portfolio review screened 4,226 text files across a repository with 4,665 tracked files and 430 top-level directories. The ranking weighs mathematical reach, named questions resolved, precise novelty, and strength of verification. It groups related results.

This edition includes four new written proofs extending the first-ranked Manin theorem. Their ranks are editorial judgments with provisional priority. The original ten entries covered by the [earlier audit](Top10-Audit.md) retain their original audit history. The new statements and their mathematical dependencies are in [Four extensions](ManinExtensions.md).

## Ranked discoveries

1. **Manin’s bilinear undecidability conjecture.** The written proof holds up. For every fixed nonzero integral bilinear map, unimodular frame equations make it possible to simulate ring arithmetic in Manin’s coefficient-only language. The paper of Garreta–Miasnikov–Ovchinnikov supplies the precursor in a richer language. Koymans–Pagano supply the unconditional undecidability theorem for the resulting ring. Manin’s Theorem 8.1 transfers the result to the fixed-target extension dichotomy. A historical Lean replay covers the reduction under a number-ring premise. A separate provisional corollary contains one `sorry`. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/BilinearDiophantineUndecidability/PROOF.md) · [Lean](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/ManinBilinearReduction/ManinSingleFile.lean) · [Manin §§7–8](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.10632#S7) · [Prior ring interpretation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.02573#page=18) · [Koymans–Pagano](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.01768v3#S1.SS2)

2. **The missing complex Hanner inequality.** The written proof holds up for all finite complex-valued $L^p$ families in the Steinhaus case $1<p<2$. Two independent derivations checked both parts of the angular integration by parts, removal of the regularizer, and the $p=1$ endpoint. The new step is the positive-semidefinite Hessian identity. Jenkins–Tkocz already gave the convexity-to-inequality reduction. The search found no earlier proof of the missing range. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/CrossBranchResolutions/HANNER.md) · [Repository audit](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/CrossBranchResolutions/AUDIT.md) · [Jenkins–Tkocz, published §3.2](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10414688)

3. **A finite-image dichotomy for bilinear equations.** For any fixed pairing of finite modules (in the sense of finite generation) over a commutative finite-type integer algebra, coefficient-only solvability is decidable exactly when the module generated by the values is a finite set. The new step isolates a nonzero torsion-free image over an infinite prime quotient, then normalizes frames over the domain. Written proof. [Theorem 1](ManinExtensions.md#1-the-finite-image-dichotomy)

4. **Exact partition rank of the determinant over every field.** The determinant in its columns has partition rank $n-1$ for even $n\ge4$, and $n$ for odd $n$ or $n=2$. The Pfaffian gives the even-size upper bound, and a column-wise Chern class obstruction with a roots-of-unity test gives the matching bounds. This sharpens the earlier largest-prime lower bound and extends the four-column calculation. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/GrochowOpenProblemSolutions2/DETERMINANT-PARTITION-RANK.md) · [Chen–Zhao](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21015) · [Lampert–Moshkovitz](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2026.90)

5. **Universal boundary extension degrees for fixed source and target.** For each simply connected finite target outside rational H-space type, two degree coordinates on one fixed source pair realize the universal enumeration relation. Equality of the resulting degree sets is complete at the second universal level of the arithmetic hierarchy. Written proof. [Theorem 2](ManinExtensions.md#2-every-computably-enumerable-set-as-boundary-extension-degrees)

6. **Exact matrix and diagram proof degrees, with a factorial sparse-size separation.** The written suite answers the degree part of Question 7.3 and extends it to arbitrary directed diagrams and specified mixed diagrams. Over explicitly represented finite fields the exact degree is computable in deterministic polynomial bit time. On rank-gap-one diagonal projections, deleting a redundant inverse direction raises degree from two or three to $r+2$ and polynomial sparse size to $n^{\Theta(n)}$, over every field. Earlier Boolean PCR rank bounds were exponential over $\mathbb F_2$. The Lean evidence covers supporting identities. The unrestricted two-sided sparse-size question remains open. [Bounds](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/GeneralCongruenceSparseConjugacy/RESULTS.md) · [Inverse axioms and diagrams](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/MatrixInverseAxioms/RESULTS.md) · [Prior rank bounds](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.08760#page=26) · [Question 7.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S7.SS3)

7. **Degree collapse for Polynomial Calculus with the published Inversion Rule.** The block identity checks. With arbitrary substitution polynomials and degree measured on proof lines, every geometrically inconsistent system has a refutation of degree at most the maximum axiom degree. This gives degree at most three for geometrically negative cubic tensor instances and answers Conjecture 1.7 negatively under the printed convention. The substituted matrices contain the full Nullstellensatz certificate, whose encoded size can be large. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/IsomorphismProofCrossovers/circuits.md) · [Published rule](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S2.SS3)

8. **Rational-point decision on smooth geometrically integral hypersurfaces.** The Faltings and square-class reduction checks. For each fixed number field, existence of a rational point on a smooth geometrically integral affine hypersurface is computably many-one equivalent to Hilbert’s tenth problem over the field. There is also a uniform Turing reduction, and a degree-eight restriction over $\mathbb Q$. The new restriction is to smooth affine hypersurfaces, exactly the case Poonen distinguished from his earlier smooth-projective result. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/RationalPointDecision/PROOF.md) · [Poonen’s question](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/zagier/files/doi/10.4171/OWR/2009/33/fulltext.pdf#page=20) · [Earlier result](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.1782)

9. **Halting encoded by entanglement in a fixed commuting matrix-product family.** The written reduction checks. Halting is equivalent to first-qubit entanglement at some length, varying only the left boundary. Every state is positive definite and every proper marginal is maximally mixed. The new combination is exact entanglement with these promises and a fixed commuting bulk. Earlier work already gave undecidability of unbounded-length MPO positivity. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/TensorHaltingThresholds/Network.md) · [Prior positivity theorem](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.113.160503)

10. **Minimum generator sets of finite groups in deterministic logspace.** The written argument for the minimum number and witnessed optimal search survived focused checks of the chief-factor steps, crown coordinates, and suspended workspace. The new result is the $O(\log N)$ space bound for arbitrary groups given by Cayley tables, answering Question 8.1. Prior work gave polynomial time, and the available Lean evidence covers greedy replay. [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/LogspaceMinimumGeneration/Proof.md) · [Greedy Lean components](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/LogspaceMinimumGenerationRecurrentCayley/README.md) · [Published question](https://doi.org/10.46298/theoretics.25.19) · [Prior polynomial-time result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07633)

11. **Equiangular bipartite tight frames with every marginal purity.** For $d\ge2$, constructs $d^2+1$ equiangular tight-frame vectors in $\mathbb C^d\otimes\mathbb C^d$, with both reductions of every vector having prescribed purity in $[1/d,1]$. The $1/d$ endpoint answers the maximally entangled existence question, and the full interval extends numerical qubit observations. The construction checks and the public Lean file matches an existing two-root audit with the standard axioms. [Lean](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/ETFAllDimensionsAllPurities.lean) · [Wei–Cobucci–Tavakoli, §§IV and IX](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.110.042206)

12. **Geometric universality of binary rank-two matrix completion.** The coordinate-ring proof checks. Every finite-type affine scheme over $\mathbb Q$, including its nilpotent structure, is the base of a smooth surjection from a completion scheme isomorphic to an open in an affine-space product. The matrices prescribe only 0 and 1, and every rational fiber has a rational point over its characteristic-zero field. The new scope is the combination of ordinary rank two, binary entries and scheme-level universality. [Written proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/RankTwoGeometricUniversality/PROOF.md) · [Rank-three precedent](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01559) · [Nonnegative-factorization precedent](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09068)

13. **Average-case orthogonal and unitary tensor isomorphism over finite fields.** The formal result has a deterministic invariant good-source recognizer and a zero-error expected-polynomial-time decision machine. Its distinct scope includes Hermitian unitary actions, the characteristic-two standard bilinear dot product, and rectangular formats. The checked-in replay reports 35,192 constants accepted. Version 2 of the original paper covers odd-characteristic cubic orthogonal instances with stronger success-rate and output guarantees and renumbers the original Problem 1.8. Public priority for the overlap is unresolved. [Formal statement](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/Problem18/Problem18-statement.lean) · [Lean](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/Problem18/Problem18.lean) · [Replay](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/Problem18/kernel-replay.log) · [Version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.00591v1) · [Version 2, Theorem 5.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.00591v2#S5.SS2)

14. **The leading asymptotic for Panova’s central rectangular Kronecker coefficients.** For two $m\times m$ partitions and the most balanced two-row partition of $m^2$, the coefficient is asymptotic to $(3\sqrt3/\pi)(1+\mathbf1_{m\ {\rm odd}})4^m m^{-5}$. The proof resolves the first finite difference of neighboring Gaussian-binomial coefficients, including its parity factor. *Written proof with repository checks and attribution.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/CentralKroneckerAsymptotics/PROOF.md) · [Attribution](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/CentralKroneckerAsymptotics/ATTRIBUTION.md) · [Panova’s question](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.02553#S6.SS3)

15. **Halting with two pairing terms per equation on a path.** Every nonzero integral pairing simulates a binary program index using two fixed digit templates, bounded occurrence, bounded primal pathwidth, and coefficients of unit magnitude. Scalar multiplication has a linear-time one-term case. Written proof with a Lean-checked arithmetic identity. [Theorem 3](ManinExtensions.md#3-two-pairing-terms-on-a-path-suffice)

16. **A rational Nash equilibrium complexity transition from pairwise to ternary zero-sum games.** Pairwise zero-sum local tables always admit a rational equilibrium. Allowing three-player local tables makes rational equilibrium existence $\exists\mathbb Q$-complete and exact equilibrium search FIXP-complete, already with two actions per player, interaction degree at most seven, and a fixed payoff alphabet. *Written proof. The stronger halting claim has an additional arithmetic premise.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/TernaryRationalNash/PROOF.md)

17. **Finite-ring tensor isomorphism transfers across rings with the same characteristic primes.** For fixed nonzero finite commutative rings with the same characteristic prime support, the stated tensor-isomorphism problems are polynomial-time equivalent. This includes $\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z$ and $\mathbb F_2$, and covers the free and presented versions in the proof. *Written result with an audit.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/PrimePowerTensorIsomorphism/PROOF.md) · [Audit](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/PrimePowerTensorIsomorphism/AUDIT.md) · [Same-ring precedent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00735)

18. **Unit-distance realization over the reals and the rationals.** Proves $\exists\mathbb R$-completeness for the graph realization versions in the paper at every fixed dimension $d\ge2$, including strict realizations. Over $\mathbb Q^{24}$, the ordinary and injective versions are polynomial-time equivalent to Hilbert’s tenth problem over $\mathbb Q$, even with simultaneous local realizability. *Written proof. Rational undecidability is conditional on an existential definition of the integers in $\mathbb Q$.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/RationalUnitDistance/PROOF.md)

19. **Amann’s family of quasi-isometrically rigid nilpotent groups.** Proves rigidity for the stated step-three family for every parameter $k\ge2$, extending the low-parameter cases by a uniform cohomology-ring calculation. *Written proof.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/AmannNilpotentRigidity/Proof.md) · [Amann’s paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.04542)

20. **Ding–Munemasa–Tonchev Conjecture 17 and asymmetry of the associated bent codes.** Establishes the stated eventual abundance of inequivalent codes simultaneously over the parameter range, and shows that the proportion with coordinate-transitive automorphism group tends to zero. *Lean source.* [Lean](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/DMT17.lean) · [Original conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.08487)

21. **Integral halting with two bounded-denominator rational witnesses.** For every fixed nonzero integral pairing there is one fixed family whose coefficients are constant and whose targets are affine in the machine index. Rational solutions with common denominators 2 and 3 can always be supplied, while integral existence records halting. Written proof. [Theorem 4](ManinExtensions.md#4-integral-halting-completeness-with-two-supplied-rational-solutions)

22. **Exact unimodality classification for thick-grid general-position polynomials.** Classifies the full family, including exceptional ranges $m=2,n=8$, $m=3,11\le n\le17$, and $m\ge4,\ 2m+4\le n\le m+(m+1)(1+(m-1)!)$, in the theorem’s parameter convention. *Lean source.* [Lean](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/TStarUnimodality.lean)

23. **Exponential sparse Polynomial Calculus lower bounds for ordinary tensor isomorphism.** Constructs standard TI equation systems whose sparse refutations need exponentially many monomials and linear degree in the tensor side in each fixed characteristic. This supplies the lower-bound direction of Question 7.2. *Written proofs, with separate characteristic-zero treatment.* [Positive characteristic](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/TensorSparsePCBounds/Proof.md) · [Characteristic zero](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/TensorSparsePCBounds/CharacteristicZero.md) · [Question 7.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19320#S7.SS3)

24. **The Easdown–Praeger conjecture for class-two groups with abelian quotients.** Proves that the minimum faithful permutation degree cannot increase on passing from a finite group of class at most two to an abelian quotient, with arbitrary prime and exponent. *Written proof for this class of quotients.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/GrochowOrbitAndGroupAdvances/groups.md) · [Prior work](https://doi.org/10.2140/ant.2026.20.219)

25. **Nonprime-field code equivalence reduces to lattice isomorphism.** Extends the code-to-lattice construction to monomial equivalence of arbitrary linear codes over extension fields and recovers a monomial equivalence witness. The construction is polynomial in the numerical field size as well as the code size. *Written proof, audit, and example checks.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/NonprimeCodeToLattice/PROOF.md) · [Audit](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/NonprimeCodeToLattice/AUDIT.md)

26. **Sublinear distance number for sparse graphs.** Gives an $O_h(n/\sqrt{\log n})$ bound for $h$-degenerate graphs and treats bounded treewidth together with bounded degree. The result concerns straight-line realizations whose edges use few distinct lengths. *Written proof.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/SparseGraphDistanceNumber/PROOF.md) · [Source questions](https://arxiv.org/html/1907.13104v2#S6)

27. **Enumeration degrees of rational subrings and smooth varieties with Cantor-like point closures.** Given an existential definition of $\mathbb Z$ in $\mathbb Q$, the proof identifies the computable many-one degree of Hilbert’s tenth problem for each rational subring in terms of the enumeration of its inverted primes. The same premise gives a fixed low-degree smooth family and simultaneous real and $p$-adic closure consequences. *Conditional written proof. The arithmetic premise is treated as open for this ranking.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/RationalSubringsAndMazur/PROOF.md) · [Claim concerning the premise](https://github.com/elliotglazer/diophantine-z-q)

28. **Quantum compatibility bound for arbitrary-rank pairwise unbiased triples.** Establishes the generalized visibility lower bound $(1+x_n)/3$, where $x_n$ is the specified positive root of $n^2x_n^3-3nx_n-2=0$, for the stated class of triples of $n$-outcome projective measurements. This extends the scope beyond rank-one examples and proves the corresponding restricted case of Conjecture 10(3,4). The same file gives a uniform explicit improvement for rank-one MUB triples in each dimension $n\ge3$. *Lean source.* [Lean](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/PairwiseUnbiasedPVMTripleCompatibility.lean)

29. **Three separations among bivariate polynomial ranks.** Establishes all three separations of Conjecture 46 using evaluation matrices and squared Lagrange interpolation to transfer exact rank obstructions to nonnegative and sum-of-squares polynomial representations. *Written proof.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/BivariatePolynomialRankSeparations/Proof.md) · [Original conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.06680)

30. **Corrected design-isomorphism classification for Kaspers–Pott constructions.** Proves nonisomorphism for all odd prime powers $q\ge7$, identifies the isomorphic $q=5$ cases, and supplies an exact check for $q=9$. *Written proof with finite calculation.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/RecoveredResearchProofs/KaspersPottDesigns.md) · [Original problem](https://arxiv.org/html/1907.05848v2#S5)

## Previously ranked

- **Unbounded failure of Loewy-length monotonicity in the two-sided order.** The characteristic-two principal blocks associated with $A_5$ and $V_4$ give Loewy lengths five and three in the direction violating Jonsson’s Question 43. Tensor powers make the gap unbounded. *Written proof plus an exact finite-field check.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/CrossBranchResolutions/LOEWY.md) · [Audit](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/CrossBranchResolutions/AUDIT.md)

- **A counterexample to the Boolean exit-influence conjecture.** A Tribes construction has constant-order density, point-to-convex-hull $\ell_\infty$ distance $1/w$ in both directions, and maximum exit influence $O(2^{-w})$. This falsifies Lovett–Zhang Conjecture 37 under its stated normalization. *Written proof.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/LeanLibraryCrossovers/PROOF.md) · [Original conjecture](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol251-itcs2023/LIPIcs.ITCS.2023.84/LIPIcs.ITCS.2023.84.pdf)

- **Galois descent for the asymptotic spectrum of tensors.** Over a normal algebraic field extension, restriction identifies the base spectrum with the quotient by the Galois action. The result characterizes the fibers and gives finite-degree power bounds that quantify descent. *Written proof.* [Proof](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/TensorSpectraDeterminantsGroupLifts/GaloisSpectrumDescent.md)

- **A deterministic rational-formula factorization reduction to identity testing.** Formalizes the implication from deterministic white-box polynomial identity testing for rational arithmetic formulas to deterministic factorization in the stated binary rational model. The statement fixes both the computational model and the PIT premise. *Conditional Lean theorem.* [Lean](https://github.com/Dugongue/MathProofs/blob/97cab48ab2ca92c0ba830a5573041cde985fef27/FormulaFactorization.lean)
