# Exact separation of tensor subrank and coordinated restrictions on odd cycles

Let $A=I+\operatorname{Adj}(C_N)$, for $N\ge3$, and set $T=\sum_j A_{\cdot j}\otimes e_j\otimes e_j$ over a field $F$. Then the ordinary subrank of $T^{\otimes k}$ is $\operatorname{rank}_F(A)^k$. If instead the only permitted maps select the same coordinate subset in all three modes, the largest diagonal restriction has size $\alpha(C_N^{\boxtimes k})$. The coordinated restriction model is thus a precise setting in which the release’s strong-product bounds constrain a tensor invariant. [Seven-cycle release](https://charlesponeill.com/maths-releases/seven-cycle-five/) and [torus-packing release](https://charlesponeill.com/maths-releases/cube-packing/).

For ordinary subrank, the first flattening gives the upper bound $\operatorname{rank}_F(A^{\otimes k})$. Select independent columns of $A^{\otimes k}$ in modes two and three and send those columns to distinct standard vectors in mode one. This attains the bound. For coordinated coordinate restriction, the tensor entries surviving on a common subset $S$ include the diagonal entries and an off-diagonal entry of type $(i,j,j)$ exactly when two distinct indices $i,j\in S$ are adjacent in the strong product. This is exactly the independent-set condition.

The kernel of $A$ consists of three-periodic sequences satisfying $x_{i+2}=-x_i-x_{i+1}$. If $3\mid N$, the first two entries can be chosen freely and the rank is $N-2$. Otherwise periodicity forces constant coordinates and the remaining equation is $3x_0=0$. Thus the rank is $N-1$ in characteristic three, and $N$ in other characteristics. For $N=7$ the fourth ordinary subrank is $2401$ away from characteristic three and $1296$ in characteristic three, whereas the coordinated subrank is at most $112$. The fifth ordinary values are $16807$ and $7776$, against a coordinated upper bound of $392$. For $N=4m+1\equiv5\bmod8$ the coordinated third subrank is exactly $N(mN-1)/2$ by the cube-packing theorem.
