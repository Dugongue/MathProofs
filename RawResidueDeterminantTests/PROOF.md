# Determinant constraints on raw residue tensors

These are informal results for the fixed-finite-field direction of [Chen, Grochow, Qiao, Tang and Zhang, Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5). The target is a polynomial-time construction of one ordinary 3-tensor isomorphism pair for arbitrary raw special-linear tensor isomorphism. At a fixed field, one can equivalently aim to read any single prescribed weighted determinant character, then conjoin the constantly many character tests. The determinant triples of a nonempty transporter form a coset \(dH\) in the finite abelian group \((F^\times)^3\). That coset contains \(\eta\) exactly when every character test has a transporter with character value \(\chi(\eta)\), since the characters of the quotient by \(H\) separate its points. Different character tests may use different witnesses. Each test must still attach its character constraint to the same witness that matches the tensor.

## Four results

- [Intrinsic graph flags](IntrinsicGraphDeterminantTests.md) read the sign of a permutation of tensor blocks using two orders of the flags of a graph. This removes separate matching of same-tag factor orbits when a parity-compatible intrinsic graph connects the blocks. The proof handles repeated rational factors and repeated nonsplit orbits of growing degree. It gives explicit raw families with exponentially many valid matches, and combines partial character readings with verified corrections from other methods.
- [Semilinear ambient maps](SemilinearAmbientDeterminantTests.md) recover the support of raw rectangular pencils inside the Hom space of their intrinsic adjoint field. The first proof reads both matrix-mode determinant constraints in one TI instance under an active-width condition and a coprimality condition. The second computes the full triple in polynomial time when the ambient multiplier algebra has few projective classes. Its growing example has no square determinant and no bound on the relative matrix-mode dimensions.
- [Radical filtrations](RadicalFiltrationDeterminantTests.md) retain the same transporter on every grade, use actual determinant-one transvections to remove large natural simple factors from enumeration, and combine explicit narrow multiplicity readouts with certified corrections on wider factors. The proof allows arbitrary nilpotence length. A saturation test can dispense with all quotient branches when the computed determinant bounds and corrections agree.
- [Unordered low-rank moments](LowRankMomentDeterminantProfiles.md) recover raw label maps through constant-degree rank-locus certification and moment intertwining. This bypasses factorization of a determinant polynomial. The proof includes an explicit family over the three-element field with absolutely irreducible determinant, scalar stabilizer and scalar pair adjoints. The family has no rank-one slices. On the stated small-commutant class the algorithm computes the exact profile without ordinary TI calls.

[Geometric factor recovery](GeometricPencilFactorRecovery.md) supplies the bounded-degree preprocessing used in the graph proof. The rank-locus proof works from minors and field equations, with the source identities credited to the relevant Hex development. The radical and semilinear arguments use constructive finite-field algebra decomposition, ordered-module isomorphism and the typed tensor-system compiler. In every case the accepted output maps refer to the actual input tensor and carry all attached equations jointly.

## Consequence for the exact triangular tables

In the truncated-corner normalization the raw residue bimap is arbitrary. For a table in its construction bases the determinant of an algebra transporter is the product of the three residue determinants to the corner-length powers. Idempotent alignment by a radical unit has determinant one. The common diagonal parameter contribution is canceled by the chosen diagonal lengths. Thus every result above that reads the appropriate residue character applies to the corresponding exact table pair. Results computing the full triple handle all the fixed-length weights. On pencils, the proofs that read the label determinant and the matrix determinant ratio handle those two characters directly and give the stated SL test when their scalar-correction hypothesis holds.

The unrestricted case still requires a polynomial construction for residue tensors for which none of the proved extraction and correction criteria is available. In particular, the rank-locus method needs a certified and sufficiently informative low-rank point set, the graph method needs covariant blocks, and the algebra methods need a usable action on radical grades or a small active multiplier algebra. One ordinary TI instance for every arbitrary residue pair remains unproved. The four notes make no claim of external validation, publication priority or Lean verification.
