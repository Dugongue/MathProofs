# Ten results on finite group actions, elementary-symmetric circuits, and random Ritz values

1. **Minimum faithful actions for all finite nilpotent groups are in uniform NC.** From a Cayley table, deterministic logspace-uniform NC finds the minimum degree and constructs the action. This includes all finite \(2\)-groups with arbitrary class and exponent. [Proof](groups.md#1-minimum-faithful-actions-for-every-nilpotent-cayley-table-group)
2. **Cubic elementary-symmetric circuit recognition is NP-complete.** Given a cubic and a variable bound on the number of elementary-symmetric projections, recognition is NP-complete over both \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_4\). Over \(\mathbb F_4\), the reduction identifies the exact minimum with the graph vertex-cover number. [Proof](complexity.md#4-recognizing-sums-of-cubic-symmetric-projections-is-np-complete)
3. **Sharp odd-characteristic modular dihedral cutoffs.** When an odd characteristic \(p\) divides \(n\), unnormalized group-sum tensors generically recover the \(n\)-gon signal through order three, including invariant-field generation. The sole exception is the triangle in characteristic three, where the exact cutoff is four. [Proof](invariants.md#sharp-odd-modular-dihedral-moment-cutoffs)
4. **The order-two zero locus of the elementary symmetric polynomial has an exact dimension formula.** In characteristic \(p\), its dimension is \(d-1\) exactly when the first power of \(p\) strictly greater than \(d\) divides \(n-d+1\). Otherwise it is \(d-2\). This corrects Conjecture 2.11 at powers of \(p\), and the proof classifies its top-dimensional components. [Proof](complexity.md#1-exact-dimension-of-the-order-two-zero-locus)
5. **The first unbounded border affine Chow rank is at degree \(p+1\).** Every degree at most \(p\) has rank at most two. Rank two is the worst case in degrees two through \(p\) on at least three variables. The worst case at degree \(p+1\) is \(\Theta_p(n)\). [Proof](complexity.md#2-the-first-degree-with-unbounded-border-affine-chow-rank)
6. **Exact characterization of quartic elementary-symmetric projections in characteristic three.** Over an algebraically closed field, the quartics are exactly the forms \(\ell H+Q^2\) modulo the span of the \(x_i^3x_j\). A product of two nondegenerate quadratics on separate four-dimensional blocks disproves sufficiency of the earlier reducible-plus-powers criterion. [Proof](complexity.md#3-exact-quartic-projections-in-characteristic-three-and-failure-of-the-proposed-sufficient-condition)
7. **One generic real Gram graph determines the core operation.** It also recovers the regular action of \(G'Z(G)^2\) when the representation has no four-dimensional quaternionic constituent. For odd-order groups with \(C_G(G')=1\) it gives the whole multiplication table and actual regular action in polynomial time on the exact Gram matrix. [Proof](invariants.md#one-generic-real-gram-graph-recovers-core-operations-and-a-canonical-subgroup-action)
8. **Affine constituent conjugacy has deterministic search-to-decision.** This covers all permutation groups whose point orbits have size at most three and, more generally, groups with two-point or affine degree-\(p\) constituents at a common odd prime. The reduction returns a permutation on the original points. [Proof](groups.md#2-search-to-decision-for-permutation-groups-with-affine-prime-degree-constituents)
9. **Two-step Gaussian Ritz compressions of normal matrices have polynomial eigenvector conditioning.** No lower bound on the separation of the input eigenvalues is needed. Expected pseudospectral area has exponent \(5/4\) for complex Gaussian starts and \(9/8\) for real normal matrices with real Gaussian starts. [Proof](normal-ritz.md#polynomial-conditioning-for-two-step-random-ritz-compressions)
10. **One missing spectral dimension gives an exact random Ritz law.** For a complex normal matrix with \(m\) distinct eigenvalues at Krylov dimension \(m-1\), normalized independent inverse-gamma variables give the exact law of the coefficients of its Ritz polynomial. The result allows arbitrary spectral geometry and multiplicities and has a separate Haar-hyperplane formula. [Proof](normal-ritz.md#exact-gaussian-ritz-law-with-one-missing-spectral-dimension)
