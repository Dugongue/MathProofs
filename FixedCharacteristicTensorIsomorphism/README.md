# Fixed Characteristic Tensor Isomorphism

[Full informal proof](Proof.md) · [Review and prior-art scope](REVIEW.md)

For every fixed prime $p$, this note gives deterministic polynomial-time many-one reductions between ordinary tensor isomorphism over any two finite fields of characteristic $p$, on all inputs. It answers the **same-characteristic finite-field clause** of Grochow and Qiao's Open Question 7.6; it does not settle the other field comparisons in that question.

The proof uses classical Frobenius representations and the published Futorny–Grochow–Sergeichuk tensor-system compiler. It has passed internal AI-assisted review, is not Lean verified or externally peer reviewed, and makes no claim of first publication priority.
