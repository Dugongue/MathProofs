# Four extensions of the cubic jet-algebra reduction

Each result starts from an ingredient of the root-independent reduction from form equivalence to unmarked algebra isomorphism. The proposed placements are editorial estimates against the thirty-result briefing.

| Suggested placement | Result | What the proof establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3–7 | [Determinant profiles over arbitrary base rings](Tensor.md) | The jet-algebra construction recovers exact form witnesses even over nonreduced rings. It computes every transporter determinant triple. Over the integers it preserves both orbit relations for cubics on \(3\bmod 4\) variables and gives a strict ordinary versus determinant-one split in side \(22\). |
| 4–8 | [Linear-size local algebras and linear refutation degree](CompressedAlgebras.md) | An exact symmetric trilinear isometry problem on \(n\) variables fits into a commutative local algebra of dimension at most \(2n+6\). A linear-length reduction places TI inside the unmarked Gorenstein class with Hilbert vector \((1,c,c,1)\). On that class some nonisomorphism proofs require degree linear in the algebra dimension. |
| 5–9 | [Exact directional degeneration](Geometry.md) | For the jet-algebra family, ordinary three-tensor degeneration is equivalent in both directions to linear form degeneration over any algebraically closed field. Invertible slices recover the algebra specialization. A closed inverse-frame representation handles affine special-linear orbit-closure intersection. |
| 6–10 | [Proof-degree simulations in both directions](Optimization.md) | Explicit substitutions and constant-degree ideal certificates transfer Polynomial Calculus, Nullstellensatz and real Sum-of-Squares degree between forms and ungraded jet algebras. They also give polynomial-size circuit translations for the Ideal Proof System and linear degree lower bounds for square-free cubic equivalence. |

All four main results have written proofs. The finite controls check selected cases. Lean independently checked the [finite inverse identity](TruncatedInverse.lean) used in the degree simulation. The literature comparisons do not adjudicate publication priority.
