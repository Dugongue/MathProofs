# Trace Gram methods for TI and special-linear TI

The four-person research run targeted a deterministic polynomial-time reduction from SL-TI to one ordinary three-tensor isomorphism instance over the same fixed finite field. The arguments below prove such comparisons on specific families. The unrestricted direction remains open. [Chen–Grochow–Qiao–Tang–Zhang, Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5)

- **Split Pfaffian pencils.** A same-field ordinary TI instance tests all three prescribed determinants on raw alternating isotopes with independent absolute Pfaffian factors and the stated Frobenius-orbit multiplicity condition. The logarithmic Pfaffian derivatives recover the trace pairing and finite étale multiplication. A second algebra links the left-right transporter and recovers both matrix determinants even when its row and column maps differ. [Proof](SplitPencilAnchor.md)
- **Semisimple multiplication isotopes.** A deterministic polynomial-time algorithm recognizes the raw family, finds invertible multiplication anchors, and computes the entire transporter determinant coset. It handles repeated simple factors, nonsplit centers and characteristics dividing a matrix degree. [Proof](SemisimpleMultiplicationIsotopes.md)
- **Centroid field blocks.** One ordinary TI comparison enforces all three determinants when the field-module ranks are logarithmic and equal-descriptor groups meet the stated multiplicity bound. Linked Frobenius products read the field norms and a quadratic trace-zero line reads Frobenius orientation. The proof handles nonreduced centroids and quotienting out mode radicals. [Proof](CentroidNormLines.md)
- **Orthogonal residuals.** Trace Gram matrices of one or two extension-field blocks give a polynomial-size determinant test for an arbitrarily wide ordered residual under the stated neutral-lift promise. The spinor-kernel extension allows a smaller neutral subgroup. [Proof](TraceGramOrthogonalMarkers.md)

The common source is the separable trace Gram construction from Alpöge's release. We replayed the released nondegeneracy theorem in isolation and audited its transitive axioms with Lean 4.33.0. The source release's RH and Dirichlet GRH endpoints were outside that audit. The finite-field reductions use the algebraic trace construction, and their correctness is independent of the analytic RH and GRH claims. [Alpöge announcement](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf)

The reduction proofs are written mathematics. Lean also checked two scalar identities for the norm and quadratic trace-zero lines. The exact source digest and transitive axiom sets are in [Verification.json](Verification.json). [Lean companion](SemilinearDeterminantLines.lean)
