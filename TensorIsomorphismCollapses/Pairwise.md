# All directed pairwise comparisons

Rows reduce to columns. A check mark records a proved reduction. An equals sign records proved reductions in both directions. A question mark records a direction left open by this packet. TI and SL denote the endpoints, and the twenty numbered definitions are those in the preceding interval table. See [the proof index](RESULTS.md).
