# Intermediate determinant problems for tensor isomorphism

All four endpoints concern explicit three-tensors over F₃. O is ordinary TI, A is one-sign TI, B is two-sign TI, and S is SL-TI. Each displayed arrow is a deterministic polynomial-time many-one reduction. Inclusions refer to the downward closures of the decision problems.

## Ordered refinements

* [Lower interval](Lower.md). Six candidate formulations use determinant or parity on a marked permutation, code, subspace arrangement, or metric role. The proofs put them in order below A, after adjoining ordinary TI where needed to give the lower bound.
* [Middle interval](Middle.md). There is a chain (Ale Mle Tle Nle Cle A^{wedge2}le B) with four additional terms. They change which source automorphisms may waive each of two relative sign tests. All five intermediate formulations along this route are genuinely separated from their neighbors in the specified single affine transformation model, including the preceding conjunction benchmark and its comparison with B. The same mechanism gives 25 explicit predicates, of which nine are equivalent to the existing two-query conjunction.
* [Upper interval](Upper.md). There is a chain (Ble C_{43}le C_{30}le C_{14}le A^{wedge3}le(Bland A)le S). Each new comparison asks whether a relative witness can be repaired by specified linear images of signs of source automorphisms. The finite classification gives 44 coordinate types including S and the earlier cross-mode correction. Three more descriptions on the displayed chain are new candidates. Three fixed queries to any one of those three suffice to decide SL-TI, and two fixed affine-copy probes do not. The six reverse steps fail even for a disjunction of affine transformations. The [enumeration](verify_upper.py) reproduces 44 types, 193 strict ordered comparisons, and maximal chain length six among the 44 types in the single affine transformation model.
* [Relative and stabilizer conditions](StabilizerTests.md). Two prescribed projected signs on one source stabilizer are exactly two independent odd-automorphism tests. There are raw single-pair representatives for (Aland F^2) and (Bland F), where F asks for a negative determinant on a source automorphism. If (Bland F) reduces to B, it also absorbs any fixed list of such source conditions and the full three-bit stabilizer-membership test.

## What the intermediate formulations establish

The lower, middle and upper paths concatenate into 20 ordered problem formulations. Several paths use older conjunction degrees as benchmarks. The 44 upper cases are inequivalent under the stated affine compiler, even allowing a finite disjunction of its outputs. The middle proofs establish a strict subchain under single affine preprocessing. All forward inclusions are on the raw decision inputs. The reverse exclusions concern a compiler allowed to manipulate independent affine sign profiles by fixed linear maps. They give concrete targets for a reduction that needs additional information from the tensors.

The number of distinct unrestricted Karp degrees remains open. Each language here belongs to NP and has both yes and no instances, so proving any strict unrestricted pair would already imply P ≠ NP. Ladner's density theorem gives artificial intermediate languages if an endpoint gap is strict. The candidates here instead have explicit transporter or automorphism interpretations. All proofs are informal and rely where indicated on the earlier sign projection and tagged block construction.
