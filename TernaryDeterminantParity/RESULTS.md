# Determinant-sign tests for ternary tensor transporters

These are informal proofs of restricted reductions related to Open Question 1.9 of Chen, Grochow, Qiao, Tang and Zhang. The unrestricted same-field reduction remains open. The constructions here are unconditional and do not depend on the Qiao–Zhang runtime claims. [Open Question 1.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.03135v2#S1.SS5).

- [Nonsplit determinant frames](FactorParity.md). One ordinary ternary TI instance tests the special-linear condition on balanced odd-dimensional tensors whose specified slice determinant has independent geometric linear factors and logarithmically many Frobenius orbits of each degree. The individual degrees are unrestricted. The same construction composes with the logarithmic derivative to enforce all three exact determinants over split local rings with a derivation separating principal units. Arbitrary orbit multiplicities admit an adaptive same-field reduction.
- [Small permutable blocks](BlockResidue.md). One ordinary binary TI instance tests all three signs on ternary tensors with logarithmic-dimensional blocks in the three modes, including unrestricted permutations of the blocks. An intrinsic centroid decomposition gives a polynomially recognizable family to which this applies directly.
- [Binary parity marker and primary-unit extension](CompilerRoute.md). The ordered-pair quotient compiles arbitrary permutations of an explicitly represented set with a sign constraint. For monomial frames over odd finite fields, truncated augmentation marginals also track the primary components of the local scalar products.
- [Limits of functorial lifting](LiftRoute.md). Fixed-arity full-linear-group constructions from natural and dual modules require an exponentially large intermediate for sign-sensitive marks in the stated model. A separate cross-characteristic bound concerns representations and lifts with a normal $2$-group kernel.

The sign of the number of reversed ordered pairs is the classical inversion identity. The contribution of the binary construction is its polynomial-size quotient marker and composition with the tensor-system reduction. Publication priority for the new combinations has not been established. [Orientation reversal and the sign of a permutation](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10801-022-01197-0), [tensor-system compilation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.09219).
