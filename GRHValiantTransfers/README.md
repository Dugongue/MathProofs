# Four transfers from the Riemann release to VP versus VNP

| Written result | What it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| [Finite-prime circuit transfer](FinitePrimeCircuitTransfer.md) | A weighted modular certificate for bounded-degree circuit realizability over the complex numbers. Gate offsets give a prime bit bound of $O(s\log(2(m+s+d))+\log(2+\tau))$ under Dedekind GRH. The same normalization gives binary-coefficient univariate polynomials with PH-decidable coefficients, degree $O(s\log s)$, and circuit size greater than $s=n^k$ for every fixed $k$. The earlier proof of the latter bound used degree $s^4$. |
| [Quadratic reciprocal](QuadraticReciprocal.md) | The multilinear reciprocal of a single explicit quadratic matching score is VNP-complete under c-reductions. Its small-circuit problem is equivalent to small Hilbert IPS refutations of the specified form. A separate cyclotomic construction gives same-size recovery of the permanent and a prime-selection bound under Dirichlet GRH. |
| [Cohomology and border determinantal complexity](BorderCohomology.md) | A weighted cohomology-ring subquotient theorem gives sufficient tests for orbit-closure exclusion even when Betti numbers jump. For Hessian Lie algebras the tests include a size-three permanent–determinant benchmark. |
| [Exact denominators in symmetric IPS](SymmetricIPSDenominators.md) | For Boolean pigeonhole with $N$ pigeons, the least common denominator at main-placeholder degree at most $d$ is $\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,N)/\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,\min(d,N))$. The formula gives the entire integral multiplier ideal and its reductions modulo any positive integer. |

The proofs use ideas from Levent Alpöge and Claude's released work on RH and Dirichlet GRH, including character averaging and exact congruence calculations. Andrew Sutherland reports a full source replay in Lean and a full export replay in the independent nanoda kernel, with the three standard axioms for the five headline theorems. The proofs here state their analytic inputs explicitly. The Dirichlet statements cover abelian applications here. The arbitrary complex-circuit prime count and the univariate construction use Dedekind GRH. The main border and denominator results and the rational reciprocal reduction are unconditional. VP versus VNP remains open. [Alpöge's announcement](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf) · [Sutherland's verification](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/)

The four results have written proofs. The [Lean companion](CertificateIdentities.lean) and [axiom audit](CertificateIdentitiesAudit.json) cover the integral two-pigeon calculation, divisibility of orbit sums and an interpolation identity. The [Python check](check_denominators.py) enumerates exact multiplier ideals for two through four pigeons. The general results remain outside the Lean audit, and priority is unverified.
