# Pfaffian compression and the limits of universal orientation gadgets

The unrestricted one-sign reduction over the field of three elements remains open. These informal results give additional objectwise reductions to one ordinary tensor isomorphism instance, and an exponential lower bound for a class of universal gadgets. One-sign tests over the field of three elements still suffice for full special-linear tests by the previously proved seven-character assembly.

| Result | Scope |
|---|---|
| Pfaffian Taylor coefficients and restrictions | A logarithmic-dimensional intrinsic pencil can use all its low-order Taylor expansions over the base field, or full polynomial restrictions to constant-dimensional rational subspaces. These are deterministic objectwise reductions when some marked coefficient is nonzero. They can work when every base-field form is singular. [Proof](PfaffianJets.md) |
| Alternating matches from the raw tensor | Solve linear equations for identifications of the two input spaces making the tensor alternating. When the match space and a relevant exterior kernel have logarithmic dimension, a bivector of logarithmic corank can suffice. Mark its image volume and the induced volume of its quotient. Both flexible and rigid cases are handled in one ordinary comparison. [Proof](AlternatingMatches.md) |
| Remove even Pfaffian multiplicities | The canonical part left after dividing a Pfaffian polynomial by a square carries exactly the original determinant sign. A polynomial-size remaining part can be marked in one comparison even for a large pencil. The raw factorization route has bounded error, and the scalar-times-square promise has an exact Las Vegas algorithm. [Proof](SquareFactors.md) |
| Universal bounded-order markers require exponential dimension | A fixed universal gadget that lets every determinant-one change of a visible mode lift must also let a reflection lift if its total dimension is small. The theorem allows arbitrary kernels of the lifting action. [Proof](UniversalLifts.md) |

The lower bound covers systems of any number of fixed tensors of bounded order. It assumes the gadget exposes the natural tested mode, or a distinguished invariant subquotient, and that all determinant-one maps on it lift. A minimal lifting group has nilpotent Frattini kernel. A cross-characteristic dimension bound then removes the relevant two-part of the kernel, and diagonal sign weight orbits supply the reflection. This locates an obstruction in a model with flexible auxiliary representations and even nonsplit original lifts.

The constructive results use raw input structure to impose only the necessary determinant constraint. A uniform reduction would need to obtain adequate structure on every raw input or use an encoding outside the hypotheses of the lower bound. No claimed separation between ordinary and special-linear tensor isomorphism follows. Publication priority for these precise formulations remains unverified.
