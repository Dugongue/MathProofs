# Intrinsic structures in tensor and algebra isomorphism

The four directions below are AI-assisted informal mathematics. Publication priority is unverified.

| Direction | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Hochschild invariants and derived equivalence | A linear-dimension reduction from arbitrary tensor isomorphism to derived equivalence of three-simple algebras of global dimension exactly two. The proof uses a lower bound on dimensions of faithful representations of a current Lie algebra. A separate graph reduction gives the same hardness comparison for graph isomorphism even with identical Cartan matrices and full graded Hochschild cup algebras within each pair. [Linear reduction](linear-derived.md) · [Equal-cup construction](hochschild.md) |
| Recovering invariant structure from raw coordinates | A deterministic polynomial-time algorithm recovers the coefficient algebra from structure constants of the Hochschild Lie bracket in the earlier three-vertex construction. Tensor derivations also give an intrinsic coordinate frame for a recognizable GI-complete family of ordinary tensors over each fixed finite field, with determinant-prescribed search using a GI decision oracle. [Proofs](centroid.md) |
| Deformations and ring changes | Tensor isomorphism over the preceding prime-power quotient reduces with linear side lengths to comparing concise tensors with one common residue tensor and a smooth reductive residue stabilizer. All transporters are recovered blockwise. At a square-zero coefficient the three mode Gram matrices can be fixed and invertible. The entire unmarked flat deformation classification of a three-vertex square-zero algebra is also ordinary tensor isomorphism over the corresponding quotient. [Proof](deformation.md) |
| Preserving proof complexity through reductions | Linear-length encodings among the five tensor actions support constant-degree translations of ordinary Polynomial Calculus proofs in both directions. They also preserve degree up to constant factors for Nullstellensatz and real Sum-of-Squares. This transfers degree lower bounds to structured associative algebras, trilinear forms and formal cubic forms. [Proof](proof-transfer.md) |
