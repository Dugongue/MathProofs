# Four tensor-isomorphism results inspired by Masullo's trace-formula draft

These four proofs use finite algebra and real convexity. Each establishes the result under the hypotheses in its own statement and is independent of GRH.

| Result | Conclusion | Technique |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Short determinantal certificates](01-traces.md) | For any field, detect distinct semisimplifications of marked matrix tuples with a single coefficient of a short word polynomial. Extract the certificate in polynomial time under semisimplicity, including in small characteristic. | Isolate a central component and recover its multiplicity by a determinant coefficient. |
| [Sharp dimensions for étale tensors](02-character-filter.md) | Compute the minimum side dimension for an ordinary multiplication-tensor pair whose extension degrees are exactly the multiples of a given integer. Compute the exact joint GL and SL minimum in all cases, including the parity correction over odd fields. | Power Frobenius cycles and account for the orientation cost through the trace pairing. |
| [Spectral refinement for real tensors](03-rigidity.md) | Decide real GL tensor isomorphism and construct an exact factored transporter in bit-polynomial time on an effectively recognizable balanced class. An infinite rational family exhibits the benefit of the mixed moments. | Use positive polar factors to reduce to orthogonal transport, then recover coordinate lines from commuting mixed contractions and solve the remaining signs. |
| [Exact special-linear pencil counts](04-pencils.md) | Count and construct special-linear transporters of every two-slice tensor, including singular and rectangular pencils, in deterministic polynomial time at fixed field size. | Separate the nilpotent radical from the residue factors and assemble the joint determinant image and its fiber size. |

The four general proofs are informal. [FiniteChecks.lean](FiniteChecks.lean) separately kernel-checks the cyclic determinant calculation over the field of seven elements and the nonsquare calculation over the field of five elements in the pencil examples. The two statements use only the standard axioms reported by Lean 4.33.0. These results concern the stated tensor classes and particular problems from the Grochow–Qiao framework. [Grochow–Qiao on tensor isomorphism](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2021.31)

## Status of the posted GRH manuscript

Ravi Vakil's November 20, 2026 announcement calls this a proposed proof and describes Brian Conrad's review. The posted manuscript concerns Hecke $L$-functions of unitary characters over number fields. Its proposed route filters the global trace through local orbital calculations and uses carefully chosen test-function spaces to retain selected spectral data. The following checks concern the posted version. [Announcement](https://math216.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/hurried-announcement-alessandro-masullo-grh/) · [Masullo, *The Music of the Primes*, §1.7](https://math216.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-music-of-the-primes.pdf#page=35)

1. **The final divisor theorem is unfinished.** On page 522, Theorem 13.3.1.2 lacks a statement and proof and invokes a theorem with an unresolved reference. The preceding theorem reformulates Theorem 12.5.1.1 with changed test conditions and different spectral weights. It replaces vanishing on the norm-one subgroup with Mellin moment vanishing and replaces full multiplicities in a strip with capped multiplicities on the critical line. Already over the rationals a radial bump supported away from norm one can have a nonzero integral, while a radial test of integral zero can be nonzero at norm one, so the two test conditions are distinct. [Theorem 12.5.1.1, p. 519](https://math216.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-music-of-the-primes.pdf#page=519) · [Chapter 13, p. 522](https://math216.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-music-of-the-primes.pdf#page=522)
2. **The displayed uniform Archimedean bound fails for its stated cutoffs.** Take the rational field, the trivial character, and a fixed real parameter greater than one in the prescribed compact set. The nonnegative cutoff tests converge on $t>1$ to a function with right limit $1$ at $t=1$. The limiting density of the real-place integral has a positive $1/(t-1)$ singularity, so Fatou's lemma gives infinite limit inferior. The Montel argument in §1.8.4.3 needs a replacement. [Masullo, §§1.8.4.2–1.8.4.3, pp. 53–54](https://math216.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-music-of-the-primes.pdf#page=54)
3. **The full test space has infinite codimension in the rational example.** At $F=\mathbb Q$ the norm-one idèle class group is $\widehat{\mathbb Z}^{\times}$. In (13.3.1.2), for each character of this compact group the moment of order zero is its Fourier coefficient on the radial average. Choose a smooth compactly supported radial test of integral one and multiply by a chosen character. Character orthogonality makes the moment functionals linearly independent on arbitrarily large finite sets of characters. The full-space codimension asserted on page 522 is therefore false as stated. A single character sector gives a different domain. [Equation (13.3.1.2), p. 522](https://math216.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-music-of-the-primes.pdf#page=522)
4. **The toy Poisson formula omits the period factor.** In §1.2.8, write $L=\log\gamma$ and $g(u)=\varphi(e^u)$. The Fourier coefficients of the periodization are $L^{-1}\mathcal M\varphi(-2\pi i m/L)$ for the Mellin normalization shown there. Evaluation at zero gives $\sum_m\mathcal M\varphi(2\pi i m/L)=L\sum_j\varphi(\gamma^j)$. The support-gap vanishing survives the correction. The later local trace display already includes a logarithmic factor. [Masullo, §1.2.8 and local calculation, pp. 17–19](https://math216.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-music-of-the-primes.pdf#page=17)

This is a targeted reading of the posted argument and its transferable techniques. The published draft does not establish GRH as written.
