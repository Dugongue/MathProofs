# Four advances in tensor, group, and geometric isomorphism

The entries are ranked by breadth, direct bearing on existing tensor-isomorphism results, and likely interest to Joshua Grochow. The proofs are informal and publication priority for the new combinations is unestablished.

| Rank in this round | Result | Exact scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Arbitrary TI in pure symplectic shears](DYNAMICS.md) | Over every field, a linear-dimension construction puts ordinary 3TI inside polynomial conjugacy of maps $(q,p)\mapsto(q,p+\nabla H(q))$ with squarefree cubic $H$. The derivative at the origin is the identity and in characteristic $p$ the maps can all have exact order $p$. Allowing arbitrary nonlinear polynomial conjugators preserves the tensor answer. |
| 2 | [Arbitrary TI in smooth rational Fano varieties of Picard rank two](GEOMETRY.md) | Over every field, there are polynomial-size quadratic equations and an exact criterion by abstract isomorphism. | 3 | [TI-complete groups with the same commuting relation](GROUPS.md) | Over every fixed prime field, compact class-two groups retain arbitrary ordered TI while the commuting relation, complex irreducible character degrees, and the distributions of all bounded-generator marked subgroups are fixed throughout a format. The compact presentation and tensor comparison are explicit. |
| 4 | [Number-field norms in determinant-one binary pencils](PENCILS.md) | Norm membership for a degree-$d$ field is exactly SL-TI of two regular semisimple rational pencils of side $3d$, with identical squarefree determinants and trivial geometric projective stabilizer. A pair of side 12 violates the local-to-global implication despite a supplied ordinary isomorphism. |

## How the four results connect

The previous dynamics construction used Jordan levels to recover three distinguished tensor modes. The new construction encodes them in the polarization of a squarefree cubic. A hyperbolic pairing makes the centroid scalar and collapses the two independent changes of basis arising from a general polynomial conjugacy to a single change of variables up to scalar. This allows the entire linear part of the dynamics to be the identity. The exact step works in characteristics two and three. The note also gives a symmetric-power compilation for fixed-degree form equivalence.

The previous geometry result used seven blowup centers. Here a multiplication criterion on linear forms recovers individually tagged variable blocks inside a space of quadrics. The associated incidence hypersurface is a projective bundle and its anticanonical class is ample. The recovered base and both ambient projections force the original tensor transporter. The stabilizer calculations transfer the entire arithmetic genus, including the realization of any finite abelian group as the full everywhere-local genus of a rational Fano variety of geometric Picard rank two.

In the finite-group construction, direct-sum cancellation first turns arbitrary tensors into geometrically constant-rank matrix spaces. A second replication hides the entire kernel from every commutator and every rectangle spanned by at most a prescribed number of generators. Fourier duality fixes the character-degree distribution. The different noncentral class sizes still recover the two quotient modes and hence the whole tensor. For cubic side $N$, all probes by groups generated by up to $12N-1$ elements have identical homomorphism and embedding counts throughout the constructed family. The binary-pencil result isolates a different effect. Ordinary similarity is already known for the pair. A quadratic extension supplies a second norm factor absorbed by the first, and a translation removes all geometric projective symmetries. The remaining special-linear question is exactly a single input field norm. The same analysis gives deterministic polynomial-time algorithms for a wider split-component subclass of rational pencils. The reduction itself makes no assertion about the complexity of general number-field norm membership.
