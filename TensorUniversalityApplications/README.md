# Four applications of tensor isomorphism universality

The [universality proof for Grochow–Qiao Open Question 7.4](../GrochowProofTransfersIII/UNIVERSAL-TENSOR-ISOMORPHISM.md) forces each auxiliary change of basis through named product and evaluation maps. These four applications use that approach to control output dimensions, recover the original coefficient field, test witness constraints, and represent tensor products of presented modules by free tensors.

| Application | Theorem |
| --- | --- |
| [Ordinary 3-tensor dimension](BALANCED-TENSOR-DIMENSION.md) | An explicit $d\mathrm{TI}$ to $3\mathrm{TI}$ reduction of maximum side $O(d^7 n^{\lceil d/3\rceil})$. This attains the relative exponent in Open Question 7.8 as $d$ grows. The exponent of $n$ is optimal within affine algebraically equivariant reductions. The same construction family removes the exponential dependence on the number of parts for ordered partitioned 3-tensor isomorphism. |
| [Same-characteristic finite fields](SAME-CHARACTERISTIC-TENSOR-ISOMORPHISM.md) | For every fixed prime $p$, the 3-tensor isomorphism problems over any two fields of characteristic $p$ and finite cardinality are polynomial-time equivalent, uniformly in the extension degrees. This answers the same-characteristic part of Open Question 7.6. |
| [Witness search for alternating spaces](LINEAR-SIDE-AMSI.md) | Finds an isometry or counts all isometries with at most $1+nq^n$ decision queries of matrix order at most $48n+6$. The matrix order is independent of the dimension of the given matrix spaces. This meets the quantitative request in preprint Open Question 10.5. |
| [Free tensors over commutative rings](PRESENTED-MODULE-TENSOR-ISOMORPHISM.md) | Reduces 3-tensor isomorphism on explicitly finitely presented modules to ordinary free 3-tensor isomorphism over the same commutative ring. As a consequence, free 3-tensor isomorphism over any presented quotient of the coefficient ring reduces to the corresponding problem over the ring itself. |

Combining scalar restriction with the quotient reduction gives a uniform deterministic reduction from 3-tensor isomorphism over a presented finite field to integral free 3-tensor isomorphism, even with the characteristic as part of the input. The downward scalar restriction uses one named endomorphism per mode and has polynomial bit cost with variable characteristic. The integral encoding of prime-field tensors has a single rational orbit for each source format and prime. Over the rationals the presented modules are zero. Over the $p$-adic integers the same integral tensor comparison recovers the prime-field instance.
