# Four results from quotient and fiber lifting

These are informal proofs. The surface approximation questions and the cited injective poset question were answered by earlier authors. This packet gives stronger counting statements for the constructions and restricted algorithms for two other questions. No claim of first publication is made.

1. **[Counting additional shortest surface cycles](Surface.md).** Given a Boolean circuit, construct a closed orientable unweighted triangulated surface, a homology class over the binary field, and one explicit shortest representative. Its other shortest representatives are in bijection with the satisfying inputs. Thus deciding whether another exists is NP-complete on the supplied-optimum instances, and counting the others is #P-hard by a parsimonious reduction. The proof shows why every optimal lift is uniform on each chamber. The earlier approximation questions were already answered by Borradaile, Maxwell and Nayyeri. The [finite check](check_surface.py) verifies 72 terminal cuts, the manifold links and coherent orientations of two closed gluings, and the binary independent-set gadget on all 1,024 simple labeled graphs on five vertices.
2. **[Antichain counting at order dimension three](Combinatorics.md).** Exact counting is #P-complete under polynomial-time counting reductions, and parity is ⊕P-complete, already on products of an explicitly presented dimension-two poset and a chain, with product dimension exactly three. Counting is polynomial at dimension two. The source of the hardness is Dittmer and Pak's theorem on linear extensions of dimension-two posets. That theorem already answered Cooper and Kirkpatrick's question about injective occurrences in a chain.
3. **[Epimorphisms for Abelian Sylow towers](Groups.md).** The table-input algorithm finds a surjection and counts all surjections if, in each simple module type of each target layer in a compatible tower order, all but a bounded number of summands share one exponent. The number of layers, the ranks of the actions, the exponents themselves, and the multiplicities at the chosen exponents can grow. Grochow, Srivastava and Thakkar's unrestricted Question 3(4) remains open here.
4. **[Characteristic-two matrix-tuple isometry](Matrix.md).** An arbitrary-size noncommutative residue factor can be retained without enumeration when it acts invisibly on the graded obstruction spaces in the stated sense and its unitary group has no quotient of order two. After algebra decomposition, the runtime is polynomial times two to the number of bits in the active noncommutative factors that must be enumerated. This extends the earlier commutative-quotient result. The unrestricted matrix-tuple question remains open here.
