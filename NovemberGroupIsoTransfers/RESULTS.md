# Four transfers from the November group isomorphism manuscripts

These informal proofs use [Qiao–Zhang, Testing isomorphism of finite groups in time polynomial in the group order](https://github.com/sigact-stoc/stoc2027/releases/download/papers-2/1723.pdf), and [Zhang–Wang–Zhuang, On Applications of Polynomial-Time Group Isomorphism Algorithm](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.18084v1). The cited manuscript interfaces have been checked against the numbered statements. The full upstream proofs and publication priority have not been independently established.

1. **Three-tensor isomorphism with a residue-field exponential bound.** Over $\mathbb Z/p^e\mathbb Z$ and standard Galois rings, arbitrary free three-tensors have complete isomorphism cosets in $q^{O(n_1+n_2+n_3)}\operatorname{poly}(L)$ bit time, where $q$ is the residue field size and $L$ includes the binary modulus. This also handles prescribed determinants for free tensors and nonfree modules in explicit cyclic or table formats. Congruence stabilizers enter each lifting step as a computable translation subspace. The remaining quotient is an affine cubic transporter on the original residue modules. [Proof](LocalLifting.md)
2. **Search and counting for marked group epimorphisms.** For arbitrary Cayley-table source groups and target radical with $d$ abelian layers of generator rank at most $r$, decide, find and count epimorphisms in $N^{O(1+dr+r^2)}$ time, including pointwise constraints. The solvable-target case is unconditional. This covers parts of [Grochow–Srivastava–Thakkar Open Question 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07429v1#S7). [Proof](Group.md)
3. **Complete sum-rank code equivalence in singly exponential time.** Split auxiliary permuted blocks by subset stabilizers while retaining exact quotient and fiber actions. The resulting algorithm handles arbitrary fixed-order tensors with up to three unrestricted modes and supplied auxiliary blocks of bounded local action. For sum-rank codes it gives $q^{O(k+b+\sum_j(a_j+b_j))}\operatorname{poly}(L)$ including independent block operations, synchronized block permutations and optional square-block transposes. [Proof](Tensor.md)
4. **Polynomial-time cosets for finite nonassociative rings given by tables.** A marked unital envelope and a Heisenberg group of order at most $N^9$ recover any biadditive multiplication of an $N$-element abelian group, including Lie and Jordan rings. The note also gives an independent marked construction for nonfree tensor elements over chain rings. Its bounded-chain-length complexity is superseded by the residue-layer proof above when its stronger algorithmic interface is available. [Proof](Finite.md)
